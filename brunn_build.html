◆ Background: A dwarf born to the craft, Brunn has dedicated his life to perfecting the art of blacksmithing and armorcraft. Forged in the heat of the forge, he seeks to create the finest weapons and armor to protect those he holds dear.

◆ Mission Statement: Establish a legendary forge and create the finest armor and weapons in all the lands to protect kingdoms and earn lasting renown.)

◆ First Quest Note: Brunn looted a bag of dirt and threw it away. GM exposed weakness with title "Wasteful".

◆ First Quest (Level 1): Brunn's first task was to retrieve mining tools stolen from the Stonekeep dwarves' workshop. A simple task that tested his growing skills, tracking thieves through the mountain passes, negotiating with local merchants, and recovering the stolen goods. Upon completion, Brunn earned his first level and was able to select his Class Tier Abilities as a Barbarian, choosing to focus on Spell-Drawn techniques to complement his natural strength and combat prowess.
