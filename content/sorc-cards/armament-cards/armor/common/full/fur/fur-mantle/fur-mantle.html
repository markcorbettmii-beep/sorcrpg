SorC Armament, Gear, and Equipment Overview

Armaments are the core of SorC’s equipment system, representing all combat-related gear—both weapons and armor. Together with Gear, Items, and Materials, Armaments define your effectiveness in battle, your versatility in exploration, and your opportunities for customization as your character grows.

Equipment Types

  • Armaments: Weapons and armor for combat. (e.g., swords, shields, plate, fur)
  • Gear: Practical tools and supplies for survival and exploration. (e.g., backpacks, ropes, torches)
  • Items: Miscellaneous and narrative objects, including relics, quest tokens, and symbolic artifacts.
  • Materials: Raw crafting and repair components, from metals and woods to magical reagents.

Each type has a distinct role: Armaments for combat, Gear for utility, Items for story and quests, and Materials for crafting and enhancement.

Armament Mechanics

Weapons have stats for Hit, Damage, Accuracy, and Speed. Armor provides defense (Armor Score/AS), resistances, and may affect movement. Both can be enhanced through upgrades, enchantments, socketing, combining, and other methods—each requiring professional skills and carrying risks of failure. All enhancements, modifications, and magical effects are tracked on index cards, which override base stats.

Armaments are acquired by crafting, discovery, purchase, or as loot. Their rarity (common to unique) and condition (excellent, fair, broken) are randomized, affecting their stats, value, and potential. Full armor sets may offer bonuses, but swapping key pieces can break set effects.

Enhancement & Customization

  • Upgrading: Increases stats (e.g., AS, damage).
  • Socketing: Adds magical effects (stones, runes).
  • Enchanting: Imbues with magical properties.
  • Combining/Infusing: Merges traits from two similar items.
  • Nesting: Lines armor for survival trait boosts.
  • Modifying: Adds or calibrates attachments.
  • Resonating: Increases durability and repair intervals.
  • Inscribing: Applies sacred or cultist symbols for synergy and unique bonuses.

Customization carries the risk of breakage or failure, especially with rare materials or high-level modifications.

Quality, Rank, and Condition

Armaments and armor are graded and ranked by grade, material, structure, quality, and rarity. Higher grades allow for more enhancements, while condition affects performance and durability. Maintenance and repairs are essential, especially after intense encounters or when using high-rank gear.

Encumbrance & Carrying Limits

Carrying capacity is determined by race, physique, and level. Overloading limits movement or causes incapacitation; within limits, penalties may apply but characters remain functional. Party members or companions can help carry excess equipment.

Weapons & Crafting

Weapons vary from basic (common, unmodifiable) to legendary (unique, highly customizable). Crafting depends on available materials, cultural expertise, and the craftsman’s proficiency. Cultural and lineage armaments require special prerequisites and prestige, not just attributes.

Binding occurs when armaments are crafted, quested, purchased, or traded; unbound items are typically drops or auction finds and become bound upon enhancement.

Summary

The SorC Armament System offers deep options for customization, strategic risk/reward, and character development. Whether through crafting, acquisition, or magical enhancement, every armament can reflect your character’s journey and choices. For more detail, refer to the SorC Card Index and rules sections.
