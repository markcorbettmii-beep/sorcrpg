SorC Armament, Gear, and Equipment Overview

Armaments form the core of SorC’s equipment system, encompassing both weapons and armor used for combat. Alongside Gear, Items, and Materials, Armaments determine your battle effectiveness, customizability, and progression throughout your adventure.

Equipment Types

  • Armaments: Weapons and armor for combat (e.g., swords, shields, plate, fur).
  • Gear: Utility tools and survival equipment (e.g., backpacks, ropes, torches).
  • Items: Narrative and symbolic objects (e.g., relics, quest tokens, heirlooms).
  • Materials: Raw crafting and repair components (e.g., metals, woods, magical reagents).

Each category has a unique function: Armaments for combat, Gear for utility, Items for story and quests, Materials for crafting and enhancement.

Armament Mechanics

Weapons feature stats for Hit, Damage, Accuracy, and Speed. Armor provides defense (Armor Score/AS), resistances, and may affect movement. Both can be enhanced by upgrading, enchanting, socketing, combining, and other methods, each requiring professional skills and carrying a risk of failure. All enhancements and magical effects are tracked on index cards, which override base stats during play.

Armaments are acquired through crafting, discovery, purchase, or loot. Their rarity (common to unique) and condition (excellent, fair, broken) are randomized and affect their stats, value, and upgrade potential.

Armor Sets and Set Bonuses

Armor in SorC is often collected and equipped as a complete set - for example, plate, leather, or fur armor sets. Full sets may grant unique set bonuses that increase defense, grant resistances, or unlock special abilities. These bonuses reward players for keeping matching pieces together.

Only certain pieces - gloves, boots, and helm - can be individually replaced within a set. When any of these are swapped for a piece from another set, the full set bonus is lost, and the set effect is broken. The character then gains the benefits of the new gloves, boots, or helm (including magical enhancements), but loses the original set’s full bonus and the replaced item's bonus. Gloves and boots can only have one enhancement each, and cannot be further enhanced once replaced; enchanted gloves or boots do not stack additional enhancements.

The rest of the armor set may be upgraded, enchanted, or improved as normal. Attempting to enhance gear carries a risk of breakage, with higher-level upgrades increasing this risk. The durability and maintenance of armor is tracked by condition, and repairs may be needed, especially after battles or when using high-rank gear.

Enhancement & Customization

  • Upgrading: Improves stats (e.g., AS, damage).
  • Socketing: Adds magical effects (stones, runes).
  • Enchanting: Imbues with magical properties.
  • Combining/Infusing: Merges enhancements from two similar items.
  • Nesting: Lines armor for survival trait bonuses.
  • Modifying: Adds attachments or calibrates upgrades.
  • Resonating: Increases durability and reduces repair needs.
  • Inscribing: Applies sacred symbols for synergy and unique bonuses.

Customization carries risk, especially with rare materials or high-level modifications; failed enhancements may break the item.

Quality, Rank, and Condition

Every armament is defined by its grade, material, structure, quality, and rarity. Condition (excellent, fair, broken) affects both performance and required maintenance. Higher grade and rarity allow for more enhancements, but also increase upkeep and repair costs.

Encumbrance & Carrying Limits

Carrying capacity is determined by race, physique, stamina, and level. Exceeding your capacity results in movement penalties or incapacitation, restricting combat until load is reduced. Companions and party members can help carry additional gear.

Weapons & Crafting

Weapons range from basic (common, unmodifiable) to legendary (unique, highly customizable). Crafting is influenced by available materials, cultural expertise, and professional proficiency. Cultural and lineage armaments require special prerequisites and prestige, not just attributes.

Armaments are bound when crafted, quested, purchased, or traded; unbound items are typically drops or auction finds and become bound upon enhancement.

Summary

The SorC Armament System provides deep customization, set bonuses, and strategic choices for players. Full armor sets offer powerful bonuses, but swapping gloves, boots, or helm breaks these effects. All gear can be enhanced and modified, with risks and rewards reflecting your character’s journey and choices. For more details, refer to the SorC Card Index and rules sections.
