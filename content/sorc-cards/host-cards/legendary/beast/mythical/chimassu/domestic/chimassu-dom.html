Companion - Guardian

Chimassu Domestic – Lore & History

The Domestic Chimassu is a legendary celestial beast, revered in many ancient times as a guardian spirit that watches over sacred places and lost pathways between worlds. Tales say the Chimassu was created by the gods to serve as a bridge between the mortal realm and the heavens, appearing only in moments of great need or cosmic upheaval.

As a Guardian creature, Chimassu is known for its fierce loyalty and unparalleled strength.

Classes such as; Marksmen Rangers and Barbarian Beast Masters, Talented Scouts or those highly attuned to the primal or celestial forces may attempt to earn any beast or animal as their Companion - Guardian, but only may not be purchased from the SORC store. Domestic Guardians may be purchased.

The standard (not the Wild) Chimassu’s card be acquired from the SORC store, where its spirit is bound and can be summoned by those who possess it. Domestic companions, regardless of type; guardian, pet, agothian, etc do not require loyalty checks as those found in the wild or crafted.

The store-bought Chimassu displays standard enhancement effects and more typical, subdued coloration - a reflection of its mundane origins and ease of acquisition. In contrast, a Chimassu that has been captured from the wild, as with any legendary wild companion, is exceedingly difficult to obtain, and is marked by vibrant, flamboyant colorations - shimmering with radiant hues and rare patterns. These wild-captured Companions are far more striking and attractive, making each one a truly rare collector’s prize and a testament to the skill and dedication of its guardian.

Whether encountered in the wild or acquired through the SORC store, the Chimassu remains a symbol of hope, strength, and the enduring connection between the realms. For more on Companions, refer to section x.001.
