Epic Weapon – Great Bow “Ravenger”

Origins of Ravenger

Ravenger was hewn from obsidian-black yew and strung with hell-steel cord in the depths of the Hellforge Labyrinth—the infernal maze that houses the Gate of Hell to Beelzebub. Its creator and first wielder was Gorrak Bloodbrand, chieftain of the brutal Red Orcs (often called “Hell Orcs”). Rumor claims each arrow loosed from Ravenger carries a sliver of the Gate’s eternal flame.

Gorrak Bloodbrand

Gorrak rose from gladiatorial slave to overlord by sheer force and ruthless ingenuity. He bargained with a demon lord for an hour of access to the Hellforge. In that single hour he forged Ravenger, branding its limbs with sigils of rage and conquest. The bow’s “voice” is said to whisper targets’ true names, guiding each shot unerringly to its mark.

The Red Orcs (Hell Orcs)

Red Orcs are the mightiest strain among all Orc-kind—spawn of drow cruelty and ogre brawn, their bloodlines forever tainted by fiendish lust. They command most other Orc subspecies through fear and superior strength. Their skin glows faintly like banked embers; battle frenzy can make them erupt in streaks of molten crimson.

Wars of the Frozen North

Despite their dominance, the Red Orcs wage a constant war against the Arctic Orcs and the frost-born Blizzard Orcs who guard the Northern Ice Wastes. Paradoxically, Gorrak maintains an uneasy alliance with the Ice Orcs (“White Orcs”) of the Southern Arctic, trading obsidian weapons for rare glacial ores. In these frigid theatres Ravenger’s black shafts streak across white horizons like cracks of midnight, felling chieftains and ice wyrms alike.

Current Status

After Gorrak vanished within the Gate’s inner chambers, Ravenger was lost. Demon-scarred legends speak of the bow re-emerging only when a warrior’s hatred burns hot enough to melt its own restraints. Those who seek it should beware: the weapon chooses its wielder—and it has tasted only Orcish wrath thus far.
