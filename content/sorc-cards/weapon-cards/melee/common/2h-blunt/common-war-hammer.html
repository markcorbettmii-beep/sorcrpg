Description

The Common War Hammer (2h) is the standard two-handed blunt weapon of the Avenger-Cleric path. Its iron head is forged wide and heavy, designed to drive through chain and ring mail with force rather than edge. The ash handle runs long enough for full two-handed leverage, making each swing a committed and powerful blow.

Dwarf-forged war hammers are known for exceptional balance between head weight and handle length - a craft refined over generations in the deep Blacksmithing holds. A Dwarf Cleric wielding one brings both the weight of conviction and the weight of iron to every encounter.

The Common War Hammer (2h) is included in the Level 1-5 Starter Set as the primary starting armament for the Avenger-Cleric path (Set 2: 2h blunt). ATTS (+12) is added to every d100 attack roll, raising hit percentage above the base rates listed in the Base Hit Rate table. Damage is 2d6 + x, where x = STR + any other modifiers. Chain and plate wearers take an AS penalty against blunt, making them priority targets for this weapon.
