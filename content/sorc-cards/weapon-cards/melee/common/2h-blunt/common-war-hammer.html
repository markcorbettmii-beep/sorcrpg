Description

The Common War Hammer is the standard two-handed blunt weapon of the Avenger-Cleric path. Its iron head is forged wide and heavy, designed to drive through chain and ring mail with force rather than edge. The ash handle runs long enough for full two-handed leverage, making each swing a committed and powerful blow.

Dwarf-forged war hammers are known for exceptional balance between head weight and handle length - a craft refined over generations in the deep Blacksmithing holds. A Dwarf Cleric wielding one brings both the weight of conviction and the weight of iron to every encounter.

The Common War Hammer is included in the Level 1-5 Starter Set as the primary starting armament for the Avenger-Cleric path (Set 2: 2h blunt). Damage values, weapon score, and special properties are listed on the physical card.
