Book of Ignis

Glacies et Ignis, commonly called Ignis, is the first planet from Adoria: an extreme elemental world of heat, fire, golems, and elementals. The Emberscale and Primal peoples live here, reading the planet's violent changes as part of daily life.

Wondrous Places of Ignis

Ignis is a world where the landscape is alive with heat, pressure, flame, and elemental force. Its wondrous places are not safe simply because they are sacred; many are both pilgrimage sites and active hazards.

  • Marshy Hot Springs: Heated wetlands where mineral water gathers between volcanic vents and elemental growth.
  • The Lava Sea and Volcan Islands: A moving sea of molten rock surrounding islands that rise and collapse with the planet's pressure.
  • The Boiling Marsh: A steaming basin where unstable ground, hot water, and fire-born life meet.
  • The Ash Fields: Broad black expanses crossed by Emberscales, Primal travelers, fire elementals, and wandering golems.

Sacred Places of Ignis

  1. Pyraxis, the Heartforge Shrine: Within a volcanic caldera, rivers of molten rock converge around an obsidian altar. Emberscales, Primal, and fire elementals gather here to forge relics in communion with Ignis.
  2. The Ember Sanctum: A cavern lit by ever-burning crystal flames. Its walls pulse with primordial heat, and many believe it marks the beginning of fire-born life.
  3. The Cathedral of Ash: A black-stone formation veiled in ash where pilgrims seek visions through silence, heat, and the movement of elemental spirits.
  4. The Infernal Oracle: A deep fissure from which prophetic flames rise. Its interpreters read the changing fire as signs from the planet's living core.
