Module-Based Campaigns & Campaign Text

Play unique campaigns where the worlds of SorC change at your party’s hands and desires. Continue campaigns that ended with periodic expansions. These campaigns allow for dynamic storytelling, player-driven world changes, and modular adventures that grow with the group.

Definitions & Terms (Consolidated)

Below is a comprehensive list of terms used throughout SorC, including definitions and mechanics for gameplay, combat, resources, and more.

Core Terms

  • Player: Participants creating and narrating characters in SorC.
  • Character: The fictional persona controlled by a player, with traits, abilities, backgrounds.
  • Game Master (GM): The facilitator guiding the game’s narrative and mechanics.
  • Host: Creatures, NPCs, or entities in the game world.
  • Race / Species: Ethnic/cultural and biological classifications affecting abilities.
  • Monster / Creature: Hostile or neutral entities in the world.
  • Mythical Creature / Beast: Legendary, powerful beings.
  • Flora / Fauna: Plant and animal life contributing to the environment.
  • Higher Power / Humanoid / Mythical Beast / Critter / Reptilian / Elemental / Extraterrestrial: Various species and beings with unique traits.

Additional Terms

  • Class: Archetype role dictating abilities.
  • Attributes: Innate qualities like AGI, ART, INT, etc.
  • Talents: Non-combat skills learned via vocational trees.
  • Skills: Crafting, gathering, and other proficiencies.
  • Abilities: Combat techniques, spells, and special moves.
  • Traits: Survival characteristics like Courage, Vigilance, Resistance.
  • Vitality: Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance resources.
  • Gear & Items: Equipment, loot, buffs, debuffs, consumables.

Game & Combat Terms

  • Mission, Quest, Dungeon, Raid, AP, SP
  • XP, HP, AP, TP, DPS, AoE, CC, DoT, HoT
  • PvE, PvP, PK, LFM, AFK, OT, MT, RNG, OOC, OOS, LOS

Additional Resources & Misc

  • Guild, Gear, Link, Rare Find, Nerf, Buff, Debuff, Res
  • Set Bonus, Kite, Wipe, etc.

Campaign & Module Summary

In SorC, campaigns are modular adventures that evolve through player choices and expansions. The last sections include detailed mechanics about movement, combat, and resource management, as well as terms and definitions essential for understanding the game’s mechanics.

Terms & Definitions

Player: Participants creating characters and narrating their actions.

Character: Fictional persona controlled by a player, with unique traits, abilities, and backgrounds.

Game Master (GM): Facilitator guiding narrative and mechanics, resolving outcomes.

Host: Creatures, NPCs, monsters, or entities in the game world.

Race: An ethnic or cultural group within a species, affecting appearance and abilities.

Species: Broader biological classification with unique traits.

Monster: Hostile beings creating challenges.

Creature: Any living entity, including NPCs, animals, or monsters.

Mythical Creature: Legendary beings with magical traits.

Beast: Ferocious, powerful animals or monsters.

Flora & Fauna: Plant life and animals contributing to the environment.

Higher Power Species: Beings with divine or extraordinary abilities.

Humanoid: Races resembling humans like elves, dwarves.

Mythical Beast: Legendary, powerful creatures.

Critter: Small, often harmless creatures.

Reptilian & Elemental Species: Reptiles, elementals, extraterrestrials.

Additional Definitions

  • Class: Character role or archetype.
  • Attributes: Core qualities like AGI, ART, INT, etc.
  • Talents: Non-combat abilities learned through vocational trees.
  • Skills: Crafting, gathering, and non-combat proficiencies.
  • Abilities: Combat techniques, special moves, and magic.
  • Traits: Survival characteristics like Courage, Evasion, Resistance.

Vitality & Equipment

  • Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance
  • Gear: Equipment, Loot, Buffs, Debuffs

Game Features & Terminology
