Progression

Note to Reader
At the end of this article you'll find various linked articles to help support this text, including Combat, Dice n Movement, and more.
Each player character is now primarily defined by three key components:
Level (reflecting experience and growth)
Rank (determined by Rank Points and gear)
Combat Power (CP) (determined by class level and rank)

Experience

Level
A character’s Level reflects their overall experience (XP) and growth. Levels provide a Base CP and unlock access to additional abilities, talents, and skills.

XP Acquisition & Distribution
Characters earn XP from combat, exploration, roleplaying, crafting, feats, and achievements. XP is typically shared among party members, but individual tasks grant XP directly to the responsible character.

Milestone vs. Incremental XP
Campaigns may use milestone leveling, where major story events trigger immediate level increases, independent of XP totals.

Encounter Balance, and Rank System

Rank and Rank Points (RP)
Rank Points (RP)
RP measure a character’s achievements and heroic exploits separate from XP.
RP are earned through exploration, overcoming challenges, performing exceptional actions, and gear.
RP accumulate over time and determine the character's Rank.

Determining Rank
The character's Rank is directly determined by their total RP.
As RP increase, the character advances to higher Ranks.
Conversely, if gear is removed or diminished, resulting in RP loss, the character’s Rank can decrease.
Gear worn or carried affects RP, which in turn influences Rank.

Rank Titles
The current Rank titles are as follows:
Pauper (Starting Rank; RP threshold 0)
Peasant
Commoner
Adventurer
Hero
Elite
Legend
(Additional titles and thresholds can be added or adjusted based on campaign needs)

Important:
Rank is not solely based on RP; it is directly linked to gear and equipment.
Gear quality and quantity determine how many RP a character has, thus affecting their Rank.
Gear upgrades and improvements increase RP and can promote Rank.

Combat Power (CP)
CP Calculation
CP is determined by a character’s class level and Rank.
Total CP = Base CP (from Level) + Rank Bonus + Modifiers (gear, traits, abilities).
Base CP is derived from level and may be scaled according to campaign rules.
Rank Bonus depends on the current Rank, influenced by gear and RP.

Rank and CP Relationship
Higher Rank, as determined by RP, provides a CP bonus.
CP reflects a character’s combat effectiveness, influenced by gear, abilities, and traits.

Gear and Item Value (Related to RP and CP)
(Remains largely unchanged but now aligns with the new system)
Gear and armaments contribute to RP and CP.
Gear quality and rarity, influenced by encounter rank and condition rolls, affect item value and CP bonuses.
Enhanced gear increases CP further, based on quality and modifications.
