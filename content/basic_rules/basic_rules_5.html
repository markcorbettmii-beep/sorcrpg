Game Master (GM) Rules

A Game Master (GM) is the primary organizer, storyteller, and referee in a tabletop role-playing game (RPG).

The GM is responsible for creating and narrating the world, describing scenarios, controlling non-player characters (NPCs), and adjudicating the rules.

The GM sets the scene, presents challenges, resolves disputes, and ensures that gameplay is fair, engaging, and fun for all participants. While players control their own characters, the GM manages everything else in the game world, adapting the story and encounters in response to player choices and actions.

In summary: The Game Master is both the architect and narrator of the adventure, guiding players through the unfolding story and making sure the game runs smoothly.
