Talents

Talents are specialized actions acquired from Vocational Trees, used when characters face interactive challenges in the world. Talents are chosen at creation and improved as you level. Each Talent is tied to a Vocational Tree, and using it requires the right tools in your inventory. Successes grow Talent experience; failures can set you back. To use a Talent, roll the relevant dice, add modifiers, and compare to the challenge’s Difficulty Score.

Examples:
The Scout: Carving a branch to use as a lever (roll d20 + Artistry + Branches).
The Olympiad: Hurdling a canyon (roll d20 + Agility + Hurdle).
The Hacker: Decoding a lock (roll d20 + Intellect + Bypass).
The Thief: Stealing a trinket (roll d20 + Agility + Escape Artist).

Talents require matching attribute scores to improve, and performances are always influenced by gear, environment, and situational bonuses or penalties.

  • Success: +2% progress; Natural 20: +5%; Natural 1: -2%.

Talent Modifiers

  • Bonuses: Good tools/environment (+1 to +3), enchanted gear (+2+), story/GM bonus (+1 to +5).
  • Penalties: Hazards (-1 to -3), poor tools (-2+), fatigue or status (-1 to -4).

Modifiers stack up to ±5 for balance. Example: Sense Danger at night (+2), heavy rain (-2) = net +0.

Movement & Stamina

Movement is tactical and costs stamina (SP). Each combat turn is 6 seconds; each second of activity uses 1 SP. Stamina regenerates 1 SP per 3 seconds of rest in combat, 1 SP per minute out of combat. When SP falls below 25%, movement speed drops by 25% and SP costs are 50% higher. At 0 SP, you’re Exhausted and can’t act until you regain at least 25% SP.

Endurance is your total number of natural turns per day. Base speed is by race size: Goliath 40 ft, Standard 30 ft, Small 25 ft.

Initiative

Initiative order is determined by:
Formula: (2 × AGI Score) + STR Score + (Base Speed × Modifier)
Modifier is 1 by default, adjusted for buffs/debuffs (ex: 1.2 for a 20% speed buff).
Attributes, traits, and GM calls may further influence initiative.

Combo Attribute Checks

Some challenges use multiple attributes (ex: climbing rope = STR + ART, fast climb also uses AGI). Success may lower SP cost, failure increases it or causes setbacks.

Travel & The Wayfarer System

Wayfarers can be upgraded through talents, navigation, and exploration milestones. Taverns and hearths offer rest, minigames, and teleport options. As you develop your Wayfarer, you’ll unlock new travel abilities and teleportation features, including linking to specific hearths for fast travel.

Key Concepts Recap

  • Movement types have clear SP costs; STR/AGI and load affect speed and stamina use.
  • Low stamina reduces speed and increases costs; careful management is key.
  • Survival Traits can help mitigate movement costs and penalties.

Terms & Definitions

  • Game Master (GM): Storyteller and rule adjudicator.
  • Host: NPCs, monsters, and entities in the world.

  • Talents/Skills/Abilities/Traits: Personal strengths and learned actions.
  • Vitality: Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance.
  • Gear & Items: Equipment and consumables.
  • Factions, Reputation, Titles, Achievements: Social systems and rewards.
  • Common Abbreviations: XP, HP, AP, SP, DPS, AoE, CC, DoT, HoT, PvE, PvP, PK, AFK, RNG, OOC, LOS, etc.
