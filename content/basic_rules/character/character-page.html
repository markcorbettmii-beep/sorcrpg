Armaments: Armor Sets & Weapons

Armor Sets: Full sets consist of Chest/Legs, Head (Helm), Gloves, and Boots. Swapping out gloves, boots, or helm for pieces from another set removes the set bonus but grants the new item's stats.
Note: Gloves and boots may have only one enhancement each; helm, gloves, and boots are interchangeable from set, but lose set bonus if replaced. Chest/Leg piece may have multiple enhancements.
  • Default Scrubs (Cloth Armor Set) (No Set Bonus)
    Basic cloth attire, providing no combat bonuses. Designed for peasants and non-combatants; easily replaced or upgraded.
  • Weapon Types (Unarmed - Improvised)
    At level 1, peasants may wield simple tools or improvised weapons (sticks, stones, farming implements). Upgrade to swords, axes, bows, or magical foci as you advance.
  • Shield (Improvised or Basic)
    No shield equipped. Basic shields may be crafted or found; offer minimal protection until upgraded.

Armament & Accessory Details

  • Enhancement Limits
    Gloves/Boots: one enhancement max. Chest/Legs: several enhancements. Helm/Gloves/Boots interchangeable but lose set bonus.
  • Upgrade & Enchant
    Upgrades by blacksmiths, socketing by jewelers and runesmiths, enchantments by enchanters, infusing by alchemists, nesting by hunters, modification by engineers, resonation by artisans, inscription by cultists/theologians.
  • Condition & Quality
    Rolls determine if armor is excellent, fair, or broken. Quality and rank affect armor score and enhancements.
  • Set Bonuses
    Wearing a full set grants bonuses; swapping helm, gloves, or boots loses the bonus but applies new item stats.

Gear & Equipment

  • Gear (Non-combat Equipment)
    Tools for crafting and skills (utility tool, flint and steel), containers (backpacks, tool kits, water skins, pouches), light sources (torches, lamps), ritual supplies (holy water, relics), and miscellaneous supplies (first aid kits, exploration gear).
  • Items
    Everyday objects with practical or narrative value (candles, crests, quest tokens, heirlooms, keys, rare trinkets).
  • Materials
    Raw resources for crafting, repair, or trade (metals, woods, herbs, magical reagents).
  • Bag Space & Weight
    Inventory management: bag space, sheaths, coin bags. Weight capacity determined by strength + stamina; exceeding capacity results in encumbrance and incapacitation until load is reduced.
  • Encumbrance & Carrying Capacity
    Race and Physique determine base capacity; Ogre Physique 10 (+5 racial) = 15, Gnome Physique 10 (-5 racial) = 5. Movement penalties and incapacitation above limits.
