Armaments: Armor Sets & Weapons

Armor Sets: Full sets consist of Harness (curraiss, pauldrons, belt and greaves)Head (Helm), Gloves, and Boots. The harness of a set may not be broken. Swapping out gloves, boots, or helm from an armor set, removes the set bonus in favor of the new items’ stats. Shields Shields, though primarily designed for defense, can be used as a weapon if characters possess the rightability. Shields are typically heavy and are used by characters with a high strength.
Note: helm, gloves, and boots are interchangeable from set, but lose set bonus if replaced. Each may have only one enhancement, while the Harness may have multiple enhancements. Although belts are part of the harness, belt BUCKLES are accessories that may be latched onto belts with no penalty to the armor's set bonus.
  • Default Scrubs (Cloth)
    Basic cloth attire, providing no combat bonuses. All characters begin their journeys in basic clothing that reflects their background.
  • Weapon Types (Unarmed - Improvised)
    At level 1, peasants (rank) may wield simple tools or improvised weapons (sticks, stones, farming implements). Upgrade to swords, axes, bows, or magical foci as you advance.
  • Ammo n utility slots: Arrows, bolts, spell foci, or tool kits.

Armament & Accessory Details

  • Enhancement Limits
    Gloves/Boots: one enhancement max. Chest/Legs: several enhancements. Helm/Gloves/Boots interchangeable but lose set bonus.
  • Enhancements
    Enhancements such as upgrades by blacksmiths, socketing by jewelers and runesmiths, enchantments by enchanters, infusing by alchemists, nesting by hunters, modification by engineers, resonation by artisans, inscriptions by cultists/theologians.
  • Condition & Quality
    Upon acquisition the condition and quality of armaments is rolled for, while Rank (rarity) is determined by its source (for instance a heroic troll may drop a heroic weapon). Conditions are either: excellent, fair, or broken and broken items may be repaired requiring mats and services. Quality, such as “exquisite” helps determine value in currency.
  • Set Bonuses
    Wearing a full set grants bonuses; swapping helm, gloves, or boots loses the bonus but applies new item stats.

Appearance

  • Determined by Race and Armaments/Attire
    Each character’s base appearance is first set by their chosen race’s size; goliath, standard and small. Players then select from a set of face types that allow minor visual changes, including scars, tattoos and piercings, or players may upload their own (pending approval for size, style, and decency).
  • Attribute: Physique (Trait-based)
    The attribute Physique is determined by the character’s Strength and Agility traits. Higher Physique means a character appears more muscular, and can sustain more weight. Characters visually progress from thin to lean to muscular to massive as their Physique increases.
  • Scars, Body Art, Piercings, Armaments & Attire
    Scars, body art (paint or tattoos), piercings, and the Armaments and/or attire they're wearing are all part of their appearance. Character appearance is displayed on their Armament & Gear page, and their portrait is shown on their cover sheet (see Character Creation).

Weight Capacity

  • How It’s Calculated
    Weight Capacity is determined by the trait Strength or by buffs and enhancements, including potions and scrolls such as “Feathered Burden.”
  • Burdened & Encumbered
    Characters can carry any weight up to their capacity determined by their STR score. When they reach their capacity, they become burdened and begin taking movement penalties. At some point beyond this, they become encumbered and are rooted until the load is reduced.
  • Assisted Carrying & Storage
    Players may use “fellowship cards” (e.g., draft animals, mounts) and/or hosts such as “hirelings” to help carry goods. The Armament & Gear page only displays what is on the character's person. Other possessions may be stashed, stored at home (often displayed), in a bank vault or other. All things either have a card associated with them or there is text to define it. Many of these items can be traded between players, the auction house, or sold to vendors. Bound items may not be traded but can be destroyed or sundered (see professions for more on sundering).

Gear, Equipment & Items Overview

  • Gear (Utility & Survival)
    Practical tools and supplies such as backpacks, ropes, torches, containers, light sources, first aid kits, and ritual tools. Essential for exploration, crafting, and overcoming environmental challenges.
  • Items (Narrative & Miscellaneous)
    Everyday objects, quest tokens, heirlooms, keys, trinkets, and symbolic relics. Items enrich roleplay, provide story hooks, and serve as catalysts for crafting, enhancement, and quest progression.
  • Materials (Crafting & Repair)
    Raw components such as metals, woods, herbs, alchemical reagents, and special resources. Used for crafting new gear, repairing equipment, and fulfilling trade or quest objectives.
  • Bag Space § Weight
    Inventory management includes bag space, sheaths, flasks, tool kits, coin bags and more. Weight capacity = Strength + Stamina (per level). Exceeding capacity causes burdening and encumbrance, causing slowing or incapacitation respectively until load is reduced.
  • Encumbrance & Carrying Capacity
    Base capacity is determined by race and physique. Ogre (base PHY +5 racial) = 5, Gnome (Physique -5 racial) = - 5. Movement penalties apply if loaded, and total incapacitation occurs if overloaded. Smaller races, such as Lings and Gnomes at - 5 usually rely on the help of a companion.
  • Equipment Acquisition
    Gear, items, and materials can be purchased, crafted, discovered, looted, or rewarded. Some gear may be lost, stolen, or broken. At each level there is a maintenance fee for thingslike ammo, repairs and so on. The cost of the maintenance fee is determined by the character's rank.
