Armaments: Armor Sets & Weapons

Armor Sets: Full sets consist of Harness (curraiss, pauldrons, belt and greaves)Head (Helm), Gloves, and Boots. The harness of a set may not be broken. Swapping out gloves, boots, or helm from an armor set, removes the set bonus in favor of the new items’ stats.
Note: helm, gloves, and boots are interchangeable from set, but lose set bonus if replaced. Each may have only one enhancement, while the Harness may have multiple enhancements. Although belts are part of the harness, belt BUCKLES are accessories that may be latched onto belts with no penalty to the armor's set bonus.
  • Default Scrubs (Cloth)
    Basic cloth attire, providing no combat bonuses. All characters begin their journeys in basic clothing that reflects their background.
  • Weapon Types (Unarmed - Improvised)
    At level 1, peasants (rank) may wield simple tools or improvised weapons (sticks, stones, farming implements). Upgrade to swords, axes, bows, or magical foci as you advance.
  • Ammo n utility slots: Arrows, bolts, spell foci, or tool kits.
  • Shield (armor or weapon) (Improvised or Basic)
    No shield equipped. Basic shields may be crafted or found; offer minimal protection until upgraded.

Armament & Accessory Details

  • Enhancement Limits
    Gloves/Boots: one enhancement max. Chest/Legs: several enhancements. Helm/Gloves/Boots interchangeable but lose set bonus.
  • Upgrade & Enchant
    Upgrades by blacksmiths, socketing by jewelers and runesmiths, enchantments by enchanters, infusing by alchemists, nesting by hunters, modification by engineers, resonation by artisans, inscription by cultists/theologians.
  • Condition & Quality
    Rolls determine if armor is excellent, fair, or broken. Quality and rank affect armor score and enhancements.
  • Set Bonuses
    Wearing a full set grants bonuses; swapping helm, gloves, or boots loses the bonus but applies new item stats.

Gear, Equipment & Items Overview

  • Gear (Utility & Survival)
    Practical tools and supplies such as backpacks, ropes, torches, containers, light sources, first aid kits, and ritual tools. Essential for exploration, crafting, and overcoming environmental challenges.
  • Items (Narrative & Miscellaneous)
    Everyday objects, quest tokens, heirlooms, keys, trinkets, and symbolic relics. Items enrich roleplay, provide story hooks, and serve as catalysts for crafting, enhancement, and quest progression.
  • Materials (Crafting & Repair)
    Raw components such as metals, woods, herbs, alchemical reagents, and special resources. Used for crafting new gear, repairing equipment, and fulfilling trade or quest objectives.
  • Bag Space & Weight
    Inventory management includes bag space, sheaths, and coin bags. Weight capacity = Strength + Stamina (per level). Exceeding capacity causes encumbrance and incapacitation until load is reduced.
  • Encumbrance & Carrying Capacity
    Base capacity is determined by race and physique. Ogre (Physique 10 +5 racial) = 15, Gnome (Physique 10 -5 racial) = 5. Movement penalties apply if loaded, and total incapacitation occurs if overloaded.
  • Equipment Acquisition
    Gear, items, and materials can be purchased, crafted, discovered, looted, or rewarded. Some gear may be lost, stolen, or broken. Rarity and quality are rolled for when acquired; only uncommon and above may be awarded for uncommon challenges.
