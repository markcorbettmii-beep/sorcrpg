Character Creation

Character creation in SORC begins at the table by selecting a race and class, then allocating Attributes using a combination of dice rolls and point spending. As characters level up, players repeat this process to unlock talents, skills, and Traits (TSTs), tracked through the character's album and profile pages.

Characters also develop rank as they progress in class level. Inventory tracks items without penalty up to encumbrance limits. Each character is assigned a unique ID for online tracking, trading, and achievements.

Character Sheets

  • Character Profile Sheet — Displays key details such as name, race, class, level, and vital statistics, portrait, background, mission statement, and prestige.
  • Lore Sheet — Contains background story, mission statement, home logs, family logs, quest logs, vital info, archives, personal notes, maps, locations, and Wayfarer Watch logs.

Prestige

A character's Prestige measures their identity and reflects how the world perceives them — how NPCs, creatures, and other players respond. It begins developing early based on the character's profile and evolves through feats, downfalls, choices, and interactions. Titles (Boasted and Shamed), reputation, faction affiliation, and legacy all contribute to Prestige.

Religion & Politics

Faith is determined by background by default, but players may choose their own faith or play as an atheist. Players can engage with in-world politics, elections, and the ongoing war between the Noble and Purge forces — or remain entirely ambivalent.

Playable Races — Overview

Choose from 40 races and sub-races across three size categories: Goliath, Standard, and Small. Each race has innate Trait bonuses and penalties. Culture is determined by the player's chosen background and does not change base stats — every human begins with the same stats regardless of culture. Races in grey text are not available for the beginner set.

Goliath Size Races (7–9 ft)

Towering beings known for impressive strength, endurance, and commanding presence.

Bast

Agile feline humanoids with innate magic and a deep connection to nature.

+2 Agility, +1 Dexterity | −2 Toughness, −2 Constitution, −1 Wit

Bugbear

Tall, primal predators with coarse fur, impressive hunting prowess, brute force, and cunning.

+2 Strength, +1 Vigilance | −2 Charisma, −2 Wit, −1 Piety

Centyr

Mythical beings with human torsos and animal lower bodies, distinguished by horns and hooves. Favor Bard classes and ranged weapons.

+2 Agility, +1 Charisma | −2 Toughness, −2 Willpower, −1 Wit

Firbolg

Gentle giants with a profound bond to nature; telepathically communicate with animals and plants.

+2 Intuition, +1 Piety | −2 Deception, −2 Charisma, −1 Presence

Hawkbear

Majestic hybrid creature with the strength of a bear and the flight of a hawk.

+2 Agility, +1 Defense | −2 Wit, −2 Deception, −1 Charisma

Troll (Common)

Highly adaptable and versatile, with thick leathery skin and exceptional resilience in diverse environments.

+2 Toughness, +1 Constitution | −2 Wit, −2 Charisma, −1 Deception

Troll (Bridge)

Dwells near cities and bridges; known for riddles and tolls that test travelers' wit.

+2 Wit, +1 Deception | −2 Strength, −2 Agility, −1 Spirit

Troll (Mountain)

Cold-hardened, formidable climbers and fierce protectors of mountain domains.

+2 Toughness, +1 Resistance | −2 Wit, −2 Deception, −1 Piety

Troll (Sand)

Desert-adapted survivors skilled at camouflaging and enduring extreme heat.

+2 Constitution, +1 Deception | −2 Wit, −2 Charisma, −1 Willpower

Troll (Village)

Stoic protectors of their communities, living in harmony with nature.

+2 Toughness, +1 Piety | −2 Deception, −2 Wit, −1 Agility

Ogre

Towering and muscular, known for immense strength and ferocity in battle.

+3 Strength | −5 Wit

Standard Size Races (5–7 ft)

Versatile and balanced physique suitable for a wide range of roles and Abilities.

Drāco Blood

Dragon-blooded humans with innate charisma and strength, capable of wielding draconic magic.

+2 Charisma, +1 Presence | −2 Piety, −2 Luck, −1 Agility

Dwarf (Arctic)

Cold-weather explorers with exceptional endurance in the harshest icy terrains.

+2 Constitution, +1 Resistance | −2 Charisma, −2 Agility, −1 Wit

Dwarf (Ash)

Volcanic region dwellers uniquely adapted to extreme heat; excel in stonework and smelting steel for martial combat.

+1 Constitution, +1 Artistry, +1 Strength | −2 Charisma, −2 Agility, −1 Dexterity

Dwarf (Common)

Sturdy craftsmen thriving in mountain halls; master miners and forgers.

+1 Toughness, +1 Artistry, +1 Constitution | −2 Charisma, −2 Agility, −1 Wit

Dwarf (Glimmerstone)

Master miners and treasure hunters; possess Mineral Instincts to detect hidden gems and the "Greed" ability for increased rare loot.

+2 Vigilance, +1 Luck | −2 Piety, −2 Agility, −1 Apex

Dwarf (Red)

Mystical volcanic dwellers wielding mastery over fire and molten magic; feared enchanters and spellcasters.

+2 Resistance, +1 Focus | −1 Strength, −1 Defense, −2 Toughness, −1 Agility

Elf (Arch)

Elegant martial masters of sword, shield, and arcane arts; build magnificent kingdoms in mountain forests.

+1 Agility, +2 Wit | −1 Strength, −2 Constitution, −2 Deceit

Elf (Common)

Graceful forest dwellers skilled in archery and nature magic; live in harmony with their woodland environment.

+2 Agility, +1 Wit | −2 Strength, −2 Toughness, −1 Constitution

Elf (High)

Versatile spellcasters and ranged combatants with a deep appreciation for knowledge and tradition.

+2 Agility, +1 Wit | −2 Strength, −2 Constitution, −1 Deception

Helf (Half-Elf)

Adaptable hybrids inheriting traits from both humans and elves; excel in magic, diplomacy, and versatile pursuits.

+2 Agility, +1 Wit | −2 Strength, −2 Toughness, −1 Courage

Elf (Sylvan)

Deeply connected to nature; skilled in wilderness survival and communicating with flora and fauna.

+3 Agility | −3 Strength, −1 Toughness, −1 Deception

Elf (Underdark / Drow)

Shadowy subterranean elves wielding dark magic and expert combat skills.

+2 Agility, +1 Wit | −2 Strength, −2 Piety, −1 Charisma

Gnoll

Fierce hyena-like humanoids driven by primal instincts, thriving in tribal societies and combat.

+2 Strength, +1 Courage | −2 Wit, −2 Charisma, −1 Capacity

Imperials

Arch Elf–Drow hybrids. Pearl skin by day, opal at night. Charismatic and clever.

+2 Charisma, +1 Wit | −2 Constitution, −2 Toughness, −1 Courage

Human

The most versatile of all races. Every human, regardless of culture (Omnè, Nordkin, etc.), begins with the same base stats. Culture affects availability of items and recipes, not core stats.

+1 Versatility, +1 Luck, +1 Charisma, +1 Deception | −1 Toughness, −1 Constitution, −1 Luck, −1 Willpower

Note on Humans: Omnè humans are the most technologically advanced human culture — not because of race, but because they live far from Adoria and cannot rely on magic. All humans are mechanically identical at baseline.

Small Size Races (3–5 ft)

Characterized by agility, stealth, and dexterity. Excellent at sneaking and navigating tight spaces. Small races have no mount size restrictions and can even mount their pets.

Brownling

Also known as Brownies; whimsical smallfolk with artistic talents, deep cultural roots, and a love for nature and community.

+2 Charisma, +1 Knowledge | −2 Strength, −2 Toughness, −1 Presence

Gnome

Curious and inventive masters of engineering and magic, thriving in vibrant underground communities.

+3 Wit | −5 Strength

Goblorc

Clever, muscular hybrids of goblin and orc; skilled in traps, tactics, and crafting.

+2 Deception, +1 Capacity | −2 Courage, −2 Charisma, −1 Spirit

Halfling

Cheerful and resourceful; excel in stealth, luck, and survival; often live in hidden communities.

+2 Luck, +1 Agility | −2 Strength, −2 Toughness, −1 Presence

Hobbling

Muscular, agile hybrids of hobgoblin and halfling; skilled in tactical combat and stealth.

+2 Agility, +1 Dexterity | −2 Strength, −2 Charisma, −1 Presence

Miceling

Tiny, agile mouse-like beings with sharp senses; excel in stealth and exploration.

+1 Agility, +1 Wit, +1 Vigilance | −2 Strength, −2 Toughness, −1 Presence

Omnè (Small)

Advanced human subculture native to the planet Omnè; highly knowledgeable and tech-savvy.

+2 Knowledge, +1 Wit | −2 Piety, −1 Strength, −2 Intuition

Pinkling

Mysterious tricksters with pinkish skin; masters of deception, manipulation, scouting, thievery, and fishing.

+2 Deception, +1 Agility | −1 Strength, −4 Toughness, −3 Piety

Ratkin

Nimble subterranean survivors with sharp claws and excellent senses; expert at stealth.

+1 Dexterity, +1 Wit, +1 Vigilance | −2 Charisma, −2 Presence, −1 Luck

Dwarf (Snow)

Hardy mountain dwellers with exceptional endurance in icy and snowy environments.

Wood Elf

Small, stealthy guardians of forests; masters of archery and natural magic.

+2 Agility, +1 Wit | −2 Strength, −2 Toughness, −1 Constitution

Calm, long-lived sloth-like humanoids with exceptional stealth and connection to nature; often serve as monks or rangers.

+2 Focus, +1 Apex | −2 Agility, −2 Strength, −1 Knowledge
