Movement System Overview

The movement system revolves around stamina, terrain, and character actions. Each combat turn is 6 seconds, costing stamina points (SP) per second of activity. Movement depends on race-based base speeds, terrain, and environmental factors.

Movement Types & Costs

  • Walking: 1 SP per 5 ft
  • Sprinting: 2 SP per 5 ft
  • Rough terrain: 1.5 SP per 5 ft walking, 3 SP dashing
  • Climbing: 4 SP per 5 ft
  • Swimming: 2.5 SP per 5 ft

Attributes & Prerequisites

Strength and agility influence movement capabilities, with load affecting climbing and sprinting. Carrying capacity is STR×15 lbs. Climbing and sprinting require specific attribute checks and have penalties if overloaded.

Stamina & Endurance

  • Regenerates: 1 SP per 3 seconds (combat), 1 SP per minute (travel)
  • Low SP: Reduces speed by 25%, increases SP costs by 50%, and causes exhaustion at 0 SP.

Advanced Movement

Environmental effects, momentum, group tactics, obstacles, and special gear influence movement and stamina costs.
