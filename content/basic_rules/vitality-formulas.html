Vitality Formulas

This page holds the full math behind Vitality, Apex, Spirit, and Wellness, pulled out of Basic Rules pg. 2 so that page stays plain-language only. Nothing here is required reading to play, Basic Rules pg. 2 covers everything a player actually needs. This is for anyone who wants to see the formulas themselves, or verify a number. The GM Codex still carries its own full copies of this math as well, this page doesn't replace that, it's an additional, player-accessible reference.

Total Vitality Formula

Total Vitality for a given resource = [Base + ((Character Level + Growth) x per-level rate) + Trait modifier] x (1 + 0.01 x Effective Apex allocated to that resource). Effective Apex is a character's Fortitude-derived Apex as currently adjusted by Wellness (see Effective Apex/Spirit Formula, below). Growth uses the same per-level rate as Character Level but arrives on its own slower schedule (see Growth: Vitality, GM Codex Ch. II, for the full table).

Per-level rates: Life +18, plus Constitution score. Mana +12, plus Wit score. Stamina +12, plus Willpower score. Endurance +11, no Trait modifier. These rates are consistent across all sizes, size differences are reflected in the base values (see Base Vitality by Size, Basic Rules pg. 2).

Spirit Regeneration Formula

Life, Mana, and Stamina each regenerate 1 Chip per Turn for every point of Spirit allocated to that resource, always active, in combat, out of combat, and during rest, and it stacks with any Nature or Spell Ability that also recovers Vitality (see Trait Secondary Effect Formula, below, for the separate Chip regeneration Constitution, Willpower, Grit, and Vigor each grant on their own). Endurance is never affected by Spirit, it resets to full at the start of each day instead.

Effective Apex/Spirit Formula

Effective Apex = Apex (Fortitude's score) minus any points currently lost to Wellness. Effective Spirit = Spirit (Willpower's score) minus any points currently lost to Wellness. Wellness only ever removes a point from Apex or Spirit when it drops below 50%, each new threshold crossed under 50% costs 1 point from whichever of the two the GM assigns it to; climbing back up through that same threshold restores it. At 50% Wellness and above, Apex and Spirit always pay out at full, derived value. See Wellness, Basic Rules pg. 2, for the full threshold rules.

Worked Example: Kaida's Vitality

Kaida is a Standard-size, Character Level 30, Growth 8 character with Fortitude 40, Willpower 16, Constitution 38, and Wit 14. Level + Growth = 38 for every resource.

Life: Base 50 + (38 x 18) + Constitution 38 = 772 pre-Apex. Apex 40 is split 25/Life, 10/Stamina, 5/Endurance, 0/Mana. Wellness sits at 100%, so Effective Apex (Life) = 25. Multiplier = 1 + (0.01 x 25) = 1.25. Total Life = 772 x 1.25 = 965.

Stamina: Base 32 + (38 x 12) + Willpower 16 = 504 pre-Apex. Effective Apex (Stamina) = 10. Multiplier = 1.10. Total Stamina = 504 x 1.10 = 554.4, rounds to 554.

Endurance: Base 35 + (38 x 11) = 453 pre-Apex, no Trait modifier. Effective Apex (Endurance) = 5. Multiplier = 1.05. Total Endurance = 453 x 1.05 = 475.65, rounds to 476.

Mana: Base 32 + (38 x 12) + Wit 14 = 502 pre-Apex. Kaida allocates no Apex to Mana, she's not a caster, so the multiplier stays 1.00. Total Mana = 502.

Her Spirit of 16 is split 10/Life, 6/Stamina, 0/Mana (she has no use for Mana regeneration), regenerating 10 Life Chips and 6 Stamina Chips every Turn, on top of whatever her Traits regenerate at Score 5+ (see Trait Secondary Effect Formula, below).

Reminder: Every stat, cost, and threshold in this book is formulated, none are hand-tuned by the designers. These formulas exist so the logic is never hidden, not so a player needs to run them. See Basic Rules pg. 2 for the plain-language version, and the GM Codex, Ch. II, for full step-by-step walkthroughs and the Wellness tick-rate tables.

Trait Secondary Effect Formula

Effect tier = floor(Trait Score ÷ 5), 0 below a score of 5. Constitution, Willpower, Grit, and Vigor add [tier] Chips to their aligned Vitality resource (Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance respectively) at the start of each Turn. Every other Trait lets the player designate one Ability aligned with that Trait's theme; that Ability's Cooldown or Recharge resets [tier] times per encounter instead of waiting on its normal Turn or Rest timer.

Ability Cooldown & Recharge Formula

Cooldown (⏳): Applies to Round-tier Abilities. Charges = the Ability's Uses/Round number (3/Round = 3 Charges, 2/Round = 2 Charges, 1/Round = 1 Charge). One Ability Reset/Recharge Chip returns to the Card per Turn (6 seconds) until all Charges are restored.

Recharge (🔋): Applies to Day-tier Abilities. Charges = the Ability's Uses/Day number (3/Day = 3 Charges, 2/Day = 2 Charges). Chips return at the next Long Rest, or Short Rest if the Card specifically allows it.

Unlimited-Use Abilities carry neither symbol and use no Chips, there's nothing to track.
