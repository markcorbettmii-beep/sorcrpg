Vitality & Vitality Aura Formulas

This page holds the full math behind Vitality, Vitality Aura (Apex and Spirit), and Wellness, pulled out of Basic Rules pg. 2 so that page stays plain-language only. Nothing here is required reading to play, Basic Rules pg. 2 covers everything a player actually needs. This is for anyone who wants to see the formulas themselves, or verify a number. The GM Codex still carries its own full copies of this math as well, this page doesn't replace that, it's an additional, player-accessible reference.

Total Vitality Formula

Total Vitality for a given resource = [Base + ((Character Level + Growth) x per-level rate) + Trait modifier per level] x (1 + 0.04 x Effective Apex allocated to that resource). Effective Apex is a character's purchased Apex as currently adjusted by Wellness (see Effective Apex/Spirit Formula, below). Growth uses the same per-level rate as Character Level but arrives on its own slower schedule (see Growth: Vitality, GM Codex Ch. II, for the full table).

Per-level rates: Life +18, plus Constitution score. Mana +12, plus Wit score. Stamina +12, plus Willpower score. Endurance +11, no Trait modifier. These rates are consistent across all sizes, size differences are reflected in the base values (see Base Vitality by Size, Basic Rules pg. 2).

Effective Apex/Spirit Formula

At 75% Wellness or above: Effective Apex = Purchased Apex, and Effective Spirit = Purchased Spirit, full value, flat, no matter how far past 75% Wellness sits.

Below 75% Wellness: Effective Apex = Purchased Apex x (Wellness ÷ 75). Effective Spirit = Purchased Spirit x (Wellness ÷ 75). Each result is still separately capped at the character's Fortitude (Apex) or Resilience (Spirit) score.

Vitality Aura Points (VAP) Progression

Cumulative VAP earned = sum of 1d4 rolled once per year of age lived, the same yearly cadence Innate Aptitude uses, not tied to Character Level at all. Average roll is 2.5 VAP/year. Points affordable if saved toward a single score = floor(Cumulative VAP ÷ 8). A player may split earned VAP between Apex and Spirit in any combination. Worked Example: Kaida's Life

Base = 50 (Standard size)
Character Level 30 + Growth 8 = 38
38 x 18 (Life's per-level rate) = 684
Trait modifier = Constitution 38
Pre-Apex subtotal = 50 + 684 + 38 = 772

Apex = 6, 4 of it allocated to Life, under her Fortitude cap of 40
Wellness = 100%, at or above the 75% threshold, so Effective Apex (Life) = 4, full value
Multiplier = 1 + (0.04 x 4) = 1.16

Total Life = 772 x 1.16 = 895.52, rounds to 896

The remaining 2 points of Kaida's Apex are allocated to Stamina, accounting for her Stamina total of 544 (Base 32 + (38 x 12) + Willpower 16 = 504, x 1.08 for 2 Apex = 544). Her Mana (502) and Endurance (453) receive no Apex allocation.

Reminder: Every stat, cost, and threshold in this book is formulated, none are hand-tuned by the designers. These formulas exist so the logic is never hidden, not so a player needs to run them. See Basic Rules pg. 2 for the plain-language version, and the GM Codex, Ch. II, for full step-by-step walkthroughs and the Wellness tick-rate tables.

Trait Secondary Effect Formula

Effect tier = floor(Trait Score ÷ 5), 0 below a score of 5. Constitution, Willpower, Grit, and Vigor add [tier] Chips to their aligned Vitality resource (Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance respectively) at the start of each Turn. Every other Trait lets the player designate one Ability aligned with that Trait's theme; that Ability's Cooldown or Recharge resets [tier] times per encounter instead of waiting on its normal Turn or Rest timer.

Ability Cooldown & Recharge Formula

Cooldown (⏳): Applies to Round-tier Abilities. Charges = the Ability's Uses/Round number (3/Round = 3 Charges, 2/Round = 2 Charges, 1/Round = 1 Charge). One Ability Reset/Recharge Chip returns to the Card per Turn (6 seconds) until all Charges are restored.

Recharge (🔋): Applies to Day-tier Abilities. Charges = the Ability's Uses/Day number (3/Day = 3 Charges, 2/Day = 2 Charges). Chips return at the next Long Rest, or Short Rest if the Card specifically allows it.

Unlimited-Use Abilities carry neither symbol and use no Chips, there's nothing to track.
