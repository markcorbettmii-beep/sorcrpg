Playable Classes

After choosing a race, players select from 16 main class trees. Each tree has 3 paths, and each path has 3 branches — for a total of 9 specialized branch options per class. Each main class contains 30 Abilities that grow progressively in power (Levels 1–30). Native Abilities (Levels 1–3) are available immediately; path Abilities unlock at Level 4, and branch Abilities at Level 21.

Characters are not restricted to one class tree. You can select Abilities from multiple trees to create versatile or specialized characters, provided you meet proficiency requirements. A character may learn up to 59 Abilities in any order.

Ability Cap: 59 total Abilities across all classes. This can mean 30 in one tree (maxed) + 29 in another, or spread almost 4 Abilities across all 16 main classes — trading depth for diversity. There is no wrong approach; each has tradeoffs.

Learning Abilities

Class Abilities are granted as characters level, but characters must visit a class trainer before learning them. Trainers are found in towns, villages, and metropolises. If leveling in a dungeon, use the Wayfarer skill (if available) or coordinate with a mage or nearby trainer to mark a location for later. Some Abilities require completing quests or visiting dangerous areas.

Armament & Armor Restrictions

Each class path specifies its own armor and weapon types. Players are not restricted from owning a weapon, but they cannot use it effectively without choosing an Ability that utilizes it. Certain paths impose additional restrictions:

  • Monks and Clerics cannot use edged weapons.
  • Warlocks cannot use holy weapons; Paladins cannot use unholy weapons.
  • Fallen classes have their own trees requiring 10 levels in the former main class (see GM Codex).

The 16 Main Class Trees

Avenger

Holy warriors wielding blunt weapons. Restricted to blunt weaponry; variety of armor types depending on path.

Paths: Cleric (1h blunt + shield, chain or lighter) · Monk (blunt wood only, cloth/leather) · Priest (1h or 2h blunt, cloth or leather)
Cleric Branches: Holy · Oracle · Warpriest
Monk Branches: Chanter · Shapeflow · Qirateka
Priest Branches: Shadow Weaver · Discipline · Salii

Barbarian

Strong and agile warriors. Players choose between chain mail strength or leather athleticism based on playstyle.

Paths: Destroyer · Cimmerian · Mountaineer
Destroyer Branches: Bloodrager · Frenzied · Mauler
Cimmerian Branches: Beast (Lion/Raven/Ram forms) · Nomad · Ghost
Mountaineer Branches: Amazon · Beast Master · Predator

Bard

Musical combatants and enchanters. Centyrs favor this class. Instruments vary by path: strings, percussion, or wind.

Paths: Strumalong (strings) · Percussionist (drums) · Serenade (hypnosis/wind)
Strumalong Branches: Stroller · Melodist · Plucker
Percussionist Branches: Pounder · Distorter · Rattler
Serenade Branches: Sprite · Songbird · Charmer

Doom Knight

Dark warriors wielding necrotic power, bleeding effects, and heavy armor. Three distinct playstyles: dread, death, and radiant corruption.

Paths: Death Guard · Death Bringer · Lordsman
Death Guard Branches: Scourge · Umbral Lord · Dread Knight
Death Bringer Branches: Death Lord · Death Knight · Decaymancer
Lordsman Branches: Radiant Knight · Paragon Knight · Sentinel

Druid

Nature-bound spellcasters with celestial, primal, and dream abilities. Can transform into beasts in certain paths.

Paths: Celestial (damage/heals) · Hyborian (primal) · Dreamcryer (chants/combos)
Celestial Branches: Starduster · Lunar · Solar
Hyborian Branches: Ursidae · Metamorpher · Rager
Dreamcryer Branches: Dreamcaster · Nightmare · Lorekeeper

Fighter

Versatile physical combatants ranging from bare-knuckle brawlers to heavily armored juggernauts to gadget-equipped heroes.

Paths: Brawler (unarmed/blunt) · Juggernaut (shields/Toughness) · Hero (versatile)
Brawler Branches: Bruiser · Enforcer · Unarmed
Juggernaut Branches: Vanguard · Reaver · Rumbler
Hero Branches: Combatant · Merc Zealot · Vigilante

Magic User

Masters of arcane manipulation, trickery, and reality-bending magic. The Illusionist branch can change shapes and sizes of living beings.

Paths: Trickster · Mage · Illusionist
Trickster Branches: Jester · Shadow Dancer · Arcana
Mage Branches: RuinMaster (portals) · Chronomancer · Blood Mage
Illusionist Branches: Metamorphiser · Astral Weaver · Shapeshifter

Marksman

Ranged weapon specialists from classic archers to black-powder gunslingers to nature rangers.

Paths: Bowman · Gunner (black powder) · Ranger
Bowman Branches: Bandit · Archer · Amazon
Gunner Branches: Gun Slinger · Duelist · Wrangler
Ranger Branches: Keeper · Forest Guardian · Wood Warden

Overseer

Divine warriors and paladins. Holy crusaders in heavy armor. Paladins cannot use unholy weapons and may fall if alignment is broken.

Paths: Crusader (arms) · Paladin · Archon
Crusader Branches: Devout · Blessed Knight · Trojan
Paladin Branches: Radiant · Templar · Guardian
Archon Branches: Battleguard · Defender · Legionnaire

Shaman

Spiritual warriors combining healing, elemental magic, and dark ritual arts. Range from medicine men to voodoo ritualists.

Paths: Medicine Man · Primordial (mantras) · Ritualist (voodoo/black magic)
Medicine Man Branches: Fire Breather · Bone Collector · Earthbound
Primordial Branches: Astromancer · Ancestral · Stormbringer
Ritualist Branches: Ceremonial · Runecrafter · Hexbinder

Sorcerer

Powerful arcane casters spanning conjuration, prophecy, and wizardry. Among the most magically potent classes.

Paths: Conjurer · Seer · Wizard
Conjurer Branches: Planes Walker · Magus · Psionic
Seer Branches: Mystic · Clairvoyant · Prophet
Wizard Branches: Magister · Battlecryer · Transmuter

Strategist

Rogues, shadow stalkers, and spellblades who excel at stealth, assassination, and arcane melee. Agile, deceptive, and deadly.

Paths: Rogue · Shadow Stalker · Spellblade
Rogue Branches: Assassin · Recon · Shroud
Shadow Stalker Branches: Wraith · Abyssal · Specter
Spellblade Branches: Shadowblade · Phantom Blade · Sprightly (animated weapons)

Tyrant

Aggressive dual-weapon and two-handed weapon specialists; also includes a War Cryer caster path with unique vocal combat abilities.

Paths: Berserker (dual weapon) · Demolisher (2h weapon) · War Cryer
Berserker Branches: Blitzer · Shredder · Fury
Demolisher Branches: Brute · Rampager · Bone Crusher
War Cryer Branches: Celestial · Paincaster · Dagger Dancer

Warlock

Dark pact casters wielding fiendish, vampiric, and necromantic powers. Cannot use holy weapons. Powerful but alignment-sensitive.

Paths: Grimlock · Bloodlock · Necromancer
Grimlock Branches: Fiend · Changeling · Hexblade
Bloodlock Branches: Vampire · Blood Knight · Tormentor
Necromancer Branches: Summoner · Inquisitor · (developing)

Warrior

Well-rounded martial warriors skilled in both ranged and melee. The Warlord subpath introduces caster abilities via runes and elemental throws.

Paths: Armorer (ranged + melee) · Blade Master (all melee/spear/hand-to-hand) · Warlord (sub-caster)
Armorer Branches: Ironclad · Champion · Bulwark
Blade Master Branches: Savage Slayer · Gladiator · Duelist
Warlord Branches: Stormbringer · Runeblades · Sun Thrower

Witch

Arcane practitioners of frost, lunar, and alchemical brewing. Range from icy cryomancers to moonfire dreamweavers to hag brewmasters.

Paths: Norwitch (frost) · Lunar · BrewMaster
Norwitch Branches: Frostbane · Cryomancer · Arctic Witch
Lunar Branches: Dreamweaver · Moonfire · Starborn
BrewMaster Branches: Hag · Green Witch · Shrew
Science Fiction Classes: Technologically advanced classes are pending development, available for planets like Omnè, Octavo, Angeligla, and Tredici. Stay tuned for updates.

Class Structure Summary

Each of the 16 main class trees follows this structure:

  • Native Abilities (Levels 1–3): Available immediately upon choosing the main class. Usable with any weapon the class supports.
  • Path Abilities (Levels 4–20): Chosen after selecting one of 3 paths. Each path has 17 Abilities.
  • Branch Abilities (Levels 21–30): The final specialization — 10 Abilities within a path's specific branch.

Total: 30 Abilities per complete path/branch progression. A character can spread 59 Abilities across any combination of the 16 trees.
