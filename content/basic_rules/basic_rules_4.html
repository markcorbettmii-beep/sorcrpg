II. Attributes

Attributes are the foundational qualities that define a character’s innate physical and mental prowess. These characteristics influence every action a character takes - from routine movements to complex combat maneuvers. In SorC, the primary attributes include AGI, ART, FOC, INT, STR, and WIS. Each attribute is measured by a numerical score that factors directly into task outcomes, such as the ability to carry heavy loads, execute precise actions, or react quickly in combat. For example, a high Strength score enables a character to overcome physical challenges and move obstacles, while a strong Artistry rating reflects exceptional dexterity and precision in executing delicate or exacting tasks.

III. Talents

Talents represent specialized training that allows a character to perform trained maneuvers. Each talent has its own score, chosen by players, that factors into die or dice rolls when faced with environmental challenges. For example, the "Leap" talent from the Vocational Tree Olympiad gives characters an advantage while attempting jumps across gaps, like ravines. Another talent, "Track" from the Scout Tree, allows a character to read subtle signs in the environment and effectively follow traces or hidden trails. A strong Track score may reveal hidden paths, allow the Scout to track game for the party as a food source, or warn of potential ambushes. Similarly, the "Hide" talent from the Thief Tree assists a character in remaining undetected while they hide in the shadows, or blend into or behind objects. By utilizing their score in Hide, a character can reduce the likelihood of detection during silent or covert movement in challenging environments.
