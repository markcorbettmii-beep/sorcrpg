SORC Cards

Players use a comprehensive card-based system to track Spells and Abilities, Armaments, Companions, and more. Cards make a crunchy game manageable - instead of flipping through rulebooks, players and GMs reference their own cards that link to books via code and QR.

Card Types

Card TypeDescription
Ability CardsAll combat Abilities come as cards for quick reference. Players keep a personal stack representing their character's Abilities.
Talent CardsReference non-combat vocational talents. Require challenge checks when used.
Skill CardsReference profession skills (crafting, enchanting, etc.). Must be used out of combat; require recipe, materials, and at least a makeshift workshop.
Armament CardsWeapons and armor cards including base stats, rank, and condition. Enhanced with Reference Cards (index-sized) clipped underneath to track modifications without altering the base card.
Companion CardsAllies, pets, mounts, guardians, hirelings, and summons each have their own cards with innate Stats that never change.
Lore CardsGuide parties to special Lore Quests built into the module. Rank matched to module level. Tradable. Have QR codes for digital access.
Encounter CardsNPC, Boss, Mob, Companion, Mechanical, Biological, and Environmental cards. Help GMs manage creature stats and NPCs during gameplay.
Item / Special Item CardsConsumables (healing pots, materials, etc.) and unique objects. Stack similar consumables on one card; track usage with the Item Tracker Book.
Consolidation CardsGM and player reference cards for each character's consolidated stats at a glance.
Prize CardsPhysical SORC Cards won through SORC Beyond community program. Not tied to any specific module. Fully tradable.

Lost cards can be downloaded for free online using the box set code, or ordered through the catalog. Cards have QR codes that must be refreshed periodically. Once a card is traded online, the QR code transfers to the new owner.

Armament Enhancements

Armaments can be modified and enhanced through skilled craftsmen. Each enhancement method uses a different process:

  • Combining, Enchanting, Inscribing, Modifying, Nesting (lining)
  • Reforging, Resonating, Socketing (gems/runes), Sundering, Upgrading

Enhancement risks increase with each successive upgrade. If armor breaks during enhancement, it goes to the boneyard, all previous enhancements (socketed gems, upgrades, linings) are lost, and most materials are harvested. The broken armor stays in the boneyard until reforged with new materials.

Currency System

Essentia uses a structured currency system based on the value and weight of the element used to mint each coin. Not all planets use currency - some use advanced credit systems.

CoinConversionUse
Silver CoinBase coin (~180/lb)Basic unit; everyday purchases, taxes, small expenses
Gold Coin1 Gold = 50 SilverSubstantial purchases, major economic transactions
Electrum Coin1 Electrum = 50 Silver (50 Gold/ingot)Moderate value increase over silver
Platinum Coin1 Platinum = 100 Silver (1,000 Gold/ingot)Estates, vehicles, spacecraft, one-of-a-kind relics

Characters with Vault accounts can write signed currency notes (checks) for very high-value transactions. Zailister is the universe's reserve currency hub. The last 3 planets (Futurem, Omnè, Tredici) use laser-secured credit systems only and view universal coins as worthless.

Experience & Rank

Character growth in SORC is guided by two parallel systems: Class Level and Rank.

Class Level (1–30+)

Represents accumulated experience (XP) and unlocks new combat Abilities, talents, skills, and Traits. XP is earned from combat, exploration, roleplaying, crafting, and achievements - either shared with the party or awarded individually for notable actions. Campaigns may also use milestone leveling.

Rank

Measured by Rank Points (RP) and reflects a hero's reputation, achievements, gear quality, and Companion strength. Ranks in ascending order:

Pauper → Peasant → Commoner → Adventurer → Hero → Elite → Master → Legend

Acquiring or losing key armaments or Companions can raise or lower Rank. Encounters are balanced by matching Rank - a Pauper matches against a Common NPC, a Hero against Heroic hosts, and so on.

Characters cannot use items of a rank above their own. Lore Quest items always drop in one way or another upon completion of the quest, and the box set never contains ranks above the character's current level.

Encounter Rewards

  • Defeating foes of similar Rank → standard XP
  • Defeating stronger opponents → greater XP and rewards
  • Defeating trivial or very weak opponents → little or no XP (killing harmless creatures yields nothing unless on a relevant quest)
