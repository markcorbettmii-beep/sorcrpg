I. Enchanter

Experts in magical enchantments and item enhancement. Enchanters imbue objects and performances with magical properties.

Attribute: Artistry

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

NPC Dispositions: Attuner, Priestess

Disposition Logic: Enchanters and Attunered both work with magical resonance and item infusion. Attunered refine magical energies; Enchanters channel those energies into objects. They collaborate naturally, each improving the other's output. Priestesses sanctify enchanted items, transforming them into sacred relics. A blessed enchanted sword becomes far more powerful than either blessing or enchantment alone. Enchanters gain access to temple markets and blessed material sources Priestesses control.

1. Basic Enchantment

Learn to apply simple magical properties to mundane items. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement grants precision in placing enchantment marks; Craftsmanship trait advancement improves the durability of applied effects.

2. Rune Inscription

Develop expertise in the art of inscribing runes that hold magical power. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement allows you to command runes with greater authority; Dexterity advancement ensures precise runic strokes.

3. Spell Binding

Bind prepared spells into objects for activation at a later time. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement increases the number of spell charges per item; Charisma advancement allows binding more powerful spells.

4. Aura Enhancement

Enhance an item's intrinsic magical aura through focused artistry. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement refines aura-shaping technique; Charisma advancement makes the aura more potent and noticeable.

5. Resonance Attunement

Attune items to specific magical frequencies for synchronized effects. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement improves resonance accuracy; Deceit advancement allows you to mask an item's true resonance.

6. Charm Creation

Craft protective charms that provide passive benefits to their bearer. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement makes charms more effective; Craftsmanship advancement increases their longevity.

7. Enchantment Layering

Apply multiple enchantments to a single item without conflicts. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement allows stacking more layers; Craftsmanship advancement prevents interference between effects.

8. Curse Breaking

Identify and remove curses and negative enchantments from corrupted items. Benefit: Deceit trait advancement helps you understand curse logic; Charisma advancement makes removal more forceful.

9. Artifact Enchantment

Transform ordinary items into true magical artifacts with legendary properties. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement improves artifact stability; Charisma advancement grants artifacts greater magical presence.

10. Power Infusion

Channel raw magical energy directly into items, amplifying their effects. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement allows channeling more power; Dexterity advancement prevents wild fluctuations during infusion.

11. Permanence Casting

Lock enchantments in place, preventing them from fading or being dispelled. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement makes permanence more absolute; Charisma advancement extends the permanence to multiple enchantments simultaneously.

12. Spell Reinforcement

Strengthen enchantments to resist dispelling and countermeasures. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement creates more resilient reinforcements; Dexterity advancement allows targeted reinforcement of specific aspects.

13. Arcane Weaving

Weave intricate arcane patterns that create complex magical interactions. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement enables more detailed weaving; Charisma advancement makes weaved patterns more powerful.

14. Soul Binding

Create a deep connection between an item and a soul essence for special effects. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement strengthens the soul bond; Craftsmanship advancement ensures the bond doesn't harm the bearer.

15. Mythic Enchanting

Craft enchantments of mythic power, rivaling the works of ancient enchanters. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement grants access to more powerful mythic effects; Craftsmanship advancement stabilizes the volatile nature of mythic enchantments.

16. Legendary Enchantment

Create masterwork magical items that exhibit perfect balance between form and function. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement perfects the crafting process; Craftsmanship advancement elevates items to true masterwork status.

17. Legendary Artifacts

Enchant items of legendary caliber, granting them properties that shape history. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement allows crafting items of greater legendary power; Craftsmanship advancement ensures legendary status is stable.

18. Godly Enchantment

Imbue items with enchantments of near-divine power, approaching godly authority. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement lets you channel godly aspects; Craftsmanship advancement prevents the item from being consumed by divine power.

19. Enchantment Transcendence

Create enchantments that transcend normal limitations, bending magical rules. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement allows precise bending of magical laws; Charisma advancement makes transcendent effects more pronounced.

20. Reality Weaving

Weave enchantments so profound they reshape reality itself around the item. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement anchors reality alterations; Charisma advancement expands the scope of reality-warping effects.
