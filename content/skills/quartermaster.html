Logistics (craft) Profession Tree: Quartermaster

Quartermaster Tree Description
The Quartermaster tree lets you run the day-to-day life of a ship, whether it flies, sails, or was hijacked outright, legal work and illegal work alike. Where Shipwright builds and maintains the hull itself, Quartermaster keeps it crewed, supplied, armed, and running once it's underway. Open to any Class, owning or crewing a ship-type Home (see Home and Family, Basic Rules pg. 4) is what calls for this Profession, not what Class a character took.

Application:
- Resupply and field-treat a crew mid-voyage, ammunition, rations, and combat stims alike.
- Reinforce, seal, and defend a ship's hull and passageways in a fight.
- Forge papers, appraise cargo, and move goods through black-market channels.
- Re-identify, refit, or hide cargo aboard a hijacked or smuggling vessel.
- All costs are paid in materials, time, and Wit points (see column 4).

Quartermaster spans just three tiers, Apprentice through Grandmaster, one Recipe per column at each. Unlike a deep craft tree such as Blacksmith's, its skills are ship-roles first, not a long ladder of tool refinement, a Quartermaster grows more essential to run a ship, not more numerous in what they know.

Tools and Attire as Combat Bonuses

Same rule as every other Profession (see Profession Flare, Skills pg. 1): a Quartermaster's signature gear carries a small mechanical bonus, but isn't a true Weapon Card, no Grade, can't be Enhanced, doesn't satisfy a Weapon Requirement, it just functions as its Style while held or worn.
