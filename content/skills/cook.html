Profession Tree: Cook

The Cook profession allows players to craft meals, drinks, and potions with various effects for themselves and their party. Cooks use a range of skills—such as Gourmet Preparation and Mystical Desserts—to improve health, morale, and attributes. Cooks work with both common and rare ingredients, using methods from basic fire-starting to advanced ovens and magical cooking implements. Meals can be prepared at campfires, in field kitchens, or in fully equipped kitchens with stoves, ovens, and water sources. Some recipes require special equipment or sources of heat, water, or magical energy.
