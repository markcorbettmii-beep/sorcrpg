VI. Cook

Paired Talent Tree: Olympiad

Aligned Attribute: Artistry

Masters of culinary arts and food preparation. Cooks understand nutrition, flavor, and the art of sustaining adventurers through nourishment.

Craft nourishing meals, beverages, and potions with various effects using rare and common ingredients.

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

Refer to Basic Rules - Pairing on page 6

1. Fire Starting

Ignite and maintain campfires for cooking and morale support. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement improves fire reliability; Charisma advancement inspires group morale from your efforts.

2. Ingredient Knowledge

Identify and select optimal ingredients for meals and recipes. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement lets you identify the finest ingredients; Craftsmanship advancement helps you preserve their properties.

3. Food Safety

Ensure safe meal preparation and prevent spoilage. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement improves food handling hygiene; Craftsmanship advancement extends freshness duration.

4. Preservation Techniques

Smoke, pickle, or dry food to extend shelf life and preservation. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement perfects preservation methods; Dexterity advancement speeds the process.

5. Baker

Prepare bread, pastries, and baked goods with skill and flavor. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement improves pastry delicacy; Craftsmanship advancement ensures consistent quality.

6. Brewmaster

Brew restorative and enhancing beverages with special properties. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement creates more potent brews; Craftsmanship advancement prevents failed batches.

7. Butcher

Process and prepare meat for cooking and preservation. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement improves cutting precision; Craftsmanship advancement minimizes waste.

8. Meal Preparation

Prepare complete meals that restore health and provide buffs. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement creates more effective restorative meals; Charisma advancement makes the food more appealing to recipients.

9. Cultural Cooking

Create dishes from various cultures with unique effects and properties. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement lets you master diverse culinary traditions; Dexterity advancement perfects exotic techniques.

10. Foraging

Gather wild ingredients from nature for culinary use. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement attunes you to nature's bounty; Craftsmanship advancement improves ingredient identification.

11. Stock Making

Create rich broths and stocks as cooking bases. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement deepens flavor complexity; Dexterity advancement accelerates cooking time.

12. Potion Crafting

Brew magical potions with restorative and enhancing effects. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement channels magical energy into potions; Craftsmanship advancement stabilizes volatile magical ingredients.

13. Fermentation

Ferment foods and beverages to develop complex flavors. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement accelerates fermentation time; Charisma advancement produces more exotic flavor profiles.

14. Spice Expertise

Develop expertise in exotic spices and flavor combinations. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement lets you access rarer spices; Dexterity advancement combines them with perfect precision.

15. Magical Cuisine

Create meals infused with magical properties and effects. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement makes magical infusions more potent; Craftsmanship advancement prevents magical side effects.

16. Feast Preparation

Prepare elaborate feasts for large gatherings and celebrations. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement creates more impressive presentations; Craftsmanship advancement ensures consistent quality across all dishes.

17. Herb Infusion

Infuse herbs into drinks and foods for medicinal properties. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement maximizes medicinal potency; Charisma advancement balances flavor with healing effects.

18. Chef Elite

Execute culinary masterpieces at the pinnacle of cooking skill. Benefit: Dexterity trait advancement enables perfect execution; Craftsmanship advancement elevates each dish to art.

19. Culinary Expertise

Achieve expertise in all cooking techniques and flavor combinations. Benefit: Charisma trait advancement unlocks revolutionary flavor combinations; Craftsmanship advancement perfects every technique.

20. Legendary Cuisine

Create legendary meals with reality-altering effects. Benefit: Craftsmanship trait advancement makes reality effects permanent; Charisma advancement amplifies the magnitude of effects.
