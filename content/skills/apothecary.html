I. Apothecary

Paired Talent Tree: Brewmaster

Creates healing tinctures, restorative salves, and potions using herbs and natural ingredients.

Attribute: Artistry

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

NPC Dispositions: Revitalizer, Priestess

Disposition Logic: Revitalizers restore bodies through energy and vitality; Apothecaries restore through targeted chemical remedies. Both share healing philosophy but approach differently, Revitalizers handle overall wellness, Apothecaries handle specific ailments. Revitalizers distribute Apothecary potions through healing networks. Priestesses sanctify Apothecary remedies, making them sacred tools; in exchange, blessed potions unlock spiritual healing applications no mere potion can achieve. Apothecaries gain temple legitimacy and access to sacred herb groves.
