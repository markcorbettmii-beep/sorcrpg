XXIV. Herbalism

Gatherers of medicinal plants and herbs. Herbalists identify, cultivate, and harvest beneficial plants to create healing potions, remedies, and herbal treatments that accelerate recovery and treat various ailments.

Attribute: Fortitude

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

1. Plant Identification

Identify all varieties of plants and herbs.

2. Herb Harvesting

Harvest herbs without damaging them.

3. Drying Techniques

Properly dry herbs for storage and use.

4. Tea Brewing

Brew medicinal herbal teas.

5. Pressing Oils

Press oils from herbs and seeds.

6. Fermentation

Ferment herbs for enhanced properties.

7. Herb Grinding

Grind herbs into fine powders.

8. Seasonal Harvesting

Time harvests for maximum potency.

9. Rare Herb Sourcing

Locate and harvest rare herbs.

10. Herbal Analysis

Analyze herbs for medicinal properties.

11. Hybrid Cultivation

Cultivate hybrid herbs with unique properties.

12. Moonlight Harvesting

Harvest herbs under moonlight for special effects.

13. Essence Extraction

Extract pure essences from herbs.

14. Herbal Alchemy

Combine herbs in alchemical processes.

15. Medicinal Mastery

Master creation of medicinal herb preparations.

16. Masterwork Herbalism

Cultivate masterwork quality herbs.

17. Legendary Herbs

Cultivate legendary herbs of immense power.

18. Divine Plant Growing

Grow plants infused with divine essence.

19. Herbalism Transcendence

Master herbalism beyond natural limitations.

20. Legendary Cultivation
