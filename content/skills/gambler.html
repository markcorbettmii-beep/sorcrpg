XXXI. Gambler

Paired Talent Tree: Actuary

Masters of probability, wagers, and calculated risks. Gamblers exploit the odds to gain advantage in games, negotiations, and high-stakes situations.

Attribute: Charisma

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

NPC Dispositions: Bookkeeper, Dealer

Disposition Logic: Gamblers calculate odds and exploit probabilities; Bookkeepers track wagers, payouts, and odds on a massive scale. Gamblers provide customers; Bookkeepers provide infrastructure and capital. Together they create gaming establishments where fortunes change hands. Dealers distribute cards, manage tables, and ensure fair play. Gamblers teach Dealers how odds shift, enabling smarter decisions. Dealers provide Gamblers with employment and access to the most lucrative games. This triangle creates a thriving gaming culture.
