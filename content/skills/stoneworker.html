III. Stoneworker

Masters of stone craftsmanship and masonry. Stoneworkers shape, carve, and construct with stone, creating lasting structures and detailed sculptures.

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

1. Stone Quarrying

Quarry and extract stone from natural sources.

2. Stone Selection

Select appropriate stone types for projects.

3. Stone Shaping

Shape stone blocks into desired forms.

4. Masonry Basics

Build basic stone walls and structures.

5. Stone Carving

Carve detailed designs into stone.

6. Sculpting

Create stone sculptures and statues.

7. Arch Construction

Build stone arches and vaults.

8. Foundation Laying

Lay foundations for buildings with stone.

9. Stone Polishing

Polish stone to a fine finish.

10. Intricate Patterns

Create intricate patterns with stone work.

11. Stone Mosaics

Create decorative stone mosaics.

12. Fortification Design

Design and build fortifications from stone.

13. Complex Stonework

Execute complex stonework projects.

14. Relief Carving

Create relief carvings in stone.

15. Stone Restoration

Restore damaged stone structures.

16. Monument Creation

Create stone monuments and memorials.

17. Masterwork Masonry

Execute masterwork stone projects.

18. Legendary Structures

Build legendary stone structures that endure.

19. Masterwork Stonework

Craft masterwork stone structures.

20. Legendary Stone

Work with legendary stone materials.
