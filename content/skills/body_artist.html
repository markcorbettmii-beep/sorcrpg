Artistry (craft) Profession Tree: Body Artist

Body Artist Tree Description
The Body Artist profession allows players to provide physical and magical enhancements through markings (tattoos, brands, ritual art), accessories (piercings, subdermal jewelry, enchanted items), and surgical procedures (modifications, implants, healing). All methods can have magical, social, or cultural effects depending on the materials and techniques used. Inks, branding irons, enchanted accessories, and surgical procedures may grant resistances, minor powers, or visible status within a culture or faction.

Application:
- Create tattoos, brands, or ritual markings that can grant magical or social bonuses.
- Install piercings, enchanted accessories, or subdermal jewelry for magical, defensive, or communicative effects.
- Perform surgical changes and enhancements, from healing to empowering body parts.
- All procedures require specific recipes, materials, equipment, and a clean or ritual workspace (see table).
