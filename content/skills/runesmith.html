VI. Runesmith

Masters of runic inscription and magical craftsmanship. Runesmiths inscribe powerful runes onto objects and surfaces for magical effects.

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

1. Basic Rune Carving

Carve basic runes onto objects with accuracy.

2. Rune Activation

Activate runes to trigger their magical effects.

3. Elemental Runes

Inscribe elemental runes for fire, frost, lightning, and other effects.

4. Protection Runes

Create protective runes for defense and warding.

5. Enhancement Runes

Inscribe runes that enhance abilities and properties.

6. Binding Runes

Create runes that bind magical energy.

7. Stone Inscription

Inscribe runes onto stone surfaces.

8. Metal Engraving

Engrave runes onto metal objects.

9. Rune Combinations

Combine multiple runes for enhanced effects.

10. Power Transfer

Transfer power into rune inscriptions.

11. Curse Runes

Inscribe curse runes with negative effects.

12. Blessing Runes

Create blessing runes with positive effects.

13. Ancient Runes

Learn and inscribe ancient runic scripts.

14. Rune Repair

Repair damaged rune inscriptions.

15. Conditional Runes

Create runes that trigger under specific conditions.

16. Rune Circle Creation

Create circles of runes for powerful effects.

17. Master Runesmith

Master all runic arts and ancient knowledge.

18. Legendary Runecraft

Create legendary runic works of immense power.
