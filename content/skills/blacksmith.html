Profession Tree: Blacksmith

Blacksmith Description
The Blacksmith profession empowers you to forge and repair weapons, armor, and tools from metal. With a deep understanding of metallurgy, you create durable and effective items for both combat and craftsmanship. Your skills allow enchanting, adding supernatural enhancements that elevate item capabilities.

Essence Crystals:
Aether Shards: Boost magical attacks and spell potency.
Fury Shards: Surge power to weapon strikes for melee advantage.

Sample Key Crafting Elements and Values

  • Diamond: 2 platinum/gem (Mined) – Brilliance for high-tier crafting, e.g. Legendary Sword.
  • Pyronium: 10 gold/ingot (Magma) – Heat-resistant, for fiery weapons/armor.
  • Aetherstone: 20 gold/gem (Synth) – Power advanced enchantments, energy weapons.
  • Infernium: 15 gold/gem (Volcanic Caves) – Fire-elemental, rare weapon cores.
  • Monster Ash: 2 silver/handful – Rituals, weapon infusions.
  • Star Dust: 1 electrum/pinch – Magic boosts, shielding.
  • Pixie Dust: 4 silver/vial – Illusion/flight effects, synthweaving.
  • Quantite: 25 gold/ingot (Space) – Futuristic tech/weapons.
