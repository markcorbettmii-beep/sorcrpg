I. Blacksmith

Craft, repair, enhance, reforge, enchant, and personalize armaments, gear, and advanced equipment.

Attribute: Artistry

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

1. Basic Armor Smith

Forge basic armor (Iron, Leather, Chain) and shields. Master the fundamentals of armor crafting.

2. Tool & Gear Craft

Make hammers, tongs, utility tools, spikes, arrowheads, and essential crafting components.

3. Smelt

Smelt ores into ingots, refining raw materials into usable crafting components.

4. Basic Weaponsmith

Forge basic melee weapons with reliability and durability for beginning adventurers.

5. Salvage

Break down unwanted items to recover materials and components for reuse.

6. Repair

Restore damaged gear to full functionality, extending the life of valuable equipment.

7. Advanced Armor Smith

Craft advanced armors using rare materials like Pyronium and cultural armor sets.

8. Component Smith

Make bolts, nuts, reinforced joints, and precision components for complex crafting.

9. Element Smith

Refine and combine special materials to create alloys with unique properties.

10. Advanced Weaponsmith

Forge rare and cultural weapons with exotic materials and superior craftsmanship.

11. Care

Sharpen, clean, polish, and maintain equipment to preserve quality and effectiveness.

12. Join (Welding)

Weld and fuse metals together to create composite materials and reinforced structures.

13. Upgrade

Enhance gear with bonuses, sockets, and magical enhancements for increased power.

14. Material Scouting

Track and locate rare resources for advanced crafting projects.

15. Blastercraft

Craft technological and energy-based weapons using advanced materials and techniques.

16. Batch Production

Produce gear in bulk quantities using assembly techniques and efficiency methods.

17. Mining

Extract ore from terrain and natural deposits for direct material acquisition.

18. Reforge

Remake weapons and armor for completely new stats, types, or properties.

19. Master Enchanter

Layer, remove, or overwrite enchantments on items for maximum customization.

20. Legendary Forgemaster

Craft legendary and unique armaments at the pinnacle of blacksmithing mastery.
