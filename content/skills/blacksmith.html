Artistry (craft) Profession Tree: Blacksmith

Blacksmith Tree Description
The Blacksmith tree lets you create, repair, enhance, reforge, enchant, and personalize armaments, gear, and advanced equipment. Blacksmiths shape the fate of items, giving you power over durability, upgrades, and magical or technological enhancement.

Application:
- Craft, upgrade, and repair weapons, armor, and gear.
- Embed enhancements, enchantments, and socketed gems/runes.
- Unlock advanced features like personal shielding, energy weapons, and psionic attire.
- Break down, analyze, and improve items through insight and mastery.
- All costs are paid in materials, time, and Artistry points (see column 4).
