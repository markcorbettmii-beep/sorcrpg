I. Sculptor

Artists skilled in carving, molding, and shaping materials into three-dimensional forms. Sculptors create detailed statues, decorative pieces, and artistic works.

Attribute: Artistry

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

NPC Dispositions: Inventor, Socialite

Disposition Logic: Sculptors create visual art; Inventors engineer solutions through design. Both share deep appreciation for form, function, and aesthetics. Inventors commission custom pieces that showcase mechanical and artistic fusion. Sculptors gain premium work exploring innovative forms. Socialites move through elite circles with access to wealthy patrons. Socialites prominently display Sculptor works at galas, creating visibility and prestige. Sculptors gain consistent high-value commissions through Socialite patronage networks.
