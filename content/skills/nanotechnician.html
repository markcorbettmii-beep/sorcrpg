III. Nanotechnician

Paired Talent Tree: Anchor

Manipulates nanomachines for healing, crafting, sabotage, or adaptation.

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

NPC Dispositions: Nanite Supervisor, Inventor

Disposition Logic: Nanotechnicians work at microscale; Nanite Supervisors manage large-scale production and distribution networks. Nanotechnicians design swarms and applications; Nanite Supervisors mass-produce them and connect Nanotechnicians with clients needing bulk orders. This prevents bottleneck. Inventors collaborate on experimental applications; both optimize solutions through iteration. A Nanotechnician might create programmable repair swarms while an Inventor engineers the hardware interface. Together they unlock capabilities impossible alone. Inventors gain access to microscale tools; Nanotechnicians gain macroscale applications.
