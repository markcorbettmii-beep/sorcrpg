IV. Leather Worker

Paired Talent Tree: Hunter

Aligned Attribute: Physique

Masters of hide processing and leather crafting. Leather Workers transform raw hides into quality goods, armor, and accessories.

Professional Skills progress through practicing the Skills and Proficiency.

1. Hide Preparation

Prepare raw hides for leather crafting through cleaning and curing.

2. Tanning Techniques

Apply tanning methods to transform hides into usable leather.

3. Leather Cutting

Cut leather with precision for various projects.

4. Armor Crafting

Create protective leather armor and gear.

5. Accessory Creation

Craft leather accessories like belts, pouches, and holsters.

6. Stitching Mastery

Perform expert stitching on leather goods.

7. Dye Application

Apply dyes to leather for color and finish.

8. Pattern Design

Design patterns for leather projects.

9. Embossing

Emboss decorative designs onto leather.

10. Reinforcement

Reinforce leather goods for increased durability.

11. Specialty Leathers

Work with exotic and specialty leather types.

12. Saddle Making

Craft saddles and tack for riding.

13. Leather Armor Enhancement

Enhance leather armor with protective properties.

14. Repair and Restoration

Repair and restore damaged leather goods.

15. Custom Fitting

Create custom-fitted leather items for individuals.

16. Tooling Mastery

Master advanced tooling techniques on leather.

17. Whip Crafting

Craft whips and flexible leather weapons.

18. Masterwork Leatherworks

Create masterwork leather items of exceptional quality.

19. Masterwork Leathercraft

Craft masterwork leather goods of legendary quality.

20. Legendary Leather

Craft armor and goods from legendary materials.
