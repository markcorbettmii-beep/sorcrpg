Game Master Rules & Tools

GM's are required to take a SorC Assessment, which upon passing, grants their account to GM references. Passing the GM Assessment also changes their account id, tags their profile name, for instance PlayerPC profile name would be switched to Game_MasterGM, upon passing the GM Assessment. Keep in mind, sir names (beginning with _) are optional.

On top of directing campaigns, GMs can play as Characters as well.
