Elf

Graceful and long-lived forest dwellers skilled in archery and nature magic, Elves dwell in harmony with their woodland homes. The Firstborn Elves carry the First Tongue, the oldest form of Elven speech, and the knowledge bound into ancient work and magic. Of distant Fae lineage, descended from Tenue through the Veilwood into Zailister, they carry magic in their blood and immortality in their souls. Elves measure time differently than shorter-lived races, viewing centuries as chapters in an endless story.
