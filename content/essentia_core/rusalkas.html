The Rusalkas

The Rusalkas are a tragic, terrifying supernatural threat bound to the freezing northern continent of Nivis. They are not a civilized or playable race; instead, they are vengeful water spirits born whenever a humanoid drowns anywhere in Zailister's vast oceans and waterways, with their souls violently dragged to manifest within the cold, foggy bogs of the Mucklore Wetlands.

Ecology & Lore

  • The Drowned Rebirth: Whenever a sailor, traveler, or pirate perishes by drowning in the Cabria Ocean, the Umbra Sea (#3), or the turbulent Strait of Nivithunderneathalion (#5), their soul does not pass on. It sinks into the seabed and re-emerges weeks later in the stagnant pools of the Mucklore Wetlands.
  • The Silt-Pale Apparitions: They retain a horrifyingly beautiful humanoid shape. Their skin is waterlogged and bone-white, their eyes are glassy and dilated, and their long, green hair is perpetually dripping wet, moving like independent swamp tentacles.
  • The Mournful Choir: Unlike the solitary hags of other realms, Rusalkas form loose sisterhoods. They congregate near freezing shorelines, using hypnotic, overlapping giggles and weeping cries to lure entire passing vessels or shore patrols into the deep muck.

Combat Mechanics & Regional Traits

Regional Effect: The Sinking Fog

The air around the Mucklore Wetlands and connected shores is perpetually blanketed in a dense, freezing mist. Any creature tracking through this region has disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sight or hearing due to the echoing, phantom laughter of the Rusalkas.

Faction Actions & Abilities

  • Vengeful Giggle: Ranged Spell Attack. A Rusalka unleashes an eerie, echoing laugh targeted at one creature within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or become Frightened for 1 minute as hallucinations of their own drowning flash before their eyes.
  • The Willow's Embrace: Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) cold damage. The target is grappled by the Rusalka's animated, vine-like wet hair. While grappled, the target takes automatic cold damage at the start of each of its turns.
  • Water-Bound Rejuvenation: If a Rusalka starts its turn submerged in a natural body of water within Zailister, it regains 10 hit points. If a Rusalka is completely dried out or exposed to extreme heat, its movement speed is halved, and it loses its resistances.

Notable Campaign Hooks

  • The Drowned Highway: Ships traveling through the Strait of Nivithunderneathalion (#5) have begun vanishing at an alarming rate. Surviving sailors claim the water itself rose up, taking the shape of a weeping mother and daughter who dragged the hull down into the freezing depths.
  • The Frozen Silt: A local noble's daughter drowned near the Pearl Coast (#2), but her body was never recovered. Divination magic reveals she has transformed into a Rusalka, and she is currently terrorizing the borderlands of Nivis, hunting for her living family.
