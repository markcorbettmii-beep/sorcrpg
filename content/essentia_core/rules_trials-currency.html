Trials of Combat Currency

The Trials of Combat are competitions where adventurers face battles and challenges to earn points and rewards. Led by Field Marshalls, participants can earn Honor, Valor, and Auctorati PTS, which are spent on gear, potions, and knowledge. The trials include arenas, coliseums, and strategic objectives, emphasizing skill and teamwork for powerful rewards and reputation.
