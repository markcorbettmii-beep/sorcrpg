Formulas

This page holds every formula behind the game, Vitality, Apex, Spirit, Wellness, Traits, Abilities, Combat, Crafting, and Spellcasting alike, pulled out of the Player-facing chapters so those stay plain-language only. Nothing here is required reading to play, the Basic Rules chapters cover everything a Player actually needs at the table. This is for anyone who wants to see the math itself, verify a number, or homebrew off the same logic the book uses. The GM Codex carries its own step-by-step walkthroughs of the same formulas as well, this page doesn't replace that, it's an additional, Player-accessible reference.

Note: Displayed values are formulated, meaning nothing is hand tuned; although, our formulas are hidden to avoid confusion. GMs, and even Players, can use Cards and their values as common reference points to calculate, and simulate, or create homebrew material.

How These Formulas Work

Every formula on this page runs off a small set of discrete character values: Attribute scores, Trait scores, Apex, Spirit, Character Level, Growth, and the flat numbers on a piece of gear. None of them move on their own between turns, a stat only changes the moment one of its inputs changes, a Trait score rises, a new piece of gear goes on, a buff or Hex lands or ends. That's the whole point of writing it this way: nobody re-runs math every round. A value gets resolved once, when it actually changes, and whatever it resolves to gets written down and used as-is until the next change.

Buffs, Hexes, and other temporary modifiers work the same way. When one lands, resolve its effect against the current score once, write down the new number, and use that number for as long as the buff or Hex lasts, don't re-add the modifier on every roll. When it ends, the score reverts to whatever it was before. A Card or Trait entry that grants a temporary bonus states what it changes and by how much, this page is where the underlying formula for that base value lives, so a GM can always verify the new number is right.

Ability, Martial or Spell alike, roll against the same target a basic weapon attack does, the target's Protection Score (PROTS), never a Difficulty Score (DIFS). DIFS is reserved for Trait checks, resisting an effect, sensing a lie, forcing a check, and skill or Crafting checks, see Trait Checks & Opposed Rolls, below, for what sets a DIFS in each case. SORC doesn't use a Challenge Rating, DIFS, Armor Score (AS), and PROTS together cover every target number an attack, Ability, or check ever needs.

Trait Checks & Opposed Rolls

Trait Check Formula: d100 + Trait score vs. DIFS. Success resists the effect or lands the offensive attempt, failure lets it proceed. A DIFS is set one of two ways: a fixed, GM-set difficulty for an impersonal or environmental challenge, or an opposed creature's own Trait score when another character is the source, see the table below for which applies to which Trait. An ongoing effect, poison, a sustained physical effort, a collapsing structure, calls for a fresh check every round against the same DIFS until it's cured, ends, or fails outright, the DIFS itself doesn't change round to round, only whether that round's roll clears it.

More than one Trait can apply, always, not just in a few named cases: Traits may be interchangeable between one another for certain tasks, for instance Strength and Agility may both aid in Checks against Grapple, or Wit and Intuition may aid a character in finding their way out of a maze or windy labyrinths. The table below lists the default fit for each Trait, not an exclusive lock on that situation. Every entry in this table is swappable the moment a Player's stated approach earns a different Trait, a stuck door can run on Strength (forcing it) or Dexterity (picking it), talking down a hostile crowd can run on Charisma or Courage, spotting a forgery can run on Wit, Intuition, or Knowledge. This is a GM call made fresh from the fiction each time, never a fixed lookup, and it isn't limited to the pairs named here.

Assertive vs. defensive is the same mechanic: a Trait Check run on offense and the same Trait Check run on defense both resolve as d100 + Trait score vs. DIFS, just aimed a different direction, never two separate systems. Defense, Resistance, Constitution, Luck, Focus, and Health are the deliberate exceptions and never resolve against a DIFS at all, see the note below the table above. A Trait resolving as a Check doesn't stop it from also being a flat bonus grafted onto a wholly separate roll, an attack roll, a damage roll, a Critical Hit bonus, several Traits do both. Traits also stack with Talents and Skills whenever the theme genuinely lines up, adding their score on top of that Talent or Skill's own roll at the GM's discretion, not a fixed list, whatever the fiction supports.

Constitution, Focus, Luck, and Health don't resolve as opposed checks, Constitution feeds the Total Vitality Formula directly (see Vitality, below), Focus is a passive concentration bonus (see Panic Engine, GM Codex), Luck adds its own roll on top of any other roll rather than being checked against anything itself (see Luck Formula, below), and Health modulates Wellness's tick rate instead of rolling against anything (see Wellness Tick Formula, below).

Reminder: Resistance and Defense never both apply to the same incoming attack. Resistance adds to PROTS only against a Spell-sourced attack, Defense adds to PROTS only against a martial or Nature-sourced attack. They don't stack against each other's source, exactly one of the two is ever live on any given attack, see PROTS Formula, below.

Luck Formula

Luck roll = 1d4 + Luck score, added on top of basic Hit rolls, Defense rolls, all Trait checks, Skills, Talents, and all DIFS checks, never ability-based hit rolls. On tied Initiative, Luck breaks the tie. The final probability of any outcome still can't exceed 100%.

Vitality

Total Vitality Formula

Total Vitality for a given resource = [Base + ((Character Level + Growth) x per-level rate) + Trait modifier] x (1 + 0.01 x Apex allocated to that resource). Apex always equals Fortitude's score directly, no adjustment, Wellness never touches it. Growth uses the same per-level rate as Character Level but arrives on its own slower schedule (see Growth: Vitality, GM Codex Ch. II, for the full table).

Per-level rates: Life +18, plus Constitution score. Mana +12, plus Wit score. Stamina +12, plus Willpower score. Endurance +11, no Trait modifier. These rates are consistent across all sizes, size differences are reflected in the base values (see Base Vitality by Size, Basic Rules pg. 2).

Spirit Regeneration Formula

Life, Mana, and Stamina each regenerate 1 Chip per Turn for every point of Spirit allocated to that resource, always active, in combat, out of combat, and during rest, and it stacks with any Nature or Spell Ability that also recovers Vitality (see Trait Secondary Effect Formula, below, for the separate Chip regeneration Constitution, Willpower, Grit, and Vigor each grant on their own). Endurance is never affected by Spirit, it resets to full at the start of each day instead. Spirit always equals Willpower's score directly, no adjustment, Wellness never touches it either.

Worked Example: Kaida's Vitality

Kaida is a Standard-size, Character Level 30, Growth 8 character with Fortitude 40, Willpower 16, Constitution 38, and Wit 14. Level + Growth = 38 for every resource.

Life: Base 50 + (38 x 18) + Constitution 38 = 772 pre-Apex. Apex 40 is split 25/Life, 10/Stamina, 5/Endurance, 0/Mana. Multiplier (Life) = 1 + (0.01 x 25) = 1.25. Total Life = 772 x 1.25 = 965.

Stamina: Base 32 + (38 x 12) + Willpower 16 = 504 pre-Apex. Multiplier (Stamina) = 1.10. Total Stamina = 504 x 1.10 = 554.4, rounds to 554.

Endurance: Base 35 + (38 x 11) = 453 pre-Apex, no Trait modifier. Multiplier (Endurance) = 1.05. Total Endurance = 453 x 1.05 = 475.65, rounds to 476.

Mana: Base 32 + (38 x 12) + Wit 14 = 502 pre-Apex. Kaida allocates no Apex to Mana, she's not a caster, so the multiplier stays 1.00. Total Mana = 502.

Her Spirit of 16 is split 10/Life, 6/Stamina, 0/Mana (she has no use for Mana regeneration), regenerating 10 Life Chips and 6 Stamina Chips every Turn, on top of whatever her Traits regenerate at Score 5+ (see Trait Secondary Effect Formula, below).

Wellness Tick Formula

Wellness ranges from 0% (minimum) to 150% (maximum), starting at character creation and modified by play. Wellness never directly touches Apex, Spirit, or any Vitality resource numerically, instead, it modifies Apex and Spirit's effective values (see Wellness Impact on Apex and Spirit, below). Wellness ticks automatically based on the Character's health patterns. Below 75% Wellness, only deliberate neglect, poison, disease, and injury cause ticking downward. At 75% Wellness and above, both positive and negative effects accelerate, and ticking occurs in both directions depending on the character's pattern.

Wellness RangeDays per Tick (base)Direction
0% - 74%Variable (see below)Downward only (neglect, poison, disease, injury)
75% - 99%4Either direction (depends on character pattern)
100% - 124%2Either direction
125% - 150%1Either direction

Each tick moves Wellness 1% in the direction it's already heading (up for healthy patterns, down for neglect). Landing on a new band immediately adopts that band's day-count for the next tick.

Health Trait Modulation: Tier = floor(Health score ÷ 5). Below 75% Wellness (declining), each tier adds 2 days to the tick length, slowing decline and giving robust characters more time before their health deteriorates. At 75% Wellness and above (climbing or neutral), each tier removes 1 day from the tick length (minimum 1 day), allowing well-cared-for characters with high Health to reach peak condition faster. A Health score below 5 (tier 0) applies no modulation, the base schedule above runs unmodified. This creates an asymmetric benefit: high Health characters are more resilient against decline but not necessarily better at recovering (the difference is smaller going up than going down).

Wellness Impact on Apex and Spirit: Apex and Spirit values are modified by a Wellness multiplier (never increased or decreased, only multiplied). From 0% to 74% Wellness, the multiplier decreases by 1% for every 1% below 75%, so: 75% Wellness = 1.0x, 50% Wellness = 0.75x, 0% Wellness = 0x. At 75% Wellness and above, the multiplier increases by 1% for every 1% above 75%, so: 75% Wellness = 1.0x, 100% Wellness = 1.25x, 150% Wellness = 1.75x. Spirit regeneration and Apex caps are both affected by this multiplier; low-Wellness characters have smaller resource pools and regenerate slower, while high-Wellness characters have larger pools and regenerate faster.

Diet Modifier: Diet applies a small, steady modifier that builds over Growth. Good diet: +0.5% Wellness per Growth tier (accumulates slowly but consistently). Poor diet: -0.5% Wellness per Growth tier. Excellent diet (via Cooks): +1% per Growth tier. Starvation/Poisoned food: -2% per Growth tier. Diet changes apply between long rests, not during encounters.

Rest Modifiers: Rest accelerates Wellness ticking based on rest quality and type. Breather (10 min): +1 day tick speed (base schedule speeds up 1 day). Short Rest (1 hour): +2 days tick speed. Full Rest (8 hours, requires bed): +4 days tick speed. Environmental conditions modify rest effectiveness: ideal conditions (warm, safe, comfortable, well-fed) grant full bonus; poor conditions (cold, noisy, uncomfortable, hungry) reduce bonus by 50%; hostile conditions grant 0 bonus. Without a bed, Full Rest grants only +1 day tick speed for Wellness (though Endurance restores normally).

Module-Specific Adjustments: GMs adjust base tick rates according to module context and character circumstances. Examples: harsh survival conditions slow Wellness ticking 50%; luxurious/safe environments accelerate it 50%; presence of supportive allies/community grants +1 day per week; isolation or trauma applies -1 day per week. These adjustments stack with all other modifiers and represent environmental and social factors affecting character wellness trajectory.

Stance Benefits (Practitioner Relationship): Characters with Stance (established relationship with a primary Practitioner) receive cumulative benefits representing friendship, gifting, and care. Initial Stance (first regular visit): +1 day tick speed for Wellness recovery. Established Stance (5+ visits): +2 days. Strong Stance (10+ visits, regular contact): +3 days, plus +0.5% Wellness per month of regular visits (accumulated care). Friendship gifts from Practitioners (healing tonics, wellness advice, etc.) provide additional +1% Wellness per gift (one per quarter maximum). Broken Stance (3+ months without contact or betrayal of trust): all bonuses lost until rebuilt through repeated visits and actions restoring trust.

Reminder: Every stat, cost, and threshold in this book is formulated, none are hand-tuned by the designers. These formulas exist so the logic is never hidden, not so a Player needs to run them. See Basic Rules pg. 2 for the plain-language version, and the GM Codex, Ch. II, for full step-by-step walkthroughs and the Wellness tick-rate tables.

Trait Secondary Effects

Trait Secondary Effect Formula

Effect tier = floor(Trait Score ÷ 5), 0 below a score of 5. Constitution, Willpower, Grit, and Vigor add [tier] Chips to their aligned Vitality resource (Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance respectively) at the start of each Turn. Every other Trait lets the Player designate one Ability aligned with that Trait's theme; that Ability's Cooldown or Recharge resets [tier] times per encounter instead of waiting on its normal Turn or Rest timer.

Abilities

Vitality Cost Formula

Chip Cost = ceil(Slot Level ÷ 2), minimum 2. A Level 3 Tier pick costs 2, a Level 5 Path pick costs 3, a Level 21 Branch pick costs 11, a Level 30 capstone costs 15. Martial spends Stamina, Spell spends Mana, both use the same formula. Costed independently per pick, a Level that grants two picks at once (a Character Level and its midpoint) simply adds both picks' costs together. Both Nature and Spell Abilities cost at least 1 action as well.

Ability Uses Formula

N = floor(Character's Max Pool for that resource ÷ Ability's Cost), the number of times a full pool alone affords the Ability with zero regeneration. Match N against the Tier table below. Every Ability Card states both Cost and Uses, Uses is the hard ceiling, Cost is whether the Character can currently afford to reach it. Regardless of Tier, no Ability may be used more than once in the same Turn, the Round/Day Tiers govern spacing across Turns, never a same-Turn multiplier.

A handful of Cards override the formula outright, Resurrection, permanent transformations, and other narrative capstone effects are fixed at Uses: 1/Day by design, a Tier the formula alone can never produce, Breather/Short Rest regeneration always leaves room for a second use before a Long Rest.

Ability Cooldown & Recharge Formula

Cooldown (CD): Applies to Round-tier Abilities. Charges = the Ability's Uses/Round number (3/Round = 3 Charges, 2/Round = 2 Charges, 1/Round = 1 Charge). One Ability Reset/Recharge Chip returns to the Card per Turn (6 seconds) until all Charges are restored.

Recharge (RECHRG): Applies to Day-tier Abilities. Charges = the Ability's Uses/Day number (3/Day = 3 Charges, 2/Day = 2 Charges). Chips return at the next Long Rest, or Short Rest if the Card specifically allows it.

Unlimited-Use Abilities carry neither symbol and use no Chips, there's nothing to track.

Combat

PROTS Formula

PROTS = Base Armor Score (AS) + all positive and negative modifiers to Protection. Because Defense and Resistance never both apply to the same attack (see Trait Checks & Opposed Rolls, above), PROTS actually resolves as two separate numbers off the same Base AS:

PROTS vs. martial or Nature-sourced attacks = Base AS + Defense Trait score + shield AS + environmental/situational modifiers + buffs/debuffs.
PROTS vs. Spell-sourced attacks = Base AS + Resistance Trait score (typed, see Trait Checks & Opposed Rolls) + environmental/situational modifiers + buffs/debuffs.

An attacker must roll equal to or above whichever of the two applies to their attack's source to hit. A character with no Defense score simply uses Base AS + modifiers against martial/Nature attacks, a Character with no Resistance score does the same against Spells, neither Trait is required to have a PROTS at all.

Initiative Formula

Initiative = Base Speed + Agility + Intuition. Luck breaks ties (see Luck Formula, above).

Critical Hit Formula

A roll of 96-100 is a Critical Hit, dealing 2x damage dice. Add a bonus score on top of that doubled roll depending on the attack's source: Strength for melee, Agility for martial ranged (Shoot), Acuity for Spells. A roll of 1-5 is a Botch/Fizzle instead, see Hit Results, below.

Weapon Damage Formula

dmg = xd6 ± x. x = Strength (Melee), Agility (Shoot), Acuity (Spells) ± other modifiers (enchantments, Traits, buffs, environmental factors, situational bonuses). Hit Bonus and damage dice both scale by weapon Rank, see the Appendix, below, for the full Rank tables.

Unarmed & TAW Combat

Unarmed Hit: d100 + Strength vs PROTS. Damage (main): 1d4 + Strength. Damage (off): 1d2 + Strength.
TAW Melee: Agility replaces Strength for both hit and damage. Hit: d100 + weapon rank + Agility vs PROTS. Damage: weapon rank dice + Agility + modifiers.

Skills & Crafting

Crafting Check Formula

d100 + Skill Score + Attribute Modifier vs. DIFS. The Attribute modifier used depends on the specific skill, a Blacksmith check might use Strength or Intellect, a Jeweler check uses Artistry or Intellect, an Herbalist check uses Wisdom or Vigilance, the skill card or GM determines which. DIFS depends on the item's complexity, rarity tier, and required quality, see Difficulty by Rarity in the Appendix, below, for the baseline bands.

Spellcasting

Meditated Spells Formula

Max spells meditated = Base 3 + Ch. Lvl + Willpower score + Rank Modifier, see the Appendix, below, for the Rank Modifier table.
