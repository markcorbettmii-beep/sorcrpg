Chapter III: Fallen & Risen

Stub: Fallen and Risen Classes are described from the player side in Basic Rules pg. 3. This chapter follows that page and will not go further until it does. Only Paths bound to a Higher Power can Fall or Rise, not Classes as a whole, alignment lives at the Path level. Lawful and Moral Paths may Fall, Evil and Amoral Paths may Rise, Ambivalent Paths can do neither. A Falling or Rising Path's Higher Power breaks with the character once current alignment drifts below Moral or rises above Amoral, respectively, the character keeps every Ability already learned from that Path but loses its bonuses, can no longer learn new Abilities from it, and begins to take penalties and tests from that Path's former Higher Power.
