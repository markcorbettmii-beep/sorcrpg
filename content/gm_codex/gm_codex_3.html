GM Codex

Chapter III: Fallen & Risen

These rules are under heavy development. This is currently the full extent of the rule; more will be added over time.

Fallen and Risen Classes are described from the player side in Basic Rules Pg. 3. Only classes bound to a Higher Power can fall or rise, Lawful classes may Fall, Evil classes may Rise. Falling or Rising characters whose alignment drifts all the way to Neutral can no longer perform or earn new Abilities or Spells from the class tree that is either lawful or evil, and they begin to take penalties and tests from their respective higher powers.

GM Tool, Physical and Sensory Effects: The GM may alter a falling or rising character's physical appearance and scent as their alignment shifts, alongside the Boasted and Exposed Titles that shame or celebrate them. A Lawful shift may manifest as a faint halo or holy aura. An Evil shift may manifest as a gathering stench, though not automatically, an Evil character who bathes and avoids squalor need not smell of it, but one who neglects hygiene, resides underground, or keeps foul company may develop that stench, and draw vermin, as a visible consequence. These are GM-discretion flavor tools, not mechanical penalties.
