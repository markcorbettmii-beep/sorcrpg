GM Codex

Chapter III: Fallen & Risen

Fallen and Risen Classes are fully described from the player side in Basic Rules Pg. 3, which system, which classes qualify, the Punishment System's two-part escalation, the Neutral Threshold, the Level 20 Gate, and the Two Trees. That section points here, to the GM Codex, four separate times for the GM-facing procedures that make the system actually runnable at the table. Those four gaps are this chapter.

Still To Do, Chapter III

To Do, Weighing Boasted vs. Exposed Titles: For an Undeclared (Drifting) character, Basic Rules Pg. 3 says the GM averages a character's Boasted and Exposed Titles at Level 20 to confirm whether the Fall or Rise goes through, but the actual weighting formula isn't written yet. This needs to define whether all Titles count equally or by rarity/severity, how a tie resolves, and how the running tally already tracked on the GM Sheet (Ch. I) feeds into that average.
To Do, Class-Specific Test Charts: Basic Rules Pg. 3 gives three worked examples (Falling Paladin, Rising Warlock, Falling Cleric) and says the complete chart, adversaries, what they attempt (steal, trick, trap, harm, curse, hex, etc.), and what they seek, lives here for every class capable of falling or rising. This chapter needs one chart per Lawful class (Fall) and per Evil class (Rise), scaled to the Stage 1-9 escalation already defined player-side.
To Do, Redemption Before Level 20: Basic Rules Pg. 3 states the Neutral Threshold cutoff can be reversed by "turning back before Level 20 and restoring standing with the original Higher Power," but doesn't define what that requires. This needs the actual redemption procedure, what a character has to do, what the GM adjudicates, and what it costs, to undo a Fall or Rise in progress.
To Do, Reclaiming Dormant Abilities: Basic Rules Pg. 3 notes that because old ability trees are locked rather than erased after a full transformation, "certain redemption paths may exist to reclaim dormant Abilities." This chapter needs to define whether that's possible post-transformation (not just pre-Level 20), and if so, the quest/procedure structure for it.
To Do, Narrating the Level 20 Transformation: Not yet promised explicitly player-side, but a genuine gap, GM-facing pacing and narration guidance for running the actual Level 20 moment, the 10-Ability loss and the player's choice of which 10 to carry forward, at the table.
To Do, Pacing a Multi-Level Fall/Rise Arc: Projected from how other TTRPGs run long-form corruption or redemption tracks (Vampire: The Masquerade's Humanity, Werewolf: The Apocalypse's Harano), a Fall or Rise plays out over many sessions between Stage 1 and Level 20. This chapter needs pacing guidance, how often Class-Specific Tests should actually land relative to session count, and how to keep a slow-burn arc from either stalling or overwhelming the table.
To Do, When the Party Reacts to a Falling/Rising Member: A convention in games with visible corruption or morality tracks, other party members can usually see the drift happening (Alignment bar, accumulating Titles) and may try to intervene, exploit it, or distance themselves. This chapter needs guidance for handling a party that opposes, aids, or tries to stop a member's Fall or Rise mid-arc.
