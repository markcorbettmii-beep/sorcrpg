GM Codex

Introduction

Every SORC book, including this one, is available in hardback, softback, and PDF editions.

This book is free to read by anyone, GM, Player, or guest, so you know exactly what you're getting into before ever signing in. The SORC Assessment below is what actually gates access to Lobbies, not this book, passing it issues a GM Code alongside the GM tag and profile update, so other players' time isn't wasted on an unassessed GM. A player who hasn't passed the Assessment can still read everything here, they just can't run or assess into a Lobby as a GM until they pass it..

Chapter I: GM Sheet

The GM Sheet is where a GM keeps everything about a character that either must stay hidden from the player or requires GM-side confirmation before it's reflected on the Character Sheet. One GM Sheet is kept per character in the campaign. Fields marked "(see rules)" on the Character Sheet's Progression Tracker are the ones this sheet resolves.

GM Sheet under development.

GM Profile

Identifying fields kept once per campaign, not per character, filled in before any Per-Character Progression tracking begins below.

FieldWhat It Tracks
GM NameThe GM's real or account name.
NicknameThe GM's display name at the table, optional, defaults to GM Name if left blank.
Campaign/Module NameThe module or homebrew campaign currently being run.
Date StartedWhen this campaign began, for reference against character Growth and Age tracking.
Player RosterThe characters (and their players) this GM Sheet's Per-Character Progression tables below cover.

Per-Character Progression

FieldWhat It Tracks
Current GrowthMirrors the Character Sheet.
Current Character Level (Ch. Lvl)Mirrors the Character Sheet. Advances faster than Growth, see Growth and Character Level, Basic Rules pg. 4.
Years to Next GrowthNo hidden threshold to reveal, Growth is purely time-based, so this is just a countdown of years remaining on the current escalating wait (see Growth Development, Ch. II).
Drawn Abilities Picks LogOne entry per Growth, recording which of the two categories, Nature Drawn or Spell Drawn, the character chose and which specific Ability was learned, see Drawn Abilities, Basic Rules pg. 7.
Current RankMirrors the Character Sheet.
RP to Next RankPublished Rank Point Progression value (Ch. II), kept here for quick reference during play. Advancing also requires meeting the next rank's Ch. Lvl and Growth (Beginning Age + Years), see Rank Level Prerequisites, Ch. II.
Alignment Position + DriftMirrors the Character Sheet's Alignment bar, see Basic Rules pg. 2 and pg. 3.
Drifted Past a Path's Higher PowerYes/No flag per invested Path. A Lawful or Moral Path's Higher Power breaks with the character once current alignment drifts below Moral (Falling); an Evil or Amoral Path's breaks once it rises above Amoral (Rising); Ambivalent Paths are never affected. A Falling or Rising character keeps every Ability already learned from that Path, but loses its Higher Power bonuses, can no longer learn new Abilities from it, and begins to take penalties and tests from its former Higher Power, see Fallen and Risen Classes, Basic Rules pg. 3. This rule is under heavy development, currently the full extent of it.
Mission Statement StatusThe character's life quest chain, developed and managed by the GM, it never truly ends, so this tracks where the current chain stands and what it's evolved into. See Mission Statement, Basic Rules pg. 2.
Downtime/Recovery Time RemainingComatosis, prison, infirmary stays, and the Death Penalty's Time Lost all use the same formula, Penalty Time = Base Time × (Growth Rate)^(Nature of Offenses). This field is the countdown until the character is free to act normally again. See More on Downtime, Basic Rules pg. 5.
Age / Retirement CountdownCharacter's current in-world age (Beginning Age chosen at creation from a 5-year range centered on their race's Starting Age, see Age, Basic Rules pg. 2) against their Retirement Age (Starting Age + 40; see Retirement, Basic Rules pg. 4). This same age is what's checked against each rank's Growth (Beginning Age + Years) requirement, see Rank Level Prerequisites, Ch. II.

Simulated Characters

GMs may simulate a character's Ch. Lvl and Growth (age), but never their Rank, Rank is only ever earned, no exceptions. A simulated character's gear access is gated by their actual, earned Rank, regardless of what their simulated Ch. Lvl or Growth (age) would otherwise allow.

FieldWhat It Tracks
Simulated Ch. LvlThe Character Level (Ch. Lvl) the GM has assigned for this module, if simulated.
Simulated Growth (Age)The Growth (Beginning Age + Years) the GM has assigned for this module, if simulated. Like Simulated Ch. Lvl, this can satisfy the Level/Age gate in Rank Level Prerequisites, it just never substitutes for actually-earned RP.
Actual Earned RankNever simulated directly, Rank still requires actually-earned RP no matter how high a character's Simulated Ch. Lvl or Simulated Growth (Age) go. Characters MUST earn Rank; there is no exception.
Actual Earned RPThe real total behind the Actual Earned Rank above.
Equippable Rank TierSet by Actual Earned Rank, not Simulated Ch. Lvl or Simulated Growth (Age). Sourced from prior modules/expansions or the downloadable PDF version's back-catalog matching that Rank.
Since adventures are module-based, and each module includes Cards up to its own Rank tier (see Module Level to Card Rank, Quick Reference below), a simulated character reaches a new module already assumed to hold the Rank-appropriate gear of every prior expansion. Simulating Rank instead of tracking it honestly breaks this assumption, see RP Earning from Modules, GM Codex pg. 3.

Companion Tracking

Tracked per Companion, not per character, a character with multiple Companions needs one row per Companion.

FieldWhat It Tracks
AilmentsTemporary debuffs the GM applies quietly after specific events; not tied directly to Mood, though Mood affects how fast they resolve. The GM determines when they lift.
Performance Tier ProgressSustained Mood duration toward the next Performance Tier (Basic/Intermediate/Advanced). Current tier itself is recorded on the Character Sheet once reached, this row tracks progress toward that.
Caretaker EffectivenessIf the Companion is left at a safe keep, the GM's assessment of the assigned caretaker's quality, which affects how well Mood is maintained in the owner's absence.
Boneyard StatusRare rank and above remain in the boneyard until resurrected, repaired, or restocked, track what's waiting there and its resurrection/repair progress. Common and Uncommon Companions that die are permanently lost and never enter the boneyard. A resurrected Companion's Mood resets to Annoyed, the lowest tier.

Attunement

Heroic rank armaments and above require Attunement before they can be enhanced or Bound, tracked per character, since Attunement automatically unattunes an item from its previous bearer.

FieldWhat It Tracks
Attuned Items (per character)What each character is currently Attuned to, and at what Rank, Attunement gets harder the higher the Rank.
Boss-Attuned Items Claimed (campaign-wide)Items attuned to Bosses exist only once per campaign and cannot be duplicated, this is the GM's running list of which ones are already spoken for, checked before any Boss-attuned item is awarded again.

Campaign Log

FieldWhat It Tracks
Current ModuleModule name, Level range, and included Card Rank (see Quick Reference below).
Dilemma Card ChoicesLogged as they're made, for consistency at pivotal moments later in the campaign.
NPC Disposition SummaryA running summary of Stance shifts; full detail stays on each NPC's own Encounter Card.
In-Game TimeRounds converted to hours and days as needed between encounters.

Retirement

Every character retires at the Retirement Age (see Retirement, Basic Rules pg. 4). Tracked per retired character, since a retired PC becomes a permanent piece of the campaign's world.

FieldWhat It Tracks
Rank at RetirementLocked in permanently, determines whether the character is commemorated as a Legend (monuments, statues, songs) or retires more quietly.
Mortal or ImmortalDetermines what happens next, see below.
Living NPC StatusImmortal retirees only, where they are in the world, and whether/how the party can go visit them.
CommemorationMortal retirees, what monument, statue, or song (if any) the world raised in their name, and where it can be found.
Lore Cameo LogEvery module or expansion a retired character has appeared or been referenced in, to avoid contradicting earlier appearances.
Placeholder: The exact Retirement Age (same across all races) has not been finalized. Retired-character NPC stat conversion and the Legend commemoration procedure (what a GM actually narrates/builds for a monument, statue, or song) also still need to be written. Refer to Chapter II once complete.
