Appendix

This appendix will collect the most frequently referenced tables, formulas, and quick-reference material from across the GM Codex - the same role Basic Rules' own appendix plays for the player-facing book. It's a placeholder for now since Chapters II through VI don't have their detailed content yet; once they do, their tables get pulled in here for at-the-table lookup without hunting through each chapter.
