Vitality Cost Formula, Step by Step

Formula: Chip Cost = ceil(Slot Level ÷ 2), minimum 2. See Vitality Cost Formula, Basic Rules pg. 2 and pg. 6, for the player-facing summary.

Example 1, a Level 5 Path pick:

  • Step 1: Slot Level = 5.
  • Step 2: Divide by 2: 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5.
  • Step 3: Round up (ceiling): 2.5 → 3.
  • Step 4: Compare to the minimum of 2: 3 already clears it, so Chip Cost = 3.
  • Step 5: Nature costs 3 Stamina; Spell costs 3 Mana, whichever half of the Split was picked.

Example 2, a Level 3 Tier pick (odd Level, needs rounding):

  • Step 1: Slot Level = 3.
  • Step 2: Divide by 2: 3 ÷ 2 = 1.5.
  • Step 3: Round up: 1.5 → 2.
  • Step 4: Compare to the minimum of 2: equal, so Chip Cost = 2.

Example 3, a Level 30 capstone:

  • Step 1: Slot Level = 30.
  • Step 2: Divide by 2: 30 ÷ 2 = 15, already a whole number.
  • Step 3: Compare to the minimum of 2: 15 clears it easily, so Chip Cost = 15.

Ability Uses Formula, Step by Step

Formula: N = floor(Character's Max Pool for that resource ÷ Ability's Cost), the number of times a full pool alone affords the Ability with zero regeneration. Match N against the Tier table: N≥12 Unlimited, N=6-11 3/Round, N=4-5 2/Round, N=3 1/Round, N=2 3/Day, N=1 2/Day. Every Ability Card states both Cost and Uses side by side, Uses is the hard ceiling, Cost is whether the character can currently afford to reach it. Regardless of Tier, no Ability may be used more than once in the same Turn (Uses Cap, Per Turn, Basic Rules pg. 5), an "Unlimited" or "3/Round" Ability is still capped at one use inside any single Turn, the Tier governs spacing across Turns within a Round or a day, never a same-Turn multiplier. See Ability Uses Formula, Basic Rules pg. 2 and pg. 6.

Example 1, a real ability from the book, Avenger's Shield-Wall Guard (Cleric Path, LVL 4, Slot A: Protector), Standard character:

  • Card stat-line: Range: Touch | Style: Blunt | Hand: 1h Main Offhand Shield
  • Step 1: Slot Level = 4 (a LVL 4 Path pick).
  • Step 2: Cost = ceil(4 ÷ 2) = ceil(2) = 2, already clears the minimum of 2, so Cost = 2 Stamina (Nature Drawn).
  • Step 3: Max Pool: at Level 4 a character is almost certainly still at Growth 0 (Growth 1 takes a full year of play, Character Level moves far faster), so Max Pool = base Standard Stamina = 32.
  • Step 4: N = floor(32 ÷ 2) = 16.
  • Step 5: Match against the Tier table: N≥12, so Uses = Unlimited.
  • Filled Card: Range: Touch | Style: Blunt | Hand: 1h Main Offhand Shield / Cost: 2 Stamina / Uses: Unlimited.

Example 2, a Level 9 pick, Standard character:

  • Step 1: Cost = ceil(9 ÷ 2) = 5 Stamina.
  • Step 2: Max Pool = 32 Stamina.
  • Step 3: N = floor(32 ÷ 5) = 6.
  • Step 4: Match against the Tier table: N=6-11, so Uses = 3/Round.

Example 3, a Level 13 pick, Standard character:

  • Step 1: Cost = ceil(13 ÷ 2) = 7 Stamina.
  • Step 2: Max Pool = 32 Stamina.
  • Step 3: N = floor(32 ÷ 7) = 4.
  • Step 4: Match against the Tier table: N=4-5, so Uses = 2/Round.

Example 4, a Level 17 pick, Standard character:

  • Step 1: Cost = ceil(17 ÷ 2) = 9 Stamina.
  • Step 2: Max Pool = 32 Stamina.
  • Step 3: N = floor(32 ÷ 9) = 3.
  • Step 4: Match against the Tier table: N=3, so Uses = 1/Round.

Example 5, a Level 21 Branch pick (the book's own reference cost), Standard character:

  • Step 1: Cost = ceil(21 ÷ 2) = 11 Stamina.
  • Step 2: Max Pool = 32 Stamina.
  • Step 3: N = floor(32 ÷ 11) = 2.
  • Step 4: Match against the Tier table: N=2, so Uses = 3/Day.

Example 6, a Level 30 capstone Spell Drawn pick (the book's own reference cost), Goliath character (lowest base Mana of any size):

  • Step 1: Cost = ceil(30 ÷ 2) = 15 Mana.
  • Step 2: Max Pool = base Goliath Mana = 24.
  • Step 3: N = floor(24 ÷ 15) = 1.
  • Step 4: Match against the Tier table: N=1, so Uses = 2/Day.

Example 7, a designer override (Resurrection):

  • Step 1: Cost = 10 Mana on a Standard character's 32 Mana pool.
  • Step 2: N = floor(32 ÷ 10) = 3, which the pure formula would place at 1/Round.
  • Step 3: Because Resurrection is a narrative capstone effect, its Card overrides the formula's result outright and states Uses: 1/Day instead, a Tier the formula alone can never reach on its own, since Breather/Short Rest regeneration always leaves room for at least a second use before a Long Rest.
