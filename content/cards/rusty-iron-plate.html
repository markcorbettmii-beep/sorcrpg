Rusty Iron Plate

Common Armor Card , Ref #ARM001

Overview
A full set of pitted iron plate armor, weathered and dented from travel. Heavy but functional, rusted in places but still sound enough for a LVL 1-5 Box Set. It has protected many warriors and will protect many more, for as long as the wearer can bear its weight.

Effect
Armor Score (AS) subtracts directly from incoming damage after Vulnerabilities and Resistances are applied. Excellent condition adds +1 AS; Fair (Damaged) subtracts −2 AS; Broken halves AS. Movement penalties from Heavy Grade stack with other penalties from condition, buffs, gear, or abilities. Carry weight toward your 50% encumbrance threshold (see Encumbrance, pg. 5).
Notes
  • Grade is set by the Cuirass (main plate) alone. Lesser-Grade straps, lining, padding, and buckles in the recipe do not change what this armor IS (see Armor Grades & Materials, pg. 4).
  • AS is set at Common Rank as 45 + Rank bonuses. Uncommon adds +6, Rare +12, and higher ranks carry bigger bonuses (see Armor Quality, pg. 4).
  • Quality changes Value only. It never changes AS or combat stats (see Armor Quality, pg. 4).
  • Condition degrades at the end of an encounter in which the wearer was incapacitated, and only a blacksmith or party Armorer restores it. Broken armor halves AS, halves movement, and adds 2 lbs. per Rank.
  • Attunement (Heroic rank and above) is required before enhancement; Common rank needs none (see Armor Quality, pg. 4).
  • Common Rank armor cannot be crafted from rare or exotic materials, material rarity must match Rank.
