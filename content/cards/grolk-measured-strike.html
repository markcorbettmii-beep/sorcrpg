Measured Strike

Fighter Level 1 , Tactical Ability

Overview
A disciplined strike honed through repetition. The fighter draws upon practiced technique rather than instinct, delivering a blow with consistent precision.
Mechanics
Type: Attack Action
Range: Melee (weapon range)
Frequency: At will
Cost: 1 Action
Damage Bonus: Minor (see Ability Formulas)
Effect
Make a melee Attack roll against a single target within range. On a hit, the target takes damage as normal for your weapon plus a minor damage bonus based on your Fighter tier. This bonus is added before calculating critical effects.
Notes
  • This ability represents trained technique and muscle memory.
  • Works with any melee weapon you're proficient with.
  • Can trigger follow-up abilities that key off successful attacks.
