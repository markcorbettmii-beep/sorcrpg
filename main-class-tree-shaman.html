Shaman (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Spirit-world intermediaries who bridge the gap between the living and the ancient, Shamans channel elemental forces, ancestor spirits, and deep earth magic through rituals, mantras, and voodoo-adjacent mysticism, as comfortable calling lightning through a tribal drum as they are raising the bones of the fallen to serve their cause or whispering fire through their own breath.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Totem Rite, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Ancestor's Call, Once Per Day the Shaman summons the spirits of their lineage, healing all allies within earshot for an amount equal to their Willpower SCR.

Extra 1d6 + LVL spirit damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Shamans can Flourish their totem that lowers their target's Resistance at a number equal to their Intuition SCR.

Shamans get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Shamans get +4 plus their KNOWLEDGE SCR on Knowledge checks involving spirit lore.

Shaman Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Medicine Man - A Shaman who draws on ancestral spirits and the bones of the fallen to heal and empower their tribe.
  a. Ancestral - A Medicine Man who calls on ancestor spirits for guidance and protection.
  b. Bone Collector - A Medicine Man who draws power from the bones and remains of the fallen.
  c. Earthbound - A Medicine Man rooted deeply in the land itself, drawing strength from the earth underfoot.
ii. Primordial - A Shaman who channels the raw forces of sky and storm into devastating elemental power.
  a. Astromancer - A Primordial Shaman who reads and channels power from the stars themselves.
  b. Stormbringer - A Primordial Shaman who calls down storms to scour the battlefield.
  c. Whisperer - A Primordial Shaman who hears the land's own voice, translating it into guidance and warning.
iii. Ritualist - A ritual-focused Shaman who binds curses and blessings alike through careful ceremony.
  a. Ceremonial - A Ritualist who channels power through careful, formal rite rather than raw instinct.
  b. Hexbinder - A Ritualist who specializes in binding lasting curses onto their enemies.
  c. Runecrafter - A Ritualist who binds power into runes, empowering allies or cursing foes at a distance.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Light or Delicate (Hide, Ritual Garments).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Shaman's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
