Ignis

Ignis is an elemental world of extreme heat, nearest of the thirteen to Adoria, home to the Emberscale and the Primal. Its craft is bone, stone and hide. It produces no tech and little magic-forged steel, and it is the richest source of worked magic in Essentia.

Adorite, and why Ignis holds it

Ignis is the nearest of the thirteen to Adoria and receives more of her magic than any other world. More arrives than anything living or made there can take up. On any other world that surplus would drift off and be lost to open space, but Ignis pulls it in and keeps it, and what it keeps goes into the ground.

What settles there is Adorite. It is laid into the rock as the rock forms, in lodes where it runs with metal and in pockets where it grew as gem. Slow growth holds more, which is why one Ignis gem is worth a vein of ore.

Every world takes Adoria's magic and every world can harness it. What Ignis has is surplus. Elsewhere there is none, so nothing collects; the magic is used and that is the end of it. Ignis is the only world where it stays in the rock.

Neither people forges what they take out. Working the stone breaks the structure the Adorite was laid into and it bleeds out, which is why Ignis trades its ore and gems raw and why its armor and weapons are worked by hand rather than at a forge.
