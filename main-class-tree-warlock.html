Warlock (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Pact-bound wielders of forbidden power who draw their abilities from entities far beyond mortal comprehension, Warlocks channel dark contracts into magic that warps, drains, and reshapes everything it touches, a bargain that grows more consuming the deeper they pull from it, with their patron's fingerprints increasingly visible in everything they do and say, whether that patron demands cruelty or simply payment.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Patron's Favor, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Pact Calling, Once Per Day the Warlock calls upon their patron, dealing dark damage equal to twice their Willpower SCR to one target, with the Warlock healing for half that amount.

Extra 1d6 + LVL dark damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Warlocks can Flourish their pact weapon that lowers their target's Resistance at a number equal to their Deceit SCR.

Warlocks get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Warlocks get +4 plus their WILLPOWER SCR on Knowledge checks involving forbidden lore.

Warlock Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Bloodlock - A blood-magic Warlock who trades their own vitality, and their enemies', to fuel devastating power.
  a. Blood Knight - A Bloodlock who fights in melee, sealing every strike with their own blood-magic.
  b. Tormentor - A Bloodlock who specializes in prolonged, draining suffering rather than a quick kill.
  c. Vampire - A Bloodlock who drains life directly from enemies to sustain their own.
ii. Grimlock - A fiend-bound Warlock whose patron grants dark power in exchange for a bargain always waiting to be called in.
  a. Changeling - A Grimlock whose patron grants the ability to wear another's face.
  b. Fiend - A Grimlock bound directly to a fiendish patron's raw, dark power.
  c. Hexblade - A Grimlock who channels their patron's power directly through a cursed weapon.
iii. Necromancer - A death-focused Warlock who commands the dead and reaps souls to further their patron's dark cause.
  a. Inquisitor - A Necromancer who hunts and judges, turning death itself into an interrogation.
  b. Soul Reaper - A Necromancer who claims the souls of the fallen to further fuel their own power.
  c. Summoner - A Necromancer who commands the dead directly, raising allies from fallen enemies.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Delicate or Light (Robes, Leather).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Warlock's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
