SorC Beyond

Go beyond our core traditional TTRPG elements of rolling the dice at home, and delve into SorC Beyond for online and digital features, including:

  • Character Profile Page: Create, customize, and manage digital character sheets.

  • Trials of Combat: Trials of Combat: Fairly without paying to win, test your skills in PvP Arenas, Battlegrounds (land, sea, and air), and the Coliseum tiers based on your character's pvp combat score. Duel to the death or practice safely using wooden weapons and fizzled spells (At an in gamr currency fee, characters that die MUST be resurrected by ring judges to re-enter)
    Note: there are many modes of play, such as "reach the vault" and "battlegrounds" that support many players fighting at once, arenas that are 3 v 3 or 5 v 5, "Duel" is 1 on 1, and "Pet Party" which allows up to 5 of your fellowships in the ring.

  • Auction House & Black Market: Trade, buy, and sell rare items, gear, and collectibles.

  • SorC Lobbies: Fill in tardy character spots for your home campaign, by joining our Online Lobbies where both GMs and Players alike enter upon passing an assessment for beginner, intermediate or advanced lobbies.

  • SorC Store:Access physical SorC Cards, books, character pages along with the digital version or obtain the digital, often free, versions.

Note: BASIC SorC Cards will come in hard paper forms with basic sets, modules and their expansions. If playing at home, the GM will grant paper versions of the cards when they are discovered or earned while playing. When discovered or obtained in the wild, players must roll digital dice to earn the digital version(s) of cards to prove they earned them, free. Hard printed cards are purchased in packs or stand alone. Each hard version has a QR code, and with it players can obtain the digital versions.

Explore Essentia

Begin on Zailister, where arcane sorcery and advanced technology clash. Journey between worlds using rifts, portals, starships, and forbidden hell gates. Each planet's biomes and societies are shaped by their distance from Adoria (the magical sun) and Tawdry Dwarf (the dying star). Those near Adoria thrive on magic; those on the fringes rely on machinery and ingenuity.

Zailister is torn by war. Noble, Feral, and Purge factions unite against the Omnè, invaders from a dying planet. Seek the nineteen crowns forged from falling stars and fulfill the prophecy: only true heirs of each race can unlock their crown's power.

Choose your race, class, and fate. Master distinctive SorC combat with Limb Specific Targeting, build your reputation, earn notoriety, and shape Essentia's history. Will you become a frog skinner, or ascend as a dragon slayer?

The Story

Awaken to Essentia, a universe of thirteen planets orbiting two suns. Begin your legend on Zailister, where war and prophecy collide.

Nineteen radiant figures formed from magical rings crashed into Zailister, forging crowns destined for their true heirs. Denial and chaos followed, as believers and challengers battled over the prophecy.

Only the chosen heirs of the nineteen races can claim their crown, dividing the world between those sworn by blood and those who seek power.

Many crowns have been lost, hidden, or destroyed. Essentia's fate hangs in the balance.
