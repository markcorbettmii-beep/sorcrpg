Explore Essentia

Begin on Zailister, a lush life-sustainable planet, where arcane sorcery and steel meet advanced technology. Journey between 13 planetary worlds using rifts, portals, starships, and forbidden hell gates.

Zailister is torn by war. Noble, Feral, and Purge factions unite against the Omnè, invaders from a desperate planet whose fate relies on the death of Adoria's sister star, Taedry Dwarf.

Choose your race, class and fulfill your destiny of becoming a legend. Build your reputation, earn notoriety, and shape your story throughout Essentia's history.

Campaign Mode-Free

The online lobbies feature of SORC Beyond. Any Player or GM can join or host live sessions with fellow adventurers. GMs manage encounters, distribute rewards, and run the full SORC experience online. Pro members unlock exclusive cosmetics and session recording for their lobbies.

Trials of Combat-Free

Open to all members. Choose your mode - Arena (1v1 to 5v5), Battlegrounds, the Coliseum, War, Pet Party, or Battle at Sea. All members compete in tournaments, appear on the top 100 leaderboard, and have access to custom UI with debuff tracking and DPS meters. No pay to win - Pro has zero combat advantage. Pro: Exclusive maps and game modes, top 200 leaderboard, Practice Grounds (NPCs, fizzled spells, wooden weapons, laser tag - you cannot die in Practice Mode), priority matchmaking with all members (Basic queues with Basic only), audio queue alerts, post-match damage meters, advanced stat tracking, exclusive titles and skins, exclusive tournament prizes and achievement tracking.
