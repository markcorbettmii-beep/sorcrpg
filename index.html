Slayers of Rings § Crowns (SorC)

By Ogre Adventurer (OA)

Embark on an epic sci-fantasy journey through the universe of Essentia. Thirteen planets, two suns, endless adventure.

Join the Adventure

Explore Essentia

Begin on Zailister, the balanced planet where sorcery and technology collide. Traverse to other worlds via rifts, portals, space flight, and even hell gates. Each realm possesses distinct biomes, seasons, and levels of technological development, determined by its position between Adoria and the fading star Tawdry Dwarf. Worlds closer to Adoria depend on its magical rays, shaping societies built on magic and steel. In the outer realms, where Adoria’s influence fades and Tawdry Dwarf’s energies wane, technology becomes essential for survival.

Zail is war-torn, with Noble, Feral, and Purge forces uniting against the invading Omnè seeking dominion in fear their technological efforts of saving their planet, Omné, will fail. Seek the nineteen mystical crowns forged from falling stars, and uncover the prophecy that only true heirs can bear these crown's powers.

Choose your race and class, master combat with Limb Specific Targeting, and define your legacy through reputation, affiliations, and heroic deeds. Will you be a frog skinner or a dragon slayer?

The Story

Awaken to Essentia, a vibrant universe of 13 unique planets featuring two suns and many moons. Begin your journey on planet Zailister, the most life-sustaining planet in a harsh, yet diverse, solar system where war, greed and tyranny welcome heroism.

Chaos reigned on Zail as nineteen radiant figures formed by its magical rings shined for the universe to see, crashed into its lands shaping as elegant crowns as each burned through the atmosphere, revealing the true heirs of as many crowns to fulfill an ancient prophecy. Yet denialists bearing witness of the event still oppose believers.

The prophecy claims only the true heirs of the nineteen respective races can don their respective crown, causing chaos between those sworn in by their own accord and inheritance and those in search of the prophecies' rightful leaders.

One by one the crowns were found after impact, but opposition and confusion have sent some lost and others destroyed for eternity.
