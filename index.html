Explore Essentia

Begin on Zailister, a lush life-sustainable planet, where arcane sorcery and steel meet advanced technology. Journey between 13 planetary worlds using rifts, portals, starships, and forbidden hell gates.

Zailister is torn by war. Noble, Feral, and Purge factions unite against the Omnè, invaders from a desperate planet whose fate relies on the death of Adoria's sister star, Taedry Dwarf.

Choose your race, class and fulfill your destiny of becoming a legend. Build your reputation, earn notoriety, and shape your story throughout Essentia's history.

SORC Beyond

The free digital companion platform for SORC. Earn Community Points, join Online Lobbies, and manage your character sheets. Upgrade to Pro with a box set to unlock the Trading Post, Character's Home, Private Campaign Rooms, and more.

SORC Cards

A physical card system for tracking abilities, gear, fellowships, and encounters. Box sets include punch-out Common and Uncommon Cards. Lore Cards carry QR codes for digital access — trade on the server and ownership transfers instantly.

Online Game Modes

Level Up Your Game — jump in free or go Pro with a box set.

⚔

Trials of Combat — Free

Open to all members. No box set required. No paid advantage — only in-game rewards. Test your skills in the Arena (1v1 to 5v5), Proving Grounds battlefields, and the Coliseum. Join the War between the Purge and Noble forces — or stand neutral with the Feral Races. Take the battle to sea in epic naval clashes across oceans and waterways.

Enter the Trials →

Crownmaster Tier — Pro

Level Up Your Game with the full community toolkit. Share compendium content with your party, run homebrew SORC campaigns, and manage advanced sessions with the SORC Ambiance App. Box set required.

Legend Tier — Pro
