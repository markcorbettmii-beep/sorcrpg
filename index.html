Embark on an epic sci-fantasy journey through the universe of Essentia. Thirteen planets, two suns, endless adventure await.

Join the Adventure

Explore Essentia

Start your SorC saga on Zailister, where sorcery and technology collide and ancient prophecies shape the fate of worlds. Journey across the universe using rifts, portals, starships, and forbidden hell gates. Each planet has unique biomes, seasons, and technological levels, determined by their place between the magical sun Adoria and the dying star Tawdry Dwarf. Those closer to Adoria wield powerful magic, while those in the outer reaches must survive with their wits and machines.

Zailister is a planet at war. Noble, Feral, and Purge factions battle the invading Omnè, who seek to save their own doomed world by conquering others. Seek out the nineteen mystical crowns born from falling stars and fulfill the prophecy—only the true heirs of each race can unlock the crowns' legendary power.

Choose your origin, class, and playstyle. Master SorC's unique combat system with Limb Specific Targeting, build your reputation, join affiliations, and carve your name into Essentia’s ever-changing history. Will you be a frog skinner or rise to become a dragon slayer?

The Story

Awaken to Essentia—a universe of 13 planets, two suns, and many moons. Begin on Zailister, the most life-rich planet, where conflict, greed, and tyranny set the stage for heroes to rise.

Chaos engulfed Zail as nineteen radiant figures formed from the planet's magical rings crashed to the surface, shaping themselves into elegant crowns. As they burned through the atmosphere, their arrival revealed the true heirs destined to fulfill an ancient prophecy. Yet, not all believe, and many still oppose the chosen.

The prophecy says only the true heirs of the nineteen races may don their crown, sparking a battle between those born to rule and those who seek power for themselves.

The crowns were found, but opposition and confusion have scattered some, while others have been lost forever.
