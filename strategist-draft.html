Strategist (Playable in Starter Set)

Calculating predators who win before the first blow lands, Strategists use intelligence, positioning, and layered deception to dismantle opponents from angles they never considered, equally dangerous in the shadows, in plain sight, or threading a spelled blade through a gap in armor that nobody else saw was there.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Tactical Precision, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Killing Edge, Once Per Day the Strategist strikes from an unseen angle, dealing damage equal to twice their Deceit SCR to one target, with the attack always counting as a Critical Hit.

Extra 1d6 + LVL shadow damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Strategists can Flourish their blade that lowers their target's Perception at a number equal to their Deceit SCR.

Strategists get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Strategists get +4 plus their DECEIT SCR on Stealth checks.

Strategist Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Rogue - A stealth-focused Strategist who strikes from the shadows before the fight ever truly starts.
  a. Assassin - A Rogue who ends fights before they begin, striking once from perfect position.
  b. Recon - A Rogue focused on scouting and information, staying unseen until the moment it matters.
  c. Shroud - A Rogue who fights from concealment, wrapped in shadow and silence.
ii. Shadow Stalker - A Strategist who fights from between shadows themselves, unnerving enemies as much as outmaneuvering them.
  a. Abyssal - A Shadow Stalker who draws on something darker than shadow alone.
  b. Specter - A Shadow Stalker who lingers just out of sight, striking before ever being seen.
  c. Wraith - A Shadow Stalker who moves like smoke, barely touching the world they haunt.
iii. Spellblade - A blade-and-spell hybrid Strategist who weaves magic directly into every strike.
  a. Phantom Blade - A Spellblade whose weapon strikes as much through illusion as through steel.
  b. Shadowblade - A Spellblade who weaves shadow magic directly into every strike.
  c. Sprightly - A Spellblade who fights with quick, light footwork, always a step ahead.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Light (Leather, Studded Leather).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Strategist's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
