Tomes of Essentia

Ten legendary figures whose deeds shaped Essentia. Each is LVL 30, built to the rules as written. Their Traits are TST scores against the 199 total and individual cap of 100. PROTS, Initiative, Hit and damage resolve on the formulas in the Basic Rules. What is recorded here is what the archives know. What they would tell you is quite another matter.

The Foremost: Marcellus Ferox

⚔ Featured Legend ⚔
[Character Portrait Placeholder]
Marcellus Ferox
Human Fighter   ·   LVL 30   ·   Divine   ·   Sir Knight of the Realm
Marcellus Ferox began life in shadow and despair. Born into neglect and suffering in a thuggish village, he learned early that only strength and resilience could offer escape. Every hardship became discipline; every scar became purpose. He rose from street life to join the Noble Forces as a Mariner, then fought through hell itself. He was captured by hell orcs, enslaved by the Kestrull dominion, cast into the void of space, and rescued by the mystic Yawnies. Now, knighted by King Percius himself and sworn to recover the lost crown, Marcellus walks a path etched in blood and sacrifice. His oath: to take revenge on Kestrull and every hell orc that crosses his path, and to one day rule an enclave of his own.

The Path Through Fire: Marcellus was captured during a skirmish with hell orcs and thrust into an infernal realm where he fought through legions of demonic foes, evading Beelzebub's grasp through encounters real and illusory. A Omnè-crafted portal trap drew him into the cold void of space, where he was discovered and sold into slavery to the Dominion of Kestrull. There, as a gladiator in their coliseum, he faced raging bears, cunning minotaurs, and groups of armed opponents—and never lost a single match. His mythic reputation earned him a cruel semblance of freedom, spurring his escape. He killed several Kestrull guards and collapsed in the Bottomless Swamp, where the Yawnies found him and restored him to life.

🔱 Readied Weapon

Vow of Vengeance
A greatsword forged in the flames of Kestrull's own forges from metal taken from a guard's blade. The steel is dark as charcoal and etched with symbols in the Yawnie tongue—ancient glyphs meaning "Return, Remember, Reclaim." With every swing, Marcellus feels the weight of every slave he fought beside. Divine rank, 2-handed, 30d6 + 62 Damage.

✦ Divine Armaments

Plate of Resurrection (Divine)
Dark iron plate inscribed with prayers in three languages—human, Yawnie, and the forgotten tongue of the infernal realm. It remembers every death Marcellus should have suffered. When he falls below 15 Life, the plate glows and grants +4 to all Defensive rolls until his next turn.
Talisman of the Yawnies (Legendary)
A charm carved from bone and woven with hair from three shamans. Grants healing of 1d8 + Wisdom once per day and allows Marcellus to sense nearby Yawnies or those with Yawnie blood.

🏰 Estate & Sanctuaries

The Reclaimed Hold, Border Marches between Noble and Kestrull Territory
The Gladiator's Ring: A training circle carved from stone where Marcellus practices daily. Every scar from his time in Kestrull's coliseum is recreated on training dummies so he might master every opponent he ever faced.
The Vault of Names: A chamber holding records of every slave Marcellus fought beside. Each name is carved into stone. He has sworn an oath to memorialize them all before his death.
Yawnie Sanctuary: A portion of the Hold reserved entirely for his Yawnie companions. Built in their style with running water channels and moss gardens. A sacred space where shamanic rituals are performed.
The Armory of Conquest: Weapons collected from every arena opponent he defeated, organized by region. Hundreds of blades, each a memory of a kill or a test survived.
War Room: Maps marking every known Kestrull settlement, marked with tactical notes and strategic positions. His plans for eventual conquest are detailed in encrypted journals.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family & Guardians

The Yawnies: Five surviving tribal members who escaped Kestrull's massacre. They are his only true family, bonded by shared survival and shamanic teaching. They teach him magic, healing, and the old ways.
King Percius: His sovereign who knighted him Sir Marcellus after hearing his oath. The King has tasked him with recovering the lost crown—a sacred duty that now drives his every action.
Sir Lucas, the Revered Knight: The paladin who personally knighted Marcellus and serves as his mentor in the ways of chivalry and honor—concepts foreign to his earlier life.
Guardian Shamans: Yawnie elders who continue to guide his development in ancient magic and meditative combat techniques. He is now a shaman-warrior—a bridge between two worlds.

✨ Animated Wayfarer

Liberator - Mechanical War-Beast (Behemoth Class)
Void Chambers: Grants understanding of spatial navigation. Teaches how to survive in airless environments.
Infernal Forge: Reveals techniques for tempering weapons in magical flames. Weapons treated this way gain +1 to Hit rolls.
Crown Pathways: Maps routes to locations where pieces of the lost crown are rumored to exist. Unlocked through recovering specific artifacts.
Yawnie Mysteries: Grants access to shamanic spells and healing rituals unknown to human mages.

🔮 Secret Places & Discoveries

The Void Node
Somewhere in the space between worlds exists a rift where Marcellus was cast. He has spoken to no one of it, but possesses a fragment of something found there—a crystalline object that hums with an energy unknown to Essentia's scholars. It is locked in his most private chamber.
Beelzebub's Mark
During his time in the infernal realm, Marcellus was marked by the Arch devil himself— a symbol burned into his shoulder that only shamans can see. Whether it grants power or curse remains unknown.
The Gladiator's Paradox
While training in the coliseum, Marcellus realized he lost a match against a Minotaur three times in his memory—but no Kestrull record shows any loss. He is beginning to suspect the infernal realm left him trapped between multiple realities, and memories of other timelines bleed through.

The Nine Pillars of Legend

The following nine heroes stand as pillars supporting the realm of Essentia. Each has earned their place through courage, sacrifice, and deeds that will echo through the ages.

Valdris the Shattered
Half-Orcus Barbarian · LVL 30 · Divine
Valdris carries the wounds of the Cataclysm in both his body and spirit. A warrior of legendary rage, he has carved a domain from volcanic wastelands and now tends to it with the same fury he brings to battle. His companions fear his anger; his family loves him anyway. The Shattered Forge bears his name because what stands there was rebuilt from ruins.

🔱 Readied Weapon

Kinburst
A volcanic iron axe forged in his own smithy, its handle bears his family's mark burned into the wood. As Valdris grew in power, Kinburst seemed to grow with him. Its edges remain sharp after countless battles, and the metal glows faintly amber during combat. Divine rank, 2-handed, 28d6 + 56 Damage.

✦ Divine Armament

Plate of the Ironheart (Divine)
Black iron plate etched with ancestral runes. When Valdris takes Vitality damage, the runes flare and reduce the next incoming damage by 1d6.

🏰 The Shattered Forge Estate

Volcanic Wastes, Central Realm
Forge-Hall: The central anvil still radiates heat. Trophy wall displays weapons of fallen rivals, each with a burned nameplate.
Vault: A sealed stone chamber holding obsidian coins and a meteorite containing memories of his ancestral homeland.
Training Grounds: Practice dummies carved from petrified wood. A combat ring marks where ceremonial duels are fought.
Stable: Four warhorse-elk hybrids, reserved for ceremonial processions that shake the earth.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family & Guardians

Gressa (Wife): An orcus warrior managing forge operations and teaching children combat.
Three Children: All showing unexpected bardic talent. The youngest charms the furnaces with song.
Mother-in-Law: Tends the smokehouse; her herbal remedies are sought across the realm.
Throk (Guardian): A stone elemental bound to the property. Speaks only in resonant rumbles.

✨ Animated Wayfarer

Pyroclast - Mechanical Fire-Drake
Ancient Forge Site: Teaches weapon enhancement and tempering.
Lava River Secret: Grants Heat Resistance. River hot enough to forge steel in its currents.
Ancestor's Blessing: Reveals family tree carved into stone—three hundred years of lineage.

🔮 Secret Place & Discovery

The Obsidian Cavern
Beneath the Vault lies a hidden chamber. Inside, a mirror pool reflects starlight despite being underground. Bathing in its waters shows visions of his homeland before the Cataclysm—vast forests, cities of crystal, peoples long vanished.
Kinburst's Soul Bond
Valdris discovered that Kinburst responds to his raw emotion with perfect clarity. The hotter his rage, the brighter the blade glows. The weapon might be bonded to his very soul.
