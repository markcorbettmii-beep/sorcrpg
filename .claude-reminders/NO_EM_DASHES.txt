⚠️ SORC WEBSITE RULE: NO EM DASHES IN PROSE

Rule from CLAUDE.md (line 32):
"No em dashes in prose. `<td>&mdash;</td>` in a table cell is fine."

WHAT THIS MEANS:
- ❌ DO NOT use em dashes (—) in regular paragraph text, descriptions, or any prose
- ✅ DO use em dashes ONLY in HTML table cells: `<td>&mdash;</td>`
- ✅ Use periods, commas, or parentheses instead in prose

EXAMPLES:
❌ Wrong: "Aelthora embodies divine judgment — tempered with mercy"
✅ Right: "Aelthora embodies divine judgment, tempered with mercy"

This rule applies to ALL files on sorcrpg.com
