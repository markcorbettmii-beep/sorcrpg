Digital

📄 Downloadable PDF versions of SORC books are lighter editions - full rules and content, without images, to keep file sizes small and load fast on any device. PDF downloads for books other than the Basic Rulebook require proof of physical book ownership.

Basic PDF versions of our games only use common and uncommon basic SORC Card cut-outs that are printable. There is no matted, intricately designed card stock with the light PDF version.

Trading Post

🔄 Trade SORC Cards with other members. When Cards are traded, digital access transfers to the new owner and the original QR code is permanently invalidated. Players keep the physical versions of their Cards and can continue using them in home campaigns - but the QR code, which refreshes periodically for digital access, is tied to ownership and transfers with the trade. Trades are peer-to-peer and final.
