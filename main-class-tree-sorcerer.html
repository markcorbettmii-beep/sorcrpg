Sorcerer (Playable in Starter Set)

Born rather than trained, Sorcerers carry raw arcane power in their blood and channel it through stave and cloth into spells of extraordinary breadth, their magic growing more unstable and powerful the deeper they push their natural gift, making them capable of the most dramatic single effects in Essentia at the price of anything resembling physical resilience.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Arcane Ascendance, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Bloodborn Surge, Once Per Day the Sorcerer unleashes raw innate magic, dealing arcane damage equal to twice their Focus SCR to one target, with the Sorcerer taking damage equal to half that amount.

Extra 1d6 + LVL arcane damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Sorcerers can Flourish their stave that lowers their target's Defense at a number equal to their Focus SCR.

Sorcerers get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Sorcerers get +4 plus their FOCUS SCR on Knowledge checks involving arcane lore.

Sorcerer Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Conjurer - A summoning-focused Sorcerer who calls forth allies and constructs from beyond to fight at their side.
  a. Magus - A Conjurer who fuses stave and blade into a single hybrid weapon.
  b. Planes Walker - A Conjurer who tears momentary rifts to nearby planes, stepping through and back at will.
  c. Psionic - A Conjurer who channels raw mind-power instead of any physical focus.
ii. Seer - A far-seeing Sorcerer who reads fate itself, striking with knowledge as much as raw power.
  a. Clairvoyant - A Seer who reads the immediate future, striking where the enemy hasn't moved yet.
  b. Mystic - A Seer who channels raw, intuitive insight into their spellcasting.
  c. Prophet - A Seer whose visions reach far beyond the battlefield, foretelling what's still to come.
iii. Wizard - An elemental Sorcerer who wields fire and force with the discipline of formal study.
  a. Battlecryer - A Wizard who channels magic through voice and command as much as spellcraft.
  b. Fire Breather - A Wizard devoted to raw, elemental fire above all other schools.
  c. Transmuter - A Wizard who reshapes matter itself, turning the battlefield's own materials into a weapon.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Delicate (Cloth Robe and Cloak, all paths).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Sorcerer's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
