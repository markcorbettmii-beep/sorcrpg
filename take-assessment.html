Player & GM Roles

Who Can Join: Any assessed Player can join open Lobbies. Civilians must complete the Role Assessment first - there's no getting into a Lobby without it.

Who Can Create: Creating a Lobby requires a valid SORC box set code. Admins and Owners can create without a code. An Assessed GM must be present in Lobby to launch a room.

Lobbies vs. Rooms: Lobbies are recruiting hubs - text chat and party assembly only. Private Campaign Rooms are launched from a Lobby by the GM, and include voice, dice, and full session tools.

Whatever you assess into, you're already a Basic Member - Civilians get that status too, free digital rules, and downloadable content. Becoming a Leo Member requires a Box Set (in development) Code.

Members that have already discovered Essentia may reassess as many times as they'd like, regardless of their results. Just click the Assessment Choice above.
