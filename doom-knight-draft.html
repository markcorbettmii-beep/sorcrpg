Doom Knight (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Corrupted warriors who channel death, decay, and necrotic energy into devastating martial combat, Doom Knights serve dark powers with equal parts blade and curse, treating entropy itself as a weapon and every fallen enemy as fuel for their own continued existence, in service of forces mortals were not meant to understand, some out of genuine malice, others out of cold necessity.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Grave Pact, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Deathless March, Once Per Day the Doom Knight refuses to fall, surviving at 1 Life when reduced to 0 and draining Life from the nearest enemy equal to their Resistance SCR.

Extra 1d6 + LVL necrotic damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Doom Knights can Flourish a 2h weapon that lowers their target's Resistance at a number equal to their Toughness SCR.

Doom Knights get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Doom Knights get +4 plus their RES SCR on Intimidation checks.

Doom Knight Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Death Bringer - The Executioner among Doom Knights, binding a target in place before delivering one final, decisive blow rather than a drawn-out fight.
  a. Death Knight - A Death Bringer devoted purely to the blade, delivering death in a single stroke.
  b. Death Lord - A Death Bringer who commands death itself, striking with a sovereign's cold authority.
  c. Decaymancer - A Death Bringer who hastens decay itself, rotting armor, flesh, and will alike.
ii. Death Guard - A frontline Doom Knight who anchors the battle line, drawing on death's own weight to protect allies.
  a. Dread Knight - A Death Guard whose mere presence instills fear that breaks enemy resolve.
  b. Scourge - A Death Guard who spreads rot and plague to weaken every enemy nearby.
  c. Umbral Lord - A Death Guard cloaked in shadow, commanding darkness to shield and blind.
iii. Lordsman - A commanding Doom Knight who leads from the front, tempering death's power with a lord's discipline.
  a. Paragon Knight - A Lordsman held up as the ideal of disciplined, honorable death-craft.
  b. Radiant Knight - A Lordsman who tempers death's power with a flicker of stolen light.
  c. Sentinel - A Lordsman who holds the line, standing watch so allies never fall unguarded.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Heavy or Medium (Plate, Full Chain).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Doom Knight's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
