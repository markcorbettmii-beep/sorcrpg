SORC Beyond

The digital companion suite for Slayers of Rings & Crowns. Everything here is earned — not purchased. Build your reputation in Essentia and the platform grows with you.

SORC Beyond is entirely community-driven. There are no subscription fees and no paywalls. Every feature — from Basic to Pro — is unlocked by earning Community Points through your activity in Essentia. The more you play, contribute, and participate, the more the platform opens up to you.

Point integrity rules: Points are only awarded for posts and threads that remain up and undeleted for at least 24 hours. Points are revoked if you delete your own content after earning them. Points are not revoked if content is removed by an Admin or Owner. Posts flagged and removed by an Admin result in a −10 pt deduction. Posts that receive 3 or more reports are hidden pending Admin review — points are frozen until a decision is made. Uploaded content must be Admin-approved before points are awarded. Spam, duplicate posts, and bad-faith activity earn no points.

Game Masters and Admins can message any member directly without a request. This is by design — GMs need to be able to reach players quickly for session coordination, and Admins need direct access for moderation and community management.
The Black Market — A live player-run lobby where exchanges happen in real time without the Auction House interface. Access requires earning the Trader achievement, which is unlocked by reaching a threshold of combined Community Point transactions and SORC Store purchases. All Black Market exchanges are server-recorded. No real money may be transferred between players on SORC servers — only in-game currency earned through recorded Online Lobby sessions is valid for player-to-player trade.
The SORC Store

Rare Cards & Module Box Sets

Physical and digital premium content available for purchase. Every purchase earns Community Points and contributes to your progression in Essentia. Content gathered from purchased sets can be uploaded to earn additional points.

Free PDF Modules

Lighter digital versions of official modules are available to all accounts at no cost. A great way to get started and earn your first Community Points through downloads and community uploads.

Community Points in the Store

Community Points can be spent on select basic items and raffle tickets in the SORC Store. It typically takes a significant number of points to make a purchase this way — but it is entirely possible for a dedicated member of the community to access store items without spending real money.

Online Lobbies & Recorded Sessions

How In-Game Currency Works

In-game currency is earned exclusively during SORC Beyond Online Lobby sessions where all activity is server-recorded. It cannot be earned outside of live recorded sessions. Currency is used at the Auction House and the Black Market — and no real money transfers between players are permitted on SORC servers under any circumstances.

Items Eligible for Trade

Only items obtained during server-recorded SORC Beyond sessions are eligible for auction or trade. This ensures the integrity of every transaction on the platform and keeps the economy fair for all players.
