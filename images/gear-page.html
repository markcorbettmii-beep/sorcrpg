Armaments & Accessories
Slayers of Rings & Crowns, SorC

Armaments

  • Dragonspine Longsword (Martial Melee)
    Forged from the bones of ancient wyrms, this blade deals extra damage to draconic creatures. Its balance and edge rival that of any dwarven smith.
  • Crownbreaker Mace (Bludgeoning)
    Designed to shatter royal defenses, this heavy mace grants advantage against armored foes and magical shields.
  • Ringshadow Bow (Ranged)
    A longbow crafted from shadowed wood, firing arrows that whisper as they fly. Favored by assassins and scouts.
  • Sorcerous Staff of the Eclipse (Arcane Focus)
    Channel your spells with amplified power. Once per day, unleash a pulse to dispel magical darkness or blind foes.
  • Vigilant Shield (Defensive)
    Etched with runes, this shield can absorb one magical attack per day and alert its bearer to incoming danger.

Accessories

  • Ring of Silent Steps
    Wearers move unseen and unheard; perfect for thieves and infiltrators.
  • Crown of Insight
    Grants the wearer keen perception and resistance to mind-affecting spells.
  • Amulet of the Slayer
    Increases critical hit chance and grants the bearer courage in the face of overwhelming odds.
  • Belt of Unyielding Strength
    Boosts physical might and allows for feats of heroism.
  • Cloak of Crow's Flight
    Enables short bursts of flight or swift gliding escapes from peril.
