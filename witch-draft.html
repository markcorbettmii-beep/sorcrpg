Witch (Playable in Starter Set)

Ancient practitioners of earth, moon, and brew magic who draw power from the natural world's oldest cycles, the pull of the moon, the chill of the north wind, and the bubbling of a well-crafted cauldron, Witches apply curses, potions, and elemental forces with an unpredictable mastery that feels less like a learned skill and more like a conversation with something that has always been listening.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Hexweave, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Coven's Curse, Once Per Day the Witch lays a hex upon one target, dealing nature damage equal to twice their Willpower SCR and reducing their Resistance until the end of the encounter.

Extra 1d6 + LVL nature damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Witches can Flourish their focus that lowers their target's Resistance at a number equal to their Intuition SCR.

Witches get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Witches get +4 plus their KNOWLEDGE SCR on Knowledge checks involving curse and brew lore.

Witch Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. BrewMaster - A potion-focused Witch who brews curses and cures alike from whatever's close at hand.
  a. Green - A BrewMaster who works entirely from herb, root, and growing things.
  b. Haggard - A BrewMaster whose rough, battle-worn brews hit harder than they taste.
  c. Shrew - A BrewMaster whose small, sharp-tongued potions pack an outsized punch.
ii. Lunar - A moon-bound Witch whose power waxes and wanes with the night sky.
  a. Moonfire - A Lunar Witch who channels the moon's pale light into searing, silver fire.
  b. Starborn - A Lunar Witch whose power was never fully of this world to begin with.
  c. Zodiac - A Lunar Witch who draws power from the full wheel of the stars, not the moon alone.
iii. Norwitch - A frost-touched Witch who reads fate in wyrd-signs and channels the cold itself.
  a. Arctic Witch - A Norwitch who channels the deep cold itself into every curse and blessing.
  b. Spiritbound - A Norwitch bound to a guiding spirit that lends its own power in battle.
  c. Wyrdreader - A Norwitch who reads fate itself in wyrd-signs and old, frozen omens.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Half Heavy through Delicate (Half Leather, Bat Wing, Wool, Spider Silk).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Witch's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
