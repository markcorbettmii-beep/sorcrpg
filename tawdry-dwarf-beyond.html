Tawdry Dwarf & Beyond

The Final Four Books

Tawdry Dwarf & Beyond covers Planets 10-13 (Corpus Caeleste, Undeximus, Omnè Malum, Tredeki), chronicling the effects of Tawdry Dwarf's decline and the technological mastery of civilizations fighting for survival as their sun dies.

Overview of the Omnè

The Omnè are a native sub-human species hailing from the twelfth planet, Omnè Malum, Omnè in short, which orbits the dying star Tawdry Dwarf. Their unique characteristic is a subtle, glowing hue that matches their skin tone, setting them apart from other species. This trait is a result of their technological adaptation to the planet's peculiar environment. They come in a variety of skin, eye, and hair colors and types, reflecting their diverse origins.

The Omnè have established a society based on strength, manipulation, and fear, bolstered by their advanced technology and its ruthless application. Their path to dominance was marked by a fateful decision to align themselves with malevolent forces, leading them down a corrupting trajectory. This has made them feared across the galaxy as they continue to expand their influence and suppress resistance, particularly from the altruistic Futurem.

Omnè warriors are instrumental in this expansionist effort, deploying advanced technology to achieve their objectives. Their arsenal includes formidable spacecraft and biological cavalry units, designed for swift and decisive strikes against their enemies. The Omnè have prepared for the impending demise of Tawdry Dwarf by developing the mith3r ships and halos, technological marvels designed to protect their planet from the cataclysmic destruction that will soon engulf it.

As Tawdry Dwarf draws to its final moments, the Omnè find themselves at war with the neighboring Futurem, who occupy the eleventh planet. The Futurem have allied themselves with the Tredeki of the thirteenth planet, and hold a trading stance with the tenth planet and every world before it that will have them, Ignis excepted, whose people do not trust technology and will not deal with them. The Omnè seek to claim Varanis for themselves but are thwarted by the Futurem's resistance.

In an effort to mitigate the catastrophic effects of Tawdry Dwarf's impending demise, the last three planets (the eleventh, twelfth, and thirteenth) are working on a technology that aims to draw Tawdry Dwarf into Adoria, their primary star. This process would potentially dampen the damage to the rest of the universe by consuming the energy released by Tawdry Dwarf's collapse. The Omnè's fate is thus intertwined with this technological endeavor and their ongoing struggle for dominance in the galaxy.

Ranks of the Omnè

Overlord

Description: The supreme leader of the Omnè, commanding respect and fear. The Overlord embodies the essence of darkness, often employing vast armies to achieve their goals.

Armor: Wears an ornate, black obsidian armor embedded with gemstones that pulse with dark energy. A long, tattered cloak flows behind them, and an intricate mask hides their true visage, instilling dread in all who gaze upon them.

Powers: Masters of psychological warfare and manipulation, they can bend subordinates to their will through fear alone and command the Omnè's most devastating weaponry.

Dark Warlord

Description: A high-ranking general serving directly under the Overlord. They command massive legions of loyal Omnè troops and oversee critical operations against the Futurem.

Armor: Their armor is sleek and angular, composed of dark steel with crimson accents. It is designed for both durability and agility, featuring a helmet that enhances perception and battle instincts.

Powers: Skilled combatants, they wield powerful war blades and can channel dark energy to enhance their physical abilities.

Shadow Knight

Description: Elite warriors trained in the art of stealth and assassination. They are the personal enforcers of the Dark Warlords and are often sent on covert missions.

Armor: Lightweight and agile, their armor consists of a dark, matte finish with adaptive camouflage technology, allowing them to blend seamlessly into their environment.

Powers: Experts in using daggers and stealth tactics, they can also manipulate shadows to confuse and mislead enemies.

Brute Enforcer

Description: Heavy infantry designed for frontline combat. These imposing figures strike fear into the hearts of their opponents and are invaluable in siege warfare.

Armor: Bulky, reinforced armor that showcases spikes and aggressive designs. The chest piece displays the Omnè insignia, and they are often equipped with heavy weapons like energy blasters and grenades.

Powers: Towering strength and resilience, capable of delivering devastating blows and withstanding considerable damage.

Dread Acolyte

Description: Practitioners of forbidden bio-technology and neural conditioning, these specialists wield significant power. They serve the Overlord and the Dark Warlords, deploying experimental tech to sow chaos.

Armor: Robes adorned with sigils of power and dark metallic accents. Often they wear hoods that obscure their faces, enhancing their mysterious and formidable presence.

Powers: Their abilities include neural disruption devices, engineered afflictions, and deploying bioengineered creatures grown from shadow and energy.

Corrupted Sentinel

Description: Lesser warriors who serve the Omnè, often recruited from conquered species or fallen heroes. These soldiers are imbued with dark energy, twisting their forms and minds.

Armor: Standard military-grade, consisting of black armor mixed with organic elements, showing signs of their corrupted nature. The designs often reflect a horrific transformation.

Powers: Enhanced strength and cybernetic augmentation, making them dangerous foes despite their lower rank.

Nightmarish Sentry

Description: Basic foot soldiers in service to the Omnè. They often outnumber their foes through sheer brutality and overwhelming force.

Armor: Basic but menacing, composed of dark metals that absorb light, often painted with frightening symbols. Their helmets have menacing visors that conceal their identity.

Powers: Trained in melee weapons, they are relentless attackers, bolstered by dark chants that empower them for battle.

Harbinger Drone

Description: Mechanized units controlled by the Omnè, serving as reconnaissance and support in battle. These drones can be equipped with various weaponry.

Armor: Sleek, futuristic design with a matte black finish, allowing for stealthy operations. They have retractable shields for defense.

Powers: Capable of hacking enemy systems and deploying energy bursts that disrupt targets.

Omnè Cavalry Ranks

Dread Chariots

Description: Heavily armored vehicles that can tear through enemy lines. The chassis resembles a dark, menacing beast, often fortified with spikes and flamethrowers.

Usage: Typically employed in shock assaults, these chariots are manned by Nightmarish Sentries and Brute Enforcers who unleash havoc upon their foes.

Capabilities: Capable of high-speed charges and can carry additional infantry or supplies. Equipped with heavy cannons and shields for protection.

Shadow Raptor Riders

Description: Cavalry units that ride on massive birds of prey, known as Shadow Raptors. These creatures are agile and fierce, with talon-like appendages for both combat and flight.

Usage: Shadow Raptor Riders are used for aerial reconnaissance and hit-and-run tactics, engaging enemies from above.

Capabilities: Equipped with crossbows or boosting energy lances to attack from the air, they can dive swiftly for surprise offensives.

Void Steeds

Description: Dystopian ethereal creatures resembling warped horses, shrouded in dark energy that can traverse both land and air.

Usage: Typically employed for rapid troop movement, reconnaissance, and flanking maneuvers during ground assaults.

Capabilities: Capable of short-distance flying and carrying armored soldiers efficiently, they are equipped for both combat and evasion.

Wraith Banshees

Description: Biomechanical hovercraft designed to resemble giant predatory creatures. They utilize advanced technology to maintain speed and agility.

Usage: They are used for quick strikes and flanking maneuvers, often paired with ground troops for synchronized assaults.

Capabilities: Equipped with plasma cannons and stealth technology, they can evade detection and strike unsuspecting opponents.

Cavalry Phantoms

Description: Armored drones that serve as light cavalry mounts. They hover and glide swiftly across the terrain, providing reconnaissance and support.

Usage: These can swiftly traverse the battlefield, delivering communication and intelligence or launching light strikes against enemy lines.

Capabilities: Equipped with energy disruptors and sensor suites, they serve to confuse and gather intel from enemy formations.

Omnè Spacecraft and Fighter Ranks

Void Starfighter

Description: The primary fighter craft of the Omnè, designed for speed and maneuverability. Its silhouette resembles a bird of prey ready to dive into attack.

Usage: Used in dogfights and defensive operations. Piloted by Corrupted Sentinels, these fighters swarm and outmaneuver enemies effortlessly.

Capabilities: Equipped with twin plasma cannons and a cloaking device, they can strike quickly and retreat before enemy fire can reach them.

Wraith Bombardier

Description: A heavier variant outfitted for bombing runs. Resembling a monstrous flying creature, it is built to endure heavy fire.

Usage: Utilized for strategic bombing and ground support, often deployed when larger forces face organized resistance.

Capabilities: Equipped with gravity bombs and turrets on all sides, it has enhanced hull integrity for durability during sustained operations.

Harbinger Cruiser

Description: A large capital ship that leads fleets into battle. Its imposing presence on the battlefield strikes fear into the hearts of enemies.

Usage: Acts as a command ship from which the Dark Warlords can issue orders and coordinate attacks.

Capabilities: Built with heavy armor and extensive systems of weaponry, including energy beams and missile launchers for anti-ship operations.

Nether Blades

Description: Swarm drones designed for aerial skirmishing and reconnaissance, these small craft are sleek and designed to operate in groups.

Usage: Utilized primarily for diversionary tactics or overwhelming enemy defenses with sheer numbers of fast-moving attacks.

Capabilities: Each drone is equipped with light energy weapons and enhanced agility, making them difficult to target.

Ravager Transports

Description: Robust transport ships designed to carry troops and supplies into battle. They feature heavily armored hulls to withstand enemy fire.

Usage: Essential for deploying ground forces to the battlefield, the Ravager can also evacuate troops from danger.

Capabilities: Can drop off troops directly into battle zones and provide light fire support with onboard plasma turrets.

Additional Omnè Weapons and Technology

Quasar Pistol

Description: A sleek, portable sidearm that harnesses focused gravitational waves to incapacitate targets.

Unique Attack Result: Pulse of Attraction. On a successful hit, create a gravitational pull effect that drags the target towards the shooter, potentially exposing them to follow-up attacks or environmental hazards. Mechanics involve rolling to determine the distance pulled.

Singularity Sidearm

Description: A compact firearm that fires projectiles engineered to create miniaturized black holes upon impact.

Unique Attack Result: Singularity Burst. Upon hitting a target, the bullet creates a microsingularity that momentarily distorts space, causing a small explosion that deals area damage to nearby enemies within a specified radius.

Gravity Well Pistol

Description: A futuristic handgun that generates localized gravity wells when fired.

Unique Attack Result: Gravitational Pull. On a critical hit, create a localized gravity well that immobilizes the target for one turn, leaving them vulnerable to follow-up shots.

Kinetic Imploder

Description: A pistol that fires kinetic rounds capable of compressing matter upon impact.

Unique Attack Result: Kinetic Compression. After dealing damage, the target may be forced to make a saving throw. On a failure, their armor is temporarily weakened or their defensive capabilities are reduced.

Phase Disruptor

Description: An advanced sidearm that uses phased energy projectiles to disrupt the molecular structure of the target.

Unique Attack Result: Molecular Instability. Target takes damage and must roll for a chance to be additionally disoriented. If disoriented, the target's next attack roll or action is made at disadvantage.

Horizon Breaker

Description: A high-tech pistol utilizing energy waves to create a concussive force upon impact.

Unique Attack Result: Concussive Force. On a hit, not only does the target take damage, but they are knocked back several feet, potentially knocking them into other enemies or environmental hazards.

Overview of the Futurem

The Futurem are a resilient human race that inhabits the eleventh planet, although part Human they are a race on their own, they share human blood, known for its lush landscapes and a technocratic society built upon cooperation and altruism. Unlike the Omnè, who are marked by their dark adaptations, the Futurem do not bear any unique physical traits; they are diverse in appearance but unified by their principles of hope and justice. Their society is characterized by advanced technology, emphasizing defense and peacekeeping technologies over heavy offensive capabilities.

Aligned predominantly with the ideals of lawful good, the Futurem actively combat the oppressive influence of the Omnè, aiming to protect their own territory and help those in need across the galaxy. They engage in warfare not out of desire for conquest but to ensure the safety and prosperity of their allies and planets.

The Futurem stand allied with the Tredeki of the thirteenth planet, and trade with the tenth planet and every world before it that will have them, forming a strategic alliance to counter the imminent threats posed by the Omnè. Their commitment to communal welfare drives them to develop innovative technologies and strategies to resist the aggressive expansion of the Omnè while maintaining their core values.

Ranks of the Futurem

Grand Master

Description: The supreme leader of the Futurem and the ultimate guardian of their ideals. The Grand Master is responsible for guiding the overall strategy and moral direction of the Futurem forces.

Armor: Wears ornate armor resembling a blend of ceremonial garb and functional battle gear, often adorned with symbols of peace and unity. A flowing cape represents honor and tradition.

Powers: Highly skilled in both combat and diplomacy, the Grand Master can channel energy for defensive measures and inspire allies with powerful oratory.

Battle Commander

Description: High-ranking officers who lead tactical offensives against the Omnè. They are skilled strategists and charismatic leaders, ensuring the success of their missions.

Armor: Adaptive combat suits designed for mobility and protection, featuring embedded technologies for communication and battlefield awareness.

Powers: Proficient in both close and ranged combat; they can call upon energy shields to protect allies during skirmishes.

Lightbearer

Description: Elite warriors trained in the ways of combat and the use of advanced technology. They act as the Futurem's special forces, engaging in stealth and precision strikes.

Armor: Lightweight, high-tech suits that reduce noise and enhance agility, often equipped with cloaking capabilities for covert operations.

Powers: Skilled in energy blades and ranged weaponry; they harness light-based techniques to dazzle foes and gain tactical advantages.

Guardian Sentinel

Description: Heavily armored defenders who hold the line against the Omnè. They form the backbone of Futurem forces, focusing on defense and crowd control.

Armor: Heavy, defensive exosuits capable of absorbing significant damage, with integrated plasma projectors used for area suppression.

Powers: Masters of defensive tactics, they can create energy barriers and fortifications to protect their allies and vital locations.

Tactician

Description: Officers skilled in strategy and battlefield management. They analyze enemy movements and direct troops efficiently, ensuring coordinated responses to threats.

Armor: Standardized uniforms with command interfaces, allowing for rapid communication and tactical planning on the field.

Powers: Excellent problem solvers; they possess abilities to foresee enemy tactics and provide buffs to allies.

Apprentice of the Light

Description: Novice members of the Futurem forces, undergoing training to become full-fledged soldiers. They learn the values of justice and unity while training in both combat and humanitarian missions.

Armor: Basic combat gear that reinforces their adaptability while reflecting their training status. These suits can be enhanced as they progress.

Powers: Basic combat skills, focusing on support roles, using medical supplies and energy pulses to aid injured comrades.

Peacekeeper

Description: Basic Infantry tasked with frontline engagements against the Omnè forces. They leverage numbers and teamwork to achieve victory.

Armor: Standard combat garb with protective enhancements to withstand minor damage.

Powers: Trained in melee and ranged combat, they rally together to perform coordinated strikes and overwhelming assaults.

Aegis Drone

Description: Automated units built for reconnaissance and support, providing critical data to the Futurem commanders during battle.

Armor: Sleek, lightweight design with cloaking features. They can also deploy shield barriers for temporary protection.

Powers: Capable of scanning enemy movements and providing strategic updates while offering light fire support via energy weaponry.

Futurem Spacecraft and Technology

The Aegis Sentinel

Description: A massive defensive starship outfitted with advanced energy shields and heavy armament. It serves as a mobile command center and refuge for the Futurem forces.

Features: Heavily fortified with layers of energy shields, molecular shielding for deflecting attacks, and repair drones for on-the-fly damage control.

The Nebula Strider

Description: A swift fighter designed for aerial skirmishes and dogfights, ideal for rapid hit-and-run tactics against the Omnè fleet.

Features: Equipped with adaptive thrusters for exceptional maneuverability, ion torpedoes capable of disabling enemy ships, and an advanced targeting system.

The Sentinel's Edge

Description: A light cruiser specialized in defensive formations, offering fire support for ground troops during orbital assaults or evacuations.

Features: Plasma cannons for effective engagement with enemy ships, cloaking devices for stealth operations, and extensive life support systems for long missions.

The Null Drift

Description: A versatile hovercraft designed for rapid response and support missions in both atmospheric and space operations.

Features: Rearmed with light plasma guns for defense, the Null Drift can swiftly accommodate troop transport and emergency evacuations.

The Agile Specter

Description: A nimble cyberbot designed for stealth operations and special reconnaissance missions.

Features: Cloaking field allowing it to become invisible, adaptable weapons for close-quarters combat, and rapid maneuvering capabilities across the terrain.

Summary

The Omnè and the Futurem present two contrasting forces in the galaxy. The Omnè, driven by fear and manipulation, seek to expand their dominion through advanced technology and ruthless conquest. Meanwhile, the Futurem stand as champions of justice and cooperation, committed to defending their territories and helping those in need. These opposing forces create a dynamic backdrop for conflict and intrigue within the galaxy, as their respective technologies, ranks, and philosophies influence the ongoing struggle for power and stability.
