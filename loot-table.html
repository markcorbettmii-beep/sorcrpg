Probability Loot, Crafting & Discovery System

Setting Reward Chances with Dice

Use Xd10 (rolling X d10 dice) to control how rare or common rewards and events are.

  • Rare chances (like 0.005) require more dice, rolled as 2d10 x 2 (see below).
  • Larger chances (like 50%) need fewer dice.

How to Choose X

  • For tiny probabilities (like 0.005), roll 2d10 x 2 and succeed if the sum is 2 or less.
  • For larger probabilities (like 50%), roll 2d10 and succeed if the sum is less than or equal to the success threshold.

How to Use

You can add as many items as you want with any drop rate between 0.05% and 99%.

Asmodeus cannot die.
He never drops certain items (set their chance to 0%) - success range: none.
He always drops certain items (set their chance to 100%) - success range: all.
Or, he flees, and only drops items if the party meets certain conditions - success ranges depend on those conditions.

How Success Ranges Work

  • For tiny chances (like 0.05%), success if sum of 2d10 ≤ threshold (e.g., 2).
  • For common chances (like 50%), success if sum ≤ threshold (e.g., 100).
  • For almost guaranteed (like 99%), success if sum ≤ 7920.

How to Determine Success

  1. Roll 2d10.
  2. If sum ≤ threshold, the item drops. If sum > threshold, it does not drop.

Summary

  • For extremely rare items (0.05%), succeed if both dice roll 1 (sum = 2).
  • For less rare items, thresholds are higher: 6, 120, 200, 800.
  • Always succeed for items set at 100%.

Currency System

Value is determined using five types of coins in ascending order: Copper, Silver, Gold, Electrum, and Platinum. Copper is used for trivial purchases, Silver for everyday purchases, Gold for substantial purchases, Electrum for mid-tier and regional trade, and Platinum for exceptionally high-value items.

Economy of the Planets

Zail is the universe's reserve currency holder and global economic hub. Rich in life-dependent natural resources, it serves as the central hub for currency exchange. The first planet (Ignis) and the last three planets do not recognize currencies from any other planet.

PlanetsTrade StatusCurrency
Ignis (1st)No interest in trade. Underground humanoid societies trade via teleportation and magic.No standard currency
2nd & 3rd PlanetsActive trade with Zail. Interested in magical minerals and Zail's technology.Zail-adjacent
Zail (4th)Universal reserve currency hub. Central exchange point.Reserve Currency
5th–10th PlanetsFree trade. Zail's laws generally respected. Smuggling and piracy occur.Universal coins (Zail standard)
Last 3 (Futurem, Omnè, Tredici)Futuristic credit card systems backed by laser-secured gem/mineral vaults.Credit systems only
There is illegal smuggling between all 13 planets. Each planet has different "doors" - portals, sophisticated trading posts, and a gate to hell. Planets further from Zail require a pass to enter their airspace. The evil race Omnè, knowing their planets are endangered by the dying star Tawdry Dwarf, view universal coins as worthless and would simply melt them down.

Additional Notes: All items can be sold to associated tradesmen for at least base price. Bartering may affect outcomes positively or negatively. Mined items may also be siphoned by magic or alchemy, but mining yields the highest success rates.
