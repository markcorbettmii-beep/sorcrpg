Campaign Lobbies

Join or host live SORC sessions online. Creating a lobby requires a box set code. Joining is free for all members.

👥 Who Can Create

Lobby creation requires a valid SORC box set code. Game Masters [GM] and Player Characters [PC] with a box set code can host. Civilians assess into either GM or PC and can join any open lobby.

⚔ Who Can Join

Any player who has assessed into the lobbies as a PC or GM can join open lobbies. Lobbies hold up to 20 players - no box set required to participate as a player.

💬 Lobbies vs. Private Rooms

Lobbies are public recruiting rooms where players find and join campaigns. They have no voice, dice, or VTT - just party assembly. Lobbies hold up to 20 members.

Private Campaign Rooms are separate, password-protected spaces created once a party is assembled. They include voice, dice, and full VTT tools. Private rooms hold 1 GM and up to 5 PCs.

If a PC created the lobby, they must recruit a GM before converting - the GM takes control of the private room. GMs may also enable Spectate Mode to allow observers.

🔧 Campaign Room Tools

Private Campaign Rooms give GMs everything needed to run a full session: voice chat, a virtual tabletop (VTT) with token and map support, live access to the SORC rules compendium, shared loot tables, and character sheet integration.

Each campaign's map shares the same world coordinates as every other campaign in Essentia - but events are fully dynamic. Structures, homes, estates, castles, villages, environmental damage, and plots evolve differently in each campaign, making every session's map uniquely shaped by the players who ran it.
