NPC Professions

NPC Professions encompass all specialized roles available to Non-Player Characters, including those also available to Player Characters (marked with *). NPCs may pursue professions without the Talent Tree pairings that bound Player Character development, allowing greater flexibility in NPC specialization and social roles within the game world.

A. Commerce

Auctioneer

Masters of valuation and sales, auctioneers broker high-value items to elite buyers, creating competitive bidding environments that maximize prices and prestige.

Census Taker

Officials responsible for recording population, property ownership, and demographic data for taxation and governance purposes.

Chamberlain

High-ranking household administrators who manage estates, staff, finances, and daily operations for nobles and wealthy patrons.

Customs Officer

Officials who inspect and tax goods crossing borders, collect duties, and enforce trade regulations between territories.

Innkeeper

Proprietors of taverns and inns who provide lodging, food, and hospitality to travelers while serving as social hubs for communities.

Landlord

Property owners who lease buildings, land, and housing, managing tenants and collecting rents for income.

Quartermaster

Supply officers responsible for provisioning armies, ships, or large organizations with food, equipment, and logistics.

Reeve

Local administrative officials who oversee towns or regions on behalf of higher authority, handling disputes and law enforcement.

Tax Collector

Officials who assess and collect taxes from citizens and businesses, maintaining records of revenue for governance.

Town Crier

Public announcers who proclaim news, laws, and important information throughout towns and cities on behalf of authorities.

Upkeep

Maintenance workers responsible for managing and repairing public infrastructure, roads, buildings, and community assets.

Vendor

Merchants who sell goods in markets, shops, or stalls, providing retail distribution for crafted items and trade goods.

B. Construction

Archeologist *

Experts in ancient artifacts and historical sites who excavate, preserve, and interpret discoveries to uncover historical truths.

Architect *

Masters of structural design and urban planning who envision and construct buildings, fortifications, and cities with precision and foresight.

Mechanic *

Specialists who repair and craft devices manipulating time, space, or probability through mechanical and technical innovation.

Sculptor *

Artists who create detailed works of art from various materials, producing sculptures and decorative pieces of lasting beauty.

Shipwright *

Expert ship builders and maritime specialists who construct vessels and navigate air and sea commerce with skill and innovation.

Stoneworker *

Masters of stone craftsmanship and masonry who shape, carve, and construct lasting structures and detailed stonework.

Woodworker *

Craftspeople who fell, prepare, and shape wood into buildings, furniture, and decorative items vital for communities.

Diplomat

Emissary

Appointed representatives who carry messages and conduct business on behalf of leaders in foreign or neutral territories.

Specialists in matters of the heart who facilitate romantic bonds, arrange matches, and provide intimate counseling.

Master of Revels

Officials who organize and oversee celebrations, festivals, entertainments, and public events for communities or nobility.

Portent

Socialite

Elite figures who move through high society, wielding influence through patronage, social networks, and cultural prestige.

Performers and historians who preserve and share tales, legends, and knowledge through narrative performance and writing.

Taskmaster

Supervisors who organize labor, assign duties, manage schedules, and oversee the completion of projects and missions.

D. Justice

Bailiff

Officers who enforce court orders, execute warrants, and maintain order during legal proceedings.

Bodyguard

Hired protectors who safeguard individuals or groups from harm through vigilance and combat readiness.

Bounty Hunter

Specialized trackers and hunters who capture or eliminate targets for rewards offered by authorities or patrons.

Escort

Professionals who accompany and protect travelers, merchants, or dignitaries through dangerous territories.

Executioner

Officials responsible for carrying out capital punishment as decreed by legal authorities.

Gatekeeper

Guard

Armed protectors who patrol and defend locations, people, or possessions from theft and threat.

Interrogator

Specialists trained in questioning techniques who extract information from prisoners and suspects.

Jailer

Wardens responsible for managing prisons, guarding inmates, and maintaining order in detention facilities.

Judge

Arbiters of justice who preside over legal proceedings, interpret law, and render verdicts in disputes.

Magistrate

Porter

Workers who carry goods, luggage, and cargo, transporting heavy items for merchants and travelers.

Legal specialists who present evidence and arguments against accused parties on behalf of the state.

Torturer

Specialists in extracting information through pain and psychological pressure, employed by authorities and criminal organizations.

Warden

High-ranking prison officials who oversee detention facilities, discipline, and prisoner management.
