Code of Conduct

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Slayers of Rings & Crowns is a community built around shared adventure, creativity, and respect. Whether you're at the table, in the forums, or competing in Trials of Combat, these standards apply to everyone on SORC Beyond and all associated platforms.

1. Respect Every Member

Treat all players, GMs, and community members with respect regardless of race, gender, age, disability, sexual orientation, religion, or background. Personal attacks, harassment, and hate speech are not tolerated and will result in immediate action up to and including permanent ban.

2. Keep It Fair

Do not cheat, exploit bugs, use unauthorized tools, or engage in any behavior that gives you an unfair advantage over other players. This includes but is not limited to: item duplication, stat manipulation, and unauthorized account sharing. Report exploits responsibly to corbett@sorcrpg.com.

3. No Harassment or Bullying

Bullying, targeted harassment, doxxing, and threatening behavior of any kind - in public forums, private messages, or live sessions - will result in removal from the platform. Use the report tools built into SORC Beyond to flag bad behavior; do not retaliate.

4. Appropriate Content

All content posted publicly - forum threads, session recaps, community uploads, profile content - must be appropriate for an audience of 18+. No sexually explicit, graphically violent, or shock-value content. No real-world political or religious extremism. Uploaded content is subject to Admin review before Community Points are awarded.

5. Honest Trading

Trading at the Trading Post must be conducted in good faith. No scamming, no fraudulent listings, and no real-money exchanges between players on SORC servers. Only in-game currency earned in recorded Online Lobby sessions is valid for player-to-player trade.

6. Respect the GM

Game Masters have authority within their sessions. Follow their rulings and decisions during play. If you have a genuine dispute, raise it after the session - not during. GMs are also bound by this Code of Conduct and may not abuse their position to target or disadvantage players unfairly.

7. No Spam or Self-Promotion

Do not spam the forums, flood private messages, or use the platform primarily for self-promotion or advertising. Posts made in bad faith to farm Community Points will have points revoked and may result in account suspension.

8. Enforcement

Violations are handled by Admins at their discretion. Consequences range from a warning and point deduction to temporary suspension or permanent ban depending on severity and history. Decisions by Owners and Admins are final.

To report a violation, use the in-platform report tools or email corbett@sorcrpg.com.

9. Changes to This Code

We may update this Code of Conduct at any time. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated standards.
