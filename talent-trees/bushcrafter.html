Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 24 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Bushcrafter

Paired Profession: Woodworker

Masters of wilderness survival and environmental craftsmanship who provide cover in any terrain and traverse obstacles with ease through the ability to craft functional tools and structures from natural materials like vines and branches.

Associated Profession - TBD

Disposition - Ranger

Traits: Knowledge, Wit, Focus

Pairing Logic: Bushcrafters and Woodworkers both draw power from forests and natural materials: one harvesting sustainable resources, the other transforming them into art and function. Bushcrafters understand which trees provide the best wood, when to harvest for quality, and how to source materials sustainably. Woodworkers understand how to select, treat, and transform those materials into lasting creations. A Bushcrafter's knowledge ensures a Woodworker receives pristine materials at peak quality. A Woodworker's reputation spreads the value of sustainable Bushcraft practices. Both understand that forests are not to be conquered but honored and worked with. Together they create a partnership where wilderness wisdom meets artisanal excellence.
