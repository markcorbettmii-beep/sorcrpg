Thief Talent Tree

Navigates confined spaces, picks locks, and pockets items with precision. Includes the Mole talent: grants +5 ft. movement speed while underground; consumes 5% additional food/water daily—refer to race Aptitude.

Attributes: Agility, Intellect, Dexterity

Lockpicking

Pick locks and open secured containers with finesse and precision using proper tools.

Stealth Navigation

Move through confined spaces and narrow passages without being detected, exploiting shadows and cover.

Pocket Items

Steal or conceal items on your person with deft precision, maintaining stealth throughout.

Escape Artist

Free yourself from bonds, restraints, and confined spaces through flexibility and technique.

Safe Cracking

Open safes, vaults, and other secure containers through understanding of mechanical principles.

Mole

Grants +5 ft. movement speed while underground; consumes 5% additional food/water daily—refer to race Aptitude.

Master Safecracker

Your mastery of locks, safes, and securing mechanisms is supreme. Gain a bonus to all Tinker checks (must know the Profession Tinker). You can open any container, crack any code-lock, and bypass the most complex mechanical security systems.
