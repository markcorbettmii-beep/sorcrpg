Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=23} talent trees is paired with a corresponding character profession, offering thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Thief Talent Tree

Navigates confined spaces, picks locks, and pockets items with precision. Includes the Mole talent: grants +5 ft. movement speed while underground; consumes 5% additional food/water daily—refer to race Aptitude.

†Associated Profession - Alchemist

†Profession Disposition - Vault Keeper

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Traits: Agility, Intellect, Dexterity

a. Escape Artist

Trait Bonus: Agility

Free yourself from bonds, restraints, and confined spaces through flexibility and technique. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Lockpicking

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Pick locks and open secured containers with finesse and precision using proper tools. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Safecracker (†Profession Bonus†)

Trait Bonus: Focus

Your skill with locks, safes, and securing mechanisms is supreme. You can open any container, crack any code-lock, and bypass the most complex mechanical security systems. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively. You gain a bonus† to all Alchemist checks (see Professions).

d. Mole

Trait Bonus: Agility

Grants +5 ft. movement speed while underground; consumes 5% additional food/water daily—refer to race Aptitude. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Pocket Items

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Steal or conceal items on your person with deft precision, maintaining stealth throughout. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Safe Cracking

Trait Bonus: Focus

Open safes, vaults, and other secure containers through understanding of mechanical principles. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Stealth Navigation

Trait Bonus: Agility

Move through confined spaces and narrow passages without being detected, exploiting shadows and cover. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

h. Arcana

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master magical theory and understanding of the arcane arts. Gain bonuses to rolls involving magical knowledge, identifying magical effects, and understanding magical lore and theory. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
