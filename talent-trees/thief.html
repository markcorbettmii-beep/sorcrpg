XXIII. Thief

NPC Disposition: Alchemist

Navigates confined spaces, picks locks, disables traps, hides and pockets (and picks) items with precision.

Traits: Agility, Dexterity and Deceit

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

1. Escape Artist

Trait Bonus: Agility

Free yourself from bonds, restraints, and confined spaces through flexibility and technique. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Pickpocketing

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Lift items from others without detection. Master sleight of hand and misdirection. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Lock Picking

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Pick locks and open secured containers with finesse and precision. Your understanding of mechanical locks allows you to defeat even complex security mechanisms. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Stealth

Trait Bonus: Agility

Move through spaces without being detected, exploiting shadows, cover, and silent footfalls. Your mastery of concealment allows you to remain hidden even in plain sight. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Safe Cracking

Trait Bonus: Focus

Open safes, vaults, and other secure containers through understanding of mechanical principles and careful manipulation. Your expertise defeats even the most complex locking mechanisms. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Acrobatics

Trait Bonus: Agility

Navigate through confined spaces with grace and precision, executing flips, rolls, and fluid movements. Your body control allows you to access areas others cannot reach. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Climbing

Trait Bonus: Strength

Scale vertical surfaces, walls, and structures using ropes, grappling hooks, or bare hands. Your technique allows you to ascend 90-degree vertical surfaces when equipped with proper equipment. Strength trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
