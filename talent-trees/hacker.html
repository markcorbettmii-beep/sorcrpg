Hacker Talent Tree

Manipulates security systems and deciphers complex codes and mechanisms.

†Associated Profession - Engineer

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Intellect, Technical expertise

a. Bypass Security

Decode and disable security systems with precision. Crack locks, disable alarms, and circumvent technological safeguards.

b. Code Cracking

Decipher complex codes, ciphers, and encrypted messages. Unlock hidden information encoded in text and symbols.

c. Device Manipulation

Understand and operate technological devices, machinery, and mechanical systems beyond normal capability.

d. Master Hacker (†Profession Bonus†)

Your mastery of systems and code is legendary. You can penetrate the most sophisticated security systems, decrypt encrypted data, and manipulate digital networks with supreme precision. You gain a bonus† to all Engineer checks (see Professions).

e. Network Navigation

Navigate complex systems and interconnected networks to find information or circumvent barriers.

f. Trap Detection

Identify and disable mechanical and technological traps before they trigger.
