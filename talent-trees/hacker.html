ix. Hacker

Masters of systems and exploits. Hackers break through security, uncover secrets, and solve problems through unconventional methods.

Profession Disposition (NPC): Cipher Master

Traits: Knowledge, Focus and Determination

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Code Breaking

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Decipher and break encryption, codes, and security protocols. Understand the logic behind security systems and find their weaknesses. Your analytical mind can crack codes others think impossible. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. System Analysis

Trait Bonus: Intellect

Study complex systems to understand their design, function, and vulnerabilities. Analyze magical or technological networks to find entry points and blind spots. Your deep understanding of systems reveals what others cannot see. Intellect trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Security Bypass

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Circumvent security measures through clever manipulation or physical bypasses. Disarm traps, override locks, and disable alarms without triggering them. Your techniques allow you to access supposedly protected areas. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Problem Solving

Trait Bonus: Focus

Approach complex problems creatively and find solutions where others see only obstacles. Break seemingly impossible challenges into manageable pieces. Your unorthodox thinking leads to unexpected breakthroughs. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Lateral Thinking

Trait Bonus: Determination

Develop unconventional perspectives and question assumptions that limit others. Find solutions that work because they break expected patterns. Your creative approaches often achieve results faster than conventional methods. Determination trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Rapid Adaptation

Trait Bonus: Agility

Quickly adjust to new situations and unexpected changes in circumstances. Learn new systems and exploit them with minimal preparation time. Your mental flexibility allows you to adapt faster than most people could comprehend. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Information Gathering

Trait Bonus: Perception

Extract valuable information from conversations, documents, and observations. Piece together clues and details others overlook to form a complete picture. Your attention to detail reveals secrets hidden in plain sight. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
