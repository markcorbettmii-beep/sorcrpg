XVI. Performer

Masters of entertainment, music, and captivating audiences.

NPC Disposition: Socialite

Traits: Charisma, Agility and Focus

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

1. Dancing

Trait Bonus: Agility

Move with grace and precision through choreographed performances and improvisational routines. Enchant observers and gain bonuses to acrobatic and rhythmic movement rolls. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Musician

Trait Bonus: Focus

Play musical instruments with skillful technique and artistic expression. Conjure emotional responses through instrumental and vocal performance. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Storytelling

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Captivate audiences through compelling narratives and dramatic narration. Gain bonuses to persuasion and social rolls when engaging through stories and tales. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Magician

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Master illusion, misdirection, and magical performance. Dazzle audiences with tricks that blur the line between reality and wonder, creating lasting impressions through sleight of hand and showmanship. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Actor

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Portray characters with depth and believability through performance. Adopt convincing roles and personas to fool observers. Gain bonuses to deception and social rolls when performing different characters. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Crowd Control

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Command attention and direct audience behavior through performance and presence. Gain bonuses when managing groups and swaying public opinion through entertainment. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Inspire Hope

Trait Bonus: Focus

Uplift spirits and provide emotional support through your art and presence. Gain bonuses to morale-related rolls and provide temporary inspiration to your allies. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
