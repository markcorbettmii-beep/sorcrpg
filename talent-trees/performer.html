Performer Talent Tree

Masters of entertainment, music, and captivating audiences.

Attributes: Charisma, Disposition, Agility

Singing

Master vocal performance with perfect pitch and emotional resonance. Charm audiences through song and gain bonuses to bardic and musical performance rolls.

Dancing

Move with grace and precision through choreographed performances and improvisational routines. Enchant observers and gain bonuses to acrobatic and rhythmic movement rolls.

Instrument Mastery

Play musical instruments with masterful technique and artistic expression. Conjure emotional responses through instrumental performance.

Acting

Portray characters with depth and believability through performance. Gain bonuses to deception rolls when adopting roles or personas.

Storytelling

Captivate audiences through compelling narratives and dramatic narration. Gain bonuses to persuasion and social rolls when engaging through stories and tales.

Master Performer

Your mastery of performance and entertainment is legendary. Gain a bonus to all Enchanter checks (must know the Profession Enchanter). You can move crowds to tears, inspire movements through your art, and captivate any audience regardless of size or disposition.
