S O R C

Theologist Talent Tree

Centers on faith, protection, and healing.

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Wisdom, Disposition

Sense Deceased

Detect the presence of undead creatures and spirits within a certain radius. Gain awareness of their general direction and relative power.

Bless Water/Wood

Create holy items through ritualistic blessing. Convert ordinary water into holy water or bless wooden items for sacred purposes.

Light Heal

Restore health to yourself or allies through divine energy. Mend wounds and cure minor ailments.

Faith

Provide an area morale boost to your party, increasing their resolve and confidence in challenging situations.

Prayer

Channel divine protection, granting resistance to danger and harmful effects to yourself and nearby allies.

Judgment

See through deception and lies. Gain insight into the true nature of others and reveal hidden falsehoods.

Master Priest

Your divine knowledge and spiritual authority are supreme. You can perform sacred rites, counsel the faithful, and channel divine power with masterful precision. You gain a bonus† to all Witchcrafter checks (see Professions).
