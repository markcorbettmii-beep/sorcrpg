Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

I. Actuary

Paired Profession: Investigator

Mathematicians and probability experts. Actuaries calculate odds, manage risk, and advise allies on the likelihood of success and failure.

Associated Profession - Gambler

Disposition - TBD

Traits: Intellect, Focus, Determination

Pairing Logic: Actuaries and Investigators both master patterns and probability, one through mathematics and statistics, the other through evidence and deduction. Actuaries provide the analytical framework for understanding likelihood and risk across cases. Investigators test those theories in the field, discovering which statistical patterns actually predict outcomes. An Actuary's probability calculations help Investigators prioritize investigations and predict suspect behavior. An Investigator's real-world findings teach Actuaries which statistical models matter in practice versus theory. Both understand that truth requires both rigorous analysis and empirical verification. Together they combine mathematical certainty with practical evidence to solve mysteries that would be impossible through either discipline alone.

1. Probability Calculation

Trait Bonus: Focus

You possess an innate ability to calculate odds and probabilities with mathematical precision. Quickly assess the likelihood of success or failure in various situations. Your calculations account for multiple variables and provide practical guidance. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
