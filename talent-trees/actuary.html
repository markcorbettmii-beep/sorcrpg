I. Actuary

Mathematicians and probability experts. Actuaries calculate odds, manage risk, and advise allies on the likelihood of success and failure.

Associated Profession - Gambler

Disposition - TBD

Traits: Intellect, Focus, Determination

1. Probability Calculation

Trait Bonus: Focus

You possess an innate ability to calculate odds and probabilities with mathematical precision. Quickly assess the likelihood of success or failure in various situations. Your calculations account for multiple variables and provide practical guidance. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Risk Assessment

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Evaluate potential hazards and threats based on empirical analysis and data. Identify which dangers are statistically significant and which are manageable risks. Your assessments help parties make informed decisions about dangerous undertakings. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Statistical Insight

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Recognize patterns and trends through statistical analysis. Understand underlying probabilities in games, natural phenomena, and combat scenarios. Your insights into patterns grant bonuses when predicting likely outcomes or making strategic decisions. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Success Advisor

Trait Bonus: Focus

Advise your allies on their likelihood of success before they attempt risky tasks. Provide meaningful counseling based on their abilities, the difficulty, and environmental factors. Allies informed by your analysis gain confidence and bonuses to key rolls when attempting tasks you've assessed. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Luck Calculation

Trait Bonus: Determination

You've learned to track and predict luck, understanding its ebb and flow like mathematical certainty. Sense when fortune favors or abandons you and your companions. Help others understand the statistical nature of their good or bad fortune. Determination trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Contingency Planning

Trait Bonus: Focus

Master the art of planning for multiple outcomes and probability scenarios. Develop backup strategies for likely failures and capitalize on probable successes. Your ability to think through contingencies gives your party significant advantages in crisis situations. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Odds Mastery

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

You've transcended simple probability and achieved mastery of odds themselves. Understand the mathematical underpinnings of chance, fate, and fortune. Navigate situations where others see only random chaos, always finding the path with the best odds. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

8. Certainty Calculator

Trait Bonus: Focus

Your mastery of probability and risk is legendary. You can calculate the odds of virtually any situation with remarkable accuracy. Advise nobles on investments, guide generals in strategy, and counsel adventurers on their survival prospects. You gain a bonus to all profession checks related to probability analysis and risk management. Your insights have shaped fortunes and destinies. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
