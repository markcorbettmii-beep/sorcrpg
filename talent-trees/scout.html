Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

XIX. Scout

Paired Profession: Herbalist

Emphasizes exploration and survival.

Associated Profession - Hunter

Disposition - Nature Warden

Traits: Intellect, Agility, Disposition

Pairing Logic: Scouts and Herbalists form an essential wilderness partnership: one finding and protecting valuable plants, the other knowing their properties and applications. Scouts locate rare botanical specimens and create safe passages for Herbalists to reach growing regions. Herbalists teach Scouts which plants support survival and healing in any terrain. A Scout's knowledge of wilderness routes ensures Herbalists access pristine growing regions untouched by civilization. A Herbalist's expertise ensures Scouts know which plants restore health and endurance. Both understand that wilderness survival depends on working with nature's gifts. Together they create systems of sustainable harvesting where respect and harvest combine to support both community and ecosystem.

1. Decipher Map

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Read and interpret maps, plans, and charts with expert precision. Understand symbols, scales, and geographical references. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Geology & Geography

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Understand terrain types, geological formations, and geographical features. Read landscapes to predict weather, identify resources, and navigate safely. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Hawk Eye

Trait Bonus: Perception

Possess enhanced vision to spot details others might miss. Gain bonuses to perception rolls at range and identify distant objects and movement. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Lead the Way

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Guide your group with confidence, improving party morale and initiative through your leadership and example. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Guide

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Your expertise in leading expeditions and navigating the wilderness is renowned. You can safely lead groups through dangerous terrain, find the best routes, and ensure party survival in any environment. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively. You gain a bonus to all Hunter checks (see Professions).

6. Sense Danger

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Spot threats in your surroundings with heightened awareness and intuition. Gain bonuses to perception checks for detecting ambushes and environmental hazards. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Track

Trait Bonus: Perception

Follow trails and signs left behind by prey, enemies, or allies. Read subtle environmental clues to determine direction, pace, and intent. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

8. Animal Handling

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Understand and work with animals, calming frightened beasts and commanding domesticated ones. Bond with creatures through patience and understanding, gaining their trust and cooperation. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

9. Nature

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Possess comprehensive knowledge of the natural world, including plants, animals, weather patterns, and ecosystems. Gain bonuses to rolls involving natural phenomena, identifying plants and creatures, and understanding the wilderness. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

10. Veterinarian

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Your expertise in animal care and healing enables you to treat wounds and ailments in your Companions. You understand animal physiology, can diagnose illnesses, and apply effective treatments to keep your bestial allies healthy and battle-ready. Your compassionate touch and knowledge of natural remedies make you invaluable to any creature depending on your care. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

11. Scaling

Trait Bonus: Agility

Master the art of climbing vertical and perpendicular surfaces using ropes, grappling hooks, and climbing devices. Your technique allows you to ascend sheer cliffs, scale fortress walls, and navigate treacherous heights with efficiency and control. Requires proper equipment such as ropes or grappling hooks to scale 90-degree vertical surfaces and access areas previously thought unreachable. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

12. Gatherer

Trait Bonus: Perception

Master the art of locating and harvesting valuable resources from the natural world. Your knowledge of flora, fungi, and natural materials allows you to find edible plants, medicinal herbs, and rare ingredients others overlook. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
