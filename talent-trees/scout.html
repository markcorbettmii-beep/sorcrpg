Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=11} talent trees is paired with a corresponding character profession, offering thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Scout Talent Tree

Emphasizes exploration and survival.

†Associated Profession - Hunter

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Intellect, Agility, Disposition

a. Decipher Map

Read and interpret maps, plans, and charts with expert precision. Understand symbols, scales, and geographical references.

b. Geology & Geography

Understand terrain types, geological formations, and geographical features. Read landscapes to predict weather, identify resources, and navigate safely.

c. Hawk Eye

Possess enhanced vision to spot details others might miss. Gain bonuses to perception rolls at range and identify distant objects and movement.

d. Lead the Way

Guide your group with confidence, improving party morale and initiative through your leadership and example.

e. Guide (†Profession Bonus†)

Your expertise in leading expeditions and navigating the wilderness is renowned. You can safely lead groups through dangerous terrain, find the best routes, and ensure party survival in any environment. You gain a bonus† to all Hunter checks (see Professions).

f. Sense Danger

Spot threats in your surroundings with heightened awareness and intuition. Gain bonuses to perception checks for detecting ambushes and environmental hazards.

g. Track

Follow trails and signs left behind by prey, enemies, or allies. Read subtle environmental clues to determine direction, pace, and intent.
