vii. Detective

Masters of investigation, deduction, and evidence analysis. Skilled at uncovering hidden truths, solving mysteries, and piecing together clues that others overlook.

Profession Disposition (NPC): Guard

Traits: Knowledge, Perception and Wisdom

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Evidence Collection

Trait Bonus: Perception

Systematically gather, preserve, and catalog physical evidence from crime scenes and investigations. Identify crucial details others miss and maintain evidence integrity. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Deduction

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Analyze clues and piece together information to reconstruct events and identify suspects. Draw logical conclusions from disparate facts to solve complex mysteries. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Surveillance

Trait Bonus: Vigilance

Observe targets and gather intelligence without being detected. Track movements, document activities, and recognize patterns of behavior through patient, methodical watching. Vigilance trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Interrogation

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Question suspects and witnesses to extract reliable information. Detect deception, identify inconsistencies, and build rapport with sources. Gain bonuses when determining truth from lies. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Forgery Detection

Trait Bonus: Perception

Identify forged documents, altered records, and falsified evidence through careful examination and expert knowledge. Spot inconsistencies in paper, ink, handwriting, and seals that reveal deception. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Crime Scene Analysis

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Analyze crime scenes to understand what occurred, when it happened, and how the perpetrator acted. Interpret blood patterns, weapon marks, and environmental clues to reconstruct events. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
