Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Tribalist

Paired Profession: Body Artist

Masters of tribal culture and collective power who unite communities through tradition, ritual, and shared purpose to achieve feats impossible for individuals alone.

NPC Disposition: Elder

Traits: Resolve, Knowledge, Determination

Pairing Logic: Tribalists build community identity through shared marks and rituals; Body Artists create those permanent marks that bind communities. Tribalists commission meaningful tattoos that signify tribal status and achievement. Body Artists gain prestige through tribal work that carries deep cultural significance. Both understand that permanent body modification represents identity and belonging. Tribalists use Body Artist expertise to create visible bonds within their community; Body Artists reach cultural niches unavailable to ordinary markets. Together they create a partnership where identity and artistry merge.
