Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=14} talent trees is paired with a corresponding character profession, offering thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Olympiad Talent Tree

Focuses on athletic and physical feats.

†Associated Profession - Cook

†Profession Disposition - Stable Hand

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Strength, Agility, Nimbleness

a. Athleticism

Trait Bonus: Agility

Master a variety of athletic skills: Climb (scale vertical surfaces), Sprint (burst speed), Acrobatics (graceful movement), Vault (leap over obstacles), Swim (water navigation), Jump (impressive distances). Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Force

Trait Bonus: Grit

Deliver powerful unarmed attacks with devastating effect: Kick (powerful leg strike), Punch (strong hand strike), Bite (feral attack), Head Butt (direct impact). Grit trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Athletic Trainer (†Profession Bonus†)

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Your knowledge of physical conditioning and athletic performance is legendary. You can teach others to reach peak physical condition and overcome limitations through proper technique and enhancement. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively. You gain a bonus† to all Cook checks (see Professions).

d. Root

Trait Bonus: Grit

Maintain sure-footed movement and balance in any terrain, resisting attempts to knock you prone or off-balance. Grit trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Swimming

Trait Bonus: Agility

Master the art of water-based movement and navigation. Navigate through rivers, lakes, and oceans with grace and speed, holding your breath and diving with exceptional ability. Your streamlined technique and powerful strokes make you at home in aquatic environments, allowing you to traverse water obstacles and underwater passages with confidence. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
