Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=23} talent trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

xiv. Olympiad

Focuses on athletic and physical feats.

Profession Disposition (NPC): Stable Hand

Traits: Strength, Agility and Resolve

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Athleticism

Trait Bonus: Agility

Master a variety of athletic skills: Climb (scale rough surfaces; 90-degree vertical walls require equipment), Sprint (burst speed), Acrobatics (graceful movement), Vault (leap over obstacles), Swim (water navigation), Jump (impressive distances). Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Force

Trait Bonus: Grit

Deliver powerful unarmed attacks with devastating effect: Kick (powerful leg strike), Punch (strong hand strike), Bite (feral attack), Head Butt (direct impact). Grit trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Balance

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Maintain sure-footed movement and balance in any terrain, resisting attempts to knock you prone or off-balance. Gain bonuses to rolls for maintaining balance in difficult situations. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Root

Trait Bonus: Strength

Ground yourself with powerful stances that resist displacement and knockdown effects. Become immovable when you choose to stand your ground. Strength trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Swimming

Trait Bonus: Agility

Master the art of water-based movement and navigation. Navigate through rivers, lakes, and oceans with grace and speed. Your streamlined technique and powerful strokes make you at home in aquatic environments. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Endurance

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Build exceptional stamina and resilience for sustained physical exertion. Push your body to limits others cannot reach. Gain bonuses to endurance checks and resistance against fatigue. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Peak Performance

Trait Bonus: Strength

Execute athletic feats at the height of your abilities. Gain bonuses to all physical performance checks when performing at your best. Strength trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
