xiii. Leprechaun

Masters of fortune, trickery, and probability manipulation.

Associated NPC Disposition: Inventor

Traits: Charisma, Knowledge and Luck

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Charm and Charisma

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Your natural charisma and winning personality endear you to others. Gain bonuses to social rolls and persuasion attempts. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Fortune's Favor

Trait Bonus: Luck

Bend circumstance to your will. Gain bonuses to rolls involving chance, gambling, negotiation, and swaying probability in your favor. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Lucky Strike

Trait Bonus: Luck

Your attacks carry an uncanny tendency to succeed. Gain bonuses to critical hit rolls and improve your chances of landing devastating strikes. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Luck Manipulation

Trait Bonus: Luck

Represents chance and fortune, allowing for various amounts of extra chances in both combat and non-combat situations. Luck rolls 1d4 and adds the Luck Manipulation score to the result, stacking with basic Hit, Defense rolls, Trait checks, all DIFS, and probability-based outcomes. On tied Initiative, Luck Manipulation is used as a tiebreaker. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Gambler

Trait Bonus: Luck

Your skill with chance, fortune, and probability is legendary. Gain bonuses to all gambling, negotiation, and probability-based rolls. You can read odds with precision, influence outcomes in your favor, and always find advantage in games of chance. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Pot of Gold

Trait Bonus: Perception

A knack for finding wealth and valuables. Gain bonuses to rolls for discovering treasure, identifying valuable items, and appraising worth. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Trickster

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Master the art of deception and misdirection. Gain bonuses to all deception, bluffing, and sleight of hand rolls. Your clever tricks and quick wit allow you to escape difficult situations and manipulate social situations. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

h. Fortune Master (†Profession Bonus & Disposition Access†)

Trait Bonus: Luck

Profession Bonus: You gain a bonus† to all trickery, luck manipulation, gambling, and probability-based checks.

Inventor Access: As a Fortune Master, you establish a trade relationship with Inventors—elite NPC tinkerers and innovators. You gain access to rare gadgets, innovative devices, cutting-edge technology, and experimental designs. Inventors also provide device customization services, consultation on mechanical solutions, access to prototype technologies, and knowledge of advanced contraptions and mechanisms. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Leprechaun Philosophy

The Leprechaun draws power from luck, probability, and the manipulation of fortune itself. These talented individuals understand that fate can be guided, probability can be influenced, and fortune favors the clever. A true Leprechaun doesn't just rely on chance—they actively reshape it to their advantage, using wit, charm, and cunning to turn circumstances in their favor.
