Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=23} talent trees is paired with a corresponding character profession, offering thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Leprechaun Talent Tree

Masters of fortune, trickery, and probability manipulation.

†Associated Profession - Tinker

†Profession Disposition - Inventor

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Perception, Intellect, Disposition, Agility

a. Charm and Charisma

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Your natural charisma and winning personality endear you to others. Gain bonuses to social rolls and persuasion attempts. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Fortune's Favor

Trait Bonus: Luck

Bend circumstance to your will. Gain bonuses to rolls involving chance, gambling, negotiation, and swaying probability in your favor. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Lucky Strike

Trait Bonus: Luck

Your attacks carry an uncanny tendency to succeed. Gain bonuses to critical hit rolls and improve your chances of landing devastating strikes. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Luck Manipulation (†Profession Bonus†)

Trait Bonus: Luck

Description: Represents chance and fortune, allowing for various amounts of extra chances in both combat and non-combat situations.

Application: Luck rolls 1d4 and adds the Luck Manipulation score to the result, 4% (d4) + Luck Manipulation score, stacking with basic Hit, Defense rolls, all Trait checks, Skills, Talents, all DIFS, and probability-based outcomes like loot drops and crafting. Luck does not stack with ability-based hit rolls. On tied Initiative, Luck Manipulation is used as a tiebreaker. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Luck and Outcome Probability: Luck Manipulation rolls 1d4 and adds the Luck Manipulation score to the result, expressed as 4% (d4) + Luck Manipulation score. For probability-based outcomes, loot drops, crafting success, and similar checks, the d4 result represents up to 4% and the Luck Manipulation score is added on top. The final probability of any outcome cannot exceed 100%.

Luck Cap and Application: Luck does not influence outcomes with a base probability of 0%, unless the GM explicitly decides otherwise. When multiple effects modify probabilities, combine all results first, then cap at 100%. Luck Manipulation can be advanced up to a score of 40 (same cap as other talents).

GM Discretion: Some outcomes or story events may be designated as unaffected by Luck Manipulation, at the GM's discretion. You gain a bonus† to all Tinker checks (see Professions).

e. Gambler

Trait Bonus: Luck

Your skill with chance, fortune, and probability is legendary. Gain a bonus to all Gambler checks (must know the Profession Gambler). You can read odds with precision, influence outcomes in your favor, and always find advantage in games of chance and commerce. Luck trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Pot of Gold

Trait Bonus: Perception

A knack for finding wealth and valuables. Gain bonuses to rolls for discovering treasure, identifying valuable items, and appraising worth. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Leprechaun Philosophy

The Leprechaun draws power from luck, probability, and the manipulation of fortune itself. These talented individuals understand that fate can be guided, probability can be influenced, and fortune favors the clever. A true Leprechaun doesn't just rely on chance—they actively reshape it to their advantage, using wit, charm, and cunning to turn circumstances in their favor.
