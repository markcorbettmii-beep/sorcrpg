Excavator

Paired Profession: Miner

Masters of digging, tunneling, and extracting resources from the earth and stone.

Traits: Strength, Fortitude, Knowledge

Pairing Logic: From raw stone torn from the earth, the Sculptor breathes art into every excavated block.

1. Rock Breaking

Trait Bonus: Strength

Master the art of breaking stone and rock with precision tools and technique. Shatter boulders and excavate hard materials efficiently without wasting effort. Strength trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Ore Identification

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Identify mineral deposits and ore veins by sight and touch. Understand ore quality, purity, and mining difficulty. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Tunnel Navigation

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Navigate through cave systems and tunnels with expert precision. Understand structural integrity and avoid dangerous collapses. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Prospecting

Trait Bonus: Perception

Locate promising mining sites and identify areas rich with valuable deposits. Read geological signs to predict ore locations and abundance. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Reinforcement

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Properly shore up and reinforce tunnel walls and ceilings to prevent collapse. Install supports and understand structural engineering to maintain safe mining passages. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Deep Mining

Trait Bonus: Fortitude

Endure the harsh conditions of deep underground mining, including poor air, extreme temperatures, and physical strain. Work effectively in depths others cannot tolerate. Fortitude trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Gem Extraction

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Carefully extract gemstones and precious materials from rock without damage. Use delicate technique to preserve the value and integrity of rare finds. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
