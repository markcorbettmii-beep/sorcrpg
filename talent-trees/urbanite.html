Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 25 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Urbanite

Paired Profession: Architect

Masters of urban navigation and city survival who command the built environment with knowledge of structures, hidden paths, and the politics of civilization.

Associated Profession - Architect

Disposition - Developer

Traits: Knowledge, Focus, Wit

Pairing Logic: Urbanites and Architects form the complete city system: one thriving in urban complexity, the other designing structures that enable that thriving. Urbanites know the hidden paths and opportunities of the cities Architects build, providing invaluable feedback on what works in practice. Architects design with Urbanite input, creating spaces that serve real community needs rather than theoretical perfection. An Urbanite's intimate knowledge of city dynamics teaches Architects where to place structures for maximum utility. An Architect's vision transforms an Urbanite's chaotic home into an organized, thriving metropolis. Both understand that cities are living organisms requiring both vision and adaptation. Together they create urban environments where architectural brilliance enables human flourishing at scale.
