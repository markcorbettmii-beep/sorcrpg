Cartographer Talent Tree

Masters of mapmaking, navigation, and spatial awareness. Cartographers chart unknown lands and guide others through complex terrain.

†Associated Profession - TBD

†Profession Disposition - TBD

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Intellect, Perception, Focus

a. Map Making

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

You possess the skill to create accurate, detailed maps from observation and memory. Your maps are clear, precise, and invaluable for navigation. Gain bonuses to rolls when creating or interpreting cartographic records. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Spatial Awareness

Trait Bonus: Perception

Your heightened sense of space and position allows you to maintain an exceptional mental map of your surroundings. Rarely become lost, and quickly identify the best routes through complex terrain. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Route Planning

Trait Bonus: Focus

You excel at analyzing terrain and planning optimal routes for travel. Consider terrain difficulty, resource availability, and potential hazards when plotting courses. Your party gains bonuses to travel rolls and reduced travel time when you lead. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Landmark Recognition

Trait Bonus: Perception

You possess an remarkable ability to identify and remember distinctive landmarks, geographic features, and terrain markers. Use these visual anchors to orient yourself and guide others to specific locations. Gain bonuses to perception rolls when identifying locations previously visited or mapped. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Celestial Navigation

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

You understand the stars, constellations, and celestial patterns, using them for navigation across open terrain. Read the night sky to determine direction and maintain course even in unfamiliar lands. This ancient art allows you to navigate by the heavens when terrestrial landmarks are scarce. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Terrain Analysis

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Analyze geological and geographical features to understand terrain composition, stability, and resource distribution. Identify safe routes through dangerous ground and predict environmental hazards. Your expertise in reading the land prevents catastrophic mishaps and reveals hidden passages. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Surveying

Trait Bonus: Focus

Master the technical art of surveying and measuring distance, angles, and elevation. Use tools and techniques to create accurate representations of territory. Your precision enables accurate large-scale mapping and planning of construction or settlement. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

h. Chart Master (†Profession Bonus†)

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Your mastery of navigation and mapmaking is legendary throughout the world. You can chart unknown territories, guide expeditions through any terrain, and create maps of extraordinary accuracy and detail. You gain a bonus† to all profession checks related to navigation and cartography. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Cartographer Kit and Tools

Mapping Kit: A comprehensive set of tools for creating accurate maps and navigational charts.

  • Parchment and Paper (for creating maps)
  • Inks and Pigments (various colors for detailed mapping)
  • Compass (for determining direction)
  • Measuring Chains and Rope (for surveying distances)
  • Astrolabe (for celestial navigation)
  • Drafting Tools (rulers, straightedges, dividers)
  • Surveying Equipment (levels, transit tools)
  • Reference Charts (star charts, terrain guides)
  • Wax Seals and Binding Materials (for preserving maps)
  • Traveling Supplies (rations, water skins)
  • Rope and Climbing Equipment
  • Lantern and Oil (for night navigation)
  • Protective Case (for storing valuable maps)
