xv. Patriarch

Natural authority and command. Leadership through strength, presence, and primal hierarchy.

Associated NPC Disposition: Bodyguard

Traits: Strength, Charisma and Resolve

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Authority's Shield

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Your leadership extends protection to those you lead. Grant defensive bonuses to nearby allies and reduce their vulnerability to fear and psychological effects through your stalwart presence. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Command the Initiative

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Seize the moment of battle with decisive action. You may grant a bonus to your party's initiative rolls, and reorder turn order to your tactical advantage in specific situations. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Commanding Presence

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Your natural authority establishes dominance in any group. Those around you feel the weight of your leadership and respond with increased focus and discipline. Gain bonuses to initiative rolls for your party and increased confidence in group situations. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Dominating Will

Trait Bonus: Courage

Enforce your authority through sheer force of personality and physical presence. Gain bonuses to intimidation and dominance rolls. Lower-ranked opponents may hesitate before opposing you directly. Courage trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Hierarchical Authority

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Establish clear rank and structure within your group. Lower-ranked members respond more effectively to your commands and gain bonuses when acting in coordinated efforts. Perfect for organizing larger groups or organizations. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Pack Bonding

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Create deep loyalty and cohesion within your group through shared hardship and mutual respect. Followers gain resistance to psychological effects that would separate them from the group. Enhances companion bond strength. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Rally the Group

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Inspire your allies to push beyond their limits through rousing words and example. Boost party morale and provide temporary bonuses to their rolls when facing adversity. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

h. Patriarch Leader (†Profession Bonus & Disposition Access†)

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Profession Bonus: You gain a bonus† to all leadership, command, and authority-based checks.

Bodyguard Access: As a Patriarch Leader, you establish a trade relationship with Bodyguards—elite NPC protectors and defenders. You gain access to skilled protection services, security expertise, tactical defense knowledge, and safe havens. Bodyguards also provide personal protection, coordination on defense strategies, access to secure locations, and knowledge of threats and counter-measures. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Patriarch Leadership Philosophy

The Patriarch tree draws from natural hierarchies found throughout nature and civilization. Leadership in this tradition is not about trickery or manipulation—it is about embodying the strength, vision, and stability that others naturally follow. A true Patriarch leads by example, through presence and will, earning loyalty through demonstrated capability and commitment to their group's wellbeing.

Whether leading a small adventuring party, a criminal organization, or a faction of warriors, the Patriarch uses authority to optimize group cohesion and effectiveness.
