S O R C

Patriarch Talent Tree

Natural authority and command. Leadership through strength, presence, and primal hierarchy.

Attributes: Wisdom, Disposition, Strength, Charisma

Alignment: Open (suitable for all alignments)

Commanding Presence

Attribute: Charisma / Disposition

Your natural authority establishes dominance in any group. Those around you feel the weight of your leadership and respond with increased focus and discipline. Gain bonuses to initiative rolls for your party and increased confidence in group situations.

Rally the Group

Attribute: Wisdom / Disposition

Inspire your allies to push beyond their limits through rousing words and example. Boost party morale and provide temporary bonuses to their rolls when facing adversity. The effect scales with your Disposition score.

Tactical Direction

Attribute: Intellect / Wisdom

Coordinate party members with decisive commands that optimize group effectiveness. Grant bonuses to coordinated actions and multi-target attacks when leading the assault.

Dominating Will

Attribute: Strength / Willpower

Enforce your authority through sheer force of personality and physical presence. Gain bonuses to intimidation and dominance rolls. Lower-ranked opponents may hesitate before opposing you directly.

Authority's Shield

Attribute: Wisdom / Courage

Your leadership extends protection to those you lead. Grant defensive bonuses to nearby allies and reduce their vulnerability to fear and psychological effects through your stalwart presence.

Pack Bonding

Attribute: Disposition / Wisdom

Create deep loyalty and cohesion within your group through shared hardship and mutual respect. Followers gain resistance to psychological effects that would separate them from the group. Enhances companion bond strength.

Command the Initiative

Attribute: Wisdom / Charisma

Seize the moment of battle with decisive action. You may grant a bonus to your party's initiative rolls, and reorder turn order to your tactical advantage in specific situations.

Succession Planning

Attribute: Intellect / Wisdom

Develop and train others to be future leaders. Grant experience bonuses to companions and followers training under your tutelage. Create a legacy of leadership.

Unyielding Resolve

Attribute: Courage / Willpower

Your unwavering commitment to your cause inspires those around you to stand firm. Gain resistance to effects that would shake your resolve, and grant your followers bonuses when standing beside you.

Hierarchical Authority

Attribute: Strength / Disposition

Establish clear rank and structure within your group. Lower-ranked members respond more effectively to your commands and gain bonuses when acting in coordinated efforts. Perfect for organizing larger groups or organizations.

Patriarch Leadership Philosophy

The Patriarch tree draws from natural hierarchies found throughout nature and civilization. Leadership in this tradition is not about trickery or manipulation—it is about embodying the strength, vision, and stability that others naturally follow. A true Patriarch leads by example, through presence and will, earning loyalty through demonstrated capability and commitment to their group's wellbeing.

Whether leading a small adventuring party, a criminal organization, or a faction of warriors, the Patriarch uses authority to optimize group cohesion and effectiveness.
