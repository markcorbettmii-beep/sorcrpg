xxi. Tactician

Specializes in strategic and leadership abilities.

Associated Profession - Hacker

Profession Disposition - Cohort

Traits: Intellect, Wisdom, Disposition

NOTE: Leadership-focused talents from this tree are being consolidated into the new Patriarch tree. Refer to Patriarch for dedicated leadership mechanics.

a. Adaptability

Trait Bonus: Focus

Switch tactics efficiently, responding to changing circumstances with grace and precision. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Commanding Voice

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Direct and lead others through decisive communication. See Patriarch tree for dedicated leadership mechanics. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Strategist

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Your skill in tactical planning and strategic warfare is unparalleled. You can design battle plans, analyze enemy movements, and coordinate complex operations with precision. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively. You gain a bonus to all Hacker checks (see Professions).

d. Multitasker

Trait Bonus: Focus

Perform multiple actions per turn, allowing greater tactical flexibility in combat and challenging situations. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Showmanship

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Distract or impress enemies through performance and misdirection, gaining tactical advantages in social and combat situations. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
