Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=11} talent trees is paired with a corresponding character profession, offering thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Tactician Talent Tree

Specializes in strategic and leadership abilities.

†Associated Profession - Hacker

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Intellect, Wisdom, Disposition

NOTE: Leadership-focused talents from this tree are being consolidated into the new Patriarch tree. Refer to Patriarch for dedicated leadership mechanics.

a. Adaptability

Switch tactics efficiently, responding to changing circumstances with grace and precision.

b. Commanding Voice

Direct and lead others through decisive communication. See Patriarch tree for dedicated leadership mechanics.

c. Master Strategist (†Profession Bonus†)

Your mastery of tactical planning and strategic warfare is unparalleled. You can design battle plans, analyze enemy movements, and coordinate complex operations with precision. You gain a bonus† to all Hacker checks (see Professions).

d. Multitasker

Perform multiple actions per turn, allowing greater tactical flexibility in combat and challenging situations.

e. Showmanship

Distract or impress enemies through performance and misdirection, gaining tactical advantages in social and combat situations.
