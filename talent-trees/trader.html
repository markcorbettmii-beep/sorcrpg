S O R C

Trader Talent Tree

Relates to negotiation, commerce, and social influence.

Attributes: Disposition, Intellect

Etiquette

Master social grace, proper manners, and courtly behavior. Navigate high society with confidence and gain bonuses to social rolls in formal settings.

Negotiation

Bargain effectively in any situation, reaching favorable deals and agreements. Master the art of compromise and mutual benefit.

Appraise

Accurately assess the value of items, services, and properties. Determine fair prices and identify undervalued treasures.

Persuasion

Influence others through compelling arguments and charisma. Gain bonuses to rolls when convincing, inspiring, or swaying opinions.

Streetwise

Navigate urban environments with expertise. Know the right people, understand local customs, and find opportunities in city life.

Master Merchant

Your mastery of commerce and trade is legendary. Gain a bonus to all Quartermaster checks (must know the Profession Quartermaster). You understand market values, can broker the most complex deals, and always find profit in any transaction.
