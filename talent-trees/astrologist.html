Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 talent trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

III. Astrologist

Scholars of the stars and cosmic forces. Astrologists read celestial patterns and divine the influence of the heavens on mortal affairs.

NPC Disposition: Astronomer

Traits: Knowledge, Intuition and Focus

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

1. Star Reading

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

You possess deep knowledge of constellations, stellar patterns, and their meanings. Read the stars to gain insight into timing, fortune, and cosmic influence. Your understanding of celestial mechanics informs navigation and divination. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Celestial Timing

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Your intuition regarding celestial events and planetary movements allows you to sense auspicious and inauspicious times. Understand when the heavens favor or oppose particular actions. Gain bonuses when timing activities to align with celestial cycles. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Planetary Influence

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

You comprehend the cosmic influence of planetary bodies on fate and fortune. Understand how celestial positions affect personality, destiny, and probability. Your knowledge of planetary mechanics grants insight into cause and effect across vast scales. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Horoscope Casting

Trait Bonus: Focus

You master the art of casting horoscopes and divining personal destiny through celestial alignment. Provide individuals with insights into their cosmic fortune and potential futures. Your readings offer perspective on major life decisions and personal challenges. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Cosmic Cycles

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Understand the great cycles of the cosmos—seasons, moons, planets, and epochs. Recognize patterns in celestial events and predict future occurrences. Your knowledge of cosmic time reveals connections between disparate events across years or lifetimes. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Fate's Whisper

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Through study of the stars and cosmic forces, you've developed an ability to sense destiny and probability. Feel subtle nudges of fate that guide or warn you. Your heightened cosmic awareness allows you to perceive patterns others miss and sense when events align with cosmic will. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Astronomical Knowledge

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master the technical science of astronomy—the motion of celestial bodies, their distances, and compositions. Calculate planetary positions and predict celestial events with remarkable accuracy. Your scholarly understanding transcends superstition and reaches toward cosmic truth. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Astrologist Kit and Tools

Astronomical Equipment: Tools for observing, calculating, and interpreting celestial phenomena.

  • Telescope or Lens (for detailed star observation)
  • Astrolabe (for measuring star positions)
  • Star Charts and Reference Books
  • Astronomical Calculation Tables
  • Parchment and Inks (for recording observations)
  • Compass (for directional reference)
  • Planisphere (rotating star map)
  • Horoscope Templates
  • Crystal Orb or Scrying Device
  • Candles and Incense (for meditation)
  • Protective Case (for delicate instruments)
  • Ephemeris (astronomical almanac)
  • Lodging Supplies (for extended observation sessions)
