Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

XVIII. Renaissance

Masters of diplomacy, knowledge, and political influence. Expert negotiators and scholars with deep understanding of history, religion, and espionage.

NPC Disposition: Scribe

Traits: Knowledge, Wisdom and Charisma

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

1. Religion

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Understand religious practices, doctrines, and theological concepts across cultures. Gain bonuses to rolls involving religious knowledge, rituals, and spiritual matters. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Diplomacy

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Master the art of negotiation and political maneuvering. Navigate complex social hierarchies, broker peace between factions, and influence decisions through tact and charm. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. History

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Possess deep knowledge of historical events, civilizations, and significant figures. Recall details about past occurrences and understand historical context to predict future patterns. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Espionage

Trait Bonus: Intellect

Master the art of gathering intelligence and uncovering secrets. Gain expertise in surveillance, coded communications, and covert operations to extract sensitive information. Intellect trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Mentor

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Share your knowledge and wisdom with others, accelerating their growth. Provide guidance that helps allies improve their skills and understanding more rapidly through your teaching. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Herald

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Your skill in communication, representation, and influence is legendary. You can speak with authority on behalf of groups, negotiate complex political arrangements, and inspire others through powerful oratory. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Rhetoric

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Master the art of persuasive speech and compelling argument. Craft speeches that sway opinions, negotiate from positions of strength, and articulate positions with irrefutable logic. Gain bonuses to persuasion and influence checks through masterful oratory. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
