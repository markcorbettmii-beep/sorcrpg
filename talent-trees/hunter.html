Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=23} talent trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

xi. Hunter

Masters of wilderness survival and prey pursuit. Hunters excel at tracking, trapping, and processing game in their natural environments.

Profession Disposition (NPC): Ranger

Traits: Agility, Perception and Resolve

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Tracking

Trait Bonus: Perception

Follow trails and signs left behind by prey, enemies, or allies through wilderness and terrain. Read subtle environmental clues to determine direction, pace, species, and intent of tracked subjects. Your expertise allows you to follow quarry others cannot detect. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Trap Setting

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Set sophisticated traps for hunting and wilderness defense. Construct snares, pitfalls, deadfalls, and other mechanisms to capture or deter prey. Your traps are hidden and effective, remaining undetected until sprung. Your techniques allow you to set traps in any wilderness environment with minimal materials. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Skinning

Trait Bonus: Dexterity

Process animal hides with precision and skill, preserving quality for crafting. Efficiently remove pelts and hides from kills without damaging valuable materials. Your technique extracts maximum usable material from every animal. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Dressing Game

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Dress and prepare game meat for preservation and consumption. Understand animal anatomy and butcher game efficiently to maximize usable meat and minimize waste. Your preparation ensures meat stays fresh longer and tastes better. Your knowledge of preservation keeps game edible during long journeys. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Taxidermy

Trait Bonus: Focus

Preserve and mount animal remains for display and preservation. Create realistic mounts and preserved specimens that maintain their appearance indefinitely. Your work captures the majesty of fallen creatures and serves as both art and documentation. Your preserved specimens provide reference for other Hunters. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Fishing

Trait Bonus: Patience

Catch fish and aquatic prey using various methods: rods, nets, traps, and bare hands. Understand fish behavior and water conditions to locate productive fishing grounds. Navigate rivers, lakes, and streams to access prime fishing locations. Your fishing skills provide reliable food sources in aquatic environments. Patience trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Wilderness Sabotage

Trait Bonus: Determination

Disrupt enemy operations in the wilderness through strategic sabotage and destruction. Destroy supplies, disable equipment, and create obstacles that impede pursuit or passage. Your wilderness knowledge allows you to sabotage effectively while remaining hidden. Leave no trace of your interference except the results. Determination trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
