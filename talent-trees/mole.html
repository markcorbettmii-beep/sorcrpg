S O R C

Mole Talent Tree

Specializes in stealth, spying, and infiltration.

Attributes: Intellect, Disposition, Agility, Luck, Nimbleness

Feather Foot

Move silently through any environment, leaving no trace of your passage.

Pick Pocket

Steal items unnoticed from unsuspecting targets with precision and finesse.

Sleight of Hand

Manipulate objects quickly and dexterously, performing tricks and deceptions.

Stealth Abilities

Master advanced stealth techniques including: Chameleon (blend with surroundings), Invisibility (become unseen), Backstab (strike from concealment), Sign Language (silent communication), Lip Reading (read conversations), Amplified Hearing (detect distant sounds).

Trapper

Expert in trap-related skills: Locksmith (pick locks), Trap Detection (find hidden traps), Trap Identification (understand trap mechanics), Explosives (handle dangerous materials), Poisons (craft and use toxins), Gas Expertise (understand atmospheric hazards).

Escape Artist

Master escape techniques including: Contortion (squeeze from bonds), Hiding Objects (conceal items), Evasion (avoid capture and harm).

Master Locksmith

Your expertise in locks, mechanisms, and security systems is unmatched. Gain a bonus to all Locksmithing checks (must know the Profession Locksmith). Your understanding extends to safes, vaults, and the most complex mechanical puzzles.
