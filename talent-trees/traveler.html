Traveler Talent Tree

Features talents for movement, adaptation, and survival.

Attributes: Endurance, Disposition, Agility

Pack Mule

Efficiently carry more weight than normal. Reduce encumbrance penalties and maintain mobility while heavily laden.

Dietician

Survive longer without food or water through knowledge of nutrition and resource management. Gain bonuses to survival rolls during extended journeys.

Blend In

Camouflage yourself in any environment, becoming less noticeable to observers. Gain bonuses to stealth rolls in varied terrain.

Gold Panning

Find valuable resources and precious materials in natural environments. Locate gemstones, metals, and other treasures worth mining.

Master Navigator

Your expertise in navigation and wayfinding is unparalleled. Gain a bonus to all Hauler checks (must know the Profession Hauler). You can chart courses through unmapped wilderness, calculate distances with precision, and never lose your way.
