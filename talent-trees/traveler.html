S O R C

Traveler Talent Tree

Features talents for movement, adaptation, and survival.

†Associated Profession - Hauler

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Endurance, Disposition, Agility

a. Appraise

Accurately assess the value of items, services, and properties. Determine fair prices and identify undervalued treasures.

b. Blend In

Camouflage yourself in any environment, becoming less noticeable to observers. Gain bonuses to stealth rolls in varied terrain.

c. Dietician

Survive longer without food or water through knowledge of nutrition and resource management. Gain bonuses to survival rolls during extended journeys.

d. Etiquette

Master social grace, proper manners, and courtly behavior. Navigate high society with confidence and gain bonuses to social rolls in formal settings.

e. Gold Panning

Find valuable resources and precious materials in natural environments. Locate gemstones, metals, and other treasures worth mining.

f. Master Navigator (†Profession Bonus†)

Your expertise in navigation and wayfinding is unparalleled. You can chart courses through unmapped wilderness, calculate distances with precision, and never lose your way. You gain a bonus† to all Hauler checks (see Professions).

g. Negotiation

Bargain effectively in any situation, reaching favorable deals and agreements. Master the art of compromise and mutual benefit.

h. Pack Mule

Efficiently carry more weight than normal. Reduce encumbrance penalties and maintain mobility while heavily laden.

i. Persuasion

Influence others through compelling arguments and charisma. Gain bonuses to rolls when convincing, inspiring, or swaying opinions.

j. Streetwise

Navigate urban environments with expertise. Know the right people, understand local customs, and find opportunities in city life.
