Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

XXIV. Traveler

Paired Profession: Stitcher

Features talents for movement, adaptation, and survival.

Associated Profession - Hauler

Disposition - Transporter

Traits: Endurance, Disposition, Agility

Pairing Logic: Travelers and Stitchers form the essential expedition team: one pushing through harsh terrain, the other ensuring their equipment survives the journey. Travelers need reliable gear that only master Stitchers can create, garments that last through extreme conditions. Stitchers gain insights into real-world durability through Traveler feedback and field experience. A Traveler's knowledge of environmental hazards helps Stitchers design garments suited to specific challenges. A Stitcher's craftsmanship ensures Travelers can survive and thrive in hostile environments. Both understand that successful expeditions depend on equipment as much as spirit and determination. Together they create partnerships where journey experience drives innovation, and innovative gear enables greater adventures.

1. Appraise

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Accurately assess the value of items, services, and properties. Determine fair prices and identify undervalued treasures. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Dietician

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Survive longer without food or water through knowledge of nutrition and resource management. Gain bonuses to survival rolls during extended journeys. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Etiquette

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Master social grace, proper manners, and courtly behavior. Navigate high society with confidence and gain bonuses to social rolls in formal settings. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Gold Panning

Trait Bonus: Perception

Find valuable resources and precious materials in natural environments. Locate gemstones, metals, and other treasures worth mining. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Navigator

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Your expertise in navigation and wayfinding is unparalleled. You can chart courses through unmapped wilderness, calculate distances with precision, and never lose your way. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively. You gain a bonus to all Hauler checks (see Professions).

6. Negotiation

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Bargain effectively in any situation, reaching favorable deals and agreements. Master the art of compromise and mutual benefit. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Pack Mule

Trait Bonus: Grit

Efficiently carry more weight than normal. Reduce encumbrance penalties and maintain mobility while heavily laden. Grit trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

8. Persuasion

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Influence others through compelling arguments and charisma. Gain bonuses to rolls when convincing, inspiring, or swaying opinions. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

9. Streetwise

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Navigate urban environments with expertise. Know the right people, understand local customs, and find opportunities in city life. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

10. Explorer

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Master the art of discovery and adventure in unknown lands. Sense opportunities for exploration, find hidden paths, and thrive in untamed wilderness with confidence and curiosity. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
