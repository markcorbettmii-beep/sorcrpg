Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Geomancer

Paired Profession: Miner

Masters of earth energy who sense mineral deposits, understand geological patterns, and manipulate stone through understanding of the earth's deep currents.

NPC Disposition: Stone Whisperer

Traits: Knowledge, Intuition, Resolve

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

Pairing Logic: Geomancers and Miners form a symbiotic partnership with earth and stone: one sensing the deep currents and patterns, the other physically extracting what the earth offers. Geomancers perceive mineral veins, structural integrity, and geological anomalies through attunement to earth's subtle energies. Miners locate valuable deposits through Geomancer insights, extracting resources with precision and safety. A Geomancer's understanding of stone patterns helps Miners find the richest deposits and avoid structural collapses. A Miner's physical knowledge teaches Geomancers which earth patterns actually correlate with valuable resources. Both understand that the earth has secrets for those attuned enough to listen. Together they transform mere digging into a disciplined art where sensation and labor merge to unlock the earth's hidden bounty.

1. Mineral Sense

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Develop sensitivity to mineral and ore deposits through proximity. Sense their presence, type, and approximate quality. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Earth Reading

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Read geological formations and understand soil composition, stone layers, and bedrock structure. Predict stability, identify mineral-rich zones, and understand subterranean geography. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Tremor Sense

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Detect vibrations and tremors through stone and earth. Sense movement underground, predict cave collapses, and identify structural weaknesses. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Stone Affinity

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Develop a spiritual connection to stone and earth, gaining comfort and protection in underground environments. Resist fear of enclosed spaces and find solace in deep earth. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Vein Tracking

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Follow mineral veins through stone with remarkable accuracy. Understand how deposits extend through layers and predict their continuity. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Geothermal Sense

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Feel heat and thermal gradients beneath the surface. Identify magma chambers, hot springs, and thermal phenomena underground. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Stone Shaping

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Manipulate stone and earth through will and focus, creating passages, shifting stones, or reinforcing caverns. Channel earth energy to reshape your surroundings. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

8. Resonance Mastery

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Achieve mastery over stone resonance and harmonic frequencies. Create vibrations that shatter or reinforce stone. Understanding the earth's song gives you power over its physical form. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
