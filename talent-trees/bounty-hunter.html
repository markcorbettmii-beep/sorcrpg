Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=23} talent trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

iv. Bounty Hunter

Focuses on tracking, combat, and intimidation.

Profession Disposition (NPC): Henchman

Traits: Strength, Courage and Wisdom

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Cheap Shot

Trait Bonus: Agility

Execute surprise attacks against unsuspecting opponents. Gain bonuses when striking from ambush or exploiting openings. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Executioner

Trait Bonus: Courage

Specialize in specialized finishing moves against weakened opponents. Deal devastating final strikes with precision and lethality. Courage trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Interrogate

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Extract information from reluctant subjects through intimidation, deduction, and interrogation techniques. Gain insight into hidden truths. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Intimidate

Trait Bonus: Courage

Cause fear and dread through menacing presence and threats. Gain bonuses to rolls when attempting to frighten or dominate through force of personality. Courage trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Tracking

Trait Bonus: Perception

Follow trails and signs left behind by your targets through wilderness and terrain. Read subtle environmental clues to determine direction, pace, and intent of tracked subjects. Your expertise allows you to follow quarry others cannot detect. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Stalker

Trait Bonus: Vigilance

Follow targets discreetly without being detected. Master the art of silent pursuit and shadowing. Vigilance trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Capture Specialist

Trait Bonus: Determination

Master the techniques of subduing and capturing targets alive. Use restraints, tactics, and precision to apprehend without killing. Your expertise in non-lethal takedowns makes you invaluable for bounty hunting and prisoner transport. Determination trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
