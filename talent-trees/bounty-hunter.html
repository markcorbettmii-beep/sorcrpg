S O R C

Bounty Hunter Talent Tree

Focuses on tracking, combat, and intimidation.

Attributes: Strength, Disposition, Wisdom

Executioner

Specialize in specialized finishing moves against weakened opponents. Deal devastating final strikes with precision and lethality.

Intimidate

Cause fear and dread through menacing presence and threats. Gain bonuses to rolls when attempting to frighten or dominate through force of personality.

Stalker

Follow targets discreetly without being detected. Master the art of silent pursuit and shadowing.

Cheap Shot

Execute surprise attacks against unsuspecting opponents. Gain bonuses when striking from ambush or exploiting openings.

Interrogate

Extract information from reluctant subjects through intimidation, deduction, and interrogation techniques. Gain insight into hidden truths.
