Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Cyberneticist

Masters of creation and cybernetic enhancement who design and birth organic living beings with or without cybernetic support. Cyberneticists meld flesh, biology, and technology to craft extraordinary augmented lifeforms and pure biological wonders.

Associated Profession - TBD

Disposition - Augmentor

Traits: Focus, Knowledge, Determination

Pairing Logic: Cyberneticists and Vivisectionists both perfect living beings through radical transformation: one blending technology with flesh, the other refining biology through surgical artistry. Cyberneticists design systems; Vivisectionists provide the anatomical knowledge to integrate them safely. A Vivisectionist's precision enables Cyberneticist augmentations to achieve perfect symbiosis between flesh and machine. A Cyberneticist's technology gives Vivisectionists capabilities beyond traditional surgery. Both see the body as a canvas for improvement and transcendence. Both understand that transformation requires respecting the systems being enhanced. Together they create beings that exceed the limitations of either flesh or machinery alone, achieving transcendent synthesis.
