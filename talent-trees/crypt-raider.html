Crypt Raider Talent Tree

Intrepid explorers specializing in the perilous depths of ancient dungeons and hidden tombs.

†Associated Profession - Artificer

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Prerequisite: AGI 2 | INT 1 | DET 1 | Alignment: Open

a. Bandwagoner

Attribute: Agility (AGI)

You boost morale and cohesion in chaotic situations, providing party member movement toward the group with bonuses to their DEFS against threats within their path. The bonus is determined by the sum of your Bandwagoner and AGI Scores.

b. Chimneying

Attribute: Agility (AGI) / Nimbleness (NIM)

Your agility in maneuvering through vertical passages grants bonuses to rolls for climbing and descending, allowing navigation into new areas. Your controlled and precise movement through tight, hostile environments showcases exceptional body control and coordination, enabling you to navigate through cramped spaces with finesse and move quietly and quickly, avoiding detection in darkness.

c. Dark Senses

Attribute: Wisdom (WIS)

Your enhanced perception in darkness encompasses all senses—vision, touch, hearing, and more. You grant bonuses to perception checks when navigating in darkness, improving your awareness of threats and environmental details. This talent encompasses your ability to sense vibrations through earth and stone, granting echolocation that allows you to detect movement and structures through solid ground. The Score of this Talent cannot exceed your Dark Vision Score.

d. Dark Vision

Attribute: Determination (DET)

You are granted 2 feet of dark vision for every Score in this Talent. This enhanced ability to see clearly in the dark provides an advantage in low-light or nighttime conditions within dungeon environments.

e. Detect Trap

Attribute: Wisdom (WIS)

Your keen intuition gives you bonuses to rolls for recognizing the signs of traps, allowing you to avoid dangerous situations before they activate. If your native Score is half of the DIFS (Difficulty Score) of the trap, you'll passively detect it without an active check.

f. Drip Drop

Attribute: Flexibility (FLEX) / Determination (DET)

Your ability to identify water sources ensures hydration for you and your party, giving bonuses to rolls related to survival. Through heightened intuition and deep understanding of underground structures, you possess an uncanny knack for locating water sources in the most unlikely places within dungeons, crypts, and tombs. Whether it's the faint sound of dripping water, subtle shifts in air moisture, or small visual cues, your instincts lead you unerringly to sources of water in otherwise arid environments.

g. Dungeoneering

Attribute: Wisdom (WIS)

Your enhanced understanding of complex environments aids in navigating treacherous structures and their claustrophobic paths, improving your survival chances in dungeons and tombs. This talent consolidates your innate sense for danger and heightened perception, granting bonuses to rolls for detecting subtle changes in your environment and improving awareness of potential dangers.

Your excellent memory helps you navigate complex environments with ease, providing bonuses to rolls when retracing your steps or finding your way. Provided there is no interference, tools (like pebbles) in your kit can aid navigation. Interference may relocate your trails or attempt to trick the party into danger—use your senses to oppose this.

Bonus to Survival Rolls against traps and nocturnal foes.

h. Keep Calm

Attribute: Determination (DET) / Resilience

Your composure in high-pressure situations underground aids your allies by reducing panic and fear. If one or more of the party is feared and they miss on a Courage roll, you can omit the fear on their next turn. Your resilience allows you to rally your team in the face of adversity, inspiring them to push through even the toughest obstacles.

i. Language Deceased

Attribute: Wisdom (WIS)

Your skill in deciphering ancient texts provides bonuses to rolls for understanding valuable historical inscriptions often overlooked by others. You can interpret the meanings and stories held within ancient writings, revealing secrets and knowledge lost to time.

j. Leprechaun's Sack

Attribute: Intellect (INT)

You've practiced the magical art of reducing the weight of gems and coins, transporting both without becoming encumbered. This talent allows you to carry significantly more treasure than would normally be possible based on weight alone.

k. Mole

Attribute: Agility (AGI)

Grants +5 ft. movement speed while underground; consumes 5% additional food/water daily—refer to race Aptitude.

l. Puzzle Solver

Attribute: Intellect (INT)

Your analytical mind enhances your effectiveness with mechanisms and riddles, providing aid when deciphering cryptic puzzles and mazes. This talent showcases your ability to process and analyze information, think critically, and problem-solve. You recognize patterns, make connections, and draw logical conclusions, allowing you to solve complex puzzles and navigate through mazes with ease. You are the party member hosts want representing the group when sinister entities try to deceive through riddles or complex challenges.

m. Rat's Ribs

Attribute: Agility (AGI)

Your unmatched flexibility helps you slip through tight spaces, aiding Characters attempting to squeeze through short, narrow passages. By utilizing your body's nimble movements, you navigate seamlessly through restrictive surroundings, showcasing your adaptability and quick reflexes in tight and dark environments.

n. Rope and String Proficiency

Attribute: Artistry (ART)

Your skill in using ropes and strings enhances your effectiveness in setting traps and securing items. You excel at rigging rope systems for climbing, creating makeshift bridges in challenging terrain, and utilizing ropes for various tactical purposes within dungeons.

o. Ruin Keeper (†Profession Bonus†)

Attribute: Wisdom (WIS)

Your study of ancient artifacts, relics, and runes allows you to understand the origin and the properties they hold. The more rare the object, the more difficult they are to identify. You gain a bonus† to all Artificer checks (see Professions). You can roll to reveal the history of structures navigated and understand how ancient civilizations lived and worked.

p. Spot Structure

Attribute: Intellect (INT)

Your ability to identify architectural anomalies is heightened. These structures are often ancient and forgotten, hidden in the dark, under sand dunes, under water, or thick within a forest. You excel at spotting where a trap, ambush, or shortcut would work best, providing bonuses to planning rolls and architectural analysis.

q. Treasure Sense

Attribute: Wisdom (WIS)

Your ability to detect valuable items provides the ability to sense hidden treasures when within 5 feet per Score in Treasure Sense. This innate sense for precious items, artifacts, and valuables makes you invaluable for locating loot and understanding what's worth taking from a crypt.
