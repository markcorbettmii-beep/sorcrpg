Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=23} talent trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

vi. Crypt Raider

Intrepid explorers specializing in the perilous depths of ancient dungeons and hidden tombs.

Associated NPC Disposition: Excavator

Traits: Agility, Intelligence and Determination

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Bandwagoner

Trait Bonus: Charisma

You boost morale and cohesion in chaotic situations, providing party member movement toward the group with bonuses to their defenses against threats within their path. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Chimneying

Trait Bonus: Agility

Your agility in maneuvering through vertical passages grants bonuses to rolls for climbing and descending, allowing navigation into new areas. Your controlled and precise movement through tight, hostile environments showcases exceptional body control and coordination, enabling you to navigate through cramped spaces with finesse and move quietly and quickly, avoiding detection in darkness. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Dark Senses

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Your enhanced perception in darkness encompasses all senses—vision, touch, hearing, and more. You grant bonuses to perception checks when navigating in darkness, improving your awareness of threats and environmental details. This talent encompasses your ability to sense vibrations through earth and stone, granting echolocation that allows you to detect movement and structures through solid ground. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Dark Vision

Trait Bonus: Perception

You are granted 2 feet of dark vision for every Score in this Talent. This enhanced ability to see clearly in the dark provides an advantage in low-light or nighttime conditions within dungeon environments. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Detect Trap

Trait Bonus: Perception

Your keen intuition gives you bonuses to rolls for recognizing the signs of traps, allowing you to avoid dangerous situations before they activate. If your native Score is half of the Difficulty Score of the trap, you'll passively detect it without an active check. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Drip Drop

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Your ability to identify water sources ensures hydration for you and your party, giving bonuses to rolls related to survival. Through heightened intuition and deep understanding of underground structures, you possess an uncanny knack for locating water sources in the most unlikely places within dungeons, crypts, and tombs. Whether it's the faint sound of dripping water, subtle shifts in air moisture, or small visual cues, your instincts lead you unerringly to sources of water in otherwise arid environments. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Dungeoneering

Trait Bonus: Vigilance

Your enhanced understanding of complex environments aids in navigating treacherous structures and their claustrophobic paths, improving your survival chances in dungeons and tombs. This talent consolidates your innate sense for danger and heightened perception, granting bonuses to rolls for detecting subtle changes in your environment and improving awareness of potential dangers. Your excellent memory helps you navigate complex environments with ease, providing bonuses to rolls when retracing your steps or finding your way. Provided there is no interference, tools (like pebbles) in your kit can aid navigation. Interference may relocate your trails or attempt to trick the party into danger—use your senses to oppose this. Vigilance trait scores stack with this talent when used actively. Bonus to Survival Rolls against traps and nocturnal foes.

h. Master Raider (†Profession Bonus & Disposition Access†)

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Profession Bonus: You gain a bonus† to all dungeoneering, trap detection, navigation through dangerous tombs, and artifact identification checks.

Excavator Access: As a Master Raider, you establish a trade relationship with Excavators—elite NPC explorers and tomb specialists. You gain access to rare archaeological knowledge, ancient maps and charts, specialized excavation tools, and information on dangerous tomb locations. Excavators also provide tomb-clearing services, access to forbidden archaeological sites, consulting on artifact preservation, and sources for rare historical artifacts and treasures. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

i. Ruin Keeper

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Your study of ancient artifacts, relics, and runes allows you to understand the origin and the properties they hold. The more rare the object, the more difficult they are to identify. You can roll to reveal the history of structures navigated and understand how ancient civilizations lived and worked. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

j. Spot Structure

Trait Bonus: Perception

Your ability to identify architectural anomalies is heightened. These structures are often ancient and forgotten, hidden in the dark, under sand dunes, under water, or thick within a forest. You excel at spotting hidden passages, secret doors, and shortcuts, providing bonuses to planning rolls and architectural analysis. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

k. Treasure Sense

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Your ability to detect valuable items provides the ability to sense hidden treasures when within 5 feet per Score in Treasure Sense. This innate sense for precious items, artifacts, and valuables makes you invaluable for locating loot and understanding what's worth taking from a crypt. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

l. Frogman

Trait Bonus: Agility

Your adaptation to deep cavern diving grants exceptional breath-holding capacity and underwater maneuverability. Navigate submerged passages with ease, holding your breath far longer than normal mortals. Your streamlined movement through water and ability to remain calm in pressure-filled depths make you invaluable for exploring flooded tombs and cave systems. Additionally, your exceptional agility allows you to scale flat surface walls without any equipment, granting access to otherwise unreachable areas. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
