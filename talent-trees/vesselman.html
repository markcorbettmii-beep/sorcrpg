Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

XX. Vesselman

Masters of pressurized suits that allow them to breathe deep under the sea or in space. Vesselmen explore extreme environments and survive in hostile waters and the vacuum of space.

Associated Profession - TBD

Disposition - TBD

Traits: Intellect, Determination, Focus

Pairing Logic: The Vesselman masters the seas; the Voyager masters the journey—together they command exploration itself.

1. Pressure Adaptation

Trait Bonus: Agility

Your body has adapted to movement in extreme pressure environments, whether crushing ocean depths or weightless void. Gain bonuses to movement and combat rolls in both underwater and zero-gravity conditions. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Extreme Navigation

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master navigation through lightless depths and endless void using sonar, stellar markers, and celestial navigation. Plot courses between distant locations across oceans and space. Understand the mechanics of both aquatic and cosmic travel. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Hostile Environment Survival

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Your physiology or training allows you to survive exposure to the ocean abyss or vacuum of space. Hold your breath longer than normal, resist decompression and crushing pressure alike. Remain conscious and functional in both hostile aquatic and void environments. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Suit Expertise

Trait Bonus: Focus

You understand spacesuits and pressure suits intimately, maintaining them and using them to their fullest potential. Gain bonuses when working with environmental suits and maximize their protective capabilities. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Extreme Mechanics

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Understand the physics of movement and mechanics in both deep water and zero-gravity environments. Calculate trajectories, manage momentum, predict movement in weightless conditions, and navigate through currents and pressure differentials. Apply this knowledge to solve complex spatial problems in any extreme setting. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Environmental Awareness

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Develop an intuitive sense for extreme phenomena, stellar radiation, underwater currents, thermal vents, spatial hazards, and gravitational anomalies. Sense approaching danger from radiation storms, meteor fields, pressure anomalies, or aquatic threats. Your sensitivity provides early warning in any hostile extreme environment. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Vessel Operation

Trait Bonus: Focus

Master the operation of spacecraft, submarines, and deep-sea vessels. Understand their systems and capabilities. Pilot ships through asteroid fields or dangerous ocean trenches, navigate treacherous space routes or underwater passages, and perform emergency procedures in any extreme environment. Your expertise makes you invaluable at the helm of any vessel. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

8. Extreme Explorer

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Your mastery of exploration in the harshest environments is legendary. You understand the mysteries of both the cosmic void and the ocean abyss, and can lead expeditions into uncharted regions of space or unexplored depths. Map previously unknown territories and discover wonders beyond known boundaries. You gain a bonus to all Profession checks related to extreme exploration and discovery. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Vesselman Kit and Tools

Pressurized Suit Gear: Specialized equipment for surviving and operating in extreme underwater and space environments.

  • Pressure Suit (rated for both vacuum and deep water)
  • Helmet with Communication System and Viewports
  • Oxygen/Breathing Apparatus (extended duration, works in vacuum and liquid)
  • Magnetic Boots and Traction Gear (for zero-gravity and slippery surfaces)
  • Tether and Grappling Line (safety equipment for void and currents)
  • Thermal Regulation Layer (maintains temperature in cold depths and space)
  • Radiation Shielding and Pressure Compensation
  • Navigation Computer (stellar and sonar capable)
  • Stellar Charts and Depth Maps
  • Emergency Beacon
  • Repair Kit (suit and equipment)
  • Propulsion Pack (for maneuvering in water and vacuum)
  • Viewport/Scanner Module (detects both cosmic and aquatic hazards)
