Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

XVII. Quartermaster

Paired Profession: Shipwright

Masters of logistics, supplies, and vessel provisioning. Quartermasters manage the resources, crew, and provisions that keep vessels functioning across any environment and journey. Quartermaster profession gains bonuses to all ship journeys, whether through seas, skies, space, depths below the waves, or realms on the far side of known waters. Shipwrights build the vessels; Quartermasters ensure they're provisioned to survive anything, anywhere.

NPC Disposition: Travel Guide

Traits: Knowledge, Wisdom and Agility

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

Pairing Logic: Quartermasters and Shipwrights form the foundation of maritime commerce: one outfitting voyages with supplies, the other building vessels worthy of those journeys. Quartermasters coordinate the provisioning that keeps Shipwright-built vessels operating across vast distances. Shipwrights design vessels that meet Quartermaster logistical requirements for long voyages. A Quartermaster's logistics expertise helps Shipwrights design efficient cargo holds and storage systems. A Shipwright's mastery ensures vessels can actually carry the supplies Quartermasters organize. Both understand that neither merchant navy nor exploration depends on vessel alone. Together they create maritime systems where excellent ships meet flawless provisioning to enable expeditions that change the world.

2. Navigator

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Chart precise courses through air and sea, reading celestial bodies, weather patterns, and maritime landmarks. Gain bonuses to navigation and wayfinding checks in aquatic and aerial environments. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Pirate

Trait Bonus: Courage

Master the tactics of boarding and capturing vessels. Specialize in raiding ships for treasure and theft, or commandeering vessels for your own use through combat and cunning. Courage trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Port Authority

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Understand harbor operations, docking procedures, and maritime law. Navigate bureaucracy, negotiate with port authorities, and manage ship operations in major ports. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Ship Maintenance

Trait Bonus: Focus

Maintain and repair vessels in peak condition. Diagnose hull integrity, manage rigging, and perform critical repairs to keep ships seaworthy during long voyages. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Smuggler

Master the art of concealment and covert maritime operations. Hide contraband, avoid naval inspection, and operate illicit trade routes with skill and discretion. Dexterity trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Storm Sailing

Navigate treacherous waters and turbulent skies with masterful skill. Command vessels through storms, read weather patterns, and guide crews through the most dangerous maritime conditions. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

Quartermaster Philosophy

The Quartermaster draws power from mastery of seas and skies, commanding vessels and navigating the boundless horizons. These talented mariners understand wind, wave, and weather as intimately as land-dwellers know forests and mountains. A true Quartermaster is as comfortable commanding a merchant vessel as commanding a pirate ship, and can read the sea like others read a map.
