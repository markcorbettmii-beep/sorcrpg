ii. Artificer

Masters of craftsmanship and magical innovation. Artificers create wondrous devices and artifacts through meticulous design and arcane enhancement.

Profession Disposition (NPC): Craftmaster

Traits: Knowledge, Focus and Determination

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

a. Blueprinting

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master the art of detailed design and planning. Create precise blueprints and schematics for devices, buildings, and magical artifacts. Your designs are clear, functional, and beautiful. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Arcane Crafting

Trait Bonus: Focus

Channel magical energy into crafted items during creation. Imbue objects with magical properties and enchantments through meticulous craftsmanship. Your artifacts retain magical potency far longer than ordinary creations. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Precision Engineering

Trait Bonus: Focus

Execute perfectly measured and balanced mechanical work with exceptional accuracy. Create complex machinery with tight tolerances and intricate mechanisms. Your devices operate smoothly and reliably even under stress. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Component Harvesting

Trait Bonus: Perception

Identify and extract usable components from broken items, remains, and natural sources. Salvage materials that others would discard and prepare them for reuse. Transform waste into valuable crafting resources. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Innovation

Trait Bonus: Determination

Push the boundaries of known craftsmanship and invent entirely new device types. Experiment with untested combinations of materials and magical forces. Your creations sometimes possess unexpected properties or capabilities beyond their intended design. Determination trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Magitech Integration

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Seamlessly blend magical and technological systems into unified devices. Understand how arcane forces and mechanical principles can complement each other. Create hybrid devices that leverage both magic and engineering. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Rapid Assembly

Trait Bonus: Agility

Work with exceptional speed without sacrificing quality or precision. Assemble and modify devices quickly in urgent situations. Your practiced hands can construct, repair, and modify items faster than most craftspeople could manage. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
