Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 talent trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

X. Hauler

Masters of logistics and transportation. Haulers excel at moving goods, managing cargo, and ensuring safe delivery across any terrain.

NPC Disposition: Quartermaster

Traits: Strength, Agility and Resolve

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

1. Load Management

Trait Bonus: Strength

Organize and secure cargo efficiently, maximizing capacity while ensuring items remain protected during transport. Pack items strategically to distribute weight evenly and prevent damage. Your knowledge of stacking and securing ensures nothing is lost or broken. Strength trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Route Planning

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Calculate optimal routes considering terrain, weather, and hazards. Understand supply chains and know the best paths for delivering goods quickly and safely. Your planned routes save time and reduce risk of ambush or environmental disaster. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Heavy Lifting

Trait Bonus: Grit

Develop exceptional strength for moving and handling massive weights. Lift, carry, and move heavy objects that would overwhelm ordinary people. Your powerful frame and technique allow you to handle cargo others cannot. Grit trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Beast Handling

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Work effectively with draft animals and beasts of burden. Understand their needs, train them for hauling, and keep them healthy during long journeys. Your partnership with animals makes transportation efficient and reliable. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Navigation

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Develop an intuitive sense for direction and location, even in unfamiliar territory. Read landmarks, weather signs, and subtle environmental clues to maintain your course. Your natural sense of direction ensures you rarely get lost. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Vehicle Mastery

Trait Bonus: Focus

Master the operation of wagons, carts, and transport vehicles in any condition. Maintain vehicles in excellent working order and coax maximum performance from them. Your skill with vehicles means faster journeys and better cargo protection. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Endurance

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Build exceptional stamina and resilience for long journeys across harsh terrain. Withstand fatigue, hunger, and hardship that would break ordinary travelers. Your body adapted to rigorous hauling schedules and environmental extremes. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
