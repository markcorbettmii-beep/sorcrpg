Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Representative

Paired Profession: TBA

Masters of negotiation, political influence, and knowledge exchange.

NPC Disposition: Scribe

Traits: Charisma, Intuition and Knowledge

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

1. Herald

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Inspire through eloquent communication and powerful rhetoric. Gain bonuses to persuasion and public speaking engagements. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Negotiator

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Master the art of negotiation and deal-making. Understand what others want and find mutually beneficial agreements. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Diplomat

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Navigate complex political situations with grace and diplomacy. Build alliances and defuse conflicts through skilled communication. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Linguistics

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master multiple languages and understand cultural communication styles. Speak, read, and write in various tongues with fluency. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Etiquette

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master proper social protocols, customs, and formal behaviors. Navigate high society with confidence and avoid social missteps. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Trade Master

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Establish and manage trade networks and commercial relationships. Understand market dynamics and establish profitable trading routes. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Ambassador

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Represent groups and interests to others with authority and credibility. Build trust and secure agreements between parties. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
