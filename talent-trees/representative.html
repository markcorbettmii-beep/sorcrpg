Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=12} talent trees is paired with a corresponding character profession, offering thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Representative Talent Tree

Masters of diplomacy, knowledge, and political influence. Expert negotiators and scholars with deep understanding of history, religion, and espionage.

†Associated Profession - Diplomat

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Intellect, Wisdom, Charisma

a. Religion

Understand religious practices, doctrines, and theological concepts across cultures. Gain bonuses to rolls involving religious knowledge, rituals, and spiritual matters.

b. Diplomacy

Master the art of negotiation and political maneuvering. Navigate complex social hierarchies, broker peace between factions, and influence decisions through tact and charm.

c. History

Possess deep knowledge of historical events, civilizations, and significant figures. Recall details about past occurrences and understand historical context to predict future patterns.

d. Arcana

Master magical theory and understanding of the arcane arts. Gain bonuses to rolls involving magical knowledge, identifying magical effects, and understanding magical lore and theory.

e. Espionage

Master the art of gathering intelligence and uncovering secrets. Gain expertise in surveillance, coded communications, and covert operations to extract sensitive information.

f. Mentor

Share your knowledge and wisdom with others, accelerating their growth. Provide guidance that helps allies improve their skills and understanding more rapidly through your teaching.

g. Herald (†Profession Bonus†)

Your skill in communication, representation, and influence is legendary. You can speak with authority on behalf of groups, negotiate complex political arrangements, and inspire others through powerful oratory. You gain a bonus† to all Diplomat checks (see Professions).
