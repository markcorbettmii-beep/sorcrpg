Spaceman Talent Tree

Explorers of the void and masters of zero-gravity environments. Spacemen navigate the cosmos and survive in the vacuum of space.

†Associated Profession - TBD

†Profession Disposition - TBD

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Traits: Intellect, Determination, Focus

a. Zero-Gravity Adaptation

Trait Bonus: Agility

Your body has adapted to movement in zero-gravity environments, allowing natural and efficient motion where others would flounder. Gain bonuses to movement and combat rolls in weightless environments. Agility trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

b. Void Navigation

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master the art of navigating through the emptiness of space using celestial markers and stellar navigation. Plot courses between distant worlds and understand the mechanics of cosmic travel. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

c. Vacuum Survival

Trait Bonus: Resolve

Your physiology or training allows you to survive brief exposure to the vacuum of space. Hold your breath longer than normal and resist the effects of decompression. Remain conscious and functional in hostile void environments. Resolve trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

d. Suit Mastery

Trait Bonus: Focus

You understand spacesuits and pressure suits intimately, maintaining them and using them to their fullest potential. Gain bonuses when working with environmental suits and maximize their protective capabilities. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

e. Microgravity Mechanics

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Understand the physics of movement and mechanics in low or zero-gravity environments. Calculate trajectories, manage momentum, and predict movement in weightless conditions. Apply this knowledge to solve complex spatial problems. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

f. Cosmic Awareness

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Develop an intuitive sense for cosmic phenomena, stellar radiation, and spatial hazards. Sense approaching danger from radiation storms, meteor fields, or gravitational anomalies. Your cosmic sensitivity provides early warning of threats in space. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

g. Vessel Operation

Trait Bonus: Focus

Master the operation of spacecraft and orbital vessels, understanding their systems and capabilities. Pilot ships through asteroid fields, navigate dangerous space routes, and perform emergency procedures. Your expertise makes you invaluable at the helm of any spacecraft. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

h. Cosmic Explorer (†Profession Bonus†)

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Your mastery of space exploration and cosmic navigation is legendary throughout the void. You understand the mysteries of the cosmos and can lead expeditions into uncharted regions of space. Map previously unknown celestial territories and discover wonders beyond known space. You gain a bonus† to all profession checks related to space exploration and cosmic discovery. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
