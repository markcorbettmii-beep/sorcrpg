Treasure Hunter

Paired Profession: Gem Crafter

Experts in locating valuable treasures, identifying precious gems, and uncovering hidden wealth.

Traits: Perception, Knowledge, Intuition

Pairing Logic: Treasure Hunters and Gem Crafters form the complete luxury economy: one discovering raw gems and valuables, the other transforming rough finds into legendary treasures. Treasure Hunters find the raw materials that Gem Crafters need; Gem Crafters create the prestige that drives Treasure Hunters' prospecting. A Treasure Hunter's field experience teaches Gem Crafters which stones possess real value beyond surface beauty. A Gem Crafter's reputation and auction success motivates Treasure Hunters to seek even greater discoveries. Both understand that raw materials matter less than refinement and presentation. Together they create a complete system where discovery feeds artistry, and masterwork craftsmanship creates markets for discovery.

1. Appraisal

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Assess the value and authenticity of gems, precious metals, and artifacts with expert precision. Identify quality, rarity, and market value at a glance. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Gem Identification

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Identify gemstones by type, quality, and origin. Understand the properties and characteristics of precious and semi-precious stones. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Treasure Sense

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Develop an intuitive sense for the presence of valuable treasures nearby. Gain bonuses to perception checks when searching for hidden wealth or detecting magical auras of valuable items. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Detect Forgery

Trait Bonus: Perception

Identify counterfeit gems, fraudulent artifacts, and false treasures through close examination. Detect forgeries and imitations with expert scrutiny. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Excavation

Trait Bonus: Strength

Master the techniques of careful excavation and extraction of treasures from difficult locations. Safely recover valuable items from ruins, tombs, and hidden caches without damage. Strength trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Treasure Mapping

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Decipher treasure maps, cryptic clues, and ancient records that lead to hidden wealth. Understand symbols and codes used by historical treasure hoarders. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Expert Negotiation

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Negotiate favorable prices for treasures and gems, securing the best deals in any market. Your reputation as a knowledgeable collector opens doors and gains respect from merchants and collectors. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
