Talents represent unique abilities your character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the {NUM_TALENT_TREES=12} talent trees is paired with a corresponding character profession, offering thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Field Medic Talent Tree

Specialists in medicine, healing, and treatment of injuries and ailments. Expert diagnosticians and healers who save lives through medical knowledge and practice.

†Associated Profession - Doctor

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Attributes: Intellect, Wisdom, Focus

a. First Aid

Provide immediate medical treatment to stabilize injured allies. Apply bandages, stop bleeding, and perform emergency procedures to keep allies conscious and fighting during battle.

b. Diagnosis

Identify illnesses, diseases, and medical conditions through careful examination and analysis. Gain bonuses to rolls for determining the cause and severity of afflictions affecting patients.

c. Anatomy

Possess deep knowledge of body structure and function. Understand vital organs, weak points, and how to treat injuries effectively. Gain bonuses to surgical procedures and medical interventions.

d. Herbalism

Identify and cultivate medicinal plants and herbs. Create healing potions, remedies, and herbal treatments that accelerate recovery and treat various ailments naturally.

e. Triage

Quickly assess multiple injured allies and prioritize treatment based on severity. Efficiently allocate resources and determine who needs immediate attention to save the most lives.

f. Disease Knowledge

Understand diseases, plagues, and infections. Gain bonuses to rolls for treating disease, identifying symptoms, and preventing spread of contagious ailments. Know remedies and treatments for known diseases.

g. Poison Antidotes

Identify poisons and toxins, then create or administer effective antidotes and countermeasures. Gain bonuses to rolls for treating poisoning and understanding toxins.

h. Doctor (†Profession Bonus†)

Your medical expertise is unparalleled. You can diagnose any ailment, perform complex surgical procedures, and save lives through your mastery of medicine and healing arts. You gain a bonus† to all Doctor checks (see Professions).
