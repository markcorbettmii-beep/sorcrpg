Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the 23 Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

VIII. Field Medic

Paired Profession: Cook

Specialists in medicine, healing, and treatment of injuries and ailments. Expert diagnosticians and healers who save lives through medical knowledge and practice.

NPC Disposition: Innkeeper

Traits: Knowledge, Wisdom and Focus

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

Pairing Logic: Field Medics heal trauma and illness; Cooks sustain through nutrition. Both understand that survival depends on maintaining body vitality. Field Medics need nutritious food to help patients recover; Cooks need medics to treat injured workers and diners. A Medic's knowledge of nutrition enhances Cook recipes for healing. A Cook's nutritional expertise complements Medic treatment regimens. Together they create comprehensive wellness: Cooks prevent illness through proper nutrition, Medics cure it through treatment. This symbiosis keeps communities healthy and strong.

1. First Aid

Trait Bonus: Focus

Provide immediate medical treatment to stabilize injured allies. Apply bandages, stop bleeding, and perform emergency procedures to keep allies conscious and fighting during battle. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Diagnosis

Trait Bonus: Intellect

Identify illnesses, diseases, and medical conditions through careful examination and analysis. Gain bonuses to rolls for determining the cause and severity of afflictions affecting patients. Intellect trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Anatomy

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Possess deep knowledge of body structure and function. Understand vital organs, weak points, and how to treat injuries effectively. Gain bonuses to surgical procedures and medical interventions. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Triage

Trait Bonus: Focus

Quickly assess multiple injured allies and prioritize treatment based on severity. Efficiently allocate resources and determine who needs immediate attention to save the most lives. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Disease Knowledge

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Understand diseases, plagues, and infections. Gain bonuses to rolls for treating disease, identifying symptoms, and preventing spread of contagious ailments. Know remedies and treatments for known diseases. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Poison Antidotes

Trait Bonus: Intellect

Identify poisons and toxins, then create or administer effective antidotes and countermeasures. Gain bonuses to rolls for treating poisoning and understanding toxins. Intellect trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Healer Elite

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Master the art of healing and become a legendary medical practitioner. Your treatments save lives that would otherwise be lost. Gain bonuses to advanced medical procedures and surgical interventions. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
