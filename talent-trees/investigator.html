XII. Investigator

Masters of inquiry and discovery. Investigators uncover hidden truths through interviews, research, and careful analysis of clues.

Profession Disposition (NPC): Scholar

Traits: Knowledge, Perception and Determination

Every Talent Tree grants access to specialized NPC Disposition services, providing unique items, information, and trade opportunities.

1. Interrogation

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Interview subjects skillfully to extract information. Read subtle cues in voice, body language, and reactions to detect deception. Your questions lead subjects to reveal secrets they intended to keep hidden. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

2. Deduction

Trait Bonus: Knowledge

Analyze facts and evidence to reach logical conclusions that others miss. Connect disparate clues into coherent patterns and theories. Your deductive reasoning often solves cases that seemed unsolvable. Knowledge trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

3. Scene Analysis

Trait Bonus: Perception

Examine locations thoroughly to extract meaningful evidence from small details. Identify signs of struggle, tampering, or deception that others overlook. Your systematic analysis of scenes reveals the truth. Perception trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

4. Background Research

Trait Bonus: Focus

Dig through records, documents, and archives to uncover relevant history and connections. Access information networks and build comprehensive profiles. Your thorough research provides essential context for investigations. Focus trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

5. Motive Analysis

Trait Bonus: Intuition

Understand human nature and predict behavior based on motives and psychology. Identify who had reason to commit crimes or cause harm. Your insight into motivations often points directly to the guilty party. Intuition trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

6. Surveillance

Trait Bonus: Vigilance

Monitor subjects and locations for extended periods without being detected. Notice suspicious activity and track patterns of behavior. Your surveillance work provides evidence and confirms suspicions. Vigilance trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.

7. Undercover Work

Trait Bonus: Charisma

Adopt convincing cover identities and maintain deception for investigation purposes. Infiltrate groups and organizations to observe from within. Your undercover personas are convincing enough to fool even cautious targets. Charisma trait scores stack with this talent when used actively.
