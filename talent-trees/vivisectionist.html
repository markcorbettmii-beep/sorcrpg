Talents represent unique abilities your Character acquires and develops as they advance. Each of the Talent Trees offers thematic abilities and specialized advantages that shape your playstyle.

Vivisectionist

Paired Profession: Cyberneticist

Masters of surgical precision, biological modification, and anatomical expertise who enhance organisms through meticulous alteration and scientific innovation.

NPC Disposition: Surgeon

Traits: Knowledge, Focus, Wit

Pairing Logic: Vivisectionists and Cyberneticists both perfect living beings through radical transformation: one through surgical artistry, the other through technological integration. Vivisectionists provide the surgical precision that allows safe cybernetic integration without rejecting foreign systems. Cyberneticists provide technological capabilities that Vivisectionists alone could never achieve. A Vivisectionist's anatomical mastery ensures cyberware integrates seamlessly with living tissue. A Cyberneticist's innovation expands the possibilities beyond purely biological enhancement. Both see the body as a canvas for improvement and transcendence. Both understand that ethics and respect matter in transformation work. Together they create enhanced beings that represent the synthesis of flesh and technology, achieving potentials impossible through either discipline alone.
