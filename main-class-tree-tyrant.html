Tyrant (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Agents of pure destruction who revel in combat for its own sake, Tyrants channel rage, dominance, and shock-and-awe into relentless offensive pressure, whether through twin weapons blurring faster than defense can respond, a two-handed weapon that turns armor into scrap, or a voice alone that breaks enemy formations before a single blade is drawn.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Alignment: Path and Branch Dependent

Higher Power: Path Dependent (see Path below)

Tyrant's Paths individually restrict by alignment from LVL 4 on, see each Path above for its specific Alignment.

Refer to Races for details on favored and averse paths and branches.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Warlord's Dominance, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Reign of Ruin, Once Per Day the Tyrant unleashes overwhelming force, dealing physical damage equal to twice their Strength SCR to one target and knocking them prone.

Extra 1d6 + LVL physical damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Tyrants can Flourish their weapon that lowers their target's Defense at a number equal to their Strength SCR.

Tyrants get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Tyrants get +4 plus their STRENGTH SCR on Intimidation checks.

Tyrant Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Berserker - A rage-fueled Tyrant who abandons caution entirely for overwhelming, relentless offense.
  a. Blitzer - A Berserker who closes distance in a single, unstoppable burst.
  b. Fury - A Berserker who abandons all restraint the longer a fight goes on.
  c. Shredder - A Berserker whose rapid, relentless strikes tear through any defense.
ii. Demolisher - A Tyrant who LVLs anything in their path with sheer, brutal force.
  a. Bone Crusher - A Demolisher whose blows are built to shatter, not just wound.
  b. Brute - A Demolisher who relies on raw physical force above all else.
  c. Rampager - A Demolisher who tears through everything in a straight, unstoppable line.
iii. War Cryer - A Tyrant whose voice alone can break enemy morale, rallying allies and terrifying foes in the same breath.
  a. Celestial - A War Cryer whose voice carries a weight that feels almost otherworldly.
  b. Dagger Dancer - A War Cryer who weaves blade-work into every cry, striking between words.
  c. Paincaster - A War Cryer who turns suffering itself into a weapon against enemy morale.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Light or Medium (Studded Leather, Light Chain).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Tyrant's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
