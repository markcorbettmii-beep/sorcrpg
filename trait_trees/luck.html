Luck Trait Tree

Description: Luck represents a character’s uncanny knack for favorable outcomes, whether in craftsmanship, combat, interaction, or exploration. It offers limited chances to reroll, nudge fate, or receive unexpected windfalls, but never guarantees success and is rarely reliable in succession.

Application: Bonus re-rolls, second-chance outcomes, or beneficial coincidences on craftsmanship, combat, and interactive rolls.
Defensive/Offensive: May avoid misfortune or seize rare opportunities, but with diminishing reliability.
ATT: Alignment varies (DM/Player chooses per campaign or character concept)
