Toughness (res) Trait Tree

Description: Physical durability and toughness, enabling characters to stand firm and withstand penetrating and physical attacks without armor.

Application: Bonus to absorbing initial damage before ongoing effects, resist penetrating attacks, and withstand physical assaults.
Defensive: Increased durability against physical harm.
