Agility Trait Buffs Flow Chart

  • Offense (Enhances martial/ranged attack precision and damage)
    • Hit
      • Laser Focus: Slightly increases accuracy against moving targets.
      • Rapid Aim: Reduces chance of attack missing due to movement.
      • Steady Hand: Improves attack stability during rapid fire.
      • Target Lock: Enhances hit chance when attacking from cover.
      • Focus Burst: Temporarily boosts accuracy after quick repositioning.
    • Crit
      • Eye for Weakness: Slightly increases critical hit chance on precise shots.
      • Deadeye Precision: Boosts crit rate when targeting exposed areas.
      • Opportunist: Slight chance to add extra critical damage after successful hits.
      • Focus Shot: Critical chance increases after quick repositioning.
      • Sharp Senses: Slightly increases crit chance when enemies are distracted.
    • Damage
      • Piercing Edge: Slightly increases damage against armored targets.
      • Explosive Impact: Adds minor damage burst on hit.
      • Poison Coating: Adds poison damage over time.
      • Sharp Blades: Slight damage boost with cutting weapons.
      • Forceful Shot: Adds minor knockback effect.
  • Defense (Passive buffs against martial ranged attacks)
    • Projectile Resistance
      • Bulwark Stance: Slight passive reduction in damage from ranged attacks.
      • Intercepting Cover: Passive bonus reducing damage when behind cover from ranged enemies.
      • Counterfire: Slight chance to deflect or reduce damage from ranged hits.
      • Sharpened Reflexes: Slight passive increase in evading ranged projectiles.
      • Armor Plating: Minor passive damage reduction against ranged attacks.
    • Dodge & Evasion
      • Quick Reflexes: Increased chance to dodge ranged projectiles.
      • Rapid Movement: Better positioning to avoid ranged strikes.
      • Cover Tactics: Passive bonus to evade shots when near cover.
      • Silent Step: Slight chance to avoid detection and avoid triggering ranged attacks.
      • Reactive Dodge: Chance to evade after detecting incoming fire.
    • Resist Effects
      • Stability: Passive resistance to effects like slowing or staggering from ranged attacks.
      • Fortified Skin: Slight resistance to poison or toxin effects from certain projectiles.
      • Focus Reflexes: Slight passive resistance to disorienting effects caused by ranged attacks.
      • Darkening Veil: Slight passive resistance to visual impairments from ranged magic or effects.
      • Damage Dampening: Reduces the impact of ranged damage over time.
  • Mobility (Passive buffs for positioning, falling, and hampering enemies)
    • Speed & Positioning
      • Sprint Advantage: Slight passive increase in quick repositioning speed.
      • Quickstep: Reduces time to change position in combat.
      • Haste: Slight passive bonus to overall movement speed.
      • Momentum: Restores movement speed after sudden stops.
      • Rush: Passive bonus to quickly close distance or escape.
    • Falling & Terrain Control
      • Soft Landing: Passive reduction in fall damage and stun from high drops.
      • Climber's Instinct: Slight passive bonus to maintaining footing on uneven terrain.
      • Wall Runner: Slightly reduces fall damage when vaulting or running along walls.
      • Obstructed Path: Slight passive chance to hinder enemies' movement by quick repositioning.
      • Quick Vault: Reduces fall impact when vaulting over obstacles or terrain.
    • Hampering Enemies
      • Disruptive Movement: Slight passive chance to slow enemies' ranged attacks or repositioning.
      • Knockback Tactics: Minor passive chance to push enemies back when in close combat.
      • Cover Disruption: Slight passive chance to make enemies' ranged attacks less accurate.
      • Unpredictable Footwork: Slight chance to cause enemies to miss their shot or lose target.
      • Silent Exit: Passive ability that hampers enemy pursuit after disengaging.
