Agility Trait Buffs Flow Chart

  • Offense (Enhances martial/ranged attack precision and damage)
    • Hit
      • Laser Focus: Slightly increases accuracy against moving targets.
      • Rapid Aim: Reduces chance of attack missing due to movement.
      • Steady Hand: Improves attack stability during rapid fire.
      • Target Lock: Enhances hit chance when attacking from cover.
      • Focus Burst: Temporarily boosts accuracy after quick repositioning.
    • Crit
      • Eye for Weakness: Slightly increases critical hit chance on precise shots.
      • Deadeye Precision: Boosts crit rate when targeting exposed areas.
      • Opportunist: Slight chance to add extra critical damage after successful hits.
      • Focus Shot: Critical chance increases after quick repositioning.
      • Sharp Senses: Slightly increases crit chance when enemies are distracted.
    • Damage
      • Piercing Edge: Slightly increases damage against armored targets.
      • Explosive Impact: Adds minor damage burst on hit.
      • Poison Coating: Adds poison damage over time.
      • Sharp Blades: Slight damage boost with cutting weapons.
      • Forceful Shot: Adds minor knockback effect.
  • Defense (Improves evasion, resistance, and damage mitigation)
    • Resistances
      • Fire Ward: Slight passive resistance to fire damage.
      • Frost Shield: Reduces damage from cold attacks.
      • Poison Buffer: Slight resistance to poison effects.
      • Dark Veil: Passive resistance to dark magic damage.
      • Physical Guard: Slight reduction in physical damage taken.
    • Shielding
      • Damage Absorption: Slight damage reduction from incoming attacks.
      • Barrier Aura: Reduces damage from ranged attacks.
      • Reinforced Skin: Adds minor damage mitigation during combat.
      • Fortify: Passive bonus that lessens impact of heavy blows.
      • Damage Buffer: Slight chance to absorb part of damage.
    • Avoidance
      • Evasion Boost: Slightly increases chance to dodge attacks.
      • Parry Readiness: Slight bonus to block incoming melee or ranged strikes.
      • Dodge Reflex: Improves movement to avoid hits.
      • Counter Momentum: Chance to counterattack after evading.
      • Deflect: Passive chance to deflect incoming projectiles or melee strikes.
  • Mobility (Enhances positioning, quick movement, and falling damage mitigation)
    • Speed
      • Sprint Boost: Slightly increases movement speed in combat.
      • Quickstep: Reduces time to change positions.
      • Haste: Slightly boosts overall movement rate.
      • Dash: Temporarily grants faster movement for repositioning.
      • Momentum: Restores speed after quick movement.
    • Evasion
      • Roll Advantage: Slight passive chance to evade attacks during movement.
      • Leap: Small bonus to avoid ground hazards or obstacles.
      • Vault: Reduces fall damage when vaulting over obstacles.
      • Slide: Slightly reduces fall impact when sliding down slopes.
      • Backflip: Passive chance to avoid attacks when flipping away.
    • Control
      • Climb Aid: Slight bonus to positioning on vertical surfaces.
      • Wall Run: Temporarily extends movement range along walls.
      • Jump Boost: Slightly reduces fall damage and increases jump height.
      • Teleportation Prep: Passive advantage reducing fall impact after quick teleportation.
      • Precise Landing: Reduces fall damage and prevents fall stun.
