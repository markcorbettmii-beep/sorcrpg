Agility (phy) Trait Tree

Agility Tree Description

Agility represents quickness, balance, and speed. It helps evade ranged attacks and speed-based effects, and enhances offensive precision with ranged attacks.

Application Resistance to martial ranged attacks, balance disruptions, and speed-based effects. Bonus to martial ranged attack rolls, damage, and critical hits.
