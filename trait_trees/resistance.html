Resistance (res) Trait Tree

Description: Represents magical and spiritual resolve, allowing characters to withstand and lessen the effects of supernatural forces. Resistance bolsters defenses against elemental onslaughts, charming essence, and divine intervention.

Application: Bonus to resist spell damage, magical effects, and mitigation checks.
Defensive: Reduces damage and effects from spells, enchantments, and supernatural assaults.
ATT: Resilience
