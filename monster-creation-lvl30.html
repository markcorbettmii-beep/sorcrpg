The character begins with three negative traits. Before any growth can happen in Wit, Deceit, and Piety, points must first be spent just climbing back to zero. Climbing out of negatives costs the same 2 points per score point.

Wit        2 points × 2 = 4 points just to reach 0

Deceit   2 points × 2 = 4 points just to reach 0

Piety      2 points × 1 = 2 points just to reach 0

Total: 10 points spent before any real growth in those traits
