How Talent Trees Work & Full Tree Reference

How Talent Trees Work

A Talent Tree is a themed bundle of individual talents, not a single thing a character "has." Opening a tree and raising a talent inside it are two separate purchases: REM Points first open the tree's current Proficiency Tier, unlocking every talent available at that tier for purchase, further REM Points then raise the score of whichever individual talents a character actually wants, one at a time. Opening a tier doesn't hand over everything inside it, talents left unraised sit at 0, purchasable, not owned. Each Talent Tree grants specialized gear, a Kit or Backpack, that a character must carry to effectively use whichever talents they've actually learned. See Talents (TST), Basic Rules pg. 2.

Race vs. Class Bonuses

A Race's favored Talent Tree grants +1 to talent rolls within that tree. A Class's favorite Talent Tree instead waives the cost to open that tree, a different mechanism, not a bigger or smaller version of the same bonus.
