Fighter (Playable in Starter Set)

The most adaptable martial class in Essentia, Fighters are defined by mastery of raw combat fundamentals, developing unmatched weapon mastery through sheer repetition and battlefield Experience (XP) that no trained academic can replicate, making them the most reliable class in sustained combat regardless of the situation they find themselves in.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Combat Focus, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Battle-Forged, Once Per Day the Fighter draws on raw battle instinct, gaining a bonus to Hit and Damage equal to their Grit SCR for the rest of the encounter.

Extra 1d6 + LVL physical damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Fighters can Flourish their weapon that lowers their target's Defense at a number equal to their Strength SCR.

Fighters get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Fighters get +4 plus their STR SCR on Athletics checks.

Fighter Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Brawler - An unarmed Fighter who relies on fists and raw grit over steel.
  a. Bruiser - A Brawler who wears down enemies through sheer, relentless punishment.
  b. Enforcer - A Brawler who intimidates as much as strikes, breaking enemy resolve with brute presence.
  c. Rager - A Brawler who fights harder the more damage they take.
ii. Hero - A versatile Fighter who takes up any cause, adapting their fighting style to whatever the moment demands.
  a. Combatant - A Hero who masters the fundamentals of every fighting style equally well.
  b. Merc - A Hero who fights for coin and contract, adapting to whatever the job demands.
  c. Vigilante - A Hero who takes justice into their own hands, striking wherever it's needed most.
iii. Juggernaut - A heavy-hitting Fighter who wades into the thick of battle, built to take and deal punishment in equal measure.
  a. Reaver - A Juggernaut who tears through enemy lines with unstoppable forward momentum.
  b. Rumbler - A Juggernaut whose ground-shaking strikes stagger everything nearby.
  c. Vanguard - A Juggernaut who leads the charge, absorbing the first and heaviest blows.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Versatile (varies by Path).

Note: Grade and Type still apply to a Fighter's Armaments the same as any other class, this Path's street-clothes framing just emphasizes a class that doesn't lean on traditional Armaments, it isn't an exception to the Grade & Type system itself.

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Fighter's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
