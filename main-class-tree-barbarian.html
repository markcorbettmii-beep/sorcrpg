Barbarian (Playable in Starter Set)

Fierce and relentless hunters who draw power from primal instinct and raw physical dominance, Barbarians trade refinement for devastation, excelling in brutal melee combat across every environment from frozen mountain peaks to untamed wild plains, where rage is a resource and restraint is a weakness.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Blood Frenzy, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Barbzerker, Once Per Day the Barbarian roars when reaches half of their Life they get an extra attack per turn.

When a barbarian reaches 0 Life, they instantly get life equal to their Constitution Trait SCR, and get 3 attacks at that turn.

Extra 1d6 + LVL damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Barbarians can

Flourish a 2h Melee weapon that lowers their target's PROTS at a number equal to their Athletics SCR.

Barbarians get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Barbarians get +4 plus their STR SCR on Intimidation checks.

Barbarian Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Cimmerian - A versatile Barbarian who adapts weapon and stance to the fight at hand, as comfortable dual-wielding as bearing a single blade.
  a. Ghost - A stealth-focused Cimmerian who strikes from hiding and vanishes before the counter.
  b. Nomad - A mounted raider who strikes fast and rides on.
  c. Woodwose - A wild-born survivalist who needs neither shelter nor armor to endure.
ii. Destroyer - A brute-force Barbarian who commits fully to a single devastating two-handed weapon, trading defense for maximum damage.
  a. Bloodrager - A dual-wielding berserker who trades caution for reckless momentum.
  b. Frenzied - An armor-shedding berserker who trades protection entirely for speed and fury.
  c. Mauler - A heavy-weapon specialist who crushes armor and bone alike.
iii. Mountaineer - A ranged-focused Barbarian who fights from a distance with spear or bow, mastering trap-craft and rugged terrain.
  a. Amazon - A spear-and-bow specialist skilled in trap-craft and wilderness survival.
  b. Beast Master - A Barbarian bonded to a Companion, fighting as one with a beast ally.
  c. Predator - A pursuit specialist who runs prey to ground before it knows it's being hunted.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Light (Fur, Leather).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Barbarian's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
