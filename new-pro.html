A. Crafting Professions

Cook (Crafting)

Category: Crafting

Masters of culinary arts and nourishment. Cooks prepare meals, craft recipes, and sustain adventurers through food that heals and fortifies.

Full Profession

NPC Synergies: Innkeeper, Vendor

Synergy Logic: Inns need consistent food service; Cooks need reliable kitchens and steady clientele. Innkeepers and Cooks anchor each other's success through food quality and hospitality reputation. Vendors distribute preserved foods and specialty meals Cooks produce, creating retail presence beyond one kitchen. Cooks gain market reach without managing shops; Vendors gain high-margin food products that build customer loyalty.

A. Commerce Professions (NPC)

Auctioneer (Commerce)

Category: Commerce

Masters of valuation and sales. Auctioneers broker high-value items to elite buyers, creating competitive bidding that maximizes prices and prestige.

Full Profession

Synergies: Gem Crafter, Sculptor

Synergy Logic: Auctioneers create competitive bidding environments that drive luxury prices upward. Gem Crafters produce premium jewelry; Auctioneers maximize profit through elite client networks. Sculptors gain visibility at galas where wealthy patrons commission masterworks. Auctioneers build reputation on handling exceptional pieces; craftspeople gain access to buyers who value quality and pay premium prices.
