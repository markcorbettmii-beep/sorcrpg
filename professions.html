Professions

Professions represent specialized skills and expertise your character develops through focused practice and dedication. Each of the {NUM_PROFESSIONS=28} player professions is paired with a corresponding talent tree, forming a symbiotic relationship where talents provide the foundational abilities and professions deepen specialization and expertise. Additionally, each talent tree is also paired with a unique Profession Disposition, representing the specialized roles and expertise of NPCs that interact directly with your character. Professions grant bonuses when a character invests talent points into the associated talent tree's profession bonus talent, creating a direct mechanical link between talent development and professional mastery. By selecting complementary talents and professions, characters can develop deep expertise in their chosen fields and excel at tasks related to their professional focus.

†Profession Bonus†: Each talent tree contains a profession bonus talent that grants bonuses to all checks related to its associated profession when the talent is advanced.

What is a Profession Disposition?

A Profession Disposition is a specialized NPC role that directly interacts with player characters. Each Profession Disposition is paired with a talent tree and represents a specific type of NPC expertise that characters will encounter and engage with in the game world. For example, an Investigator character might interact with a Guard (Profession Disposition paired with Detective), negotiate with a Scribe (paired with Representative), or seek help from an Innkeeper (paired with Field Medic). Profession Dispositions allow Game Masters to create consistent, thematic NPC interactions that complement player character professions and create meaningful mechanical and narrative connections throughout the game.

Playable Professions

I. Alchemist

Transmutes substances into potions, explosives, and elixirs with unpredictable effects.

a. Potion Crafting: Master the creation of potions with specific effects, understanding ingredient combinations and brewing techniques.

b. Explosive Synthesis: Craft volatile compounds and explosives for combat or demolition purposes.

c. Transmutation: Transform substances into different forms or properties through alchemical processes.

II. Apothecary

Creates healing tinctures, restorative salves, and potions using herbs and natural ingredients.

a. Herbal Knowledge: Identify and harvest medicinal herbs, understanding their properties and potency.

b. Tincture Creation: Brew healing tinctures and tonics for various ailments and conditions.

c. Salve Preparation: Craft restorative salves and ointments for wound treatment and healing.

III. Archeologist

Experts in ancient artifacts, historical sites, and preservation of knowledge. Archeologists unlock the secrets of civilizations past.

a. Artifact Identification: Recognize and date ancient artifacts, understanding their cultural significance and origin.

b. Site Excavation: Carefully excavate historical sites while preserving context and artifacts.

c. Historical Analysis: Interpret findings to understand past civilizations, their knowledge, and their practices.

IV. Artificer

Combines magic and technology to craft powerful items and unravel mechanisms.

a. Item Enchantment: Imbue items with magical properties and enhancements.

b. Mechanism Analysis: Understand and disassemble complex magical and mechanical devices.

c. Magitech Crafting: Create hybrid items combining magical and technological elements.

V. Blacksmith

Craftspeople skilled in metalworking, weapon forging, and armor creation. Blacksmiths forge the tools and weapons of legend.

a. Weapon Forging: Craft weapons with superior quality, balance, and durability for combat.

b. Armor Crafting: Design and create protective armor tailored to fit and defend.

c. Tool Smithing: Forge high-quality tools and implements for various professions and purposes.

VI. Body Artist

Designs tattoos and piercings infused with magical or technological enhancements.

a. Tattoo Design: Create magical tattoos that enhance abilities or provide ongoing effects.

b. Piercing Enhancement: Craft piercing jewelry infused with magical or technological properties.

c. Body Modification: Apply permanent enhancements to the body through artistic means.

VII. Cook

Masters of culinary arts and food preparation. Cooks understand nutrition, flavor, and the art of sustaining adventurers through nourishment.

a. Baker: Master the craft of baking breads, pastries, and other baked goods. Create sustaining provisions with proper storage properties for long journeys.

b. Brewer: Craft fermented beverages including ales, meads, and specialized potions. Understand fermentation processes and flavor profiles for various occasions.

c. Butcher: Process and prepare meats for consumption. Understand animal anatomy, proper butchering techniques, and preservation methods to ensure optimal quality.

VIII. Cyberneticist

Designs and implants cybernetic enhancements, melding flesh with technology for upgrades.

a. Cybernetic Design: Engineer cybernetic implants for specific enhancements and functions.

b. Implant Installation: Surgically implant cybernetic components into living subjects.

c. Integration Mastery: Ensure seamless integration between flesh and technology for optimal performance.

IX. Diplomat

Masters of negotiation, political influence, and knowledge. Diplomats excel at brokering peace, navigating complex social hierarchies, and understanding arcane and religious matters.

a. Negotiation: Broker peace treaties and agreements between conflicting parties.

b. Political Navigation: Understand and navigate complex social hierarchies and political structures.

c. Arcane Knowledge: Comprehend arcane and religious matters to facilitate understanding between cultures.

X. Enchanter

Experts in magical enchantments and item enhancement. Enchanters imbue objects and performances with magical properties.

a. Spell Imbuing: Infuse magical spells into objects for lasting effects.

b. Item Enhancement: Permanently enhance items to improve their capabilities.

c. Performance Magic: Imbue performances with magical properties for heightened effects.

XI. Engineer

Masters of mechanics, design, and construction. Engineers create devices, traps, and mechanical solutions to complex problems.

a. Device Construction: Design and build complex mechanical devices for various purposes.

b. Trap Design: Engineer sophisticated traps and defensive mechanisms.

c. Mechanical Solutions: Develop innovative mechanical approaches to solve complex problems.

XII. Farmhand

Assists with agriculture and livestock, supporting communities through farming expertise.

a. Crop Cultivation: Grow and maintain crops for food and resources.

b. Livestock Management: Raise, breed, and care for farm animals.

c. Agricultural Knowledge: Understand seasonal patterns, soil conditions, and farming best practices.

XIII. Gem Crafter

Shapes and enchants stones and gems, producing decorative items and magical artifacts.

a. Gem Cutting: Shape raw stones into polished gems with optimal clarity and brilliance.

b. Stone Enchanting: Imbue gems with magical properties and powers.

c. Artifact Creation: Craft magical artifacts using gems as focal points for power.

XIV. Hacker

Specialists in problem-solving, system exploitation, and information access. Hackers navigate complex systems and find solutions others miss.

a. System Exploitation: Identify and exploit weaknesses in complex systems.

b. Information Access: Gain access to secured information and restricted data.

c. Problem Solving: Find creative and unconventional solutions to seemingly impossible challenges.

XV. Hauler

Expert transporters and logistics specialists. Haulers move goods efficiently across any terrain and manage supply chains.

a. Cargo Management: Organize and load cargo efficiently for safe transport.

b. Route Planning: Determine optimal paths for transportation across various terrains.

c. Supply Chain: Manage distribution networks and ensure timely delivery of goods.

XVI. Hunter

Masters of wilderness survival and tracking. Hunters excel at pursuing quarry and navigating the natural world.

a. Tracking: Follow and locate prey through wilderness and urban environments.

b. Hunting Techniques: Employ specialized methods for capturing or eliminating various creatures.

c. Wilderness Survival: Thrive in natural environments with minimal supplies or equipment.

XVII. Investigator

Masters of investigation and deduction. Investigators solve mysteries, uncover hidden truths, and gather intelligence through meticulous analysis and clever questioning.

a. Evidence Analysis: Examine and interpret evidence to uncover truth.

b. Interrogation: Question subjects skillfully to extract information and reveal deception.

c. Deduction: Draw logical conclusions from observations and evidence.

XVIII. Mechanic

Repairs and crafts devices manipulating time, space, or probability.

a. Device Repair: Restore broken or damaged temporal, spatial, or probabilistic devices.

b. Temporal Engineering: Design and build devices that manipulate the flow of time.

c. Reality Manipulation: Craft devices affecting space, distance, or probability.

XIX. Mudder

Constructs buildings and structures using stone, brick, clay, and mud, focusing on durability and aesthetics.

a. Structure Design: Plan and design buildings and fortifications for specific needs.

b. Material Preparation: Prepare and process stone, brick, clay, and mud for construction.

c. Building Construction: Construct durable and aesthetically pleasing structures.

XX. Nanotechnician

Manipulates nanomachines for healing, crafting, sabotage, or adaptation.

a. Nanomachine Crafting: Design and program nanomachines for specific tasks.

b. Healing Application: Deploy nanomachines to repair biological systems and wounds.

c. Sabotage Engineering: Create nanomachines capable of infiltration and destruction.

XXI. Practitioner

Experts in medicine, healing, and treatment of ailments. Practitioners diagnose diseases, perform healing procedures, and save lives through medical mastery.

a. Disease Diagnosis: Identify illnesses and conditions through examination and analysis.

b. Surgical Procedures: Perform medical operations to treat serious injuries and ailments.

c. Healing Mastery: Apply advanced medical techniques to restore health and vitality.

XXII. Psionic Crafter

Crafts items or implants to amplify, focus, or shield psionic powers.

a. Amplification Devices: Create items that amplify psionic powers and abilities.

b. Focus Tools: Craft items that concentrate and refine psionic energy.

c. Protective Shielding: Design implants or items that shield against psionic intrusion.

XXIII. Roboticist

Creates and programs robots and androids for diverse tasks.

a. Robot Design: Engineer and construct robots for specific functions and environments.

b. Programming: Write code and logic protocols to govern robot behavior.

c. Android Development: Create humanoid automatons with advanced capabilities.

XXIV. Scientist

Explores scientific and arcane phenomena, innovates through experiment and inquiry.

a. Experimental Research: Conduct systematic experiments to test hypotheses and advance knowledge.

b. Arcane Study: Investigate magical and supernatural phenomena scientifically.

c. Innovation: Develop new theories and applications through inquiry and discovery.

XXV. Shipwright

Expert ship builders and maritime specialists. Shipwrights construct vessels and navigate air and sea commerce.

a. Ship Construction: Design and build seaworthy and airworthy vessels.

b. Maritime Navigation: Navigate safely across seas and manage maritime trade routes.

c. Vessel Maintenance: Repair and maintain ships to ensure seaworthiness and longevity.

XXVI. Stitcher

Repairs and creates garments, armor, and magical clothing with advanced tailoring and enchanting techniques.

a. Garment Creation: Design and craft high-quality clothing for comfort and style.

b. Armor Tailoring: Create fitted armor that optimizes protection and mobility.

c. Enchanted Clothing: Weave magic into garments for protective or enhancement effects.

XXVII. Tinker

Masters of small mechanisms, gadgets, and improvisation. Tinkers create ingenious devices and solve problems with creative solutions.

a. Gadget Design: Invent and build clever mechanical devices for various purposes.

b. Improvisation: Create solutions with available materials and improvised equipment.

c. Mechanism Repair: Fix broken gadgets and restore mechanical devices to function.

XXVIII. Woodworker

Crafts structures, furniture, and wooden items, vital for building communities and settlements.

a. Furniture Crafting: Design and build high-quality wooden furniture for homes and commerce.

b. Wooden Structures: Construct buildings, bridges, and frameworks from wood.

c. Woodworking Artistry: Create decorative and artistic wooden items of exceptional quality.
