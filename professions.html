Professions

Professions represent specialized skills and expertise your character develops through focused practice and dedication. Of the 23 player professions, some are paired with corresponding talent trees, forming a symbiotic relationship where talents provide the foundational abilities and professions deepen specialization and expertise through their three core skills. Each profession may grant bonuses when a character invests talent points into their associated talent tree's profession bonus talent, creating a direct mechanical link between talent development and professional mastery. By selecting complementary talents and professions, characters can develop deep expertise in their chosen fields and excel at tasks related to their professional focus.

Profession Bonus: Each talent tree contains a profession bonus talent that grants bonuses to all checks related to its associated profession when the talent is advanced.
