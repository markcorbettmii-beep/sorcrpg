Professions

Professions represent specialized skills and expertise your character develops through focused practice and dedication. Each of the {NUM_PROFESSIONS=14} player professions is paired with a corresponding talent tree, forming a symbiotic relationship where talents provide the foundational abilities and professions deepen specialization and expertise. Additionally, each talent tree is also paired with a unique Profession Disposition, representing the specialized roles and expertise of NPCs that interact directly with your character. Professions grant bonuses when a character invests talent points into the associated talent tree's profession bonus talent, creating a direct mechanical link between talent development and professional mastery. By selecting complementary talents and professions, characters can develop deep expertise in their chosen fields and excel at tasks related to their professional focus.

†Profession Bonus†: Each talent tree contains a profession bonus talent that grants bonuses to all checks related to its associated profession when the talent is advanced.

What is a Profession Disposition?

A Profession Disposition is a specialized NPC role that directly interacts with player characters. Each Profession Disposition is paired with a talent tree and represents a specific type of NPC expertise that characters will encounter and engage with in the game world. For example, an Investigator character might interact with a Guard (Profession Disposition paired with Detective), negotiate with a Scribe (paired with Representative), or seek help from an Innkeeper (paired with Field Medic). Profession Dispositions allow Game Masters to create consistent, thematic NPC interactions that complement player character professions and create meaningful mechanical and narrative connections throughout the game.

Playable Professions

1. Alchemist (†Associated Talent Tree - Thief)

Masters of potion-making, transmutation, and chemical expertise. Alchemists understand the properties of substances and can create powerful brews.

2. Archeologist (†Associated Talent Tree - Crypt Raider)

Experts in ancient artifacts, historical sites, and preservation of knowledge. Archeologists unlock the secrets of civilizations past.

3. Blacksmith (†Associated Talent Tree - Patriarch)

Craftspeople skilled in metalworking, weapon forging, and armor creation. Blacksmiths forge the tools and weapons of legend.

4. Cook (†Associated Talent Tree - Olympiad)

Masters of culinary arts and food preparation. Cooks understand nutrition, flavor, and the art of sustaining adventurers through nourishment.

5. Enchanter (†Associated Talent Tree - Performer)

Experts in magical enchantments and item enhancement. Enchanters imbue objects and performances with magical properties.

6. Engineer (†Associated Talent Tree - Bounty Hunter)

Masters of mechanics, design, and construction. Engineers create devices, traps, and mechanical solutions to complex problems.

7. Hacker (†Associated Talent Tree - Tactician)

Specialists in problem-solving, system exploitation, and information access. Hackers navigate complex systems and find solutions others miss.

8. Hauler (†Associated Talent Tree - Traveler)

Expert transporters and logistics specialists. Haulers move goods efficiently across any terrain and manage supply chains.

9. Hunter (†Associated Talent Tree - Scout)

Masters of wilderness survival and tracking. Hunters excel at pursuing quarry and navigating the natural world.

10. Shipwright (†Associated Talent Tree - Quartermaster)

Expert ship builders and maritime specialists. Shipwrights construct vessels and navigate air and sea commerce.

11. Tinker (†Associated Talent Tree - Leprechaun)

Masters of small mechanisms, gadgets, and improvisation. Tinkers create ingenious devices and solve problems with creative solutions.

12. Diplomat (†Associated Talent Tree - Representative)

Masters of negotiation, political influence, and knowledge. Diplomats excel at brokering peace, navigating complex social hierarchies, and understanding arcane and religious matters.

13. Practitioner (†Associated Talent Tree - Field Medic)

Experts in medicine, healing, and treatment of ailments. Practitioners diagnose diseases, perform healing procedures, and save lives through medical mastery.

14. Investigator (†Associated Talent Tree - Detective)

Masters of investigation and deduction. Investigators solve mysteries, uncover hidden truths, and gather intelligence through meticulous analysis and clever questioning.

Profession Dispositions (NPC Only)

1. Vault Keeper (†Profession Disposition - Thief)

Masters of security and inventory, guarding valuable treasures with traps, contracts, and meticulous care.

2. Runesmith (†Profession Disposition - Crypt Raider)

Artisans who inscribe magical runes onto objects, imbuing items with potent magical properties. Also skilled in identifying and mending relics.

3. Bodyguard (†Profession Disposition - Patriarch)

Trained protectors who use combat prowess and security protocols to keep individuals of importance safe in any situation.

4. Stable Hand (†Profession Disposition - Olympiad)

Caretakers for domesticated beasts and mounts, ensuring their health, training, and readiness for travel or labor.

5. Socialite (†Profession Disposition - Performer)

Masters of social interaction and politics, wielding charm and influence through extensive networks and event knowledge.

6. Bounty Hunter (†Profession Disposition - Bounty Hunter)

Skilled trackers and hunters who pursue fugitives for reward, adept in stealth, combat, and investigation—formidable foes and valuable allies.

7. Cohort (†Profession Disposition - Tactician)

Narrative-driven hirelings who support party goals, filling skill gaps and enhancing team effectiveness.

8. Transporter (†Profession Disposition - Traveler)

Specialists in logistics and navigation, moving goods and people safely across the land, sea, or air, overcoming environmental obstacles.

9. Nature Warden (†Profession Disposition - Scout)

Guardians of the natural world, mediators between civilization and nature, skilled in survival, tracking, and ecological balance.

10. Travel Master (†Profession Disposition - Quartermaster)

Coordinators of journeys, masters of routes and transportation methods, ensuring safe, efficient travel across realms.

11. Inventor (†Profession Disposition - Leprechaun)

Creative minds who develop new technologies and devices, solving problems with ingenious, unconventional solutions.

12. Scribe (†Profession Disposition - Representative)

Artisans of the written word, creating, transcribing, and appraising documents, contracts, and magical scrolls.

13. Innkeeper (†Profession Disposition - Field Medic)

Managers of inns, providing lodging, meals, and local knowledge, often serving as information hubs and safe havens.

14. Guard (†Profession Disposition - Detective)

Peacekeepers and defenders, trained to secure cities, castles, and estates, and uphold the law.
