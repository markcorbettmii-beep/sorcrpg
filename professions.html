Professions

Professions represent specialized skills and expertise your character develops through focused practice and dedication. Of the {NUM_PROFESSIONS=24} player professions, some are paired with corresponding talent trees, forming a symbiotic relationship where talents provide the foundational abilities and professions deepen specialization and expertise through their three core skills. Each profession may grant bonuses when a character invests talent points into their associated talent tree's profession bonus talent, creating a direct mechanical link between talent development and professional mastery. Each talent tree is also paired with a unique Profession Disposition, representing the specialized NPC roles and expertise that characters encounter and trade with in the game world. By selecting complementary talents and professions, characters can develop deep expertise in their chosen fields and excel at tasks related to their professional focus.

†Profession Bonus†: Each talent tree contains a profession bonus talent that grants bonuses to all checks related to its associated profession when the talent is advanced.

What is a Profession Disposition?

A Profession Disposition is a specialized NPC role that directly interacts with player characters. Each Profession Disposition is paired with a talent tree and represents a specific type of NPC expertise that characters will encounter and engage with in the game world. For example, an Investigator character might interact with a Guard (Profession Disposition paired with Detective), negotiate with a Scribe (paired with Representative), or seek help from an Innkeeper (paired with Field Medic). Profession Dispositions allow Game Masters to create consistent, thematic NPC interactions that complement player character professions and create meaningful mechanical and narrative connections throughout the game.
