A score of 1 in an Attribute is the default Prerequisite before any aligned TST can be purchased. A TST score can never exceed its aligned Attribute's score. Unspent REM Points are never lost, they sit as Remnants until spent, including on Remnant items later in the game.

Talents and Skills aren't fixed to this list, they're chosen per Talent Tree/Profession, but always align to one of these seven Attributes the same way Traits do. Check the specific Talent Tree or Profession's own entry for its aligned Attribute.

Worked Example (see Basic Rules pg. 2 for the full walkthrough)

A roll of 6 assigned to an Attribute: spend 3 REM for a Score of 1 (the Prerequisite), 2 REM on one aligned TST, 1 REM left over becomes a Remnant. 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. Nothing is ever wasted, it's either spent now or banked for later.
