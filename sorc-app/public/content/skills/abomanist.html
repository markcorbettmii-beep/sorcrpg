Abomanist

An Abomanist is a licensed practitioner of forbidden anatomy, specializing in the reshaping of living flesh and the reanimation of dead tissue. Sitting at the dark intersection of brutal medieval surgery and experimental physical science, they treat the humanoid body not as a sacred vessel, but as a malleable canvas. Their work is performed through anatomical knowledge, surgical tools, and physical procedures, without magic.

Practitioners and surgeons, Abomanists undertake reconstruction beyond the capabilities of a typical field medic. They repair damaged anatomy, graft replacement limbs, splice biological traits, and treat severe physical injuries in Characters and their living Companions. High-society nobles quietly employ them to sculpt away physical deformities and engineer artificial beauty. Warlords and eccentric patrons commission more ambitious alterations.

Abomanists also repair, reshape, and reanimate corpses to serve as undead Cohort Companions. The duration of this borrowed life varies according to rolls governing the reanimation’s persistence. Every such Companion eventually deteriorates and withers away.

Their expertise extends to examining many forms of dead and undead, whether an intact body or only its surviving parts. Through these remains, they investigate causes of death, anatomical alterations, and the physical workings of undeath. Their studies encompass the knowledge the dead retain, the auras they exhibit, and the reliability of whatever testimony they offer—including whether the dead can lie. Such work draws patrons seeking lost knowledge, evidence of hidden crimes, or answers buried with the deceased.

To the common folk and holy orders, they are feared grave-robbers and heretics; to academia, they are the ultimate masters of biological engineering.
