SORC Glossary

Essential terms and definitions for understanding Slayers of Rings & Crowns.

Terminology Expansion

The current Apex glossary remains authoritative. Additional legacy definitions are integrated below only when the term is not already defined above.

Migration note: Red entries are transferred legacy definitions. Purple notes identify migration context; black footnotes remain explanatory.

Core Mechanics

ATTR (Attribute)
Short for Attribute. The six core statistics that define a character: Strength, Agility, Cognition, Perception, Acuity, and Spirit. Each ranges from -5 to +12 and affects various character capabilities.
PROTS (Protection Score)
The target number for attack rolls and ability checks. Calculated from Defense Trait, armor, buffs, and conditions. Attacks and abilities roll d100 + modifiers vs. PROTS to hit.
Trait
A specialized modifier derived from ATTRs, ranging from +0 to +12. Applied to damage rolls, hit bonuses, and various checks depending on the weapon or ability used.
Rank
A character's power level, ranging from Common through Divine. Determines access to equipment and abilities. Weapons, armor, and items also carry rank designations matching the character's rank to use them.
Hit Bonus
Modifiers added to attack rolls from weapon rank, Trait, and proficiency. Off-hand attacks roll at half the main-hand hit bonus, reduced damage output, and reduced accuracy.
Dual-Wield
Fighting with two one-handed weapons. Requires a Nature Ability to unlock. Both hands attack on one BASE combat strike action. Off-hand rolls at half main-hand hit bonus with reduced damage.

Combat Terms

Action
A discrete unit of time in combat, measured in individual turns. Characters spend actions to attack, cast abilities, move, and interact with the environment.
BASE Combat Strike
A standard weapon attack that consumes 1 action. Includes main-hand attack and, if dual-wielding, off-hand attack on the same action.
Critical Hit
An attack that rolls within the Critical Window for the weapon's rank, dealing 2x damage dice. May trigger additional Status effects or severe injuries depending on the location hit.
Incapacitated
A condition where a character has reached 0 Life and can no longer take actions. May result in death, unconsciousness, or severe injury depending on damage severity.
Condition
A temporary status effect applied to characters during combat or exploration, such as Poisoned, Burning, Stunned, or Fatigued. May impose penalties or restrict actions.

Progression & Character Building

Ability
A special action learned from a Class Tree that provides unique combat, utility, or roleplay capabilities. Abilities are never purchased; they are granted when choosing from a Class Tree's available Slots.
Class Tree
A structured progression system offering Abilities in Paths and Branches. Players gain one Ability choice per level from any available Class Tree matching their alignment Spectrum.
Path
A thematic grouping of Abilities within a Class Tree, unlocked at level 4. Staying on one Path through level 20 opens access to a Branch at level 21.
Branch
A deeper progression within a Path, unlocked at level 21. Following one Branch through level 30 leads to the Class Tree's 30-Ability capstone.
XP (Experience Points) & Rank PTS
Experience earned through gameplay that advances characters. Rank PTS unlock higher-rank items and abilities, while general XP supports specific progression paths.
Talent, Skill, Trait (TST)
The three currency types for character customization. TST points are purchased with gold during character creation and throughout career advancement to gain Talents (passive abilities), Skills (expertise), and Traits (stat increases).

Equipment & Resources

Weapon Rank
The tier of a weapon (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Unique, Heroic, Elite, Legendary, Divine). Higher ranks grant increased hit bonuses and damage dice. Characters must match a weapon's rank to use it.
1H Weapon
A one-handed weapon with base damage listed in the weapon damage table. When used in the off-hand during dual-wield, damage is half the main-hand value, rounded up.
2H Weapon
A two-handed weapon with higher base damage than 1H equivalents. Cannot be used with shields or dual-wield; requires both hands and grants no off-hand attack bonus.
Armor
Protective equipment worn by characters. Provides PROTS bonuses and may impose movement penalties (see Encumbrance). Must match character rank to use.
Life
A character's health pool. Damage subtracts from Life; reaching 0 incapacitates the character. Maximum Life is determined by race size and modifiers like Endurance.
Mana
A resource consumed by spellcasters and ability users. Restored through rest. Characters without Acuity Trait or relevant abilities typically have 0 Mana.
Stamina
A resource used for movement in structured encounters and certain abilities. Depletes with distance traveled or action usage, restored through rest.
Endurance
A character's fatigue threshold for long-distance travel. Foot travel requires recovery after every 10 miles; mounts extend this to 50 miles before requiring a Breather.

World & Setting

Essentia
The universe in which SORC is set. Features two suns (Adoria and Tawdry Dwarf), 13 planets, multiple moons, diverse worlds, and an ancient prophecy.
Zailister
The primary starting planet for most characters. Features medieval combat, magic, advanced technology, and serves as the origin for many player campaigns.
Adoria
The primary magic-radiating sun of Essentia, the source of magical energy and divine power in the universe.
Tawdry Dwarf
The dying secondary star of Essentia, slowly fading and influencing cosmic events in the SORC universe.
Fellowship
A player organization or guild that characters can join. Fellowships have member caps (Basic tier: capped, Pro tier: unlimited) and provide group chat, shared resources, and collaborative gameplay.

Game Modes & Features

Trials of Combat
An advanced gameplay mode featuring ranked combat challenges. Basic tier receives limited access; Pro tier unlocks full features and exclusive perks.
Trading Post
A Pro-exclusive marketplace for trading items, cards, and resources between players. Basic tier players cannot access this feature.
Character Sheet
A digital representation of a character's stats, abilities, equipment, and progress. Basic tier: 2 sheets, Pro tier: 5 sheets.
Achievements
Milestones and accomplishments tracked during gameplay. Basic tier: limited achievement set, Pro tier: full achievement set with exclusive rewards.
Leaderboard
Rankings of top players by various metrics. Basic tier: top 100 visible, Pro tier: top 200 visible with expanded details.

Additional Definitions

These terms are retained from the archived terminology reference because they are not defined in the current Apex glossary.

Rule Sets

The three ways a SORC campaign can be run: Bare Bones, Modular and Complex. A Rule Set sets how much of the system is in force, not what any rule says. Chosen before the campaign begins and locked in for its duration. See Rule Sets, pg. 1.

Bare Bones

The bare bones Rule Set. A fixed preset, not a menu: its omissions are set and no Player or GM picks among them. Bare Bones keeps whatever is needed to roll, hit, hurt and advance, and drops the layers that add a lookup, a percentage or a ledger. Alignment is never omitted. Carries its own box set and PDF download. See the Bare Bones page for everything it omits and what a Bare Bones table does instead.

Modular

The Rule Set where the campaign team decides what stays in the rules and what comes out, one feature per category, agreed before the campaign begins and locked in. Modular begins from Complex, every rule in force, and subtracts. It is not a separate box set; a Modular table runs the Complex set and collapses what it dropped.

Complex

The Rule Set with every rule in force, nothing omitted. Carries its own box set and PDF download, and it is the set a Modular table works from. Both the PDF and the online version expand and collapse, so a table can show or hide each system and keep only what they chose in front of them.

Standing

A Character's relationship level with a faction, NPC, or Standing category, on an eight-tier scale carrying numeric values: Exalted +4, Cherished +3, Adored +2, Respected +1, Neutral 0, Tolerable −1, Disliked −2, Hated −3. Held across seven categories, each with its own Standing and Contributions: Family, Affiliations, Companions, Campaign, Fellows, Neighbours, and Wellness. Capitalised Standing always means this; lowercase standing is ordinary English. See Standing under Prestige, pg. 2.

Contribution

What a Character gives to a Standing category, time, Coin, labour, and their own reputation. Contribution and Neglect are one line read in opposite directions, ticking on its own like Wellness. It is the second of the two lines each category carries, the first being Standing, and it does not carry a Mark. Recorded apart from Standing: a Character may contribute heavily and hold poor Standing, or hold high Standing on a name earned long ago.

Neglect

The downward direction of the Contribution line. Neglect can only carry a Character toward Neutral, never past it; falling below Neutral into Tolerable, Disliked, or Hated takes a deliberate act. Absence makes people indifferent, not hostile.

Mark

The highest Standing a Character has ever held with a party, recorded alongside their current Standing. Never decays. Not goodwill still owed but the measure of how far a Character has fallen, and what distinguishes drifting apart from betrayal. A deliberate act drops a party as far below Neutral as their Mark stood above it, minimum one tier. A Bond Quest may seal a Mark, closing it without erasing it.

Praise

What a Character receives from the friends and allies of those they hold good Standing with. Arrives unasked. When Standing with a target moves, allies of that target move the same direction by half the tiers moved, rounded down, minimum 1.

Blowback

What a Character receives from the enemies of those they hold Standing with. Arrives unasked and does not care whether the Character wanted the association. Enemies of a target move opposite the Character's movement with that target, by the same amount as Praise. Rising with one faction is by itself a way of falling with its rivals, so no gain in Standing is neutral.

Experience (XP)

Experience Points.

AP

Action Points / Attack Power (increases the damage of physical attacks).

TP

Technique Points.

DPS

Damage Per Second (a measure of damage output).

HPS

Healing Per Second (a measure of healing output).

AoE

Area of Effect (referring to Abilities that affect multiple targets within a specified area).

PBAE

Point Blank Area of Effect.

CC

Crowd Control (Abilities or effects that hinder or control opponent actions).

DoT

Damage over Time (effects that inflict damage over a period instead of all at once).

HoT

Heal over Time (effects that heal over a period instead of all at once).

PvE

Player vs. Environment (content involving NPCs, quests, and dungeons).

PvP

Player vs. Player (content involving combat against other Players, see Trials of Combat).

PK

Player Kill.

LFM

Looking for More.

AFK

Away From Keyboard.

OT

Off Tank (secondary tank, usually handles additional Hostile Creatures).

MT

Main Tank (the primary tank in a group/raid).

RNG

Random Number Generator (often related to loot generation or effect determination).

OOC

Out of Character (in roleplaying contexts).

OOS

Out of Stamina (colloquial shorthand, not related to the Spirit score).

LOS

Line of Sight (the Ability to see or target Hostile Creatures based on obstructions).

Link

A term referring to connecting Hostile Creatures in combat, causing others to join if one is attacked.

Single Target

An Ability or attack that affects only one Hostile or ally at a time.

SD

Spell Damage.

CD

Cooldown (the time before a skill or Ability can be used again).

RMT

Real Money Trading.

ELB

Effective Life.

TBD

To Be Determined (for unresolved aspects of game mechanics or content).

OOM

Out of Mana.

RP

Role Play (acting or roleplaying as a character).

CAP

The highest number a skill or Ability can be granted without a buff.

PROTS

Protection Score. Armor Scr. + all defensive modifiers. The target number attackers must meet or beat.

Difficulty Score

Difficulty Score. The target number for non-combat skill checks and Panic Checks.

TST

Talents, Skills, and Traits, the three types of character development points.

Armor Scr.

Armor Score. The base protection value of a piece of armor before modifiers.

ATT

Attribute. The innate capacities that define a character's core capabilities. Six ATTRs: Artistry (ART), Fortitude (FRT), Intellect (INT), Physique (PHY), Vigilance (VIG), Wisdom (WIS).

Attribute Score

The numerical value assigned to an Attribute, used as a modifier and Prerequisite for gear, talents, skills, and Traits.

VIT SCR

The numerical value of any individual Vitality resource, Life, Mana, Stamina, or Endurance, tracked through Chips. Grows with Character Level, Growth, an aligned Trait, and Apex.

Trait SCR

The numerical value of an individual Trait, used as a modifier to d100 rolls and as a Prerequisite for abilities and gear. Cannot exceed the score of its aligned ATTR SCR. Capped at 40.

Apex

A race-specific Vitality score representing the volume of each Vitality resource-how much Life, Mana, Stamina, or Endurance a character can hold. Starts at a race-determined value (1-11) and grows every 5 years of age after the first year at a rate specific to that race. Every 5 Apex = 1 Chip of Vitality capacity. Splittable across multiple resources at the Player's choice.

Spirit

A race-specific Vitality score that determines regeneration rate for Life, Mana, or Stamina. Starts at a race-determined value (1-11) and grows every 5 years of age after the first year at a rate specific to that race. Every 5 Spirit = 1 Chip of regeneration per turn. Splittable across Life, Mana, or Stamina at the Player's choice. Never affects Endurance.

Wellness

A percentage (0% to 150%) tracking how well a character takes care of themselves through daily choices, rest, diet, activity, and Practitioner support. Wellness ranges from 0% (minimum, neglect and decline) to 150% (maximum, peak conditioning). Triggered by moral and conscience decisions (healthy choices raise it, unhealthy lower it), poison, disease, and status effects (cause decline), Life loss (hurts Wellness), sustained rest (restores it), practicing abilities (maintains and improves it), healthy diet (strengthens it), and Practitioner visits (provide recovery). Ticks up or down automatically like HoT and DoT effects. Below 75% Wellness, only direct neglect, poison, disease, and injury cause decline; positive effects don't tick. At 75% Wellness and above, both positive and negative effects accelerate (healthy habits snowball faster, bad habits compound quicker). Wellness directly modifies Apex and Spirit: low Wellness shrinks Vitality caps and slows regeneration, high Wellness expands caps and accelerates regeneration. The Trait Health controls tick rate (higher Health slows decline below 75%, speeds recovery at 75% and above). Multiple professions support Wellness: Practitioners, Hunters, Fishermen, Cooks, Scouts, Healers, Buffers, moral leaders, and Field Medics. Rest mechanics: Breather (10 min) ticks slowly, Short Rest (1 hour) ticks faster, Full Rest (8 hours, requires bed) ticks significantly faster; environmental conditions (temperature, comfort, safety) affect rest quality. Characters benefit most from establishing Standing with a primary Practitioner who knows them well. See Wellness, pg. 2, #wellness, and GM Codex Ch. II, #wellness.

MAC

Monsters, Animals, Creatures/Critters, any living entity in the game world.

Tech Advanced Weapons (TAW) / Tech Advanced Body Armor (TABA)

Technologically Advanced Weapons / Body Armor. Invented by races native to advanced planets (Corpus Caeleste, Undeximus, Omne Malum, Tredeki).

LST

Limb-Specific Targeting. Aim at body parts for bonus damage; penalty to hit applies.

UID

Character Unique Identification, an RNG number from SORC servers, exclusive to each online character.

Lore Quest

A special elite quest built into a module, triggered by a Lore Card. The card guides the party to it.

Animated Compass

A living magical companion that discovers and marks Safe Havens, Ports its owner and current party to chosen destinations, and develops additional forms and abilities as it LVLs. Its forms include map, elemental, and wisp manifestations. See Movement rules for full details.

Boneyard

Where passing Companions of Rare rank and above, broken gear, spent items, and incapacitated Cards go until resurrected, repaired, or restocked. Common and Uncommon Companions that die are permanently lost and do not go to the boneyard. Upon resurrection, a Companion's mood resets to Annoyed, the lowest mood tier.

Companion Slot Sizes

Characters have five Companion slots: Tiny, Small, Standard, Goliath, and Behemoth. A Behemoth occupies the Behemoth, Goliath, and Standard slots. Dragons are never Companions; drakes, wyverns and other lesser draconic creatures are. Tiny: insects, bugs, and the very smallest creatures. Small: roughly rodent to small bird in size. Standard: humanoid-sized up to the size of a large horse. Goliath: large humanoid-scale creatures up to the scale of a Centyr (4 spaces). Behemoth: colossal creatures, extremely rare. Goliath-sized races can mount Goliath or Behemoth companions.

BG

Battleground (areas for structured PvP).

GZ

Grats.

Zerg

A term sometimes used in reference to overwhelming numbers.

Guild

A Player community group.

Gear

Common shorthand for a character's equipment. See Gear under Additional Definitions above for its formal meaning.

Rare Find

Such as a Unique pet find a Hunter can attempt to calm, capture and eventually try to tame as their own pet.

Nerf

A reduction in power/effectiveness of a class, item, or Ability.

Buff

A positive effect or enhancement applied to a character.

Debuff

A negative effect imposed upon a character.

Res

Resurrection (bringing a character back to life).

Set Bonus

All armor comes as complete sets, there are no individual pieces. A three-part bonus applies to sets at Heroic rank and above: Main Bonus (all pieces), Gloves Bonus, and Boots Bonus. Only gloves and boots may be replaced, doing so removes their respective bonus and reduces the Main Bonus by 25% each. Permanently broken once pieces are swapped. Only the Cuirass may receive a set-level enhancement.

Kite

To maneuver Hostile Creatures to avoid damage or mechanics.

Wipe

When the whole party/group is wiped out during an Encounter.
