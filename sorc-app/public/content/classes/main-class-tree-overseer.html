Overseer - Class Tree

Overseer (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Devoted martial commanders who lead from the front in blessed or discipline-forged plate, Overseers combine exceptional armor mastery with auras, command abilities, and unshakable authority, inspiring allies who stand near them, breaking the will of evil through proximity alone, and serving as the unmovable standard around which armies and adventuring parties alike can rally and hold.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Alignment: Alignments range from Lawful, Moral and Ambivalent, and are Path and Branch Dependent.

To use the Abilities of this Path or Branch, you must fall within the Alignment's Spectrum. To devote to the Path's Higher Power you must fall exactly at that Alignment (see Alignment, Basic Rules pg. 2).

Higher Power: Path Dependent (see Path below)

Overseer's Paths individually restrict by alignment from LVL 4 on, see each Path above for its specific Alignment.

To learn a Path's Abilities, or any of its three Branches', your own alignment need only sit inside this Class's Spectrum.

To draw a Path's Higher Power bonus you must hold that Path's exact base Alignment, and that same base is what its three Branches require.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Overseer's Oath, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Unbroken Vanguard, Once Per Day the Overseer plants their banner, granting every ally within earshot a shield equal to their Piety SCR that absorbs damage before Life.

Extra 1d6 + LVL holy damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Overseers can Flourish their weapon to grant an ally a free Defense roll.

Overseers get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Overseers get +4 plus their PIETY SCR on Knowledge checks involving religious lore.

Overseer Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Archon - A disciplined Overseer who commands the battlefield like a seasoned legion officer.
  Alignment: Moral
  Observance: Service and care. Devotion is spent on other people rather than performed.
  Will not share a Character with: Evil and Amoral.
  The Higher Power bonus requires holding Moral exactly, and that same base carries through all three of its Branches below.
  a. Battleguard - An Archon who commands the frontline like a seasoned field officer.
  b. Defender - An Archon devoted to holding ground and protecting allies over dealing damage.
  c. Legionnaire - An Archon trained in disciplined formation-fighting, stronger the more allies stand beside them.
ii. Crusader - A zealous Overseer who leads the charge, carrying faith into battle like a weapon.
  Alignment: Lawful
  Observance: Oath, record and public rite. Devotion is witnessed and written down.
  Will not share a Character with: Evil, and any Path that takes by force what it has not earned.
  The Higher Power bonus requires holding Lawful exactly, and that same base carries through all three of its Branches below.
  a. Blessed Knight - A Crusader anointed for battle, carrying a blessing into every fight.
  b. Devout - A Crusader whose unwavering faith fuels every strike.
  c. Trojan - A Crusader who breaks through enemy lines by sheer, disciplined force.
iii. Paladin - A protective Overseer who shields allies with radiant conviction and steadfast defense.
  Alignment: Lawful
  Observance: Oath, record and public rite. Devotion is witnessed and written down.
  Will not share a Character with: Evil, and any Path that takes by force what it has not earned.
  The Higher Power bonus requires holding Lawful exactly, and that same base carries through all three of its Branches below.
  a. Guardian - A Paladin devoted entirely to shielding allies from harm.
  b. Radiant - A Paladin who channels holy light directly into devastating, purifying strikes.
  c. Templar - A Paladin sworn to disciplined defense, holding the line above all else.
