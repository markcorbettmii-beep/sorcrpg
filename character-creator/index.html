Body Type Selection

Body types are dependent on Character Physique Score.
GMs are required to ensure Players submit the proper Physique value when choosing “Body Type.”

  • Physique ≤ 1: Thin
  • Physique 2 thru 4: Lean
  • Physique 5 thru 20: Muscular
  • Physique 21 and beyond: Massive
