Playable Races

Explore the diverse array of races available in SorC, each with unique traits, cultures, and abilities. Categorized by size, these races include towering giants, medium-sized folk, and small adventurers. Choose your race carefully to shape your journey and the adventures that await!

About the Playable Races

Goliath Size Races

  • Goliath: Tall and strong, 7-9 feet. Includes Bugbears, Bast, Hawkbears, Ogres, Trolls, Firbolgs.

Standard Size Races

  • Humans: 5-7 feet, versatile and adaptable.
  • Blood Draco: Draconic traits, 5-7 feet.
  • Orc: Resilient fighters, 5-7 feet.
  • Drow: Dark elves, 5-6 feet.
  • Common Dwarf: Miners and craftsmen, 4-5 feet.
  • Glimmerstone Dwarf: Lucky gemstone miners, 4-5 feet.
  • Wood Elf: Forest dwellers, 5-6 feet.

Small Size Races

  • Brownie: Small, magical, lucky, under 4 feet.
  • Halfling: Cheerful, resourceful, about 3 feet.
  • Pinkling: Mysterious illusionists, small size.
  • Micelings, Yawnie: Tiny, under 3 feet, stealthy and clever.

Each race brings unique flavor to your character, from the mystical elegance of elves to the resilient grit of small folk. Consider how your race's traits influence your playstyle and role in the world of SorC!
