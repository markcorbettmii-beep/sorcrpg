About the Playable Races

Goliath: Towering beings known for their strength and endurance. They stand between 7 to 9 feet tall and boast a base 16 Life points. Goliaths include races like Bugbears, Bast, Hawkbears, Ogres, Trolls, and Firbolgs.

Standard Races: Medium-sized races from 5 to 7 feet tall, such as Humans (including Imperial and Eldrim), Elves (Common, Arch, High, Sylvan, Underdark), Dwarves (various types), Nordians, and Vanwa. They feature a balance of agility, magic, and craftsmanship.

Small Races: Ranging from 3 to 5 feet tall, including Halflings, Micelings, Hobblings, Brownlings, Ratkin, Wood Elves, Gnolls, and Yawnie. They excel in stealth, agility, and adaptability, often specializing as scouts, tricksters, or artisans.

Each race brings unique flavor to your character, from the mystical elegance of elves to the resilient grit of small folk. Consider how your race's traits influence your playstyle and role in the world of SorC!
