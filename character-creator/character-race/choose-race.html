Playable Races

Explore the diverse array of races available in SorC, categorized by size: Goliath, Standard, and Small. Each race brings unique traits, abilities, and cultural flavor. When selecting multiple races (up to 4), your character's traits, skills, and abilities are combined proportionally, offering a flexible and rich customization.

About the Playable Races

Goliath Size Races

  • Goliath: Tall and strong, 7-9 feet. Includes Bugbears, Bast, Hawkbears, Ogres, Trolls, Firbolgs.

Standard Size Races

  • Human: 5-7 feet, versatile.
  • Blood Draco: Draconic traits, 5-7 feet.
  • Orc: Resilient fighters, 5-7 feet.
  • Vanwa: Tall, noble, 6-7 feet.
  • High Elf: Elegant and magical, 5-6 feet.
  • Drow: Dark elves, 5-6 feet.
  • Nordian: Rugged, 5-6 feet.

Small Size Races

  • Brownie: Small, magical, lucky, under 4 feet.
  • Halfling: About 3 feet, cheerful and resourceful.
  • Pinkling: Small, mischievous illusionists.
  • Micelings: Tiny, stealthy, under 3 feet.
  • Yawnie: Very small, under 3 feet, clever and stealthy.

When combining multiple races, traits are averaged proportionally (e.g., 1/4 of each race's abilities), allowing for a highly customizable character.
