Playable Races

Explore the diverse array of races available in SorC, categorized by size: Goliath, Standard, and Small. Each race brings unique traits, abilities, and cultural flavor. When selecting multiple races (up to 4), your character's traits, skills, and abilities are combined proportionally, offering a flexible and rich customization.

About the Playable Races

Goliath Size Races

  • Goliath: Towering and strong, 7-9 feet. Includes subtypes like Bugbear, Bast, Hawkbear, Ogre, Troll, and Firbolg.
  • Bugbear: Large, stealthy, and fierce, often living in forests or mountains.
  • Bast: Feline humanoids with agility, innate magic, and a deep connection to nature.
  • Hawkbear: Mighty, beast-like warriors with keen senses and strength.
  • Ogre: Huge and strong, often solitary or in small groups.
  • Troll: Regenerative and formidable, usually dwelling in caves or mountains.
  • Firbolg: Gentle giants with nature magic, often in forests.

Standard Size Races

  • Human: 5-7 feet, versatile and adaptable.
  • Blood Draco: Draconic traits, 5-7 feet.
  • Orc: Resilient fighters, 5-7 feet.
  • Vanwa: Tall, noble, 6-7 feet.
  • High Elf: Elegant, magical, 5-6 feet.
  • Drow: Dark elves, 5-6 feet.
  • Nordian: Rugged, 5-6 feet.

Small Size Races

  • Brownie: Small, magical, lucky, under 4 feet.
  • Halfling: About 3 feet, cheerful and resourceful.
  • Pinkling: Small, mischievous illusionists.
  • Micelings: Tiny, stealthy, under 3 feet.
  • Yawnie: Very small, under 3 feet, clever and stealthy.

Hybrid (Goliath + Small only)

  • Hybrid: Up to 4 races combined, traits averaged proportionally.
    Note: Goliath and Small races cannot be combined with each other, but may be combined with Standard races. For example, a 4-race hybrid gets 1/4 the sum of the innate traits, abilities, and skills of each selected race.

Each race brings unique flavor to your character, from the mystical elegance of elves to the resilient grit of small folk. Consider how your race's traits influence your playstyle and role in the world of SorC!
