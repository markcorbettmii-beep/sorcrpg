Playable Races

Explore the diverse array of races available in SorC, each with unique traits, cultures, and abilities. Whether you prefer the towering might of Goliaths, the agility of elves, or the cunning of small folk, the world of Zailister offers a rich tapestry for your character's story. Choose your race carefully to shape your journey and the adventures that await!

About the Playable Races

Human: Versatile and adaptable, humans are found throughout Zailister, excelling in many roles.

Blood Draco: Descendants of dragons, these beings have draconic traits and formidable magic.

Orc: Fierce warriors with resilience and strength, often living in nomadic clans.

Bast: Feline humanoids with agility, innate magic, and a deep connection to nature.

Centyr: Mythical beings with human upper bodies and animal lower halves, known for their agility and musical talent.

Drow: Dark elves skilled in stealth and magic, living in the underground realms.

Common Dwarf: Hardy miners and craftsmen, known for their resilience and craftsmanship.

Glimmerstone Dwarf: Lucky miners known for uncovering rare gemstones and treasures.

Wood Elf: Forest dwellers with exceptional archery skills and a bond to nature.

Brownie: Small, whimsical folk with luck and craftsmanship skills.

Halfling: Cheerful, stealthy adventurers known for their resourcefulness.

Pinkling: Mischievous illusionists with a pink hue and penchant for tricks.

Hybrid: Crossbreeds of various races, offering unique traits and appearances.
