Small Size

Brownie
Small, magical race with a lot of luck on their side, utilizing partial features of both elves and gnomes.


Halfling
Very small cheerful, resourceful little people with hairy feet and they never travel alone (see companion fellowship.


Yawnie
Small, slothlike creatures, slow but surprisingly strong and agile. Can camouflage themselves in forest and jungle environments, and they're great climbers and swingers. Yawnies have an extremely long life-span, living well beyond 300 years.


Pinkling
Mischievous hermit-like creatures with large, luminous eyes, sharp teeth and a pink hue skin tone. These creatures prefer darkness, they're great swimmers and will eat just about anything. They crouch, crawl, and although they stand upright and walk at ease, they're surprisingly fast and run or scurry using both their hands and feet with a hunched, furtive posture.


Miceling
Small, stealthy gnomes with mice blood. These creatures can go, and hide, just about anywhere, and easily find their way in and out. They have a tremendous ability to sense danger


Wood Elf
Wood elves are agile and reclusive forest dwellers, deeply attuned to nature and skilled in archery and stealth.
