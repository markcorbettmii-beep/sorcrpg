Tomes of Essentia

This Game Compendium is a collected wisdom, lore, and rules of Essentia - the Tomes of Essentia are gathered here as lighter PDF versions of their paperback and hardback editions that hold even more secrets, portrait style illustrations and more. Explore chronicles of the nineteen crowns, histories of planetary wars, secrets through all of Essentia and guides to forbidden areas, creature and even daunting journeys only the most adventurous will be lucky enough to discover.
