Talent Trees

Talents represent unique abilities, perks, and enhancements your character can acquire and develop as they advance. Organized into {NUM_TALENT_TREES=14} specialized talent trees, each paired with a corresponding character profession, talents form thematic pathways that allow players to specialize and unlock distinct advantages shaping their playstyle. Each talent tree is deeply intertwined with its associated profession, forming the foundation of character specialization. Each talent tree contains a special profession bonus talent that grants bonuses to all checks related to that associated profession. Additionally, each talent tree is paired with a Profession Disposition, representing the specialized NPC roles that directly interact with player characters. Players earn talent points as they level up, allowing them to unlock new talents or advance through trees. By strategically selecting talents and investing in core attributes, players shape their journey and create unique builds adapted to any challenge the game presents.

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

Profession Dispositions: Each talent tree is paired with a Profession Disposition—a specialized NPC role that directly interacts with player characters. These represent consistent, thematic archetypes in the game world that your character will encounter and engage with.

How Profession Dispositions Work: When you select a talent tree and profession, you're not just defining your character's abilities—you're establishing the types of NPCs your character naturally works with, relies on, or encounters. A character with the Investigator profession works alongside a Guard (Profession Disposition), employs a Scribe's services, or hires a Transporter for travel. Profession Dispositions provide mechanical and narrative grounding, making your character's expertise meaningful within a living world. See the Profession Dispositions list for all NPC roles paired with talent trees.
