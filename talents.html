Talent Trees

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

1. Crypt Raider

Intrepid explorers specializing in the perilous depths of ancient dungeons and hidden tombs.

→ Ruin Keeper

Your study of ancient artifacts, relics, and runes allows you to understand the origin and the properties they hold. The more rare the object, the more difficult they are to identify. You gain a bonus† to all Artificer checks (see Professions).

2. Mole

Specializes in stealth, spying, and infiltration.

→ Master Locksmith

Your expertise in locks, mechanisms, and security systems is unmatched. You can open any lock, bypass complex security measures, and understand the principles of mechanical protection. You gain a bonus† to all Mole checks (see Professions).

3. Olympiad

Focuses on athletic and physical feats.

→ Master Athletic Trainer

Your knowledge of physical conditioning and athletic performance is legendary. You can teach others to reach peak physical condition and overcome limitations through proper technique and enhancement. You gain a bonus† to all Body Artist checks (see Professions).

4. Scout

Emphasizes exploration and survival.

→ Master Guide

Your expertise in leading expeditions and navigating the wilderness is renowned. You can safely lead groups through dangerous terrain, find the best routes, and ensure party survival in any environment. You gain a bonus† to all Hunter checks (see Professions).

5. Tactician

Specializes in strategic and leadership abilities.

→ Master Strategist

Your mastery of tactical planning and strategic warfare is unparalleled. You can design battle plans, analyze enemy movements, and coordinate complex operations with precision. You gain a bonus† to all Quartermaster checks (see Professions).

6. Theologist

Centers on faith, protection, and healing.

→ Master Priest

Your divine knowledge and spiritual authority are supreme. You can perform sacred rites, counsel the faithful, and channel divine power with masterful precision. You gain a bonus† to all Witchcrafter checks (see Professions).

7. Trader

Relates to negotiation, commerce, and social influence.

→ Master Merchant

Your mastery of commerce and trade is legendary. You understand market values, can broker the most complex deals, and always find profit in any transaction. You gain a bonus† to all Gem Crafter checks (see Professions).

8. Traveler

Features talents for movement, adaptation, and survival.

→ Master Navigator

Your expertise in navigation and wayfinding is unparalleled. You can chart courses through unmapped wilderness, calculate distances with precision, and never lose your way. You gain a bonus† to all Hauler checks (see Professions).

9. Bounty Hunter

Focuses on tracking, combat, and intimidation.

→ Master Interrogator

Your mastery of interrogation and information extraction is unparalleled. You can extract confessions, read lies with precision, and uncover secrets through sophisticated questioning techniques. You gain a bonus† to all Scientist checks (see Professions).

10. Hacker

Manipulates security systems and deciphers complex codes and mechanisms.

→ Master Hacker

Your mastery of systems and code is legendary. You can penetrate the most sophisticated security systems, decrypt encrypted data, and manipulate digital networks with supreme precision. You gain a bonus† to all Engineer checks (see Professions).

11. Thief

Navigates confined spaces, picks locks, and pockets items with precision.

→ Master Safecracker

Your mastery of locks, safes, and securing mechanisms is supreme. You can open any container, crack any code-lock, and bypass the most complex mechanical security systems. You gain a bonus† to all Tinker checks (see Professions).

12. Patriarch

Natural authority and command. Leadership through strength, presence, and primal hierarchy.

→ Master Leader

Your mastery of leadership and command is legendary. You can inspire movements, manage organizations, forge alliances, and guide your followers through any challenge with unshakeable authority. You gain a bonus† to all Shipwright checks (see Professions).

13. Performer

Masters of entertainment, music, and captivating audiences.

→ Master Performer

Your mastery of performance and entertainment is legendary. You can move crowds to tears, inspire movements through your art, and captivate any audience regardless of size or disposition. You gain a bonus† to all Enchanter checks (see Professions).

14. Leprechaun

Masters of fortune, trickery, and probability manipulation.

→ Luck Manipulation

Represents chance and fortune, allowing for various amounts of extra chances in both combat and non-combat situations. Luck rolls 1d4 and adds the Luck score to the result, stacking with basic Hit, Defense rolls, all Trait checks, Skills, Talents, all DIFS, and probability-based outcomes like loot drops and crafting. You gain a bonus† to all Alchemist checks (see Professions).
