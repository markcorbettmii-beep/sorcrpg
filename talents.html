Talent Trees

†Every Talent Tree has talents that fine the Talent's Score as a bonus stacking to all checks related to that Professions Score.

i. Bounty Hunter (†Associated Profession - Engineer)

Focuses on tracking, combat, and intimidation.

a. Cheap Shot: Execute surprise attacks against unsuspecting opponents. Gain bonuses when striking from ambush or exploiting openings.

Full Talent Tree

ii. Crypt Raider (†Associated Profession - Archeologist)

Intrepid explorers specializing in the perilous depths of ancient dungeons and hidden tombs.

a. Bandwagoner: You boost morale and cohesion in chaotic situations, providing party member movement toward the group with bonuses to their DEFS against threats within their path.

Full Talent Tree

iii. Leprechaun (†Associated Profession - Tinker)

Masters of fortune, trickery, and probability manipulation.

a. Charm and Charisma: Your natural charisma and winning personality endear you to others. Gain bonuses to social rolls and persuasion attempts.

Full Talent Tree

iv. Olympiad (†Associated Profession - Cook)

Focuses on athletic and physical feats.

a. Athleticism: Master a variety of athletic skills: Climb (scale vertical surfaces), Sprint (burst speed), Acrobatics (graceful movement), Vault (leap over obstacles), Swim (water navigation), Jump (impressive distances).

Full Talent Tree

v. Patriarch (†Associated Profession - Blacksmith)

Natural authority and command. Leadership through strength, presence, and primal hierarchy.

a. Authority's Shield: Your leadership extends protection to those you lead. Grant defensive bonuses to nearby allies and reduce their vulnerability to fear and psychological effects.

Full Talent Tree

vi. Performer (†Associated Profession - Enchanter)

Masters of entertainment, music, and captivating audiences.

a. Acting: Portray characters with depth and believability through performance. Gain bonuses to deception rolls when adopting roles or personas.

Full Talent Tree

vii. Quartermaster (†Associated Profession - Shipwright)

Air and Sea navigation. Masters of ships, maritime commerce, and seafaring adventures.

a. Freighter: Master the art of cargo management and maritime commerce. Navigate trade routes, negotiate shipping deals, and efficiently transport goods across seas and skies.

Full Talent Tree

viii. Scout (†Associated Profession - Hunter)

Emphasizes exploration and survival.

a. Decipher Map: Read and interpret maps, plans, and charts with expert precision. Understand symbols, scales, and geographical references.

Full Talent Tree

ix. Tactician (†Associated Profession - Hacker)

Specializes in strategic and leadership abilities.

a. Adaptability: Switch tactics efficiently, responding to changing circumstances with grace and precision.

Full Talent Tree

x. Theologist (†Associated Profession - Witchcrafter)

Centers on faith, protection, and healing.

a. Bless Water/Wood: Create holy items through ritualistic blessing. Convert ordinary water into holy water or bless wooden items for sacred purposes.

Full Talent Tree

xi. Thief (†Associated Profession - Alchemist)

Navigates confined spaces, picks locks, and pockets items with precision.

a. Escape Artist: Free yourself from bonds, restraints, and confined spaces through flexibility and technique.

Full Talent Tree

xii. Traveler (†Associated Profession - Hauler)

Features talents for movement, adaptation, and survival. Now includes trading and commerce talents.

a. Appraise: Accurately assess the value of items, services, and properties. Determine fair prices and identify undervalued treasures.

Full Talent Tree
