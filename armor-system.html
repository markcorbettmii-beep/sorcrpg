Armor System (AS Modifiers)

When you’re attacked, the attacker rolls d100. If the roll is equal to or greater than your (modified) Armor Score (AS), the attack hits. If the roll is lower, you block or mitigate the attack.

Vulnerabilities and resistances directly adjust your AS for that attack type:

  • Vulnerability: Subtract the penalty from your AS.
  • Resistance: Add the bonus to your AS.

Example:
You wear plate armor (AS 45).
• A blunt attack targets you. Plate is vulnerable to blunt (-5 AS): Your AS is 40 for this attack.
• A slashing attack targets you. Plate is resistant to slashing (+3 AS): Your AS is 48 for this attack.
The attacker rolls d100 and compares to your modified AS.
Note: These values are for basic, common armor. Higher tier, magical, or enhanced armor can have higher AS or different resistance/vulnerability modifiers.
