Panic Level Progression

Panic Levels and Effects
Panic Level Description Penalty to D100 Roll Next Step
Level 1 Fail initial Panic check -1 Next turn, roll again with -1 penalty
Level 2 Fail second Panic check at Level 1 -2 Next turn, roll again with -2 penalty
Level 3 Fail third Panic check at Level 2 -3 Next turn, roll again with -3 penalty
Level 4 Fail fourth Panic check at Level 3 -4 Next turn, roll again with -4 penalty
Level 5 Fail fifth Panic check at Level 4 -5 Next turn, roll again with -5 penalty
Success at any level Panic ends or regresses N/A If at Level 2 or higher, go back to previous level; if at Level 1, panic ends
Stabilization If the character succeeds the Panic Check N/A Can take free movement action, then end turn
