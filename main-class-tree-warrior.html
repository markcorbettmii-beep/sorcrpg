Warrior (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Comprehensive martial masters who have developed mastery across every armament type, from heavy armor and shield work through every blade discipline, spear, lance, and even command-LVL battlefield magic, Warriors are the most armor-flexible and weapon-diverse class in Essentia, capable of filling any martial role while outperforming dedicated specialists in their chosen lane at high LVLs.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Battle Stance, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Tactical Mastery, Once Per Day the Warrior adapts mid-combat, gaining a bonus to Hit and Damage equal to their Grit SCR until the end of the encounter.

Extra 1d6 + LVL physical damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Warriors can Flourish their weapon that lowers their target's Defense at a number equal to their Strength SCR.

Warriors get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Warriors get +4 plus their VIGOR SCR on Endurance checks.

Warrior Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Armorer - A defense-focused Warrior who turns armor and shield into an unbreakable wall.
  a. Bulwark - An Armorer devoted entirely to holding a line no enemy can push through.
  b. Champion - An Armorer who leads by example, standing firm at the front of every fight.
  c. Ironclad - An Armorer who trusts nothing but the heaviest armor available.
ii. Blade Master - A precision-focused Warrior devoted to mastering the blade above all else.
  a. Duelist - A Blade Master who favors precision and timing over raw power.
  b. Gladiator - A Blade Master trained for single combat, thriving in one-on-one duels.
  c. Slayer - A Blade Master whose every strike is aimed to end the fight quickly.
iii. Warlord - A commanding Warrior who channels rune-etched weapons and raw battlefield authority into overwhelming force.
  a. Runeblades - A Warlord who fights with a pair of rune-etched weapons, each empowering the other.
  b. Stormbringer - A Warlord who channels raw storm-force directly into their weapon.
  c. Sun Thrower - A Warlord who hurls sun-charged weapons, striking from range as readily as up close.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Versatile (Heavy through Light).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Warrior's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
