Welcome

Traverse through extraordinary journeys in SORC’s Into the Uncharted Worlds of Essentia. This tool by Ogre Adventurer will transport you through a captivating solar system, introducing you to its 13 planets, including the starting point for your epic campaigns, the 4th planet, Zailister (Zail).

Game Setting

Slayers of Rings § Crowns is a sci-fantasy TTRPG that takes Players through 13 diverse realms. SORC features exciting combat with limb-specific targeting, killing sprees, and burning streaks. Traverse planets where technology advances furthest from the main sun Adoria, and ancient magic is strongest near the sun, harnessing its magical properties. Play as one of many choices of classes and races, empower your Character, gain reputation, and define your legacy through our prestige system!

Planet Descriptions (Summary)

  • Glacies et Ignis: Elemental planet, extreme heat, home to golems and elementals, and to the Emberscale and the Primal.
  • Tenue: Only barely closer to Adoria than Zailister, yet cooler thanks to the dense magical overgrowth blanketing the surface; thin atmosphere, blinding light day and night, no moon, home to all Fae, connected to Zailister through the enchanted Veilwood.
  • Tribus: Habitable only below ground, ice and vapor, blind natives using echolocation.
  • Zailister (Zail): Starting world, diverse and free, “police of the world.”
  • Quintus Elementum: Second largest planet, "Subterranean Earth."
  • Hexagonum: No native life, lies between Quintus Elementum and Citrine Candenti. Walked by the Ossein, who died there and kept going.
  • Citrine Candenti: Most Earth-like, 6 seasons, 3 moons, advanced.
  • Octavo: No rotation, hot and cold sides, unique agriculture.
  • Angeligla: Largest planet (14,000 km), three identical triplet moons (Elysium I, II, III), swirling gases.
  • Corpus Caeleste: Large moon Spectros with seasonal color shifts (red, purple, blue, auburn), inhabited by ghosts and aberrations, and home of the Caelen.
  • Undeximus: Tech-advanced, near Tawdry Dwarf, home of the Futurum.
  • Omnè Malum: Gas giant (6,000 km), densely saturated with population, floating cities in atmosphere, subterranean core facilities, most technologically advanced civilization. Home of the Omnè and, older than any record of them, the Drevane.
  • Tredeki: Futuristic, technological pinnacle. Home of the Tredeki, the Imperials, the Leshy, and the Skrath.

Cosmic Dynamics & Lore

The duality of Adoria and Tawdry Dwarf shapes the fate of all life. Each planet features unique geography, climates, and challenges. Epic labyrinths, demon lords, and cosmic puzzles await those who adventure through the gates to the underworlds of Essentia.

Time is measured in Eons of Adoria (EA). The current year is 10,023 EA, the Age of Reflection.

Ages of Essentia

Essentia's history is divided into three great ages, marked by pivotal moments that shaped the fate of all life across the planets:

  • Age of Creation (Ancient): The first cognitive and symbolic life forms capable of civilization emerged on Omnè Malum and Tredeki, though various life forms existed on other planets. (Undead and spiritual aberrations are excluded from this classification as they are not native life.) Civilizations rose from the void, adapting to the unique conditions of their worlds.
  • Age of Prophecy (9,044 EA): Zailister witnessed a cataclysmic event where nineteen mystical crowns fell from the stars, forged from magic particles and the debris of the planet's rings. These crowns became symbols of true leadership, and the races of Zail spent centuries seeking their rightful bearers.
  • Age of Reflection (10,023 EA - Present): The current era, marked by the consolidation of power among crowned leaders, the spread of knowledge, and growing awareness of Tawdry Dwarf's dwindling energy. The Omnè race accelerates technological advancement to preserve life as Tawdry Dwarf faces its inevitable fate.

Natural Disaster System (SORC)

To determine if a natural disaster occurs:
GM rolls 3d20 and combines the result (e.g., 2157). Each disaster has a value or range (e.g., 1–80). If your roll falls within that value, the disaster happens.

Probability:
1–80 = 1% chance
1–800 = 10% chance
A single number (1) = 0.0125%

Refer to the SORC Probability Loot, Crafting n Discovery System for more details.

Each campaign in SORC is unique. The choices of characters and Game Masters shape Essentia for millennia to come, through conquest, exploration, and the forging of legendary reputations.

Solar System Overview

The solar system is a grand cosmic expanse characterized by the intricate dance of celestial bodies governed by the gravitational forces of two suns, Adoria and Tawdry Dwarf. At its heart lies Adoria, a vibrant and life-giving star, shining brightly and sustaining the inner planets, where diverse ecosystems and advanced civilizations thrive. The warmth and energy it provides are crucial for the survival of life, fostering an environment rich in variations, cultures, and technologies.

In stark contrast, Tawdry Dwarf is the cooler and dimmer twin, slowly dying and drifting toward its closest planet. Once a mighty hydrogen-burning star, it now exists as a white dwarf amid the remnants of its previous glory. The dramatic changes in Tawdry Dwarf have profoundly affected the outer planets, introducing significant climatic shifts and destabilizing orbits as it sheds its layers.

The collision of these two solar forces creates a unique dynamic; while the inner planets flourish under the nurturing light of Adoria, the outer planets grapple with the declining influence of Tawdry Dwarf. The gravitational interplay results in a complex system of orbital paths, shaping the movement and interactions of the celestial bodies.

The Two Suns

  • Adoria (Sun One): A large and bright star, comparable to Earth's sun, providing essential energy to the inner planets. Its warmth and intensity are critical for maintaining life and supporting advanced technologies. Adoria is the primary source of magical energy across Essentia, with magic strongest near the sun and fading with distance.
  • Tawdry Dwarf (Sun Two): A cooler and dimmer dying star that has entered the later stages of its life cycle. Once a hydrogen-burning star similar to Adoria, it has exhausted its fuel and transitioned through a red giant phase, shedding its outer layers to become a white dwarf. Its declining energy affects the outer planets, forcing technological and social adaptation among their inhabitants.

The Thirteen Planets (Outward from Adoria)

  • 1. Glacies et Ignis (12,500 km): Elemental planet, extremely hot, home to golems and fire elementals, and to the Emberscale and the Primal. Moon: Asterok (rich in valuable elements and magic; magic extraction occurs here through elemental beings during day and night cycles).
  • 2. Tenue (12,500 km): Cooler than expected due to dense magical overgrowth, blinding light day and night. Home to all Fae. No moon. Connected to Zailister through the enchanted Veilwood forest.
  • 3. Tribus (10,000 km): Habitable only in subterranean depths, ice and vapor layers, blind natives using echolocation. Moon: Cavos.
  • 4. Zailister (Zail) (11,000 km): Starting world for adventures, diverse races, balanced between magic and technology. Moons: Elder I, Elder II. Home of the prophecy of nineteen crowns.
  • 5. Quintus Elementum (9,500 km): Also known as "Elè Quint" or "Subterranean Earth." Moon: Harmony.
  • 6. Hexagonum: No native life, lies between Quintus Elementum and Citrine Candenti. Walked by the Ossein, who died there and kept going. Moons: Mercatia I, Mercatia II.
  • 7. Citrine Candenti (12,000 km): Most Earth-like of the system, 6 distinct seasons, most advanced of the inner planets. Moons: Luminara (silver-pinkish glow), Emberglow (flame-crimson shimmer), Azureveil (sky-teal flicker).
  • 8. Octavo (7,000 km): Tidally locked, no rotation. Hot side faces Tawdry Dwarf with glowing lava; cold side receives only reflected moonlight. The meridian supports agriculture and resource farming. Moon: Null.
  • 9. Angeligla (14,000 km): Largest planet in the system. Swirling gases with a mysterious core. Moons: Elysium I, II, III (identical triplet moons).
  • 10. Corpus Caeleste (13,500 km): Inhabited by ghosts, aberrations, and spiritual entities, and home of the Caelen, who took Omnè Astral Cabal vessels off the Cabal that invaded them. Moon: Spectros (large moon, changes seasonal colors, red, purple, blue, auburn).
  • 11. Undeximus (12,800 km): Tech-advanced civilization, near Tawdry Dwarf, relies on advanced technology for survival. Home of the Futurum, who stand allied with the Tredeki, at war with the Omnè, and at a trading stance with the tenth planet and every world before it that will have them, Ignis excepted, whose people do not trust technology and will not deal with them. Moon: Nihilus.
  • 12. Omnè Malum (6,000 km): Gas giant, densely saturated with population. Floating cities span the entire atmosphere, with subterranean core facilities exploiting the rocky core. Built to absolute maximum capacity, edge to edge, peak to peak. Technologically advanced Omnè civilization dominates, with subterranean anarchists and rebel militias hidden within underground populated zones. The Drevane were here first, older than any record the planet keeps. Atlantis-style underwater cities occupy ocean floors. Moons: Varanis (high-tech overflow metropolis housing feral and rational native inhabitants; they are rational beings who underestimated Omnè's rapid building capacity; some militia refugees seek alliance with Varanis natives, warning them of Omnè's intentions to conquer the entire moon); Krakus (mining moon rich in elements and valuable resources; only 10% developed; inhabited by feral, strong-willed, rational natives who demand Omnè stop expansion at 50% and have warned other races of Omnè's plans to eventually take the entire moon).
  • 13. Tredeki (6,000 km): Futuristic and tech-focused, coldest planet, home to advanced civilizations. Home of the Tredeki, the Imperials, the Leshy, and the Skrath, and allied with the Futurum of the eleventh. Although part Human, the Tredeki are a race on their own, they share human blood. Moon: Morana (huge frozen moon, half Tredeki's size, the only moon of Tredeki).

Cosmic Principles

  • Magic Distribution: Magic flows from Adoria's life-giving radiance. Magic is strongest on planets closest to Adoria (Ignis through Zailister) and fades further away. Only on Ignis does magic have a permanent storage mechanism through elemental beings extracting it from Adoria's light and Asterok's mystical properties.
  • Technology Gradient: Technology advances furthest from Adoria. Inner planets rely on magic and steel; outer planets (8-13) have developed sophisticated tech. Planets 10-13, under threat from Tawdry Dwarf's decline, have achieved technological mastery in survival systems.
  • Orbital Mechanics: Tawdry Dwarf lies between planets 9 and 10, providing dwindling resources to planets 8-13. The two suns create complex gravitational patterns, stabilizing orbits of all 13 planets while creating unique seasonal and climatic conditions on each.

Legion Forms

A people that marshals for war fields a Legion form, named by placing its Legion title in front of the race. The titles in use are Hell, Uber, Mighty, Elite, Burrowing, and Fiendish, as in Hell Orcus, Elite Ogres, and Burrowing Gnomes. A Legion title names the war body a people puts in the field, not a separate people, and a Legion form is not a playable race.

The Tomes of Essentia

Knowledge of Essentia is preserved in three primary comprehensive tomes:

  • "Coddled By Magic: Adoria's Light" covers Planets 1-5 (Glacies et Ignis, Tenue, Tribus, Zailister, Quintus Elementum), focusing on Adoria's influence, ancient magic, and magical civilizations. Zailister and Adoria serve as the primary focus of this tome.
  • "The Median Zone: Between Life & Death" covers Planets 6-9 (Hexagonum, Citrine Candenti, Octavo, Angeligla), exploring the transition zone between the two suns' influences and the diverse ecosystems that thrive there.
  • "Tawdry Dwarf & Beyond: The Dying Star's Shadow" covers Planets 10-13 (Corpus Caeleste, Undeximus, Omnè Malum, Tredeki), chronicling the effects of Tawdry Dwarf's decline and the technological mastery of civilizations fighting for survival as their sun dies.

The Trinity of Powers

Across all thirteen worlds of Essentia, the divine powers shape the destinies of mortals. Adoria, as the supreme sun-god and source of all magic, holds lawful alignment and shows particular favor to Zailister. Tawdry Dwarf, once lawful like her sister, has grown bitter over millennia of slow death, developing an evil alignment as a black hole develops behind her deteriorating mass. These cosmic forces influence all pantheons, deities, and celestial beings across the realms.
