Magic User (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Arcane scholars and innate tricksters who bend reality through illusion, transformation, and raw magical force, Magic Users sacrifice all physical durability for nearly limitless manipulation of both the material and immaterial world, equally comfortable turning an enemy into a toad, stepping sideways through time, or opening a rift to somewhere else entirely.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Spellweaver's Trance, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Reality Fracture, Once Per Day the Magic User tears a seam in reality, dealing arcane damage equal to twice their Knowledge SCR to one target.

Extra 1d6 + LVL arcane damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Magic Users can Flourish their arcane focus that lowers their target's Defense at a number equal to their Wit SCR.

Magic Users get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Magic Users get +4 plus their KNOWLEDGE SCR on Knowledge checks involving arcane lore.

Magic User Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Illusionist - A Magic User who reshapes reality and self alike, mastering illusion and transformation.
  a. Astral Weaver - An Illusionist who weaves astral threads into illusions few can see through.
  b. Metamorphiser - An Illusionist who reshapes their own form to fit any need.
  c. Shapeshifter - An Illusionist who takes on another's shape entirely, not just its likeness.
ii. Mage - A destructive Magic User who channels raw arcane power into devastating, direct spellcraft.
  a. Blood Mage - A Mage who fuels devastating spells with their own vitality.
  b. Chronomancer - A Mage who bends time itself, slowing enemies or hastening allies.
  c. RuinMaster - A Mage who specializes in outright destruction, LVLing anything in their path.
iii. Trickster - A cunning Magic User who bends reality with misdirection and illusion as much as raw spellcraft. Favors the Guile School, spells that seduce, charm, or coerce, altering another's mind to establish dominance over it.
  a. Arcana - A Trickster devoted to raw sleight-of-hand magic, bending small truths into big illusions.
  b. Jester - A Trickster who fights through misdirection, turning laughter into a weapon.
  c. Shadow Dancer - A Trickster who slips between light and shadow, never quite where they seem.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Delicate (Silk Robe).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Magic User's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
