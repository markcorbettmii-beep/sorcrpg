Bard (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Musical enchanters who blend performance and combat into a single flowing art, Bards weave spells through melody, rhythm, and song, inspiring allies to supernatural performance, confusing enemies into disarray, and reshaping the battlefield's emotional landscape through instruments that are as much weapon as they are art. Centyrs have a natural affinity for this class.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Bard's Cadence, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Crescendo, Once Per Day the Bard builds to a climactic verse, granting every ally within earshot an extra action on their next turn.

Extra 1d6 + LVL psychic damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Bards can Flourish their instrument to grant an ally advantage on their next Talent Roll.

Bards get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Bards get +4 plus their CHARISMA SCR on Persuasion checks.

Bard Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Percussionist - A percussion-driven Bard who overwhelms enemies with rhythm and volume rather than melody.
  a. Distorter - A Percussionist who twists rhythm itself, disorienting enemies with broken, off-beat drumming.
  b. Pounder - A Percussionist who drives allies forward with a war-drum's heavy, driving beat.
  c. Rattler - A Percussionist whose rapid rattling rhythm rattles enemy nerve as much as their ears.
ii. Serenade - A vocal Bard whose serenades charm and beguile, turning enemies into willing participants in the Bard's own song.
  a. Charmer - A Serenade who bends enemy will with a voice too beguiling to resist.
  b. Songbird - A Serenade whose clear, carrying voice steadies allies across the whole battlefield.
  c. Sprite - A playful Serenade who darts between allies, lifting spirits with quick, teasing verses.
iii. Strumalong - A string-instrument Bard whose melodies snare enemies in debts and favors they can't escape.
  a. Melodist - A Strumalong devoted to pure melody, weaving spellsong into every note.
  b. Plucker - A fast-fingered Strumalong who strikes as quickly with string-snaps as with words.
  c. Stroller - A wandering Strumalong who plays best on the move, never settling in one place.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Delicate or Light (Cloth, Light Leather).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Bard's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
