Racial Consumption

Racial Food and Water Consumption

Daily food and water consumption varies by race and is directly tied to maintenance fee calculations. Each character consumes food and water at a rate determined by their racial physiology, metabolism, and dietary requirements. These daily consumption requirements form the baseline for maintenance fee costs and must be factored into long-term survival and economic planning.

Baseline Reference: A Human consumes 1 unit of food and 1 unit of water per day. All other races adjust from this baseline according to their size category, metabolism, and special physiology.

Maintenance Fee Calculation: Daily maintenance fees are calculated by multiplying each character's racial consumption rates by the base cost per unit (which varies by location, availability, and quality). A Human character consumes 2 units total (1 food + 1 water) daily, forming the baseline for cost calculation. Characters requiring less consumption (e.g., Elves at 0.8 units each) pay proportionally less, while those requiring more (e.g., Ogres at 2.0 units each) pay proportionally more. Special dietary needs (carnivores requiring live meat, races using power sources) may increase costs or provide cost savings depending on local availability and character professions.

Inaptitude Integration: Some races list dietary Inaptitudes in their race cards reflecting their consumption patterns. For example, races that cannot digest certain food types, or species dependent on rare resources, list these as penalties or special requirements in their Innate Inaptitude sections. These mechanical limitations tie directly to the consumption table above.
