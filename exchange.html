Essentia Exchange

SORC's auction house split into three rooms: the Trading Post (fixed prices), the Bazaar (player-set prices), and the Black Market (hand trades and direct barter). Every Card carries a printed Coin value as the base price — no haggling in the Trading Post or Bazaar. Settled in in-game currency only; real money never moves between Players.
