Professions

Professions represent specialized skills and expertise your Character develops through focused practice and dedication. Professions are paired with corresponding Talent Trees, forming a symbiotic relationship where talents provide foundational abilities and professions deepen specialization and expertise through their core skills. Each Profession may grant bonuses when a character invests talent points into their associated Talent Tree's profession bonus talent, creating a direct mechanical link between talent development and professional mastery. By selecting complementary talents and professions, characters can develop deep expertise in their chosen fields and excel at tasks related to their professional focus.

Profession Bonus & Synergy: Each Talent Tree contains a Profession bonus talent that grants bonuses to all checks related to its associated Profession when the talent is advanced. This creates synergy—a mechanical advantage where investing in both a Talent Tree and its paired Profession yields greater returns than developing either alone. A character with Bounty Hunter talents who also pursues Engineering gains synergistic bonuses to their Engineering checks, allowing specialization through complementary skill development.

Refer to Basic Rules - Pairing on page 6

Multiple NPC Dispositions: Each Profession is compatible with multiple NPC Disposition types. A character pursuing a Profession may work with any of the listed Dispositions, establishing trade relationships, gathering specialized services, and unlocking unique opportunities with different NPC archetypes. For example, an Alchemist can collaborate with an Alchemist NPC (sharing transmutation expertise), a Scholar NPC (documenting discoveries), or an Inventor NPC (creating novel formulas). This flexibility allows players to tailor their professional networks and access diverse services based on their needs.

Alchemist (Paired Talent Tree - Thief)

Paired with Thief Talent Tree

Transmutes substances into potions, explosives, and elixirs with unpredictable effects.

Full Profession

NPC Dispositions: Chemist, Inventor

Disposition Logic: The Chemist understands molecular behavior at the same depth as the Alchemist—both transform raw matter into new forms. Chemists source rare reactive compounds Alchemists cannot obtain alone, while Alchemists teach Chemists about non-standard transmutation. The Inventor pairs because both experiment iteratively; Inventors build apparatus for Alchemical reactions, Alchemists create potent catalysts for Inventors' machines. Together, all three accelerate discovery.
