What is SORC?

Slayers of Rings & Crowns is a comprehensive tabletop fantasy RPG system built on 20+ years of game design experience. Whether you're a seasoned Game Master or discovering tabletop gaming for the first time, SORC offers three Rule Sets (Bare Bones, Modular, and Complex) that scale with your group's needs.

Explore the world of Essentia—a living, breathing fantasy realm with established history, cultures, and conflicts. Play as one of 37+ unique races, master 20 distinct classes, and forge your own legend through the choices you make in your campaigns. Every decision matters. Every story is unique.

FORGE YOUR STORY

From intimate tavern encounters to epic wars spanning continents, SORC adapts to your table. Run a single adventure, a sprawling campaign, or a living world where your players shape the outcome.

Three Rule Sets

Bare Bones for fast play, Modular for balanced complexity, and Complex for maximum depth. Pick your style.

37+ Playable Races

From Humans and Elves to Trolls and Ossein. Every race has distinct abilities, cultures, and story hooks.

20 Class Archetypes

Fighters, Mages, Rogues, Priests, and more. Deep class trees with meaningful choices at every level.

D100 Resolution System

Intuitive percentile-based mechanics for attacks, saves, and skill checks. Easy to learn, deep to master.

Four Worlds to Explore

Zailister, Ignis, Omnè, and Tredeki. Each with unique cultures, hazards, and adventure opportunities.

Extensive Documentation

Comprehensive rulebooks, GM guides, player resources, and campaign starters all freely available online.
