Slayers of Rings & Crowns (SORC)

By Ogre Adventurer

About

Slayers of Rings § Crowns © is a sci-fantasy TTRPG that takes players through 13 diverse realms. SorC features exciting combat with limb-specific targeting, killing sprees, and burning streaks. Traverse planets where technology advances furthest away from the main sun Adoria, while ancient magic is strongest near the sun, harnessing its magical properties.

SORC Beyond

SORC Beyond is the free digital companion platform for Slayers of Rings & Crowns. Create an account, join the forums, earn Community Points, and unlock features as your reputation in Essentia grows. There are no subscription fees.

A free lighter digital version of SorC is available — you supply your own components and get straight into play. For the full SORC Beyond experience including Online Lobbies, Card Trading, and the Auction House, a physical box set is required. Each box set includes a redemption code that unlocks these features on your account.

Online Lobbies support up to 20 players. GMs and Player Characters must each pass their respective role assessment before creating a lobby. Both GMs and PCs can create lobbies, but a GM must be present before a session begins. From within a lobby, private rooms of up to 5 players can be created for focused sessions. Joining a lobby you've been recruited into is free — no box set required.

Trials of Combat — PvP arenas, battlegrounds, and coliseum tiers — are accessible through the free digital version. No box set required to compete.

Point Integrity Rules

  • Points are only awarded for posts and threads that remain up and undeleted for at least 24 hours.
  • Points are revoked if you delete your own content after earning them. Points are not revoked if content is removed by an Admin or Owner.
  • Posts flagged and removed by an Admin result in a −10 pt deduction.
  • Posts that receive 3 or more reports are hidden pending Admin review – points are frozen until a decision is made.
  • Uploaded content must be Admin-approved before points are awarded.
  • Spam, duplicate posts, and bad-faith activity earn no points.

The Black Market

A live player-run lobby where exchanges happen in real time without the Auction House interface. Access requires earning the Trader achievement, which is unlocked by reaching a threshold of combined Community Point transactions and SORC Store purchases. All Black Market exchanges are server-recorded. No real money may be transferred between players on SORC servers – only in-game currency earned through recorded Online Lobby sessions is valid for player-to-player trade.

The SORC Store

Rare Cards & Module Box Sets

Physical box sets and rare cards are sold for cash through the SORC Store. When you purchase a physical box set, you receive a code that allows you to download its digital version for free. These digital versions are included at no extra cost, providing an easy way to expand your collection digitally. Additionally, there are more affordable, basic digital versions available – these are simplified, digital-only options designed for quick access and play.

Free PDF Modules

Lighter digital versions of official modules are available to all accounts at no cost. A great way to get started and earn your first Community Points through downloads and community uploads.

Community Points in the Store

Community Points can be spent on select basic items and raffle tickets in the SORC Store. It typically takes a significant number of points to make a purchase this way – but it is entirely possible for a dedicated member of the community to access store items without spending real money.

Online Lobbies & Recorded Sessions

How In-Game Currency Works

In-game currency is earned exclusively during SORC Beyond Online Lobby sessions where all activity is server-recorded. It cannot be earned outside of live recorded sessions. Currency is used at the Auction House and the Black Market – and no real money transfers between players are permitted on SORC servers under any circumstances.

Items Eligible for Trade

Only items obtained during server-recorded SORC Beyond sessions are eligible for auction or trade. This ensures the integrity of every transaction on the platform and keeps the economy fair for all players.

SORC Lotto

Our community lottery allows members – whether Basic or Pro – to purchase raffle tickets using Community Points for a chance to win prizes, including digital cards, store content, rare items, and exclusive merchandise, with three tiers of rewards designed to reward participation and engagement across all levels of membership.

Additional Features & Tools

  • Character Profile Page: Create, customize, and manage digital character sheets. Each character has a unique ID, allowing for secure storage of stats, achievements, and inventory, supporting characters with identical names.
  • Trials of Combat: Test your skills in PvP arenas, battlegrounds (land, sea, air), coliseum tiers based on your combat score, 1v1 duels, and Pet Parties up to 5 players. These modes support many players fighting simultaneously, with options for safe practice or deadly duels. Characters that die require resurrection by ring judges to re-enter.
  • Auction House & Black Market: Trade, buy, and sell rare items, gear, and collectibles. The Black Market is a live, player-run marketplace for real-time exchanges, unlocked by earning the Trader achievement.
  • SorC Store: Access physical and digital cards, books, character sheets, and more. Hard cards include QR codes for digital versions; digital cards can be earned in-game.
  • Server Support & Cinematics: Each character's stats and items are uploaded to servers. Adventure modules provide cues for streaming audio/image cues for GMs.
  • Online RNG: Generate random numbers for remote campaigns or verifying card acquisitions.
  • Videos: Rulebook tutorials, walkthroughs, and official content videos.
  • Tavern Lobbies & Voice Support: Gather with players, find groups, teleport between regions, and chat via voice in private or public rooms. Tools are available to report bad behavior or offensive names.
  • SORC Ambiance App: Downloadable app for GMs with images, music queues, monster voices, and ambiance cues.
  • Call to Arms: Signal for help during active sessions or teleport to taverns/towns using your Wayfarer Compass if available.
  • Characters Home: Customizable space to display trophies, achievements, and decorations.
  • Marketplace & Trading: Buy, sell, and trade items or characters with community points, including the Black Market for real-time exchanges.
