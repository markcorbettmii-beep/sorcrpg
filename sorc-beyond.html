Slayers of Rings & Crowns (SORC)

By Ogre Adventurer

About

Slayers of Rings & Crowns © is a sci-fantasy TTRPG that takes Players through 13 diverse realms. SORC features exciting combat with limb-specific targeting, killing sprees, and burning streaks. Traverse planets where technology advances furthest away from the main sun Adoria, while ancient magic is strongest near the sun, harnessing its magical properties.

Roles & Membership Status

These are two separate things. Your role describes who you are at the table or in the platform. Your membership status describes what platform features you have access to. One does not determine the other.

Roles

Roles are earned through assessment and reflect your function in the game.

RoleAbbr.Description
Civilian CIV A registered member who has not yet completed a role assessment. Can browse, post in forums, and participate in the community.
Player PC A verified Player who has passed the Player assessment. Can join Online Lobbies and participate in private room sessions as a Player character.
Game Master GM A verified Game Master who has passed the GM assessment. Can host Online Lobbies and run private room sessions launched from them. A GM must be present before any session begins. GMs can message any member directly without a request, they need to reach Players quickly for session coordination.

Membership Status

Membership status is determined by whether you have purchased a box set. It is not tied to your role or your Community Points total.

Example: A GM with Basic status and a PC with Pro status are both valid combinations. Your role is earned through an assessment; your membership is unlocked with a box set purchase.

SORC Beyond

SORC Beyond is the free digital companion platform for Slayers of Rings & Crowns. Create an account, download the free PDF module to become a Basic member, join the forums, earn Community Points, and unlock features as your reputation in Essentia grows. There are no subscription fees.

For the full SORC Beyond experience-including Private Campaign Rooms, the Essentia Exchange, Character's Home, and the SORC Ambiance App, a physical box set is required. Each box set includes a redemption code that upgrades your account to Pro status.

Online Lobbies support up to 20 potential Players and exist for recruiting and party assembly via text chat only. GMs and Player Characters must each pass their respective role assessment before creating a lobby. Both GMs and PCs can create lobbies, but a GM must be present before a session begins. From within a lobby, a GM can launch a private room of up to 5 Players-this is where the recruited party actually plays, with voice chat, digital dice, and the rest of the session toolset. Joining a lobby you've been recruited into is free-no box set required.

Trials of Combat - PvP arenas, battlegrounds, and coliseum modes - are accessible to all members including Basic. No box set required to compete. There is no physical or mechanical advantage for Pro members in PvP - no pay to win. All combat rewards are earned in-game only. Pro members (box set) do gain access to exclusive cosmetic skins, a longer leaderboard (top 200 vs. top 100 for Basic), a fuller achievements and trophies list, and Practice Mode. Practice Mode uses fizzled spells, wooden weapons, and laser tag for ranged combat - whether the setting is sci-fi, archery, or gunpowder - so Players can train against NPCs without real stakes.

The SORC Lottery is open to all members, Basic and Pro. Raffle tickets are purchased using Community Points-no cash required. Winners receive real prizes, including an annual box set giveaway.

Basic Membership

Basic membership is free. Create an account, verify your email, and download the free PDF module to activate it, that download is what turns a new account into a Basic member. Basic gives you a community profile and username, full forum access, free PDF module downloads, digital dice, Community Points, Tavern Lobbies, Trials of Combat, server-backed character profiles, and free official blank character sheets to print at home. There are no subscription fees and no cash is ever required. A physical box set upgrades an account to Pro, but nothing below is locked behind it.

Point Integrity Rules

  • Points are only awarded for posts and threads that remain up and undeleted for at least 24 hours.
  • Points are revoked if you delete your own content after earning them. Points are not revoked if content is removed by an Admin or Owner.
  • Posts flagged and removed by an Admin result in a −10 pt deduction.
  • Posts that receive 3 or more reports are hidden pending Admin review-points are frozen until a decision is made.
  • Uploaded content must be Admin-approved before points are awarded.
  • Spam, duplicate posts, and bad-faith activity earn no points.

SORC Cards

SORC uses a comprehensive card-based system to track abilities, equipment, companions, and more. All members can earn and acquire ranked SORC Cards through gameplay. Box sets include exclusive cards unavailable through any other means: Lore Cards with QR codes, module-specific cards, and cards of ranks matched to the box set's level. Every box set comes with essential Cards in both Common and Uncommon ranks, complete with stats, lore, and a reference code for each card.

Each Card has its original base stats, conditions, and rank. Upgradable Cards, such as armaments-are paired with a ref. Card (reference card) used to track enhancements, modifications, buffs, curses, and special traits over time. Ref. Cards can be downloaded digitally from the website or ordered by mail using the catalog in the box set.

Lore Cards & QR Codes

Lore Cards are made from high-quality matte card stock and feature QR codes that link to their digital versions. The QR code must be refreshed periodically to maintain digital access. Players should keep track of their Lore Cards and avoid losing them.

Cards can be traded through the Trading Post (Pro members only). Once a card is traded on the server, digital access transfers to the new owner and the original card's QR code is permanently invalidated. Players may keep the physical card at home, but digital access-including linking to uploaded character sheets-follows the server record, not the physical card.

Lost Common and Uncommon Cards can be re-downloaded for free using your box set code, or ordered through the catalog for a fee. Cards of Rare rank and above cannot be re-issued.

Online Lobbies & Recorded Sessions

Joining a Lobby

When a Player requests to join a lobby, their digital character sheet is automatically submitted to the lobby creator for review. The creator can view the applicant's server-verified achievements, prestige, gear, fellowship affiliations, and session history before choosing to approve or deny the request. All data on a submitted sheet is proven, it reflects only what the Character earned through recorded SORC Beyond sessions, not self-reported or home-created content.

Roll Integrity & Recorded Dice

Every roll inside a private room is a confirmed digital roll. To ensure fairness, the Game Master must type into chat what is being rolled for before any Player touches the dice. Premature rolls-any roll made before the GM declares the intent-are automatically disregarded by the server. Each character has their own assigned dice, and rolls are tied to their individual account record. Any rolls made out of order or in violation of initiative are omitted from the session log and do not count toward a character's record. Rolls only occur inside private rooms-lobbies are for recruiting and party assembly and do not generate confirmed digital rolls.

How In-Game Currency Works

In-game currency is earned exclusively inside private room sessions launched from an Online Lobby, where all activity is server-recorded. The lobby itself is only for recruiting and party assembly-no currency is earned there. It cannot be earned outside of live recorded private room sessions. Currency is used at the Trading Post-no real money transfers between Players are permitted on SORC servers under any circumstances.

Items Eligible for Trade

Only items obtained during server-recorded private room sessions are eligible for trade at the Trading Post. Lobby activity alone does not earn eligible items. This ensures the integrity of every transaction and keeps the in-game economy fair for all Players.

Trade Chat

The Trade tab in the lobby chat panel is where players can buy, sell, and negotiate trades in real-time while recruiting. Trade Chat works alongside direct lobbies using three simple tags:

  • WTS:Item (Want To Sell) — A player offers an item at its fixed in-game currency value. The price is printed on the item's SORC Card and is not negotiable. Interested buyers accept or decline the offer.
  • WTB:Item (Want To Buy) — A player wants to purchase an item and offers the in-game currency value. Again, the price comes from the item's card—no haggling. Sellers accept or decline.
  • WTT:Give>Want (Want To Trade) — A player offers one item and requests another in exchange. Both parties can negotiate by countering with different items, allowing them to find a trade both are comfortable with.

Inspecting Items: When you see a trade in the Trade tab, you can click the item name to inspect its SORC Card—showing rank, stats, abilities, and the exact Coin value the trade is priced at. This ensures both sides know exactly what they're trading before either party commits.

Hand Trade Negotiation: When a buyer or seller is interested in a WTT: trade, they can open a direct Hand Trade negotiation window. The two parties exchange counter-offers until both accept the deal, at which point the trade settles and is posted to the lobby's activity log for GM oversight.

Note: Trade Chat is exclusively for trading. Recruiting, social chat, and session coordination belong in the Main lobby chat and World Chat. Trade messages are limited to 60 characters to keep offers clear and concise.

SORC Lottery

Our community lottery is open to all members-Basic and Pro alike. Raffle tickets are purchased using Community Points for a chance to win prizes including digital cards, store content, rare items, and exclusive merchandise. No cash required.

Digital Character Sheets

Home Sheets vs. Digital Sheets

Just as with SORC Cards, character sheets created at home cannot be uploaded to SORC Beyond-home-made sheets are for home use only and carry no server verification. However, blank official character sheets are available to all members, including Basic, to save to their device and print out for free.

What a Digital Character Sheet Contains

A Player's digital character sheet is assembled entirely from data on our servers. It reflects only what the Character has genuinely acquired through recorded SORC Beyond sessions-gear, cards, achievements, prestige, and fellowship memberships that were proven on the server, not claimed at home. Nothing unverified appears on a digital sheet.

Saving & Printing Your Digital Sheet

Digital character sheets can be saved and exported as a printable document. The printout includes the Character's gear, fellowship affiliations, and their current standing-their role, prestige level, and session history-all drawn from the server record at the time of export.

The Essentia Exchange

The Essentia Exchange is the platform's Player-to-Player exchange hub, available exclusively to Pro members (box set required). Here, Players can buy, sell, and trade SORC Cards and in-game items earned during recorded private room sessions. Three rooms make up the Exchange: the Trading Post (fixed prices), the Bazaar (player-set prices), and the Black Market (direct hand trades and barter).

No real money may be transferred between Players on SORC servers. Only in-game currency earned through recorded private room sessions is valid for Player-to-Player trade. All transactions are server-recorded. When a card is traded, digital access transfers to the new owner and the original QR code is permanently invalidated.

Physical box sets are sold through the SORC Store. Purchasing a box set is the only official way to acquire SORC Cards and the only path to Pro membership. Each box set includes a redemption code for your account.
