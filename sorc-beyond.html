SorC Beyond

The digital companion suite for Slayers of Rings & Crowns — extending your tabletop adventure into the online world with server-backed tools, remote play features, and a growing community platform.

SorC Beyond is early in development. Features marked Coming Soon will be introduced progressively. All features listed here will be presented through our forums until the full server is complete. Create a free account to get started.
BASIC — Free

Included with every account

Server-Backed Character Profiles

Each character receives a unique hidden ID number, allowing multiple characters to share the same name while keeping achievements, items, and statistics tied to their individual ID — not their name. Your character's history is yours alone.

Online RNG — Digital Dice

A web-based random number generator using verified algorithms. Required for remote campaigning and to prove acquisition of certain SorC Cards. Fair, transparent, and tamper-evident.

Videos

Access rulebook videos, tutorials, walkthroughs, and official SorC content to help you learn the game and grow as a player or Game Master.

Tavern Lobbies — View Access

Browse open tavern lobbies to find groups and see active campaigns. Full participation requires a SorC Beyond Pro account and passing the appropriate player or GM assessment.

Downloadable Content

Download printable character profile pages, basic SorC Cards, and supplemental reference materials to enhance your home game.

Achievements

Earn badges and awards for reaching milestones and completing unique challenges across Essentia. Basic achievements are available to all accounts.

Community Forums

Join the SorC community — discuss rules, share campaigns, find groups, and earn community points that unlock profile features including surnames at 500 points.

PRO — SorC Beyond

Full digital experience

Tavern Lobbies — Full Access

Gather with players and GMs in persistent online taverns. Look for groups, chat, and use the Wayfarer Compass to teleport between towns or regions. Voice support included. Spectators may observe but cannot communicate with active campaigns.

Private Campaign Rooms

Once a party is assembled in a tavern lobby, the GM can teleport the campaign to a private room where the adventure begins. Each campaign's world is unique — shaped by past decisions, construction, destruction, and player ownership. GMs can keep rooms private or open them to spectators.

Cinematics

Adventure modules include cinematic cues for Game Masters — indicating when to stream audio and image files to the party for immersive storytelling moments.

SorC Ambiance App

Each module has its own companion app for GMs, featuring downloadable images, cut-out cards, music queues, and monster voices to bring every encounter to life.

Call to Arms

Shout or signal for help from other characters near your coordinates. Each ability and spell has parameter limits. If your Wayfarer Compass isn't on cooldown, teleport to tavern lobbies or towns to find NPC hirelings. Your voice carries 1d4 hundred yards.

Character's Home

A customizable digital space for displaying your character's trophies, decorations, achievements, and history. Your legacy made visible.

Trading Posts, Auction House & Stores

Buy, sell, and trade items or characters in dedicated online marketplaces. The Auction House features both player-listed goods and items randomly generated by the development team.

Leaderboards

Track player rankings, achievements, and campaign progress across the entire SorC community. Compete, climb, and make your mark on Essentia's history.

Advanced Achievements

Unlock exclusive Pro-tier badges, rare titles, and campaign awards not available to Basic members.

Profile Customization

Unlock a custom banner, website link, and social media links on your public profile page.
