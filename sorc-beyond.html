SORC Web

SORC Web © is a membership status users receive upon registering their account. SORC Web comes as Basic and Pro Membership statuses, both explained below.

SORC Web is the free digital companion platform for Slayers of Rings & Crowns. Create an account, download the free PDF module to become a Basic member, join the forums, earn Community Points, and unlock features as your reputation in Essentia grows. There are no subscription fees.

For the full SORC Web experience—including Private Campaign Rooms, Home, the SORC Ambiance App, and 8 Essentia Exchange trading slots instead of 3, a physical box set is required. Each box set includes a redemption code that upgrades your account to Pro status.

Online Lobbies support up to 20 potential Players and exist for recruiting and party assembly via text chat only. GMs and Player Characters must each pass their respective role assessment before creating a lobby. Both GMs and PCs can create lobbies, but a GM must be present before a session begins. From within a lobby, a GM can launch a private room of up to 5 Players—this is where the recruited party actually plays, with voice chat, digital dice, and the rest of the session toolset. Joining a lobby you've been recruited into is free—no box set required.

Trials of Combat—PvP arenas, battlegrounds, and coliseum modes—are accessible to all members including Basic. No box set required to compete. There is no physical or mechanical advantage for Pro members in PvP—no pay to win. All combat rewards are earned in-game only. Pro members (box set) do gain access to exclusive cosmetic skins, a longer leaderboard (top 200 vs. top 100 for Basic), a fuller achievements and trophies list, and Practice Mode.

The SORC Lottery is open to all members, Basic and Pro. Raffle tickets are purchased using Community Points—no cash required. Winners receive real prizes, including an annual box set giveaway.

Roles & Membership Status

These are two separate things. Your role describes who you are at the table or in the platform. Your membership status describes what platform features you have access to. One does not determine the other.

Roles

Roles are earned through assessment and reflect your function in the game.

RoleAbbr.Description
Civilian CIV A registered member who has not yet completed a role assessment. Can browse, post in forums, and participate in the community.
Player PC A verified Player who has passed the Player assessment. Can join Online Lobbies and participate in private room sessions as a Player character.
Game Master GM A verified Game Master who has passed the GM assessment. Can host Online Lobbies and run private room sessions launched from them. A GM must be present before any session begins. GMs can message any member directly without a request, they need to reach Players quickly for session coordination.

Membership Status

Membership status is determined by whether you have purchased a box set. It is not tied to your role or your Community Points total.

StatusHow to ObtainDescription
Basic Free-download the official free PDF module Access to community features, forums, free PDF content, digital dice, Tavern Lobbies, and Trials of Combat. No box set required.
Pro Purchase a box set (includes a redemption code) Full platform access including Private Campaign Rooms, Home, the SORC Ambiance App, 8 Essentia Exchange trading slots, and all Pro-exclusive features. The box set is the only path to Pro status.

Example: A GM with Basic status and a PC with Pro status are both valid combinations. Your role is earned through an assessment; your membership is unlocked with a box set purchase.

What Your Points Unlock

Community Points unlock specific platform features and privileges. They do not determine your membership status—Pro is tied exclusively to your box set purchase.

  • 0 pts—All Accounts
    • Character Space and username
    • Forum access—read and post
    • Free PDF module downloads
    • Digital dice (RNG)
    • Browse Tavern Lobbies
    • Private messaging (request-based)
    • Basic Space customization
  • 100 pts—Fellowships
    • Send and receive Fellowship requests
    • View Fellowship member online and in-lobby status
    • Message Fellowship members directly—no request needed between Fellowship members
    • Fellowship activity feed
    • Share media with Fellowship members
    • React and comment on Fellowship posts
  • 250 pts—Extended Basic Features
    • Server-backed character sheets with unique IDs (up to 5 characters, swappable)
    • Verified online RNG—required for remote play

    • Downloadable character sheets and printable cards
    • Basic achievements
    • Leaderboard access

  • 750 pts—Name Prefixes & Suffixes

SORC Cards

SORC uses a comprehensive card-based system to track abilities, equipment, companions, and more. All members can earn and acquire ranked SORC Cards through gameplay, trading, and progression.

Card Reference System

Each card in SORC has a unique reference code in the format MOD###-00x:

Reference codes are displayed in the card's rank color, just as their identifying text in our books and online are displayed. Cards are organized by module, ensuring players always know where to find additional details.

  • MOD = Card prefix
  • ### = Module number (e.g., 001, 002, 003)
  • 00x = Card position within the module (e.g., 001, 002, 003)

Example: A Common Skeleton from Module 1 is referenced as MOD001-001. You can search for this code in the module's downloadable PDF to find full card details, such as; loot, lore, and other additional information.

Our monster compendium, Book of Behemoths, Beasts & Bugs has summarized versions of creatures that lurk within Essentia’s worlds. Although BBBB does not have images of the creatures per reference, there are portraits of many of them throughout the book. Each Mob and Boss Card released in box sets, modules downloadable online has the Mob or Bosses actual image.
