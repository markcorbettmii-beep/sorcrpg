The Story

Pinklings and Hobblings and Brutes… What the…?

Welcome to Essentia, a universe where 13 planets, many moons and a dying star orbit Adoria, the radiant source of all magic. Further away, just past the 9th planet, Tawdry Dwarf, is a dying star that threatens to reign destruction upon anything within its realm.

This is a sci-fantasy setting that blends arcane sorcery with futuristic technology. Knights wield enchanted blades alongside plasma rifles. Starships traverse the void while dragons rule the skies of Zailister, and rifts tear in empty space momentarily allowing shifts between worlds. Ancient prophecies clash with cutting-edge science in a universe where both magic and machinery shape the universes destiny.

The World of Zailister

Your journey begins on Zailister, a lush, life-sustaining world torn apart by an endless war. Hundreds of years after a prophecy that donned true heirs of 19 races, three mighty factions - the Noble, the Feral, and the Purge - have set aside their bitter rivalries to face a common enemy: the Omnè.

The Omnè are desperate invaders from a dying world, and their survival depends on something unthinkable - the death of Tawdry Dwarf itself. If they succeed, the magical balance of Essentia will collapse, plunging all 13 worlds into chaos.

Choose Your Path

In Slayers of Rings § Crowns (SORC), you'll create a character between several unique races - from the cunning Pinklings to the stalwart Hobblings to the mighty Brutes - and forge your your character into a legend.

Will you:

  • Master the arcane arts fueled by Adoria's light?
  • Become a technological innovator on the fringes of the system?
  • Rise as a warrior in the faction wars?
  • Gamble in the world of luck?
  • Empower your character through hard work and craftsmanship? Or become rich through black market and auction house trading?
  • Explore unchartered areas, ancient ruins, and the labyrinths that lead to forbidden hell gates?

A Living Universe

Essentia isn't static - it's a living, evolving universe. Your choices matter. Build your reputation, earn notoriety, and watch as your actions ripple across the 13 worlds. Will you be remembered as a dishonorable frog skinner, or will legends speak of you as a dragon slayer?

Travel between worlds using:

  • Rifts – Tears in reality created by magical surges
  • Portals – Stabilized gateways maintained by powerful mages
  • Starships – Vessels that traverse the void between planets
  • Hell Gates – Forbidden passages with terrible costs
  • Starships – Unexpected abductions placing you in uncharted worlds and even the past and future, both near and far, leaving you and your party to find your way "home"

Go Beyond the Table with SORC Beyond

SORC Beyond extends your adventures into the digital realm with:

  • Character Sheet Pages and SORC Cards – Create and manage digital character sheets and cards online
  • Trials of Combat – Test your skills in PvP arenas, battlegrounds, and coliseum tiers using digital dice, refereed by GMs
  • Auction House & Black Market – Trade rare items and gear
  • Online Lobbies – Find groups and join campaigns
  • Online Boasting – Upload your achievements, Companions, Items, Home, Family and more
