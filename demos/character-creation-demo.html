The Story

Pinklings and Hobblings and Brutes… What the…?

Welcome to Essentia, a universe where 13 planets orbit twin suns—Adoria, the radiant source of all magic, and Taedry Dwarf, a dying star that threatens to unravel reality itself.

This is a sci-fantasy setting that blends arcane sorcery with futuristic technology. Knights wield enchanted blades alongside plasma rifles. Starships traverse the void while mages open rifts between worlds. Ancient prophecies clash with cutting-edge science in a universe where both magic and machinery shape destiny.

The World of Zailister

Your journey begins on Zailister, a lush, life-sustaining world torn apart by an endless war. Three mighty factions—the Noble, the Feral, and the Purge—have set aside their bitter rivalries to face a common enemy: the Omnè.

The Omnè are desperate invaders from a dying world, and their survival depends on something unthinkable—the death of Taedry Dwarf itself. If they succeed, the magical balance of Essentia will collapse, plunging all 13 worlds into chaos.

Choose Your Path

In SorC (Slayers of Rings § Crowns), you'll create a character from dozens of unique races—from the cunning Pinklings to the stalwart Hobblings to the mighty Brutes—and forge your own legend.

Will you:

  • Master the arcane arts fueled by Adoria's light?
  • Become a technological innovator on the fringes of the system?
  • Rise as a warrior in the faction wars?
  • Explore forbidden hell gates and ancient ruins?
  • Trade in the black markets and auction houses of the underworld?

A Living Universe

Essentia isn't static—it's a living, evolving universe. Your choices matter. Build your reputation, earn notoriety, and watch as your actions ripple across the 13 worlds. Will you be remembered as a humble frog skinner, or will legends speak of you as a dragon slayer?

Travel between worlds using:

  • Rifts – Tears in reality created by magical surges
  • Portals – Stabilized gateways maintained by powerful factions
  • Starships – Vessels that traverse the void between planets
  • Hell Gates – Forbidden passages with terrible costs

More Than a Game

SorC Beyond extends your adventure into the digital realm with:

  • Character Profile Pages – Create and manage digital character sheets
  • Trials of Combat – Test your skills in PvP arenas, battlegrounds, and coliseum tiers
  • Auction House & Black Market – Trade rare items and gear
  • Online Lobbies – Find groups and join campaigns
