Druid - Class Tree

Druid (Not Playable in Starter Set)

Ancient nature-bound spellcasters who draw power from the living world itself, Druids channel celestial forces, primal animal transformation, and dream-state consciousness to protect and reshape the natural order, equally capable of becoming the predator they summon, calling down starlight as a weapon, or walking through enemy minds while their body sits motionless in the wild.

Alignment: Path and Branch Dependent

Higher Power: Path Dependent (see Path below)

Druid's Paths individually restrict by alignment from LVL 4 on, see each Path above for its specific Alignment.

To learn a Path's Abilities, or any of its three Branches', your own alignment need only sit inside this Class's Spectrum.

To draw a Path's Higher Power bonus you must hold that Path's exact base Alignment, and that same base is what its three Branches require (see Alignment, pg. 2).

Favored Races:
Firbolg +5, Elf (Common)† +5, Dwarf (Common)† +5, Human† +5, Halfling† +5, Elf (Sylvan) +5, Yawnie +5, Drevane +5, Leshy +5

†Elf (Common), Dwarf, Human, and Halfling Playable with Starter Set.

Averse Races: Omnè -5

Refer to Races for details on favored and averse paths and branches.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Verdant Attunement, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Wild Shape Ascendant, Once Per Day the Druid fully transforms into a beast form matching their terrain, gaining a Life pool equal to their Constitution SCR and the beast's natural attacks for the rest of the encounter.

Extra 1d6 + LVL nature damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Druids can Flourish their stave to entangle a target, reducing their movement speed by half.

Druids get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter, while in natural terrain.

Druids get +4 plus their KNOWLEDGE SCR on nature-related Knowledge checks.

Druid Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Celestial - A sky-focused Druid who channels the sun, moon, and stars into their magic.
  Alignment: Moral
  Observance: Service and care. Devotion is spent on other people rather than performed.
  Will not share a Character with: Evil and Amoral.
  The Higher Power bonus requires holding Moral exactly, and that same base carries through all three of its Branches below.
  a. Lunar - A Celestial Druid whose power swells and fades with the moon's own phases.
  b. Solar - A Celestial Druid who channels the sun's raw, burning light directly into battle.
  c. Starduster - A Celestial Druid who scatters starlight to blind, guide, or heal as needed.
ii. Dreamcryer - A Druid who walks the dream-realm, drawing power and secrets from sleep and nightmare alike.
  Alignment: Amoral
  Observance: Bargain and price. Devotion is a contract kept, whatever it was for.
  Will not share a Character with: Moral.
  The Higher Power bonus requires holding Amoral exactly, and that same base carries through all three of its Branches below.
  a. Dreamcaster - A Dreamcryer who reaches into allies' sleeping minds to mend and steady them.
  b. Lorekeeper - A Dreamcryer who gathers secrets from the dream-realm most will never wake to remember.
  c. Nightmare - A Dreamcryer who drags enemies into waking nightmares of their own fear.
iii. Hyborian - A primal Druid who draws on beast-spirits and shapeshifting to fight alongside nature's fiercest forms.
  Alignment: Ambivalent
  Observance: None. Ambivalent Paths answer to no god and no patron at all.
  No Higher Power, so no exact alignment is required at this Path or at any of its three Branches below.
  a. Ravager - A Hyborian Druid who abandons restraint entirely for a beast's full, savage fury.
  b. Shifter - A Hyborian Druid who freely reshapes their own body to match the fight at hand.
  c. Ursidae - A Hyborian Druid who transforms into a bear at will, tanking with raw strength and endurance instead of armor.
