Background

Gravak grew up in the slums of Ashmark, the forgotten son of a blacksmith and a street merchant. His father was killed in a gang war when Gravak was 8; his mother abandoned him to the streets to fend for himself. He survived through theft, pickpocketing, and contract work for local crime lords. Now 45, he's looking to escape the gutter life and find wealth, redemption, or at least a new identity in the adventuring trade.

Mission Statement

Primary Goal: Earn enough wealth and reputation to buy land and establish a legitimate business (a trading post or safe house) where he's no longer beholden to crime lords.

Personal Quest: Uncover the truth about his father's death and settle old debts with the gang lords of Ashmark.
