Marksman - Class Tree

Marksman (Playable in Starter Set)

Precision-focused ranged combatants who dominate at distance through superior positioning, weapon mastery, and tactical patience, striking long before enemies can close the gap through either heavy bow work, black powder weaponry, or the swift shortbow mobility of a Ranger who turns natural terrain into cover, an edge on the hunt, and a marked shot the moment prey steps into the open.

‡ Abilities from their respective trees are summarized below. Ability Cards are released with Module and their expansions, supporting various Character Levels, and list the DIFS for any check an Ability requires. Refer pg. 6 for more on Ability Cards.

Note: Main Class baseline stats, Armament requirements and thresholds, and other Class features change depending on the Classes Tier, Path or Branch as noted in each respective Sub-Class.

Alignment: Path and Branch Dependent

Higher Power: Path Dependent (see Path below)

Marksman's Paths individually restrict by alignment from LVL 4 on, see each Path above for its specific Alignment.

To learn a Path's Abilities, or any of its three Branches', your own alignment need only sit inside this Class's Spectrum.

To draw a Path's Higher Power bonus you must hold that Path's exact base Alignment, and that same base is what its three Branches require (see Alignment, pg. 2).

Favored Races:
Elf (Common)† +5, Dwarf (Common)† +5, Human† +5, Halfling† +5, Wood Elf +5, Brownling +5, Ratkin +5, Yawnie +5, Omnè +5

†Elf (Common), Dwarf, Human, and Halfling Playable with Starter Set.

Averse Races: None recorded.

Refer to Races for details on favored and averse paths and branches.

Passive Abilities:
These passive Abilities belong to the Main Class alone, granted once and active regardless of Tier, Path, or Branch; they are not re-granted, replaced, or duplicated at any Path or Branch.

Steady Hand, lasts 6 hours (must be recast every 6 hours to stay active). No mana cost for Main Class Abilities.

Deadeye, Once Per Day the Marksman lines up the perfect shot, dealing damage equal to twice their Dexterity SCR to one target, ignoring half their Defense.

Extra 1d6 + LVL piercing damage on Critical Hits.
Once per encounter, Marksmen can Flourish their ranged weapon that lowers their target's Defense at a number equal to their Dexterity SCR.

Marksmen get +5 feet their base movement speed, once per encounter.

Marksmen get +4 plus their DEXTERITY SCR on Perception checks.

Marksman Paths and Branches
Each Path and Branch has full descriptions in their Ability section.
i. Bowman - A bow-focused Marksman who favors precision and range over raw firepower.
  Alignment: Ambivalent
  Observance: None. Ambivalent Paths answer to no god and no patron at all.
  No Higher Power, so no exact alignment is required at this Path or at any of its three Branches below.
  a. Archer - A Bowman devoted to the bow above all, mastering range and power with practiced patience.
  b. Bandit - A Bowman who strikes fast from ambush before melting back into cover.
  c. Outrider - A Bowman who fights from horseback, striking and repositioning in the same motion.
ii. Gunner - A black-powder Marksman who trades a bow's patience for a gun's immediate stopping power.
  Alignment: Amoral
  Observance: Bargain and price. Devotion is a contract kept, whatever it was for.
  Will not share a Character with: Moral.
  The Higher Power bonus requires holding Amoral exactly, and that same base carries through all three of its Branches below.
  a. Duelist - A Gunner who favors precise, close-range engagements over a firefight.
  b. Gun Slinger - A Gunner who trades a rifle's power for a pair of pistols' speed.
  c. Wrangler - A Gunner who pairs a gun with a lasso or whip to control the fight itself.
iii. Ranger - A frontier Marksman who holds ground and defends territory as readily as they strike from range.
  Alignment: Moral
  Observance: Service and care. Devotion is spent on other people rather than performed.
  Will not share a Character with: Evil and Amoral.
  The Higher Power bonus requires holding Moral exactly, and that same base carries through all three of its Branches below.
  a. Forest Guardian - A Ranger who takes the fight head-on, armored heavily enough to hold the frontline.
  b. Keeper - A Ranger who holds their ground, defending territory rather than chasing it.
  c. Wood Warden - A Ranger built for patrol and pursuit, striking and drawing weapons in one motion.

Armor Thresholds
Grade: Medium or Light (Chain, Leather).

Paths and Branches unlock various Armaments not available for Class Tier Abilities.

A Path or Branch's Weapon/Hand Proficiency profile includes every weapon already available to the Main Class, plus any new weapons that Path or Branch unlocks. A Path or Branch's Armor Threshold also rises without an Armament Penalty, typically half a Grade step above the Tier before it.

Armament Thresholds allow the given Grade and anything lighter. If Armor that is heavier than the Marksman's Threshold is worn, Characters suffer the ensuing Penalties to the Armor's SCR (see Armament Penalty, Basic Rules pg. 6).
