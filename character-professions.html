Character Professions

Professions represent specialized skills and expertise your Character develops through focused practice and dedication. Each Profession is paired with a corresponding Talent Tree, forming a symbiotic relationship where talents provide foundational abilities and professions deepen specialization and expertise through their core skills.

All Character Professions are organized into four categories, each representing a distinct sphere of expertise and economic function. Within each category, practitioners develop their craft through apprenticeship, journeyman work, and eventual mastery. Professions grant access to specialized equipment, exclusive NPC relationships, and economic networks that expand as your Character advances.

A. Crafting

Crafting professions transform raw materials into finished goods through skill, knowledge, and technique. Crafters work with their hands and minds to create items of utility and beauty: food, weapons, armor, potions, jewelry, runes, and enchanted objects. Crafters are the artisans and makers of civilization, providing essential goods and luxury items that sustain communities and enable adventurers.
