S O R C

SORC Universal d100 System

Core Rules: How the Universal System Works

  1. Find the Base Success Rate:
    • Every action starts with a base percentage set by your sheet or the GM (e.g., 50% for an attack, 40% for a crafting attempt).
  2. Add/Subtract Modifiers:
    • Add bonuses from attributes, talents, gear, buffs, cards, etc.
    • Subtract penalties from debuffs, enemy defenses, or circumstances.
  3. Calculate Final Success Rate:
    (Base + all bonuses – all penalties = Final %)
  4. Roll d100:
    • Roll two ten-sided dice (percentile dice) for a result from 1 to 100.
  5. Compare Result:
    • If your roll is equal to or less than your Final Success Rate, you succeed!
    • If higher, you fail.

How Each Subsystem Works

1. Combat

Use your weapon or combat skill's base % to hit.
Add attribute, gear, talent, and buff bonuses.
Subtract enemy defense, debuffs, or circumstances.

Example:
Mira swings her sword at a goblin.
Base Hit Chance: 60%
Physique Attribute: +10%
Sword Mastery Talent: +8%
Magic Sword: +5%
Party Buff: +10%
Goblin’s Defense: –15%
Total: 60 + 10 + 8 + 5 + 10 – 15 = 78%
Mira rolls d100. A 53 means a hit!

2. Talents

Talents give flat % bonuses to specific actions, allow rerolls, or grant special effects.

Example:
Mira uses "Quick Reflexes" talent (+12% to dodge rolls).
She must dodge a trap:
Base Dodge: 40%
Agility: +15%
Quick Reflexes Talent: +12%
Debuffed by sticky mud: –10%
Total: 40 + 15 + 12 – 10 = 57%
Mira rolls d100. A 41 means she dodges the trap!

3. Professions & Skills (Including Crafting)

Professions (Alchemist, Blacksmith, Thief, etc.) grant access to specialized skills.
Crafting, gathering, and profession actions use base %, plus modifiers.
Crafting is aligned to Artistry (not Intellect).

Example:
Mira is an Alchemist making a potion.
Base Alchemy: 40%
Artistry Attribute: +12%
Alchemist’s Kit: +8%
Rare ingredients: +5%
Rushed job: –10%
Total: 40 + 12 + 8 + 5 – 10 = 55%
Rolls a 29 - success!

4. Attributes/Traits

Attributes (Physique, Agility, Artistry, etc.) add % to relevant actions.
Traits may resist effects, empower skills, or give unique bonuses.

Example:
Mira resists poison.
Base Resist: 25%
Fortitude Attribute: +13%
"Iron Stomach" Trait: +10%
Strong poison: –15%
Total: 25 + 13 + 10 – 15 = 33%
She rolls 27 - resists the poison!

5. Vitality: Life, Mana, Stamina, and Endurance

  • Life (HP): Health points; lost when taking damage.
  • Mana: Used to cast abilities and spells; spent and regenerated.
  • Stamina: Determines if the character has energy to perform an action. Every action costs a different amount of stamina; if you lack stamina, you cannot act.
  • Endurance: The total number of actions a character can take in a day ("action cap"); tracked with tokens.
  • Spirit: Determines the rate at which Life, Mana, and Stamina are regenerated.
  • Extent: Sets the cap (maximum) for each Vitality type (how many tokens you can have for each).

Example:
Mira has:
Life: 10 tokens (Extent cap), regenerates 2 per hour (Spirit stat)
Mana: 5 tokens (Extent cap), regenerates 1 per hour (Spirit stat)
Stamina: 8 tokens (Extent cap), regenerates 3 per hour (Spirit stat)
Endurance: 15 tokens (Extent cap), resets at dawn
During combat:
- Mira attacks (costs 1 stamina, 1 endurance)
- Mira casts a spell (costs 2 mana, 1 stamina, 1 endurance)
- If Mira runs low on stamina or endurance, she cannot perform further actions until she regenerates (via Spirit rate) or the next day for endurance.

6. Cards

Cards can grant % bonuses, rerolls, or automatic successes for actions.

Example:
Mira uses a "Lucky Strike" card (+20% to next attack).
Base Attack: 60%
Attribute: +10%
Lucky Strike Card: +20%
Total: 60 + 10 + 20 = 90%
Rolls a 94 - misses, but could have succeeded with a reroll card!

7. Challenge Resolution (Generic Actions)

Any non-standard action (climbing, persuasion, resisting effects, etc.) uses base %, plus modifiers.

Example:
Mira tries to climb a wall.
Base Climb: 40%
Physique: +10%
Rope: +5%
Rainy weather: –10%
Total: 40 + 10 + 5 – 10 = 45%
Rolls a 44 - succeeds!

8. Gear/Equipment

Gear provides flat % bonuses, special abilities, or penalties.

Example:
Mira’s enchanted boots: +8% to all Stealth actions.
Base Stealth: 50%
Agility: +12%
Boots: +8%
Heavy rain: –5%
Total: 50 + 12 + 8 – 5 = 65%
Rolls a 73 - fails to sneak.

9. Buffs & Debuffs

Buffs increase your Success Rate or other stats; debuffs decrease them.

Example:
Mira is hit by a "Slow" spell (–15% to dodge).
Base Dodge: 40%
Agility: +10%
Party Buff: +10%
Slow Debuff: –15%
Total: 40 + 10 + 10 – 15 = 45%
Rolls a 31 - dodges successfully!

10. Defense/Evasion

When defending or evading, you calculate Success Rate as with other actions.
Enemy’s attack may subtract from your chance.

Example:
Mira tries to evade a fireball.
Base Evasion: 30%
Agility: +10%
"Fireproof Cloak": +10%
Enemy’s Aim Talent: –15%
Total: 30 + 10 + 10 – 15 = 35%
Rolls a 36 - gets hit!

11. Leveling/Progression

Leveling up increases attributes, talents, skills, and thus your chances (Success Rates) for future rolls. May also increase Extent or Spirit.

Example:
Mira levels up and increases her Physique by 2 (+4% Success Rate for melee and physical checks), and her Extent by 2 (gains 2 max stamina tokens).

12. Magic/Spellcasting

Spell success uses base %, with modifiers for attributes, talents, gear, buffs, and debuffs. Uses Mana and Stamina.

Example:
Mira casts "Lightning Bolt."
Base Spell Success: 55%
Magic Attribute: +14%
Staff: +6%
Enemy Magic Resist: –10%
Total: 55 + 14 + 6 – 10 = 65%
Rolls a 58 - spell succeeds! Mira spends 2 Mana and 1 Stamina.

13. Social/Interaction

Social actions (persuasion, intimidation, negotiation) use base %, plus modifiers.

Example:
Mira tries to persuade a guard.
Base Persuasion: 35%
Charisma: +12%
"Silver Tongue" Talent: +10%
Guard suspicious: –10%
Total: 35 + 12 + 10 – 10 = 47%
Rolls a 17 - success!

Vitality Subsystem: Detailed Rules & Examples

Vitality: Four Resources

  • Life (HP): Represents health. If reduced to zero, the character is incapacitated or dying. Lost when taking damage; restored by healing, rest, or regeneration.
  • Mana: Used for casting spells or powering magical items/abilities. Spent as required by the action; restored by rest, potions, or regeneration.
  • Stamina: Measures current energy for actions. Every action costs a set amount of stamina (see action cost table below). If stamina is 0, the character cannot act until it is regenerated.
  • Endurance: Limits how many actions a character can perform in a day (“action cap”). Every action also costs 1 endurance, unless otherwise stated. Endurance resets at dawn or after a long rest.

Spirit & Extent

  • Spirit: Governs the rate at which Life, Mana, and Stamina regenerate (e.g., tokens per hour). Example: Spirit 3 = Regenerate 3 tokens/hour (can be split between Life, Mana, Stamina as desired).
  • Extent: Determines the maximum number of tokens a character can have in each pool (Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance). Example: Extent 10 = 10 max tokens in each pool.

Action Cost Table (Sample)

Action Stamina Cost Mana Cost Endurance Cost
Basic Attack 1 0 1
Dodge/Evade 1 0 1
Cast Simple Spell 1 2 1
Cast Major Spell 2 4 1
Craft Item 2 0 1
Skill Use (Pick Lock/Craft) 1 0 1
Sprint/Run 2 0 1
Social Action 1 0 1

Adjust these values to match your game’s balance and action intensity.

Regeneration (Spirit)

  • At the end of each hour (or other time interval), regain a number of tokens equal to your Spirit.
  • You may divide regeneration freely among Life, Mana, and Stamina (not Endurance).
  • Example: Spirit 3 lets you regain any mix (2 Stamina, 1 Mana; or 3 Life, etc.).

Endurance

  • Maximum Endurance is set by Extent.
  • Endurance tokens are spent each time you take an action (unless specifically free).
  • When you run out of Endurance, you must rest (usually until the next dawn) before taking further actions.
  • Endurance does NOT regenerate from Spirit - only rest resets it.

In-Play Examples

Example 1: Combat Turn
Mira’s stats:
Life: 10/10 (Extent 10)
Mana: 4/6 (Extent 6)
Stamina: 5/8 (Extent 8)
Endurance: 9/12 (Extent 12)
Spirit: 3
Mira’s Turn:
- She attacks (costs 1 Stamina, 1 Endurance): Stamina: 4/8, Endurance: 8/12
- She casts a spell (costs 1 Stamina, 2 Mana, 1 Endurance): Stamina: 3/8, Mana: 2/6, Endurance: 7/12
At the end of an hour: Mira regenerates 3 tokens (from Spirit). She chooses to regain 2 Stamina and 1 Mana.

Example 2: Crafting
Mira (Alchemist) wants to brew a potion:
Action: Craft Item (costs 2 Stamina, 1 Endurance)
Current: Stamina 3/8, Endurance 6/12
She spends the resources: Stamina: 1/8, Endurance: 5/12
Her Artistry score and tools boost her chance (see Professions & Skills).

Example 3: Running Out of Resources
Later in the day, Mira has: Stamina: 0/8, Endurance: 2/12
She cannot take further actions until she regenerates at least 1 Stamina (waits for Spirit regen, drinks a potion, or receives magical aid).
If Endurance hits 0, she cannot act at all until the next dawn or a full rest.

Example 4: Life & Mana in Danger
Mira is wounded in a fight: Life: 2/10
She’s healed by a friend for 4 points: Life: 6/10
She regenerates 2 more tokens naturally in the next hour due to Spirit: Life: 8/10

Example 5: Extent & Leveling
Upon leveling up, Mira increases her Extent by 2:
New Life cap: 12
New Mana cap: 8
New Stamina cap: 10
New Endurance cap: 14

Example 6: Spirit Variations
Mira’s Spirit increases from 3 to 4.
She now regenerates 4 total tokens per hour (any mix of Life, Mana, Stamina).

Universal d100 Resolution (Quick Recap)

  1. Find base % for the action.
  2. Add/subtract all modifiers.
  3. Roll d100.
  4. Succeed if roll ≤ total %.
  5. Spend the required tokens.

Optional: Tracking Tokens

  • Many groups use poker chips, coins, beads, or a tracker sheet for Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance.
  • Remove tokens as you spend them; add them back as you regenerate or heal.

Universal Summary

  • Every action, in every subsystem, follows the same process:
  1. Find the base % chance.
  2. Add/subtract all modifiers.
  3. Roll d100.
  4. Succeed if roll ≤ total %.

For actions, check stamina and endurance tokens. For spells, check mana. For life-threatening events, check HP tokens. Spirit and Extent govern regeneration and cap.

What Makes this System Unique?

  • Universal d100 Resolution: Many games use d100 (percentile) systems (Call of Cthulhu, RuneQuest, Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay), but they don’t combine every subsystem (combat, magic, social, crafting, etc.) into one unified mechanic with explicit stacking modifiers.
  • Vitality as Multiple Token Tracks (Life, Mana, Stamina, Endurance): Most games split these into separate pools, but rarely as “tokens” with caps governed by attributes (Extent), and regeneration rate governed by another stat (Spirit).
  • Endurance as Daily Action Cap: This is very rare. Most TTRPGs let players act as much as they want per day, limited only by resources (mana, spell slots, HP), not a physical “number of actions” pool.
  • Stamina as Energy for Actions: Some games (like Shadowrun or GURPS) track fatigue or energy, but don’t tie it to every action or split it from HP, Mana, etc., with separate regeneration and cap mechanics.
  • Spirit and Extent: The use of two attributes - one for regeneration rate (Spirit) and one for cap (Extent) - is extremely rare and not seen in major TTRPGs.
  • Profession/Crafting Linked to Artistry: Most games tie crafting to Intelligence or a skill, rarely to a specific “Artistry” attribute.
  • All Actions, All Subsystems, One Process: Most games have separate systems for combat, magic, social, crafting, etc. Yours uses one process for everything.

Closest Comparisons

  • Lineage 2M (the MMO): Your system is inspired by Lineage 2M’s logic. MMOs often use behind-the-scenes stamina, mana, HP, and action caps, but TTRPGs don’t.
  • Video Game Adaptations: Some homebrew systems inspired by video games try to mimic stamina/action point pools, but none match this structure.
  • Genesys (Fantasy Flight Games): Uses a universal dice mechanic, but not universal resource pools and token caps.

In Summary

Your SORC system is unique in the TTRPG field.
It combines universal d100 resolution, modular stacking modifiers, and a token-based vitality system with action caps, energy checks, regeneration rates, and attribute-governed caps.
This offers a MMO-style, highly tactical, and resource-driven experience not found in other TTRPGs.
